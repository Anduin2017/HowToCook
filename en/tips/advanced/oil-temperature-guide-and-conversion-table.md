# Oil Temperature Guide and Conversion Table

* Oil at 120&deg;C-140&deg;C: suitable for soft-frying[^1] and velvet stir-frying[^2]. Put a chopstick into the oil: almost no bubbles form around it, no blue smoke, no sound, and the oil is calm.
* Oil at 150&deg;C-160&deg;C: the best cooking temperature. Put a chopstick into the oil: a few oil bubbles appear around it, there is slight blue smoke, and the oil rolls from the edges toward the center.
* Oil at 160&deg;C-180&deg;C: suitable for browning and crisp-frying. Put a chopstick into the oil: lots of blue smoke rises, while the surface of the oil is actually fairly calm.
* (Note) It is best to buy an infrared thermometer for oil. Be careful when using a probe thermometer.

> In Chinese online video tutorials, oil temperature is often described as "n-tenths hot" (几成油温), relative to "ten-tenths", which is 300&deg;C.
> That is, the temperature at n-tenths is T_n = 30n [degree Celsius]
> Temperature conversion formulas:
> C = 5/9 *(F - 32)
> or
> F = 9/5*C + 32
