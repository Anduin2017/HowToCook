# Steaming

## Methods

### Steamer Pot

A steamer pot has multiple tiers: the bottom holds water, and the steam produced when the water boils heats the food in the tiers above.

Add a suitable amount of water to the bottom of the steamer — put the food in the upper steamer tier — heat the steamer on the stove

### Wok

If you don't have a steamer, only an ordinary wok (not a flat frying pan), you can put a trivet in the bottom and add enough water to get an effect similar to a steamer.

Add plenty of water to the bottom of the wok — put in the trivet — place the food on the trivet — turn on the heat and cover

## Notes

* Because the heat source is steam, the bottom of food in the lower steamer tiers may get wet. Put a steamer cloth under the food to avoid this. A simple rack made of chopsticks also works.
* You can set a timer on a smart device to remind you to turn off the heat, so you don't forget and let it boil dry.
* When using a steamer, keep an eye on how much water is left at the bottom, **especially with a wok**, to avoid it boiling dry, which is a safety hazard.
* (Optional) When steaming in a wok, you can put a steaming plate on top of the trivet.
