# Boiling

## Process

Pour water into the pot — turn on the heat and set the pot on the flame — once the water boils (rolling, with lots of bubbles), add the ingredients

### Notes

* Covering the pot while heating speeds it up. **But this risks boiling over**. With continued heating, liquid churning too vigorously can spill out of the pot—that's boiling over.
* **If it's about to boil over, immediately turn down the heat and lift the lid.**
* To heat faster while avoiding boil-overs, leave the lid half open as a vent; or turn down the heat later on and keep an eye on the pot.
* Depending on the recipe, ingredients can also go into cold water. However, the water then takes longer to boil.
