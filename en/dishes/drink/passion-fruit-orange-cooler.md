# How to Make Passion Fruit Orange Cooler

This is a sweet, tart and refreshing summer drink combining the tropical aroma of passion fruit with the fresh sweetness of orange, paired with jasmine green tea or sparkling soda water, for distinct layers of flavor that are icy and cleansing. It's rich in vitamin C, which helps boost immunity and nourish the skin. There are several steps—marinating, brewing tea and juicing—but the difficulty is moderate and beginners can give it a try. The passion fruit needs to be marinated a day ahead, so the total time is about 24 hours, but the actual hands-on time is only about 30 minutes.

## Ingredients and Tools

- Ingredients:
  - Passion fruit
  - Orange
  - Jasmine green tea leaves / sparkling soda water (pick one)
  - Granulated sugar
  - Ice cubes
  - Honey (optional)
  - Mint leaves or other greens (optional, for garnish)
- Tools
  - Manual citrus juicer

## Calculation

- For the jasmine green tea version, one glass, about 380 ml
  - Orange 1 (about 200 grams, fist-sized)
  - Jasmine green tea leaves 3~6 grams
  - Boiling water 150 ml
  - Ice cubes at least 160 grams

- Marinated passion fruit (since small amounts are hard to make, this is enough for two drinks)
  - Passion fruit 3
  - Granulated sugar 30 grams
  - Honey 10 grams (if you don't have any, substitute 5 grams of granulated sugar)

## Steps

1. Marinating the passion fruit (since small amounts are hard to make, this is enough for two drinks)

  - Scoop out the pulp of three passion fruits (don't miss the juice inside either) into a container
  - Weigh out 30 grams of granulated sugar and add it
  - Weigh out 10 grams of honey and add it (if you don't have any, substitute 5 grams of granulated sugar)
  - Stir well
  - Seal the container with plastic wrap and marinate in the refrigerator for 12~24 hours

2. Making the jasmine green tea (recommended ratio => tea : water : ice = 1~2 : 50 : 30)

  - Weigh out 3~6 grams of tea leaves into a container, add 150 ml of boiling water, and steep for 6 minutes
  - Add 90 grams of ice to the steeped tea to cool it; stir to speed up cooling
  - Once the ice has melted, strain out the tea leaves to get about 240 ml of jasmine green tea

3. Preparing the orange (can be done while the tea steeps)

  - Cut the orange in half (**with the knife perpendicular to the line between the orange's two ends**), and cut one slice from one of the halves to set aside
  - Cut the orange in half again, juice the 4 resulting orange wedges with the juicer into a container, and set aside

4. Mixing

  - Choose a glass; a clear glass with a capacity of 350~400 ml is recommended
  - Put 70 grams of ice in the bottom of the glass and pour in all the orange juice
  - Place the orange slice you set aside against the inside of the glass
  - Spoon one drink's worth of marinated passion fruit over the ice
  - Slowly pour in jasmine green tea until the glass is full, to create layers
  - Place the garnish greens on the surface (optional)

Stir well and enjoy

## Additional Notes

If you don't have or don't like jasmine green tea, you can use sparkling soda water instead and skip making the tea
