# How to Make B-52 Shot

This cocktail has distinct layers: first the strength of vodka, then the sweet aftertaste of coffee and cream. Drinking it lit gives a unique fire-and-ice experience; it's a classic creative cocktail. Coffee liqueur and Baileys provide a little caffeine and cream, but nutritionally it's mostly alcohol. You need to practice layering and lighting, which is slightly tricky for beginners, and the whole process takes only about 5 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 80 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Kahlúa coffee liqueur
- Baileys Irish Cream
- Skyy vodka
- Bar spoon
- Liqueur glass
- Lighter

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Kahlúa 10ml
- Baileys Irish Cream 10ml
- Skyy vodka 10ml

## Steps

1. Pour Kahlúa into the bottom of the liqueur glass up to 1/3 full. (10ml)
2. Slowly pour the Baileys over the back of the bar spoon, also 1/3 (10ml). Pour slowly to keep the layers distinct. (If you pour too fast, the cream liqueur will mix with the coffee liqueur)
3. Finally pour the Skyy vodka on top (10ml)
4. Warm the rim of the glass with the lighter
5. The last step, light it: see the little pale blue flame?

## Additional Notes

- To drink it, bars these days usually provide a straw: insert it while the flame is still burning and quickly drink it all in one go.
- Because the straw is so thin and there isn't enough oxygen inside it, the flame goes out, so don't worry. You'll feel a fiery heat run down your throat all the way to your stomach, followed by a long sweet coffee-cream aftertaste. Very exciting and delicious.
