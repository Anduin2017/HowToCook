# How to Make Gin and Tonic

This is a classic gin cocktail: refreshing and slightly bitter, with the bright tartness of lemon and the fine bubbles of tonic water, light and easy to drink. Although it mainly provides water and calories from a little alcohol, the fresh lemon juice adds vitamin C and a lift. It's very simple; beginners can master it easily, and it takes only about 5 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 205 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

Ingredients

- Gin
- Tonic water
- Lemon
- Ice cubes
- Fresh greens (optional, for garnish)

Tools

- Manual citrus juicer

## Calculation

One glass, about 350 ml (including ice)

- Gin 30~40 ml
- Tonic water 1 can
- Lemon 1
- Ice cubes 100 grams

## Steps

Preparing the lemon

1. Cut the lemon in half (**with the knife perpendicular to the line between the lemon's two ends**), and cut one slice from one of the halves to set aside
2. Cut the lemon in half again, juice the 4 resulting lemon wedges with the juicer into a container, and set aside

Mixing

1. Choose a glass; a clear glass with a capacity of 350~400 ml is recommended
2. Put 100 grams of ice in the bottom of the glass
3. Pour in 30~40 ml of gin
4. Pour in 15 ml of lemon juice (add more, or all of it, if you like it sour)
5. Stir well with a spoon
6. Place the lemon slice you set aside
7. Slowly pour tonic water down the side of the glass until full (don't pour it onto the ice, to avoid losing the fizz)
8. Gently lift the spoon up and down to mix (don't stir in circles, to avoid losing the fizz)
9. Place the garnish greens on the surface (optional)
