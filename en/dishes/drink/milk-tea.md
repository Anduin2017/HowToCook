# How to Make Milk Tea

This is a home-style drink easily made with black tea and milk powder. The aromas of tea and milk blend together for a smooth, warm drink. The tea polyphenols in black tea, plus the protein and calcium in milk, give you a lift and everyday nutrition. It's very simple—beginners can get it right the first time—and you can be drinking homemade milk tea in only about half an hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 81 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Black tea bags (Lipton Yellow Label recommended)
- Whole milk powder or evaporated milk
- A cup, such as a measuring cup, ceramic mug or thermos

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Black tea bags 2 (about 4g)
- Milk powder 11-12g
- Sugar 5-7g

## Steps

1. Put 2 black tea bags in the cup and add 180-200mL of **boiling water**.
2. **Wait 20 - 30 minutes**.
3. Weigh out 11-12g of milk powder and 5-7g of sugar and add each to the liquid from the previous step.
4. Stir well and drink.

## Additional Notes

- After adding the boiling water, keep the inside of the cup as warm as possible, for example by using a cup with a small opening or covering it with a lid. When it's cold, measuring with a measuring cup may let the boiling water cool before it reaches the tea, so you can skip the measuring cup and just estimate the volume.
- When the waiting time is up, you can lift or stir the tea bags so the tea steeps more evenly.
