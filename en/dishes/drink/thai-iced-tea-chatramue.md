# How to Make Thai Iced Tea (ChaTraMue)

![Finished Thai iced tea](../../../dishes/drink/泰国手标红茶/泰国手标红茶.jpg)

This is a classic milk tea from the streets of Thailand, made with ChaTraMue ("Number One Hand") tea: rich tea aroma, a creamy smooth texture, and sweet milkiness from condensed milk and milk. The combination of tea and milk provides a little protein and calcium, but it's high in sugar, best as a cooling, thirst-quenching drink. The steps are simple, but you need to strain the tea repeatedly and wait for a long chill; the whole process takes about 4 hours 20 minutes, and beginners can do it with a bit of patience.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 185 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Water
- Tea powder
- Condensed milk
- White sugar
- Milk
- Kitchen scale
- Measuring container
- Hong Kong-style milk tea strainer bag

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Water (600cc)
- Tea powder (20g)
- White sugar (24g)
- Milk (18ml)
- Condensed milk (24g)

## Steps

1. Bring 600cc of water to a boil over high heat
2. Put 20g of tea powder in the strainer bag and pour the boiling water through the bag, straining it 20 times
3. Use the scale to weigh 24g of condensed milk, 24g of white sugar and 18ml of milk into a jug of at least 1000ml
4. Pour the strained tea into the jug and stir until the sugar dissolves
5. Put the jug in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours
6. You can add 6-8 ice cubes before drinking
