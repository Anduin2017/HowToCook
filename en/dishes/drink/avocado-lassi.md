# How to Make Avocado Lassi

This is an innovative drink based on lassi, the traditional Indian yogurt drink: creamy and smooth, sweet without being cloying, especially good for breakfast or an afternoon pick-me-up. Avocado is rich in monounsaturated fatty acids and dietary fiber, and with the high-quality protein and probiotics in yogurt, one glass keeps you full for a long time. It's extremely simple and foolproof even for beginners, taking only about 5 minutes from prep to enjoying.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 392 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Plain yogurt
- Ripe avocado
- Honey
- Cold milk
- Cardamom powder (optional)
- Mint leaves or chopped nuts (optional, for garnish)
- Blender

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Plain yogurt 120 ml
- Ripe avocado 1
- Honey 1 tablespoon (adjust to taste)
- Cold milk 60 ml
- Cardamom powder 1 pinch (optional)

## Steps

1. Cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, peel off the skin, and cut the flesh into small pieces.
2. Add 120ml of plain yogurt, the avocado pieces, 1 tablespoon of honey and 60ml of cold milk to the blender.
3. (Optional) Add a small pinch of cardamom powder for a distinctive flavor.
4. Start the blender and **blend for about 1 minute** until completely smooth and rich.
5. Pour into a glass.
6. (Optional) Top with a few mint leaves or sprinkle with 5g of chopped nuts as a garnish.

## Additional Notes

- The avocado must be fully ripe to give the drink its creamy texture and color.
- If you like it colder, use a bit less milk and blend in a few ice cubes.
