# How to Make Mandarin Jasmine Tea

![Mandarin jasmine tea - preview image 1](../../../dishes/drink/耙耙柑茶/citrus-tea.jpg)

Papagan tea is a fruit tea combining the sweetness of papagan mandarins (a Sichuan citrus variety) with the floral aroma of jasmine. It's refreshing with a sweet aftertaste, a creative mixed drink not tied to any particular cuisine. Papagan mandarins are rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber, and paired with green tea it's fresh and invigorating. It's very simple and beginner-friendly, taking only about 15 minutes from brewing the tea to blending.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 81 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Ingredients:
  - Papagan mandarins (see Additional Notes for substitutes)
  - Jasmine green tea
  - Ice cubes
  - [Simple syrup](../condiment/simple-syrup.md) (optional)
- Tools
  - Blender

## Calculation

One glass, about 300 ml

- Papagan mandarins 1~2 (at least 200 grams)
- Jasmine green tea 2~4 grams
- Ice cubes 60 grams
- 1 : 1 simple syrup 10 grams (optional)

## Steps

1. Making the jasmine green tea (recommended ratio => tea : water : ice = 1~2 : 50 : 30)

  - Weigh out 2~4 grams of tea leaves into a container, add 100 ml of boiling water, and steep for 6 minutes
  - Add 60 grams of ice to the steeped tea to cool it; stir to speed up cooling
  - Once the ice has melted, strain out the tea leaves to get about 160 ml of jasmine green tea

2. Mixing

  - Choose a glass; a clear glass with a capacity of about 300 ml is recommended
  - Take out the mandarin segments and peel off the membranes to get the flesh
  - Put 130 grams of flesh and 130 ml of jasmine green tea in the blender
  - Pour in 10 grams of simple syrup (optional; add it if you like it sweet)
  - Close the lid, start the blender, blend for 20 seconds, then pour into the glass
  - Tear up a little flesh and sprinkle it on top

Enjoy

## Additional Notes

- Papagan mandarins can be replaced with other citrus whose flesh separates easily, such as dekopon (ugly mandarin)
- The ratio of flesh to tea is 1 : 1; for a bigger batch, scale up proportionally
