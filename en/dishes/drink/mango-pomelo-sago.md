# How to Make Mango Pomelo Sago

This is an adapted version of a classic Hong Kong dessert (yeung chi gam lo), using chia seeds instead of sago: smooth and tender with a slight chew from the seeds, mango sweetness intertwined with the slight tartness of grapefruit, and coconut milk adding richness. It's rich in dietary fiber, vitamin C and good fats, refreshing and cooling. It's very simple—beginners can make it easily—and takes only about 20 minutes from prep to enjoying.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 661 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Glass
- Fruit knife
- Milk
- Ice cubes
- Blender

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Chia seeds 24g
- Milk 50ml
- Ice cubes 2 small
- Mango 1
- Grapefruit 1/2
- Coconut milk 150ml

Garnish:

- Shredded dried mango (optional)
- Shredded dried orange (optional)

## Steps

1. Soak the chia seeds in the milk for 10 minutes.
2. While they soak, peel and dice half the mango and the grapefruit and put them in the glass.
3. Cut the other half of the mango into small pieces and blend with the ice cubes and coconut milk into a purée.
4. Pour into the glass and top with the garnishes (if any).
5. Enjoy it while you write code!!
