# How to Make Pineapple Coffee Special

![Pineapple coffee special - preview image 1](../../../dishes/drink/菠萝咖啡特调/菠萝咖啡特调.png)

This special drink blends the richness of coffee with the sweet tartness of pineapple, with refreshing bubbles and a creamy foam top for layered flavors. Pineapple is rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber, and with coffee it gives you a lift. The difficulty is medium: you just whip cream and mix liquids, and the whole thing takes about 10 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 180 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Coffee (espresso or cold brew recommended)
- Pineapple juice (freshly squeezed or NFC)
- Ice cubes
- Soda water
- Cream
- Milk
- Sugar
- Sea salt (optional)
- Rum (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Coffee 30ml
- Pineapple juice 60ml
- Ice cubes 50g
- Soda water 30ml
- Cream 30ml
- Milk 10ml
- Sugar 8g
- Sea salt 0.5g
- Rum 5ml

## Steps

1. Add the ice, coffee, pineapple juice and soda water to the glass in that order
2. Whip the cream with the sugar to soft peaks, then mix in the rum and milk until just pourable
3. Pour the cream on top of the mixture from step 1
4. Sprinkle sea salt on top of the cream

## Additional Notes

- Coffee with floral and fruity notes is recommended. For espresso, use medium roast beans; for cold brew, ordinary washed or natural-process beans work
- When pouring the cream, keep it as low as possible so it floats evenly on the drink and forms a foam top
- Fresh coffee and pineapple juice greatly improve the overall flavor
