# How to Make Mojito

This cocktail is refreshing, sweet and tart, with the coolness of mint and a light buzz from rum—a classic summer drink. It's relatively low in alcohol, and the lime, rich in vitamin C, quenches thirst and refreshes. It's very simple and beginner-friendly, taking only about ten minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 187 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Crushed ice
- Chilled soda water
- Citrus juicer
- Highball glass
- Muddler

## Calculation

Per portion:

- One lime (cut into two halves)
- Five sprigs of mint
- Syrup 20ml
- Gold rum 45ml
- Skyy vodka 10ml

## Steps

1. Cut one of the lime halves into small pieces, put them in the highball glass, then muddle them to release the juice;
2. Rub 3-4 sprigs of mint around the rim of the glass, then put them in the glass;
3. Add 20ml of syrup;
4. Add 45ml of gold rum;
5. Squeeze the juice from the other lime half into the glass;
6. Stir gently so the sugar/syrup is half mixed in;
7. Add crushed ice to the glass until it's 3/4 full;
8. Add chilled soda water until it just covers the crushed ice;
9. Stir with a swirling motion for half a minute;
10. Top up the highball glass with crushed ice;
11. Slap the remaining sprig of mint to wake it up and stick it into the ice as a garnish.
