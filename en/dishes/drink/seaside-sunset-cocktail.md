# How to Make Seaside Sunset Cocktail

This cocktail has distinct color layers: grenadine at the bottom like the evening glow, clear orange juice in the middle, and blue curaçao on top like the sea, evoking a sunset by the sea. It tastes sweet, tart and refreshing with a hint of rum. The orange and lemon juice contain a little vitamin C, and the alcohol helps you relax. The steps aren't complicated, but pouring along the stirrer to create layers takes a little patience. Beginners can succeed by following along, and prep plus mixing takes about fifteen minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 173 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

Ingredients

- Grenadine syrup
- NFC (not-from-concentrate) orange juice
- Soda water
- White rum
- Blue curaçao
- Lemon juice
- Ice cubes
- Lemon

Tools

- Large glass
- Bar stirrer
- Jigger
- Cocktail shaker
- Straw
- Fruit knife

## Calculation

One glass (including ice)

- Grenadine syrup 15ml
- Orange juice 35~50ml
- Soda water 50ml
- White rum 30ml
- Blue curaçao 15ml
- Lemon juice 15ml
- Large ice cubes, roughly enough
- Lemon 1 slice

## Steps

Preparing the lemon

1. Wash the lemon and cut one slice

Mixing

1. Choose a glass; a clear glass with a capacity of 350~400 ml is recommended
2. Add large ice cubes and stir with the stirrer to chill the glass
3. Add the grenadine syrup
4. Pour the orange juice down the stirrer into the glass until the glass is about half full
5. Stir gently half a turn
6. Pour in the soda water
7. Take out the shaker and add the white rum + blue curaçao + lemon juice + ice cubes. Then shake, shake
8. Pour gently into the glass
9. Add the lemon slice and straw
