# How to Make Sour Plum Drink (Instant Mix)

Sour plum drink (instant mix) is a sweet, tart, refreshing summer drink in traditional Beijing style. Sour plum drink itself helps quench thirst and aid digestion. It's extremely simple—just stir in order—with no difficulty at all for beginners, and takes about five minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 603 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Sour plum crystal drink mix
- Sugar cubes (optional)
- Beijing erguotou liquor (optional)

## Calculation

- Drinking water 1177 grams
- Sour plum crystal drink mix 120 grams
- Sugar cubes 9 grams
- Beijing erguotou liquor 48 grams

All of the amounts above can be scaled up or down proportionally.

## Steps

1. Take 1177 grams of drinking water.
2. Add 60 grams of sour plum crystal drink mix and stir clockwise 50 times with a spoon.
3. Add the remaining 60 grams of drink mix and again stir clockwise 50 times with a spoon.
4. Add 9 grams of sugar cubes and stir clockwise 100 times with a spoon.
5. Add 48 grams of Beijing erguotou and stir clockwise 30 times with a spoon.

## Additional Notes

If you haven't got sugar cubes or Beijing erguotou, you can skip step 4 or step 5.

It's even better after chilling in the refrigerator.

**Don't drink and drive.**
