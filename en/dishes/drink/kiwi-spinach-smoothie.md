# How to Make Kiwi Spinach Smoothie

This is a refreshing, healthy fruit and vegetable drink, sweet, tart and smooth, with the natural fruitiness of kiwi and apple; the spinach flavor is mild and doesn't overpower. It's rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber, which help digestion. It's very simple—beginners can get it right the first time—and takes only about 10 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 184 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Ingredients:
  - Kiwis
  - Apple
  - Spinach leaves (2-5)
  - Water
  - Granulated sugar
- Tools
  - Blender

## Calculation

- Drinking water 700ml
- Kiwis 2
- Apple 1/2
- Spinach leaves 4
- Granulated sugar 12 grams

## Steps

1. Cut each kiwi in half, then cut each half into four small pieces
2. Dice the apple
3. Remove the stems from the spinach, keeping only the leaves
4. Chop the spinach
5. Put everything into the blender jar
6. Add the water
7. Add the sugar
8. Start the blender and blend for about 4 rounds of 15 seconds (stop every 15 seconds to check)

## Additional Notes

- About the sugar: full sugar is 12g; adjust anywhere from 0-12g to suit yourself
- This was developed at home to imitate IKEA's kiwi spinach juice. It's not as good as IKEA's, but it's still pretty good
- Improvements welcome!
