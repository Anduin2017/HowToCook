# How to Make Long Island Iced Tea

Long Island Iced Tea is a classic cocktail that tastes remarkably like iced tea and goes down smoothly, but is quite high in alcohol. It isn't very nutritious; an occasional drink can help you relax, but don't overdo it. It's very simple: just mix four base spirits, a liqueur and juice in proportion, and beginners can make it easily. It takes only about 5 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 281 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Gin
- Tequila
- Vodka
- White rum
- Orange liqueur
- Lemon
- Maple syrup
- Cola
- Ice cubes
- Highball glass (300ml capacity)

## Calculation

One glass, about 300ml (including ice):

- Gin 15ml
- Tequila 15ml
- Vodka 15ml
- White rum 15ml
- Orange liqueur 15ml
- Lemon juice 30ml
- Maple syrup 20ml
- Cola 75ml
- Lemon 1
- Ice cubes 100 grams

## Steps

1. Cut the lemon in half and squeeze 30ml of lemon juice into the glass;
2. Add to the glass in order:

  - 15ml gin,
  - 15ml tequila,
  - 15ml vodka,
  - 15ml white rum,
  - 15ml orange liqueur;

3. Slowly pour 20ml of maple syrup into the glass, stirring as you pour;
4. Add 75ml of cola to the glass;
5. Fill the glass with ice cubes;
6. Stir gently for 20 seconds;
7. Enjoy.

## Additional Notes

Long Island Iced Tea contains no tea but tastes like iced tea. It's a cocktail with fairly high alcohol content (about 15%), but it's smooth and easy to drink.

Reference: [International Bartenders Association: Long Island Iced Tea](https://iba-world.com/long-island-ice-tea/)
