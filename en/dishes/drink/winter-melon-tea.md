# How to Make Winter Melon Tea

Winter melon tea is a refreshing traditional drink for beating the heat, sweet and mellow with the distinctive fresh aroma of winter melon; it's even better chilled. Winter melon itself is low in calories and high in water, and cooked down with rock sugar it replenishes energy and quenches thirst. It's not complicated, but beginners will need some patience. The whole process takes about 4 to 5 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 296 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Winter melon
- Rock sugar
- Plastic wrap
- Strainer
- Large pot

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 4-5 people to drink.

Per portion:

- Winter melon 1000g
- Rock sugar 300g

## Steps

1. **Prepare the winter melon**: peel the winter melon, remove the seeds, and cut into small pieces (no larger than 4cm each).
2. **Add rock sugar**: add the rock sugar to the winter melon, mix well, cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
3. **Cook the winter melon**: by now the melon has released a lot of liquid. Pour it all into a pot and bring to a boil over high heat, then turn to medium-low and simmer slowly for 1~2 hours, stirring often to prevent burning.
4. **Strain the winter melon tea**: strain the cooked tea through the strainer, removing the melon pieces and keeping only the liquid.
5. **Cool**: let the tea cool, pour it into a clean container, and refrigerate.
6. **Enjoy**: the finished tea is a concentrate. Dilute with water or other drinks to your liking; it's good hot or cold.

## Additional Notes

- **Adjusting the taste**: adjust the amount of rock sugar to reach your preferred sweetness.
- **Storage**: keep refrigerated and drink within 1 week.
