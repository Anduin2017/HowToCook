# How to Make Gin Fizz

A classic gin cocktail, sweet and tart with a refreshing fizz. Lemon provides plenty of vitamin C, and simple syrup balances the acidity. It's very simple; beginners can mix one in just 5 minutes, perfect for relaxing at home. Note that drinking alcohol is harmful to your health; please don't overdo it.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 150 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

Ingredients

- Gin
- Sparkling soda water
- Lemon
- [Simple syrup](../condiment/simple-syrup.md)
- Ice cubes
- Fresh greens (optional, for garnish)

Tools

- Manual citrus juicer
- Cocktail shaker (optional)

## Calculation

One glass, about 350 ml (including ice)

- Gin 30~40 ml
- Sparkling soda water 1 can
- Lemon 1
- 1 : 1 simple syrup 30~40 grams
- Ice cubes 100 grams

## Steps

Preparing the lemon

1. Cut the lemon in half (**with the knife perpendicular to the line between the lemon's two ends**), and cut one slice from one of the halves to set aside
2. Cut the lemon in half again, juice the 4 resulting lemon wedges with the juicer into a container, and set aside

Mixing (without a shaker)

1. Choose a glass; a clear glass with a capacity of 350~400 ml is recommended
2. Put 100 grams of ice in the bottom of the glass
3. Pour in 30~40 ml of gin
4. Pour in 20 ml of lemon juice (add more, or all of it, if you like it sour)
5. Pour in 30~40 grams of simple syrup
6. Stir well with a spoon
7. Place the lemon slice you set aside
8. Slowly pour soda water down the side of the glass until full (don't pour it onto the ice, to avoid losing the fizz)
9. Gently lift the spoon up and down to mix (don't stir in circles, to avoid losing the fizz)
10. Place the garnish greens on the surface (optional)

If you have a cocktail shaker, you can replace steps 2~6 above with the following

1. Put the ice, gin, lemon juice and simple syrup in the shaker and shake well
2. Pour the well-shaken mixture into the glass (if the ice inside is too broken up, you can strain it out and add fresh ice to the glass)
