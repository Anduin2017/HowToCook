# How to Make Sour Plum Drink

![Sour plum drink](../../../dishes/drink/酸梅汤/imges/sour_plum_soup.jpg)

Sour plum drink (suanmeitang) is a sweet, tart, cooling traditional drink, especially refreshing when chilled. Ingredients in the recipe like smoked plums (wumei), hawthorn and licorice help quench thirst and aid digestion. It's simple and beginner-friendly: just soak, simmer and chill. The whole process, including soaking, two rounds of simmering and chilling, takes more than 6 hours, so prepare it ahead of time.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 359 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Water
- Black dates (wuzao)
- Smoked plums (wumei)
- Hawthorn slices (raw)
- Yellow rock sugar
- Licorice root
- Dried tangerine peel (chenpi)
- Red cardamom (galangal fruit)
- Dried osmanthus

## Calculation

- Water 2 liters
- Black dates 25 grams
- Smoked plums 25g
- Yellow rock sugar 100 grams
- Hawthorn slices 30 grams
- Licorice root 2 grams
- Dried tangerine peel 4 grams
- Red cardamom 1 gram
- Dried osmanthus 3 grams

All of the amounts above can be scaled up or down proportionally.

## Steps

1. Rinse the ingredients (except the dried osmanthus and rock sugar) and soak them in 1.5 liters of room-temperature water for at least two hours (except the dried osmanthus and rock sugar)
2. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, cover, turn to low heat and simmer for 40 minutes; this is the first decoction
3. Put the rock sugar in a basin, then pour in the strained first decoction while hot and stir until the rock sugar dissolves.
4. Put the herbs back in the pot with 600 ml of water, bring to a boil over high heat, cover, turn to medium heat and simmer for another 20 minutes; this is the second decoction
5. Finally, combine the second decoction with the sugar water while hot. When it's at 60-70℃, add the dried osmanthus (not above 80℃), cover and let cool, then refrigerate for at least 3 hours.
6. Remember to strain out the osmanthus before drinking. Sip it slowly like tea: icy cold on the teeth, sour enough to wake you up, just sweet enough, with a full and lingering flavor

## Additional Notes

It's even better after chilling in the refrigerator.
