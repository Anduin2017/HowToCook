# How to Make Bingfen (Ice Jelly)

![Finished shiliangfen (bingfen) 1](../../../dishes/drink/冰粉/石凉粉%28冰粉%29成品1.jpg)
![Finished shiliangfen (bingfen) 2](../../../dishes/drink/冰粉/石凉粉%28冰粉%29成品2.jpg)

Shiliangfen is a traditional snack from Xinyang, Henan: smooth like jelly, served with mint or lemon juice to cool you down, and part of Henan cuisine. It's made by hand-rubbing bingfen seeds (from the shoo-fly plant), naturally with no additives, and contains plenty of plant gum. It's very simple and beginner-friendly. The whole process takes about 3 hours, of which 2.5 hours is resting time to set, so it takes almost no work.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 110 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Bingfen seeds 200g
- A piece of cheesecloth (the kind for straining soy milk pulp)
- Cooled boiled water 2000g
- Mint syrup 10ml / mint powder 10g
- Disposable clear plastic cups (optional)
- Light-up ice cubes that glow in water (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for 5 people.

Per portion:

- Bingfen seeds 200g
- Cooled boiled water 2000g
- Mint syrup 10ml / mint powder 10g

## Steps

1. Pour the cooled boiled water into a basin;
2. Wrap all the bingfen seeds in the cheesecloth and tie the opening in a knot
3. Put the wrapped seeds into the water and rub them hard in the water for 6 minutes
4. Then leave the water to stand for 2.5 hours, and it will set
5. Spoon the shiliangfen into the prepared disposable clear plastic cups, add 10ml of mint syrup or 10g of mint powder (lemon, hawthorn or mulberry juice also work), add the light-up ice cubes, and stir slowly with a spoon until evenly mixed

## Additional Notes

- While working, watch how the color and viscosity of the water change. If the color is too light or the water isn't sticky, there aren't enough seeds, or you didn't rub hard or long enough.
