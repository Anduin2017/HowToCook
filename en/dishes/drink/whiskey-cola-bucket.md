# How to Make Whiskey Cola Bucket

The cola bucket is a simple, easy-to-mix casual cocktail that's refreshing and sweet; the bubbles of the cola and the lemon juice nicely balance the strength of the whiskey, making it great for parties. It mainly provides energy, and the lemon adds vitamin C. The whole mixing process takes only 5 minutes, and beginners can make it easily.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 450 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

Ingredients

- Bourbon whiskey
- Coca-Cola
- Ice cubes
- Lemon (optional, for better flavor)

Tools

- Manual citrus juicer

## Calculation

One glass, about 900 ml (including ice)

- Whiskey 100 ml
- Coca-Cola 500 ml
- Lemon 1
- Ice cubes 300 grams

## Steps

Preparing the lemon

1. Cut the lemon in half (**with the knife perpendicular to the line between the lemon's two ends**), and cut one slice from one of the halves to set aside
2. Cut the lemon in half again, juice the 4 resulting lemon wedges with the juicer into a container, and set aside
3. Set the squeezed lemon wedges aside in a container

Mixing

1. Choose a glass; a large glass or tin bucket with a capacity of about 1 liter is recommended
2. Put the ice cubes and squeezed lemon wedges in the glass (arrange the lemon and ice however you like)
3. Pour in 15 ml of lemon juice (add more, or all of it, if you like it sour)
4. Slowly pour Coca-Cola down the side of the glass until it's 3/4 full (keep the ratio of cola to whiskey around 5:1)
5. Pour in whiskey until the glass is full

## Additional Notes

Because the ice and cola mask the taste of the whiskey, even people who can't hold their liquor can easily drink too much without noticing. Please mix it in a way that keeps you and the drinkers safe.
