# How to Make Cucumber and Century Egg Soup

Cucumber and century egg soup is a quick home-style soup with a light flavor and the toasty aroma of century egg and garlic fried together—warming without being greasy. Cucumber cools and hydrates, and century egg provides some protein, low in fat and easy on the body. There's almost no technical barrier; even kitchen beginners can make it easily, and it takes only about ten minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 311 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cucumber
- Century eggs (pidan)
- Garlic
- Green onion
- Cooking oil (lard recommended)
- Salt
- Chicken bouillon powder (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Cucumber 1
- Century eggs 2
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Green onion
- Cooking oil 10-15 g
- Salt 2-3 g
- Chicken bouillon powder 0.5-1 g
- Water 400-500 ml

## Steps

1. Wash the cucumber and cut into thin slices 0.5-1.2 mm thick
2. Wash and chop the green onion; smash the garlic with a knife, peel it, and cut in half
3. Peel the century eggs and cut each into 6-8 pieces. Pour oil into the pot and add the cut century eggs and garlic
4. Fry over low heat until the century eggs and garlic are a bit golden brown on the surface, add the water, and bring to a boil over high heat
5. Add the cucumber slices. As soon as the water boils again, turn off the heat, season with salt and chicken bouillon, and serve
6. Ladle into bowls and sprinkle with chopped green onion

## Additional Notes

- Peel the cucumber or not, as you prefer
- You can make thin cucumber slices with a peeler
- Rubbing a little sesame oil on the knife when cutting the century eggs keeps them from sticking
