# How to Make Pork Rib Soup with Chinese Yam and Corn

Pork rib soup with Chinese yam and corn is sweet and rich: soft Chinese yam and sweet corn blended with the savory aroma of pork ribs, a home-style soup for all ages. It's rich in protein, dietary fiber and various vitamins, and is said to strengthen the spleen and stomach and boost resistance. It's simple, with clear prep and stewing steps, so beginners can pick it up easily. It takes about 1 hour in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 1054 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork ribs (prime spare ribs recommended)
- Chinese yam (iron-stick yam recommended)
- Carrot
- Corn (sweet corn or waxy corn recommended)
- Ginger
- Green onion
- Cooking wine
- Salt
- Clay pot or deep stockpot

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Pork ribs 500g
- Chinese yam 1 (about 300g)
- Carrot 1 (about 150g)
- Corn 1 ear (about 250g)
- Ginger (about 20g, sliced)
- Green onion (about 15g, tied in a knot or chopped)
- Cooking wine 15ml
- Cooking oil 10ml
- Drinking water 1500ml - 2000ml
- Salt 6g - 8g

## Steps

1. **Preparing the ingredients**:
    - Wash and peel the carrot and roll-cut into chunks of about 3cm.
    - Wash the corn and chop it into rounds about 4cm long.
    - Peel the Chinese yam (wearing gloves is recommended to prevent skin irritation from the slime) and cut into segments about 4cm long. Set aside.
2. **Blanching the ribs**:
    - Put plenty of cold water in the pot and add the rinsed ribs.
    - Add about 10g of ginger slices, the green onion knot and 15ml of cooking wine.
    - Bring to a boil over high heat, then **wait 3 minutes**, skimming off the foam with a spoon.
    - Take out the ribs, rinse off impurities in warm water of about 40℃, drain and set aside.
3. **Stir-frying the ribs**:
    - Heat the pot, add 10ml of cooking oil, and wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up.
    - Add the remaining ~10g of ginger slices and fry until fragrant.
    - Add all the prepared ribs and stir-fry over medium heat for about 1 minute until slightly browned on the surface.
4. **Stewing**:
    - Add 1500ml - 2000ml of drinking water to the pot all at once.
    - Add all the prepared carrot, corn and Chinese yam.
    - Bring to a boil over high heat, then turn to low heat, cover, and **simmer slowly for 40 minutes**.
5. **Seasoning and serving**:
    - Add 6g - 8g of salt and stir well with a spoon.
    - Keep simmering on low heat for **2 minutes** so the salt is absorbed.
    - Turn off the heat, sprinkle with chopped green onion to taste, and serve.

## Additional Notes

- **Preventing oxidation**: if you don't put the peeled Chinese yam in the pot right away, soak it in water so it doesn't oxidize and turn dark from contact with air.
- **Controlling texture**: if you like the Chinese yam extremely soft, add it with the ribs; if you like it to hold its shape, add it after the ribs have simmered for 20 minutes.
- **Adding water**: if the water level drops below the ingredients while stewing, be sure to add hot water; cold water makes the meat fibers contract suddenly and become tough.
