# How to Make Yellow Catfish and Tofu Soup

Yellow catfish and tofu soup is a home-style soup with tender, delicate fish and a rich, milky white broth. The fish has few bones and tender meat, the tofu is silky, and white pepper adds a gentle warmth—good for all ages. Fish is rich in high-quality protein, and tofu adds calcium for balanced, easily absorbed nutrition. The process isn't complicated; just pay attention to removing fishiness and keeping the fish from sticking while frying, and beginners can manage it. It takes about 40 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 321 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Yellow catfish (angci yu) or goby (shaguang yu)
* Tofu
* Green onion
* Ginger
* Cooking oil
* Cooking wine
* Salt
* Ground pepper

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Yellow catfish or goby 1
* Tofu 100 g
* Green onion 1 stalk
* Ginger 1 piece
* Ground pepper 3-5 g
* Cooking oil 15 ml
* Salt 10-15 g
* Boiling water 1L

## Steps

1. Rinse the cleaned fish (pay special attention to the blood inside the belly—if it's not washed clean it will smell fishy), put it in a large bowl, add cooking wine, 10g of ginger slices and 5g of salt, and marinate for 15 minutes
2. Cut the tofu into cubes, soak in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain and set aside
3. Before frying the fish, rub the wok with a slice of ginger to prevent sticking. Add the oil (15ml * the number of fish), and when hot, add the fish and fry for 2~3 minutes. Move the fish around a bit to keep it from sticking, and flip it once
4. When all the fish are fried, pour in the boiling water, 5ml of cooking wine and ginger slices, turn from low to high heat, cover, and boil over high heat for 10 minutes (use a bit more water, as some will evaporate)
5. When the soup turns white, add the prepared tofu, turn to medium heat and cook for another 5 minutes, season with 10g of salt and 3g of ground pepper, and finally sprinkle with chopped green onion and serve
