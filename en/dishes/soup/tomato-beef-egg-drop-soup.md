# How to Make Tomato Beef Egg Drop Soup

Tomato beef egg drop soup is a sweet, tangy, appetizing home-style soup, fresh and smooth. Beef is rich in high-quality protein and iron, tomatoes provide lycopene and vitamin C, and eggs add lecithin. It's simple; beginners can master it easily, and it's usually on the table in about half an hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 262 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Beef
- Tomatoes
- Eggs
- Green onion, ginger, garlic
- Salt
- Ground pepper

## Calculation

- Beef: 150 g/person
- Tomatoes: 1/person
- Eggs: 1/person
- Salt: 2 g/person
- Ground pepper: 0.5 g/person

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Preparing the Vegetables

1. Slice the beef thinly
2. Cut the tomatoes into small pieces
3. Chop the green onion
4. Slice the ginger
5. Mince the garlic into a paste

### Preparing the Beef

1. Put the beef in a bowl
2. Add salt and ground pepper and marinate for 15-20 minutes

### Making the Soup

1. Bring water to a boil
2. Add the ginger slices and beef slices and cook until the beef changes color
3. Add the tomato pieces and cook until the tomatoes soften
4. Beat the eggs and slowly pour them into the pot, stirring with chopsticks to form egg ribbons
5. Season with salt and ground pepper
6. Finally add the chopped green onion and serve

## Additional Notes

- Add some vinegar or sugar to adjust the taste to your liking
- If you like it spicy, add some chili or chili sauce
