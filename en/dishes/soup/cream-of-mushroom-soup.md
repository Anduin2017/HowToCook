# How to Make Cream of Mushroom Soup

This is a classic French-style cream soup, silky and velvety, blending mushroom and dairy aromas. Mushrooms are rich in dietary fiber and trace elements, and milk provides high-quality protein and calcium. It's very simple; beginners can handle it easily, and it takes about 30 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 426 kcal

---

## Ingredients and Tools

- White button mushrooms  
- Onion  
- Butter  
- Flour  
- Milk  
- Whipping cream  
- Cracked black pepper  
- Salt  
- Water

---

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1~2 people.

Total (per portion):

- White button mushrooms 200 grams  
- Onion 50 grams  
- Butter 15 grams  
- Flour 10 grams  
- Milk 200 ml  
- Whipping cream 30 ml  
- Water 100 ml  
- Salt 2 grams  
- Cracked black pepper 1 gram

---

## Steps

1. Slice the mushrooms and mince the onion. Set aside.  
2. Melt the butter in a frying pan over low heat, then add the onion and sauté until translucent.  
3. Add the mushrooms and sauté until they release their liquid and soften, then sprinkle in the flour and stir evenly.  
4. Add the milk and water, stir well, and bring to a boil over low heat, stirring constantly to prevent sticking.  
5. Simmer over low heat for about 10 minutes until the soup thickens.  
6. Pour in the cream and heat for another minute, then season with salt and black pepper.  
7. After turning off the heat, you can blend it into a smooth soup (optional).

---

## Additional Notes

- You can set aside some mushrooms, sauté them separately until fragrant, and add them as garnish.  
- Chicken stock instead of water gives better flavor; keep the total liquid under 300 ml per portion.  
- If you like a thicker soup, increase the flour and cream slightly.  
- Best eaten fresh; refrigerating or freezing is not recommended.
