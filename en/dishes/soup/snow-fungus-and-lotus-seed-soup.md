# How to Make Snow Fungus and Lotus Seed Soup

![Snow fungus and lotus seed soup](../../../dishes/soup/银耳莲子粥/银耳莲子粥.png)

Snow fungus and lotus seed soup is sweet and silky: soft, chewy snow fungus (white wood ear) and creamy lotus seeds, a common nourishing sweet soup. It's rich in natural gelatinous fiber, dietary fiber and various trace elements, and is said to calm the mind. It requires soaking and slow simmering, which tests a beginner's patience. It takes about 3.5 hours in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 282 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Snow fungus (white wood ear)
- Cored lotus seeds
- Red dates (jujubes)
- Goji berries (optional)
- Rock sugar

## Calculation

For 1 person:

- Snow fungus 60g
- Cored lotus seeds 20g
- Red dates 6g
- Goji berries 5-6g
- Rock sugar 10-20g

## Steps

1. Soak the snow fungus and lotus seeds in water for 2 hours and the red dates for 10 - 20 minutes; rinse the goji berries. Set aside
2. Pour 600ml of water into the pot, bring to a boil, then add the snow fungus, lotus seeds and red dates in order
3. When the water boils again, cover and turn to medium heat to keep simmering
4. After about 1 hour, add 5g - 10g of rock sugar and 5g - 6g of goji berries, and turn to low heat
5. Keep simmering on low for 30 minutes; by now the snow fungus should start to look thick and gelatinous
6. Add another 5g - 10g of rock sugar and stir with a spoon for 5 - 10 minutes
7. Turn off the heat and ladle into bowls

## Additional Notes

- Once the snow fungus turns thick and gelatinous, stir promptly with a spoon to keep it from burning on the bottom
