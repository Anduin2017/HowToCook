# How to Make Thickened Shiitake Soup

Thickened shiitake soup is rich, savory and smooth, a quick home-style soup. Shiitake mushrooms contain plenty of amino acids and polysaccharides, which help boost immunity. It requires some basic heat control and thickening technique, but it's still beginner-friendly. It takes only about 25 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 146 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Shiitake mushrooms
* Green onion
* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Potato starch

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Fresh shiitake mushrooms 2
* Green onion 0.5 stalk
* Chicken bouillon powder 3 g
* Cooking oil 10 ml
* Salt 3 g
* Boiling water 350 ml
* Potato starch 10 g

## Steps

1. Slice the shiitakes (each slice 0.5-1 cm thick; thicker slices are chewier than thin ones), put them in a large bowl, add 2g of salt and soak for 15 minutes
2. Put the starch in a small bowl, add 50ml of water, and stir until dissolved with no lumps (starch slurry)
3. Pour off the salt water and squeeze some of the water out of the shiitakes (to make the next frying step easier) [optional]
4. Over low heat, add the oil. When the oil starts forming small bubbles (about 30s on low, depending on your stove's power), add the shiitakes and fry each side for 10s [optional]
5. Pour in 300ml of boiling water, turn to medium heat and cook for 3-5 minutes
6. Pour in the starch slurry, stir the soup, add 3g of salt and 3g of chicken bouillon, and finally sprinkle with chopped green onion and serve
