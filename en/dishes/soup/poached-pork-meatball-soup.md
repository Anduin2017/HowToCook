# How to Make Poached Pork Meatball Soup

Poached pork meatball soup (sheng cuan wanzi tang) has fresh, tender, springy meatballs, a home-style soup with pure, natural flavor. Pork provides high-quality protein, and with wood ear mushrooms, daylily buds and so on, it's fairly balanced nutritionally. You need to mince the meat yourself and get the hang of squeezing out meatballs at the right temperature, so there's a bit of a learning curve; it's better suited to home cooks with some basics. It takes about 1 hour from prep to table.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 857 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

### For the Meatballs

* Pork front leg meat
* Salt
* Ground pepper
* Scallion-ginger-Sichuan pepper water
* Egg white from one egg
* Potato starch
* Cooked soybean oil

### For the Soup

* Wood ear mushrooms
* Dried daylily buds
* Green onion
* Soaked glass noodles (in the bottom of the bowl)
* Salt
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Ground pepper
* Sesame oil
* Cilantro

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Salt = pork weight in jin (1 jin = 500g) * 6 grams
* Ground pepper = pork weight in jin * 2 grams
* Potato starch = number of people * 40 grams; this tutorial is calculated for one person

## Steps

### Mincing the Meat

1. Cut the meat open, 3:7 fat to lean
2. Chop with the knife, then pound with the back of the knife to break up and loosen the sinew
3. Chop and pound until minced. If you want it to taste good, mince it yourself; machine-ground meat is too mushy

### Seasoning

1. Per 500g of meat: 6 grams of salt, 1 gram of ground pepper
2. Mix by hand
3. Add the scallion-ginger-Sichuan pepper water in several additions, stirring as you go and kneading by hand so the meat absorbs it fully. Use 80 grams of the water per 500g of minced meat
4. Add the egg white and keep stirring in one direction
5. Add 40 grams of potato starch and mix well
6. Add the cooked soybean oil; this keeps the meatballs tender, smooth and springy

### Poaching the Meatballs

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, then turn to low heat so it's barely simmering
2. Squeeze out meatballs by hand
3. When they all float, cook over low heat for 1 minute

### Making the Soup

1. Put the glass noodles in the bottom of the bowl
2. Add wood ear mushrooms, daylily buds and green onion, and season the base with salt, ground pepper and chicken bouillon
3. Pour the meatballs and broth into the bowl
4. Drizzle with 3-5 drops of sesame oil
5. Add a small sprig of cilantro
