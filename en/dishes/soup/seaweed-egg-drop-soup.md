# How to Make Seaweed Egg Drop Soup

Seaweed egg drop soup is a light, savory home-style soup, a simple Chinese soup dish. Laver (zicai) is rich in iodine, calcium and dietary fiber, and eggs provide high-quality protein, a nutritionally balanced pair. It's very simple and beginner-friendly, taking only about 20 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 217 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Eggs
* Dried laver (zicai seaweed)
* Chopped green onion
* Water
* Salt
* Oil
* Shrimp (to taste, optional)

## Calculation

For 1 person:

* 10g of dried laver (add more if you love seaweed)
* 2 eggs
* Salt 2 grams

## Steps

1. Soak the dried laver in water for 15 minutes, then drain and set aside.
2. Heat the pot and add 1.5 liters of water, 5ml of oil and 2g of salt. When the water boils, add the laver.
3. 3 minutes after the laver comes to a boil, slowly pour in the beaten eggs; it's ready in 30 seconds.
4. Sprinkle with chopped green onion and turn to low heat for 20 seconds.
5. Turn off the heat and add a few drops of sesame oil before serving; some people also add a few dried small shrimp, which tastes good too.

## Additional Notes

* After the water boils, turn the heat down and pour the beaten eggs in a circle around the boiling center. For more tender egg ribbons, cover the pot, turn off the heat, wait half a minute, then open it.
* If you like a thicker texture, add 2g of starch.
