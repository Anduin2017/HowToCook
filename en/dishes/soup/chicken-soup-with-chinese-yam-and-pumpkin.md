# How to Make Chicken Soup with Chinese Yam and Pumpkin

Chicken soup with Chinese yam and pumpkin is a warming soup for autumn and winter: Chinese yam strengthens the spleen, pumpkin is sweet and fragrant, chestnuts are soft and starchy, all stewed together in one pot for a sweet, savory soup. It's rich in protein, dietary fiber and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. It's simple, taking about 50 minutes from prep to serving, and beginners can make it easily.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 620 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken
- Ginger
- Cooking wine (for blanching)
- Chestnuts
- Chinese yam
- Small pumpkin
- Red dates (jujubes)
- Salt (for seasoning at the end)

## Calculation

Per portion (one portion is just enough for 2 people):

- Chicken 500 g
- Chestnuts 150 g
- Chinese yam 200 g
- Small pumpkin 1 (about 300 g; steamed and scooped out to make a purée)
- Red dates 8
- Ginger 5 slices
- Cooking wine 10 ml
- Salt 5 g

## Steps

1. Cut the chicken into pieces, add ginger and cooking wine, put in cold water and blanch. After the water boils, cook for 3 minutes, then take out and set aside
2. Peel the Chinese yam and roll-cut into chunks; shell the chestnuts. Set aside
3. Wash the small pumpkin, steam until cooked, scoop out the flesh, and mash into a purée. Set aside
4. Put the blanched chicken, Chinese yam, chestnuts and ginger slices in a pot, add water to cover, bring to a boil over high heat, then turn to low heat and simmer for 30 minutes
5. Add the pumpkin purée and stir it in, add the red dates, and cook for another 10 minutes
6. Season with salt and serve

## Additional Notes

- Add the pumpkin purée last so the soup is golden and not cloudy
- Wear gloves when peeling Chinese yam to avoid itchy hands
