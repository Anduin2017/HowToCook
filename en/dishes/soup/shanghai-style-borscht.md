# How to Make Shanghai-Style Borscht

A Shanghai-style home soup adapted from Russian beet borscht (luosong tang), built around tomatoes and beef: sweet, tangy, rich and full-bodied. It's rich in high-quality protein, lycopene and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. It's simple and friendly to beginners, taking 2–3 hours in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 2006 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Vegetable base (the trio of parsley/celery, carrot and onion)
- Beef stock (stock concentrate can be used instead)
- Beef (beef brisket or oxtail)
- Tomatoes (tomato paste, canned tomatoes)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Beef stock 500 mL
- Beef 250 g (beef brisket or oxtail)
- Canned tomatoes 2 cans (fresh tomatoes can be used, but the flavor is weaker)
- Tomato paste 5 g (for more tomato flavor)
- Potatoes 400 g
- Onion 100 g
- Carrot 100 g
- Parsley/celery 100 g
- Red sausage (Harbin-style) 100 - 200 g
- Olive oil 5 mL (for cooking the vegetables; vegetable oil can be used instead)
- Vegetable oil 5 mL (for searing the beef; don't use olive oil instead)
- Salt 18 g
- Black pepper 3 g

## Steps

### Prep

1. Dice the onion, carrot and parsley/celery into 1cm cubes
2. Cut the red sausage and potatoes into 2cm chunks
3. Remove the core from the cabbage and tear into 2cm pieces by hand
4. Season the beef with 3 g of salt and 3 g of black pepper and marinate for 5 minutes

### Searing

1. Heat a frying pan and add the vegetable oil
2. Sear the beef until **golden brown** on the surface (it can still be raw inside—just don't burn it), then remove and set aside.

### Cooking, Part 1 (First Hour)

1. Heat a stockpot and add the olive oil, diced onion, carrot and parsley/celery
2. Sauté until **the onion is translucent**, then add the tomato paste and canned tomatoes
3. Add the beef and diced potatoes and stir well
4. Add water to cover the ingredients and cook over medium heat for 1 hour

### Cooking, Part 2 (Last Half Hour)

1. Open the pot, add the cabbage and red sausage, and stir well
2. Cook over medium heat for half an hour
3. Open the lid, add the remaining 15 g of salt, mix well, and serve

## Additional Notes

- Try not to substitute fresh tomatoes; the combination of canned tomatoes + tomato paste has more flavor
- Apart from carrot, onion, parsley/celery and beef, which are essential, the other ingredients can be mixed freely
