# How to Make Squab Stewed with Mushrooms

Squab stewed with mushrooms has a clear broth and delicate meat, blending the earthy aroma of mushrooms with the savoriness of pigeon—a home-style Cantonese stew. Squab is rich in high-quality protein and various amino acids, and mushrooms provide polysaccharides and dietary fiber, making it gently nourishing. The squab is first blanched to remove gaminess, then stewed with corn and mushrooms, which requires some control of heat and timing, but beginners can manage with a bit of care. From prep to serving it takes about 45 minutes in a pressure cooker, or just over 1 hour slow-stewed in a clay pot.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 324 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Squab (young pigeon)
* Mushrooms
* Corn
* Ginger
* Cooking wine
* Salt
* Clay pot or pressure cooker

## Calculation

For 2 people:

* Squab 300 g
* Mushrooms 100 g
* Corn 200 g
* Ginger 30 g
* Cooking wine 15 ml
* Salt 10 g

## Steps

1. Rinse the squab (cleaned by a friendly vendor) in cold water
2. Put the cleaned squab in a pot of cold water with 15ml of cooking wine and ginger. As soon as the water boils, take out the squab, or it will lose nutrients
3. Put the squab in the pressure cooker or clay pot, add water to cover, and add 20 g of ginger, 200 g of corn and 100 g of mushrooms

  Note: 30 minutes in a pressure cooker; in a clay pot, bring to a boil and then slow-stew over low heat for 40-60 minutes
4. When time is up, serve into bowls and add 3~5g of salt
