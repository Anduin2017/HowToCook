# How to Make Enoki Mushroom Soup

This is a light, refreshing home-style soup with a savory flavor, a simple quick dish. Enoki mushrooms are rich in amino acids and dietary fiber, and adding egg provides high-quality protein. There's almost no barrier to entry; beginners can handle it easily, and it takes only about 10 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 149 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Enoki mushrooms
- Egg (if desired)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Enoki mushrooms 400-500 grams (one bag from the market)
- Salt 15 grams
- MSG 5 grams

## Steps

1. Pull the enoki mushrooms apart by hand—the more separated the better, **or they'll get stuck in your teeth**—rinse and set aside.
2. Cut the enoki into segments with a kitchen knife or fruit knife, as you prefer, but no longer than 5 cm.
3. Put the enoki in the pot, add water to about 1.1 times the height of the ingredients, and once it boils, **wait 3 minutes**.
4. Add the MSG and salt and stir.
5. Keep heating for about 30 seconds, then turn off the heat and serve.

## Additional Notes

- If you like egg in your soup, pour in beaten egg after the water boils in step 3.
- After serving, you can add 3-4 drops of sesame oil for aroma.
