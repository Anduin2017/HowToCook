# How to Make Pork Rib and Bitter Melon Soup

Pork rib and bitter melon soup is a Cantonese home-style soup with a clear broth and a savory, slightly bitter-sweet finish: tender ribs, and bitter melon that cools the body and cuts the richness. It's rich in protein and vitamins, and the dried shrimp adds calcium. Preparing it requires blanching to remove gaminess and carefully removing the bitter melon pith, which is a bit challenging for beginners, but an electric pressure cooker simplifies it a lot. Traditional slow simmering takes 2 to 4 hours (start 4 hours ahead); with a pressure cooker it takes only about 1 hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 905 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

Optional tools

- Electric pressure cooker (greatly simplifies the cooking process and time)
- Clay pot (better than a wok for making soup)

Required ingredients

- Pork ribs
- Bitter melon
- Dried small shrimp (xiapi)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 4 people.
Per portion:

- Pork ribs 250g to 500g
- Bitter melon 100g to 200g
- Dried small shrimp 5g to 15g
- Ginger 5~10g (optional, for removing gaminess when blanching)

## Steps

1. Rinse the ribs and cut into pieces about 4cm ±2cm * 3 ± 2cm (if you don't have a tool for chopping ribs, ask the vendor)
2. Put 700ml of cold water and the ribs in the wok, heat until boiling, then turn off the heat and take out the ribs
3. Cut the bitter melon in half lengthwise, thoroughly remove the seeds and pith, cut into strips 0.5 ± 0.3 cm thick, and rinse

  Cooking in a stockpot

  - Put 1100ml of water and the ribs in the stockpot over high heat
  - Once the water boils, add the bitter melon and dried shrimp, turn to low heat, and wait 120~240 minutes (after 120 minutes you can take out a piece of meat to taste; if the meat comes off the bone fairly easily, turn off the heat)
  - Before serving, add salt and white pepper; the recommended amount is 0.3 to 0.6g of salt and 0.1 to 0.5g of white pepper per 250ml

  Cooking in an electric pressure cooker

  - Add 1000ml of water, the ribs, dried shrimp and bitter melon
  - Select the soup function and cook (usually 45 minutes under pressure)
  - Serve, adding salt and pepper as with the stockpot

## Additional Notes

- You can use other savory ingredients instead of dried shrimp, such as dried scallops
