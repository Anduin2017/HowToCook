# How to Make Tangerine Peel Pork Rib Double-Boiled Soup

A nourishing, soothing Cantonese double-boiled soup with a clear broth and a mellow sweetness, carrying the distinctive aroma of aged tangerine peel (chenpi). Pork ribs provide high-quality protein, and together with American ginseng, dendrobium and other ingredients, it's said to moisten the lungs, strengthen the spleen and quench thirst—good for people who often stay up late. The difficulty is medium: you just prepare the ingredients, put them in a double-boiling pot, and simmer slowly for about two hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 357 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork ribs
* Aged tangerine peel (chenpi)
* American ginseng
* Dendrobium (shihu)
* Solomon's seal (yuzhu)
* Ophiopogon root (maidong)
* Double-boiling soup pot
* Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Pork ribs (pork bones also work), 4-5 pieces
* Aged tangerine peel (usually 8-20 years old); one peel usually has 3 segments, use 1 segment
* American ginseng (also called huaqi shen), 9 slices
* Dendrobium, 6 pieces
* Solomon's seal, 5 slices
* Ophiopogon root, 7 pieces
* Double-boiling pot, one per person
* Salt, 5g

## Steps

### Prep

1. Blanch the ribs in hot water to remove the blood
2. Just rinse the tangerine peel, ophiopogon root, Solomon's seal, dendrobium and American ginseng
3. Wash the double-boiling pot

### Cooking

1. Open the double-boiling pot, put the ribs on the bottom first, then add the tangerine peel, ophiopogon root, Solomon's seal, dendrobium and American ginseng in that order
2. Add hot water to the double-boiling pot, not too full
3. Add water to the outer steaming vessel and double-boil for 1.5 hours
4. Add salt and drink while hot

## Additional Notes

The double-boiling pot is very hot; be careful not to burn yourself or let it slip and break when picking it up
Cantonese slow-cooked soups (laohuo liangtang) are all about ingredients and timing—just prepare the ingredients and simmer long enough, and you're done!
