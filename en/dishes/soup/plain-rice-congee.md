# How to Make Plain Rice Congee

Plain rice congee is light and soft, an essential home-style staple that warms the stomach, with a pure rice aroma suitable for all ages. It's rich in carbohydrates with a little protein and is easy to digest; in Chinese food therapy it's said to nourish the spleen, soothe the stomach and clear the lungs. It's simple—you just need to manage the heat and water—and beginners can make it easily. It takes about 25 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 308 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Rice
* Water
* Vegetable oil (optional)

## Calculation

* One person can usually eat 60ml-110ml of rice.
* The volume of water is 9-12 times the volume of rice.
* One bowl holds 500ml.
* Latest time to stop high-heat cooking, T1: 1.5 minutes/500ml * water volume
* Earliest time the congee can be eaten, Tr: 10 minutes/500ml * water volume
* Mass of oil, Mo: raw rice volume / 10
* Chilling time Tc = raw rice volume / 10 ml/minute.

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. (Optional) Mix Mo ml of oil with the rinsed rice, *making sure it's as thoroughly mixed as possible, i.e. every grain has at least a little oil on it*
2. (Optional) Refrigerate the rice-oil mixture for time Tc.
3. Put the rice and water in the pot.
4. Turn on high heat and heat until T1.
5. Turn the heat down before T1. **If you forget this step, the water may boil over and put out the flame. Very dangerous!**
6. Keep heating until Tr. At Tr, turn off the heat.
