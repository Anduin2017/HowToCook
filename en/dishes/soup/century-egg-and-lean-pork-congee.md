# How to Make Century Egg and Lean Pork Congee

Century egg and lean pork congee is a classic Cantonese home-style congee: a smooth, creamy base blending the rich flavor of century egg with tender lean pork, warm and savory. It's rich in carbohydrates and high-quality protein, and with vegetables it's fairly balanced nutritionally and easy to digest. There are several steps, but they're intuitive; beginners can follow the process smoothly. It takes about 1.5 hours, and with a rice cooker's timer or keep-warm function, it's especially convenient for breakfast.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 776 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Drinking water
* Century eggs (pidan)
* Lean pork
* Rice
* Green onion
* Cilantro
* Lettuce
* Ginger
* Soy sauce
* Oyster sauce
* Salt
* Ground pepper
* Cooking oil
* Rice cooker
* Several small bowls

## Calculation

Wear disposable gloves when measuring and handling ingredients if you can, and wash the raw vegetables in advance and set them aside

* Drinking water 1 liter
* Century eggs 2
* Lean pork 100g
* Rice 150ml
* Green onion 1 stalk
* Cilantro 1 stalk
* Lettuce 4 leaves
* Ginger 1 thumb-sized piece
* Soy sauce 5 ml
* Oyster sauce 5 ml
* Salt 2g
* Ground pepper 1g
* Cooking oil 10ml

## Steps

### Main Ingredients

1. Rice - rinse - put in the rice cooker's inner pot - add 1 liter of drinking water
2. Lean pork - rinse - let drain briefly - add 10ml of cooking oil - rub in evenly - put in the inner pot
3. Century eggs - peel - rinse - cut in half - separate the whites and yolks - roughly chop the whites - crumble the yolks - put in the inner pot
4. Ginger - rinse - peel - remove any withered or blackened inedible parts - julienne - put in the inner pot

### Toppings

1. Green onion - rinse - remove the roots - remove any withered or blackened inedible parts - chop - set aside in a small bowl
2. Cilantro - rinse - remove the roots - remove any withered or blackened inedible parts - chop - set aside in a small bowl
3. Lettuce - rinse - remove the root end - remove any withered or blackened inedible parts - chop - set aside in a small bowl

### Seasoning

1. Soy sauce + oyster sauce + salt + ground pepper - stir well - set aside in a small bowl

### Cooking

1. Main ingredients - cook using the rice cooker's congee mode
2. Toppings - once the main ingredients are cooked, blanch the lettuce separately in hot water, then open the lid and stir it into the congee along with the other toppings
3. Seasoning - once the main ingredients are cooked, open the lid and stir it in along with the toppings

## Additional Notes

* For breakfast, you can prepare the main ingredients the night before and keep them on keep-warm until breakfast, then add the toppings and seasoning
