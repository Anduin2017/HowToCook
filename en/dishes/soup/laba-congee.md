# How to Make Laba Congee

Laba congee is a traditional sweet congee for the Laba Festival: thick, soft and sticky, blending the complex aromas of various grains, beans and dried fruits. It's rich in carbohydrates, minerals and various vitamins, and lotus seeds are said to calm the mind, making it good for people under a lot of work stress. There are many ingredients, but it's simple to make—just simmer patiently—so it's beginner-friendly. Excluding soaking and prep, simmering takes about 3 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 2042 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Drinking water
- Rice
- Glutinous rice
- Peanuts
- Red beans (adzuki)
- Red dates (jujubes)
- Congee pot (ordinary pots burn easily on the bottom; use a pressure cooker if you can)
- Medium glass bowls (or other medium stainless steel containers)
- Several small bowls

### Optional Ingredients

Since Laba congee recipes vary widely, choose the ingredients below according to your taste.

- Job's tears (coix seed)
- Black rice
- Millet
- Lotus seeds
- Mung beans
- Soybeans
- Peas
- Red kidney beans
- Longan (pitted dried longan)
- Chestnuts
- Shelled walnuts
- Raisins
- Rock sugar (or white sugar, for sweetening)

## Calculation

For one person (three small bowls), one portion is:

- Drinking water 1 L
- Rice 50 g
- Glutinous rice 50 g
- Job's tears 50 g
- Black rice 50 g
- Millet 50 g
- Lotus seeds 25 g
- Mung beans 25 g
- Red beans 25 g
- Peanuts 25 g
- Soybeans 25 g
- Peas 25 g
- Red dates 25 g
- Longan 25 g
- Chestnuts 25 g
- Shelled walnuts 25 g
- Raisins 25 g
- Red kidney beans 25 g
- Rock sugar 10~25 g

Adjust the amounts above to your taste. For rice and grains, no more than 150 g is recommended; for other beans, no more than 50 g.

## Steps

1. In advance, rinse the mung beans, red beans, peanuts, soybeans, peas and red kidney beans, put them in a clean glass bowl, fill the bowl 3/4 full with drinking water, and soak overnight (or at least 8 hours).
2. In advance, rinse the rice, glutinous rice, Job's tears, black rice, millet and lotus seeds, put them in a clean glass bowl, fill the bowl 3/4 full with drinking water, and soak for 3 hours.
3. Drain the water from the bowl of mung beans, red beans, peanuts, soybeans, peas and red kidney beans prepared in step 1, put the beans into the congee pot, add 1 liter of drinking water (or enough to cover the ingredients by a thumb's width), bring to a boil over high heat, then cover and simmer over low heat for 30 minutes.
4. Drain the water from the bowl of rice, glutinous rice, Job's tears, black rice, millet and lotus seeds prepared in step 2, add them to the congee pot, cover, and simmer over low heat for 60 minutes.
5. Rinse the red dates, longan, chestnuts, walnuts, raisins (cut the red dates into small pieces) and rock sugar, add them to the pot, cover, and simmer over low heat for 60 minutes.
6. Once the congee is thick, turn off the heat, serve, and enjoy.

## Additional Notes

- When adding ingredients to the pot, stir so they're evenly distributed, and watch the water level; if it drops below the rice and other ingredients, add water immediately.
- Control the heat—don't let it get too high—and stir regularly. If using an ordinary pot, add the ingredients after the water boils, and stir until it boils again before turning to low heat, to avoid burning the bottom. If you can, use a pressure cooker or congee pot instead.
