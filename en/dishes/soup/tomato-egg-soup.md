# How to Make Tomato Egg Soup

Tomato egg soup is a sweet, tangy, appetizing home-style soup with a silky texture. It's extremely simple, a popular dish for all ages. It's rich in lycopene, vitamin C and high-quality protein, balanced and easy to absorb. It's very beginner-friendly and quick, taking about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 258 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Tomato
- Eggs
- Sesame oil
- MSG
- Salt
- Green onion, ginger, garlic

## Calculation

- Tomato 1
- Eggs 1-2 (to taste; 2 if you love eggs, usually 1)
- Sesame oil 2 drops
- MSG 5 grams (optional)
- Salt 15 grams
- Green onion, ginger and garlic 15 grams in total

## Steps

1. Wash the tomato and cut into pieces.
2. Mince the green onion, ginger and garlic.
3. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat evenly with chopsticks (or a whisk).
4. Heat the pot and add 15 ml of oil. When you see a wisp of smoke rising from the oil, add the green onion, ginger and garlic and stir-fry for 30 seconds.
5. Add the tomato and stir-fry for 1 minute.
6. Pour in water, about 1.2 times the height of the food in the pot, and add the salt.
7. Once it boils, pour in the beaten eggs, break them up with chopsticks, and add the MSG and sesame oil.
8. Wait 30 seconds, turn off the heat, and serve.

## Additional Notes

MSG is optional.
