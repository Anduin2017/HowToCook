# How to Make Coffee Coconut Milk Jelly

![Coffee coconut milk jelly](../../../dishes/dessert/咖啡椰奶冻/咖啡椰奶冻.png)

This dessert combines the richness of coffee with the sweetness of coconut milk. Its texture is as smooth and delicate as pudding, with layered flavors. Whipping cream and coconut milk provide quality fats and plant nutrients, making it satisfying to eat. It's very approachable: just mix and heat, no complicated techniques, and beginners can get it right the first time. Excluding chilling, the hands-on time is about 1 hour; including chilling to set, it's ready in about 4 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 162 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- 125ml whipping cream
- 250ml Coconut Palm brand (椰树牌) coconut milk drink
- 35ml espresso
- 50ml coconut water
- 10g gelatin
- Strainer (optional)
- (Um... if you want to plate it nicely, add some blueberries and/or coffee powder)

## Calculation

For 3-4 people:

- 125ml whipping cream (35% milk fat)
- 250ml Coconut Palm brand coconut milk drink
- 35ml espresso (I personally don't recommend fruity coffee beans)
- 50ml coconut water (Vita Coco coconut water recommended; crack open a coconut if you can)
- 10g gelatin
- Sugar (optional)

## Steps

1. Mix the measured whipping cream, coconut milk drink, espresso and coconut water and set aside.
2. Heat the liquid for 1 minute until it reaches 50-60 degrees.
3. (Optional) If you have a big sweet tooth, add extra sugar.
4. Add the gelatin, stir until dissolved, and cook for 1 minute.
5. (Optional) Strain (this step makes the jelly smoother).
6. Pour into molds.
7. (Optional) Skim off the bubbles on the surface. This makes the jelly smoother and look better too.
8. Put it in the refrigerator and wait 3 hours.

## Additional Notes

Caution: make absolutely sure the liquid doesn't boil!!! Don't let it bubble either!!!
About washing up: a dishwasher is recommended; if you don't have one, wash with warm water. (Gelatin is hard to clean, so a dishwasher works better)
