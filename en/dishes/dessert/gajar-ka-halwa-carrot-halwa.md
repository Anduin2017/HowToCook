# How to Make Gajar ka Halwa (Carrot Halwa)

Gajar ka halwa is a well-known Indian winter dessert: grated carrots are slowly cooked down with milk, then enriched with ghee and nuts, giving a dense, sweet texture and a golden red color. It's rich in carotene, calcium and good fats, warming and nourishing. The process is simple, but beginners will need some patience. It takes about 45 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 374 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Carrots (red carrots recommended)
- Whole milk
- Sugar
- Ghee — butter can be used instead
- Cardamom powder
- Cashews
- Almonds
- Raisins
- Grater
- Heavy-bottomed pot

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 3-4 people.

Per portion:

- Carrots 500g (about 5-6 medium)
- Whole milk 500ml
- Sugar 80g (adjust to 60g-100g to taste)
- Ghee 40g
- Cardamom powder 3g
- Cashews 15g
- Almonds 15g
- Raisins 15g

## Steps

1. Peel the carrots and grate them finely
2. Put 20g of ghee (half) in the heavy-bottomed pot and melt over medium heat
3. Add the grated carrot and stir-fry for **3-5 minutes**, until *the carrot softens and smells fragrant*
4. Pour in 500ml of whole milk and stir well
5. Bring to a boil over high heat, then turn to medium-low and keep it at a *gentle simmer*
6. **Keep stirring for 25-30 minutes**, until *the milk is almost completely absorbed by the carrots and evaporated* (this is the most critical step; it takes patience, and you must keep stirring to prevent burning on the bottom)
7. When there's almost no liquid left in the pot, add 80g of sugar and stir well
8. Adding sugar will release liquid again; keep stirring over medium-low heat for **5-8 minutes**, until *the liquid evaporates again*
9. Add the remaining 20g of ghee and 3g of cardamom powder and stir-fry for 2 minutes
10. In another small pan, fry the cashews, sliced almonds and raisins separately in a little ghee until *golden* (about 1 minute). Note that the raisins will puff up
11. Stir half of the fried nuts and raisins into the halwa
12. Plate, and sprinkle the remaining nuts and raisins on top as decoration

## Additional Notes

- Gajar ka halwa can be eaten hot or cold; each has its own appeal.
- Red carrots give a nicer color than orange carrots.
- For a richer texture, replace part of the milk with cream.
- It keeps for 4-5 days sealed in the refrigerator. Microwave for 1 minute before eating.
- Almost every household in India makes this dessert; it's a must at Diwali and weddings.
