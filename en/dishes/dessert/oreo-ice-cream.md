# How to Make Oreo Ice Cream

Oreo ice cream is smooth and delicate, blending creaminess with the crunchy sweetness of Oreos, and pure dairy cream keeps it light rather than heavy. It mainly provides energy from fat and carbohydrates, a nice little dessert to beat the heat. It's fairly simple—you just need to deal with the cookie filling and whip the cream—and beginners can do it with a little patience. Prep takes about half an hour, then freeze for at least 4 hours before enjoying.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1160 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Whipping cream (recommended brand: Anchor dairy whipping cream)
- Original Oreos
- Electric mixer
- A deep container with a capacity of at least 600 ml and a small diameter (PS: make sure the electric mixer fits) (if you're using ice cream molds, the container should have a spout for easy pouring)
- Small knife (or another tool for removing the filling)
- Ice cream molds (optional)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Oreos 6
- Granulated sugar 18 grams
- Whipping cream 250 ml

## Steps

1. Twist the Oreos apart, remove the "re" (the filling), and set the cookies aside
2. Chop the cookies with chopsticks: half should become powder, and the other half should be pieces no bigger than 0.5 cm. Set aside (you can search online for "Oreo cookie crumbs" to save time and effort ^-^)
3. Pour all the cream into the deep container and add the sugar
4. Whip on the high setting with the electric mixer until, when you lift the mixer, some cream **hangs** from the beaters (0.5 cm - 1 cm) instead of **all** dripping off like a liquid (some dripping is normal).
5. Once whipped, add the cookie crumbs to the cream and stir evenly until there are crumbs all the way to the bottom.
6. Optional: pour the mixture into ice cream molds
7. Put it in the freezer (-18 degrees) for at least 4 hours before taking it out

## Additional Notes

- Don't use a fragile container for chopping the Oreos.
- Be careful not to cut your hand when removing the Oreo filling.
