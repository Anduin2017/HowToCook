# How to Make Red Pomelo Cake

Red pomelo cake is a refreshing dessert that's easy to make in an air fryer, combining the slightly tart fruitiness of red pomelo pulp with a soft, fluffy cake. It's perfect as a single-serving afternoon tea. Eggs and flour provide protein and carbohydrates, and red pomelo is rich in vitamin C, giving simple basic nutrition. The difficulty is medium; the key is mixing in stages into a smooth, pale yellow batter with no lumps. Beginners can succeed by patiently following the steps. It takes about 35 minutes from prep to finish, so it won't take too much time when you want a freshly baked dessert.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 622 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Air fryer
- Eggs
- Red pomelo pulp
- Flour
- Aluminum foil pan
- Oil
- Sugar
- Water

## Calculation

One portion is one serving for one person; scale proportionally for more people. In this example the foil pan is 18 cm in diameter; if you use a larger foil pan, scale the ingredients proportionally too.

One portion:

- Eggs 2
- Flour 80g
- Red pomelo pulp 20g
- Oil 15ml
- Water 80ml
- Sugar 15g

## Steps

1. Crack 2 eggs into the foil pan and add 20g of red pomelo pulp
2. Pour 15ml of oil into the foil pan and swirl the pan so it evenly coats the bottom
3. Add 10g of sugar, 40g of flour and 40ml of water to the foil pan
4. Stir clockwise with chopsticks until it becomes a pale yellow batter
5. Add 5g of sugar, 40g of flour and 40ml of water to the foil pan
6. Keep stirring with chopsticks until it becomes a pale yellow batter
7. Put the foil pan in the air fryer basket and bake at 180 degrees Celsius for 15 minutes
8. Open the air fryer, carefully take out the foil pan, and flip the cake with chopsticks or a spoon
9. Bake at 180 degrees Celsius for another 8 minutes
10. Take it out and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- If you like it sweeter, add 10g more sugar
- You can add more red pomelo pulp as you like, but preferably no more than 40g per portion so it doesn't spoil the look of the cake
- "Batter" means there are no lumps of flour and the color is even throughout
- The foil pan is very hot right out of the air fryer; pick it up with tongs or use something heat-insulating like a damp cloth
