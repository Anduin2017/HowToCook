# How to Make Snowflake Crisp

![Finished snowflake crisp](../../../dishes/dessert/雪花酥/雪花酥成品.jpg)

Snowflake crisp (xuehuasu) is a crunchy, sweet little treat with a rich milky aroma. It's simple, quick home baking and also great boxed up as a gift. Since it contains butter, milk powder and mixed nuts, it provides plenty of energy and protein, but it's high in calories, so it's best enjoyed occasionally. The difficulty is moderate and a bit challenging for beginners, but if you pay attention to the heat and stirring technique, it usually takes only 30 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1313 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

### Tools

- Non-stick frying pan
- Baking spatula (a regular spatula works, but be careful not to damage the non-stick coating)
- Disposable plastic gloves
- Snowflake crisp / nougat mold
- Rolling pin

### Ingredients

- Unsalted butter
- Marshmallows
- Whole milk powder
- Mixed nuts (Three Squirrels daily nut packs)
- Biscuits (not sandwich cookies; Xiaoqifu crackers or special snowflake crisp baking biscuits are recommended)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Unsalted butter 20g
- Marshmallows 75g
- Whole milk powder 40g
- Mixed nuts 60g
- Biscuits 75g

## Steps

1. If the biscuits are bigger than a one-yuan coin, cut them into small pieces first
2. Add the unsalted butter to the pan and heat over low heat until completely melted
3. Add the marshmallows to the pan and stir with the spatula until they melt and combine evenly with the butter
4. Add 20g of milk powder to the pan and stir with the spatula. As soon as the milk powder is evenly mixed into the butter and marshmallow mixture, turn off the heat
5. While it's still hot, add all the prepared mixed nuts and biscuits to the pan and stir with the spatula
6. Stir until the temperature drops to where you can touch it, then put on disposable plastic gloves and knead it in the pan or pull and stretch it with both hands, so the biscuits and nuts are evenly distributed through the marshmallow-butter-milk powder mixture.
7. Press the mixture into the mold, packing the corners firmly, and flatten with the rolling pin. On any side that doesn't fill the mold, press it into as straight an edge as you can by hand
8. Let it cool at room temperature. Once completely cool, unmold and cut into pieces along the mold's lines or into whatever size you like. Sprinkle with the remaining milk powder, trying to coat every side of each piece

## Additional Notes

- The longer the marshmallows are heated, the harder the finished product
