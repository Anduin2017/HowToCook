# How to Make Yogurt Panna Cotta

![Yogurt panna cotta - preview image 1](../../../dishes/dessert/酸奶意式奶冻/酸奶意式奶冻.png)

A dessert adapted from an Italian classic. With plain yogurt mixed in, it's fresher and smoother, sweet without being cloying, and makes a nice after-dinner treat with fruit or jam. The dairy provides plenty of calcium and protein for gentle nutrition. The steps are simple—just heat, stir and chill to set—so beginners can make it smoothly. It takes about 5 hours from prep to finished chilling.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1034 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Whipping cream
- Sugar
- Plain yogurt
- Gelatin sheets
- Sieve

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 3-6 people.

Per portion:

- Whipping cream 200g
- Sugar 40g
- Plain yogurt 250g
- Gelatin sheets 6g

## Steps

1. Cut the gelatin sheets into small pieces and soak in cold water
2. Put the whipping cream and sugar in a pot and heat to 60 degrees
3. Turn off the heat, take the gelatin out of the water, squeeze out the water, add it to the hot cream and stir well
4. When the cream has cooled to 40 degrees, add the plain yogurt and stir well
5. Strain the mixture from the steps above through the sieve twice
6. Divide into suitable containers and refrigerate for at least 4 hours

## Additional Notes

- Traditional panna cotta uses only cream and more sugar, so it's rather sweet and rich. This version is adapted to be lighter.
