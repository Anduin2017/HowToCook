# How to Make Strawberry Ice Cream

This is a homemade strawberry ice cream that's easy to make without an ice cream machine, with a rich fruity aroma and a creamy texture. Strawberries are rich in vitamin C, and paired with milky condensed milk and cream, it provides energy and calcium. It's simple and doesn't test your cooking skills; beginners can get it right the first time. The whole process takes about eight hours, but most of that is freezing time; hands-on work is only a few dozen minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 1653 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Sweetened condensed milk
- Strawberries
- Heavy cream
- Vanilla extract
- Ice cream molds (optional)

## Calculation

One portion:

- Strawberry syrup:
- Strawberries 500 grams
- Granulated sugar 45 grams
- Vanilla extract 1 gram
- Salt 1 gram
- Ice cream base:
- Vanilla extract 5 grams
- Salt 1 gram
- Heavy cream 6 grams
- Sweetened condensed milk 400 grams

## Steps

1. Make the strawberry syrup first. Wash the strawberries and remove the leafy tops. Cut the strawberries into **5mm** pieces. Set aside half of the chopped strawberries to fold into the ice cream later.
2. Put the other half of the chopped strawberries in a saucepan with the sugar. Stir and cook over medium heat until the strawberries release their liquid and form a syrup in the pan.
3. Let the strawberries keep cooking in the syrup, stirring from time to time, until they break down and lose their shape and the syrup thickens slightly.
4. When a line drawn through the syrup stays apart for **3 seconds**, it's ready. Take the syrup off the heat and stir in the vanilla and salt. Set the strawberry syrup aside to cool.
5. While the syrup cools, prepare the ice cream base. Add the sweetened condensed milk, heavy cream, vanilla extract and salt to a bowl. Whisk the mixture with a hand whisk until it becomes light and fluffy and forms soft peaks.
6. Fold the reserved chopped fresh strawberries into the ice cream base. Transfer the ice cream base to a freezer-safe container. Drizzle the cooled strawberry syrup over the ice cream, then gently swirl it into the mixture.
7. Cover the ice cream and freeze for **eight hours**, then scoop and enjoy.

## Additional Notes

- Be careful during preparation to avoid cutting or burning yourself.
