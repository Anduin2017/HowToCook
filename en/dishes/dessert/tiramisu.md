# How to Make Tiramisu

![Homemade tiramisu](../../../dishes/dessert/提拉米苏/提拉米苏成品.jpg)

This traditional Italian dessert needs no oven: the cheese cream is rich yet light, and the ladyfingers soaked in coffee are aromatic and soft. It's a beginner-level dessert. It's rich in protein and calcium, but high in sugar and calories, so enjoy it occasionally. It's easy and very friendly to baking beginners, needing only about half an hour of hands-on time, then four hours of chilling to set before eating.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 809 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Mascarpone cheese
- Ladyfingers
- Cooled espresso
- Pasteurized (salmonella-safe) eggs
- Granulated sugar
- Cocoa powder
- Rum (leave it out if you don't like alcohol, or adjust to taste)
- A container for the finished dessert (a glass Lock & Lock container is used here)
- Whisk (if you have strong arms, you can also use this as a workout)

## Calculation

- Mascarpone cheese 450g
- Ladyfingers 1 pack
- Cooled espresso 350ml
- Pasteurized eggs 4
- Granulated sugar 50g
- Cocoa powder 10g
- Rum 35ml

## Steps

1. Separate the yolks and whites
2. Add 10g of sugar to the bowl of whites and whip to soft peaks
3. Add 40g of sugar to the bowl of yolks in three additions and stir until even
4. Add the mascarpone to the yolks in three additions and stir until even
5. Finally add the rum to the yolks and stir until even
6. Fold the whipped whites into the yolk-cheese mixture in three additions
7. Dip both sides of the ladyfingers in the coffee and lay them flat in the container
8. Layer two layers of cheese cream and two layers of ladyfingers alternately in the container (adjust this step to your preference and the height of your container)
9. Chill in the refrigerator for four hours (if you're impatient, you can take it out early)
10. Take it out, sift cocoa powder over the top, and enjoy
