# How to Make Baked Egg Tarts

![Baked egg tarts](../../../dishes/dessert/烤蛋挞/烤蛋挞.png)

Baked egg tarts are a classic Chinese dessert with a crispy shell and a smooth, tender filling with a rich milky aroma. They're rich in protein, calcium and various vitamins, and provide plenty of energy. The process is very simple; beginners can pick it up easily, and the unbaked tarts can be frozen and baked whenever you want. It takes about 1 hour from prep to out of the oven.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 169 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

This recipe is the amount needed for a whole pack of tart shells. Once filled, they can be frozen directly.

- Tart shells, any brand
- Eggs
- Milk
- Whipping cream
- Granulated sugar
- Oven, any size
- Kitchen scale
- Mixing tool, including but not limited to chopsticks, a whisk, etc.
- Sieve with about 1 mm mesh

## Calculation

Per portion:

This recipe is the amount needed for a whole pack of tart shells. Once filled, they can be frozen directly.

- Tart shells, any brand; a whole pack is about 30 shells
- Eggs 8, ordinary eggs are fine
- Milk 200 ml, ordinary bagged milk is fine
- Whipping cream 450 ml, sold at baking shops or supermarkets
- Granulated sugar 80 grams; ordinary sugar is fine, caster sugar is better as it dissolves easily

## Steps

1. Put a bowl on the kitchen scale and weigh out 450 grams of whipping cream (treat the density of whipping cream as 1 here)
2. Add 80 grams of sugar (medium sweetness; adjust to taste, recommended range 60-100 grams)
3. Add 200 grams of milk (treat the density of milk as 1 here)
4. Add 8 egg yolks; save the whites for something else
5. Stir everything evenly until all the sugar has dissolved
6. Strain the mixture through the sieve to remove egg membranes, eggshell, undissolved sugar and clumps of cream
7. Now set the oven to 220 degrees Celsius and start preheating (about 10 minutes). Remember to take out the baking tray
8. Arrange the tart shells evenly on the baking tray, 0.5 cm apart
9. Pour the strained filling into the tart shells, up to 0.5 cm below the rim; don't overfill
10. At this point the unbaked tarts are finished; you can put them straight in the freezer and freeze them for at least 12 hours to store
11. Bake the tarts in the oven at 200 degrees Celsius for 25 minutes
12. They're ready to eat when baking is done

## Additional Notes

Please be very careful of the oven's high temperature to avoid burns
