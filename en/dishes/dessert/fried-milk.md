# How to Make Fried Milk

![Finished fried milk](../../../dishes/dessert/炸鲜奶/炸鲜奶.jpg)

Fried milk is a dessert with a crispy shell and a soft filling. Making it isn't hard, but the milk custard must be fully heated and chilled to set, or it will fall apart when cut and fried. Hands-on time is about 30 minutes, plus at least 2 hours of chilling to set.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1100 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Milk
- Corn starch
- Cake flour or all-purpose flour
- Breadcrumbs
- Egg
- White sugar
- A heat-resistant square container about 20cm × 15cm
- Paper towels

## Calculation

The amounts below make about 12 pieces of fried milk, enough for 3 - 4 people.

- Milk 250ml
- Corn starch 30g
- White sugar 25g
- Cake flour or all-purpose flour 40g
- Egg 1, about 50g without the shell
- Breadcrumbs 80g
- Cooking oil 700ml; the actual amount used will be less than this

## Steps

1. Take 80ml of the milk and mix it with the corn starch, stirring until there are no visible lumps
2. Pour the remaining 170ml of milk and the white sugar into a pot, heat over low heat and keep stirring until the sugar has completely dissolved
3. Stir the corn starch mixture again and pour it slowly into the pot while stirring constantly
4. Keep heating over low heat and stirring constantly for about 3 - 5 minutes, until the custard is thick and a line scraped across the bottom of the pot holds for 3 seconds
5. Evenly coat the inside of the heat-resistant container with about 2ml of cooking oil, pour in the custard and smooth the surface so it's about 2cm thick
6. Once the custard has cooled to room temperature, cover it and refrigerate for at least 2 hours; it's ready when the surface isn't sticky to a light touch and the whole thing doesn't flow
7. Unmold the set custard and cut it into strips about 6cm × 2cm × 2cm, about 12 strips in total
8. Put the flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs in 3 separate containers
9. Coat each strip evenly in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, making sure every side is covered
10. Pour 700ml of cooking oil into the wok and heat over medium heat to 165 - 175℃; without a thermometer, drop in a few breadcrumbs to test—they should float within 2 seconds and keep giving off small bubbles
11. Fry the strips in 2 - 3 batches, about 60 - 90 seconds per batch, turning them 1 - 2 times so all sides cook evenly
12. When the strips are evenly golden, remove them and drain on paper towels for about 2 minutes before eating

## Additional Notes

- Corn starch settles easily, so stir it again before pouring it into the pot.
- Adding too many strips at once makes the oil temperature drop quickly, so the shells absorb oil and crack easily.
- The freshly fried filling is very hot; break each piece open in the middle to let it cool before eating, to avoid burns.
