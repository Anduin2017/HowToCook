# How to Make Strawberry Jam

This is a sweet, tangy homemade jam with a rich fruity aroma, perfect for spreading on bread. Strawberries are rich in vitamin C, though the jam is high in sugar after cooking; adjust to taste. It's very simple; beginners can easily make it, and it takes about two hours from prep to finished jam.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 1940 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Strawberries
- Granulated sugar
- Plastic wrap

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Strawberries 1200 grams
- White sugar 400 grams (for a low-sugar diet, consider reducing to 200g)

## Steps

1. Wash the strawberries and remove the leaves
2. Chop the strawberries and put them in a suitable bowl
3. Pour the sugar into the bowl and mix evenly with the strawberries
4. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let it stand for 1 hour
5. Pour the rested strawberry and sugar mixture into a non-stick pot and bring to a boil over high heat
6. After it boils, turn to low heat and stir constantly until the jam is thick, then turn off the heat
7. After the jam cools, transfer it to a prepared airtight jar
