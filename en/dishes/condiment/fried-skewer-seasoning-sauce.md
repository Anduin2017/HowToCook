# How to Make Fried Skewer Seasoning Sauce

This all-purpose fried skewer sauce is spicy, fragrant, savory and well balanced, in the style of Chinese street food. It mainly provides fat and aromatic compounds from the spices, which stimulate the appetite. It's very simple, no pressure for beginners, and takes only about 10 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 1826 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Dried chili flakes (both coarse and fine)
- Ground cumin
- Ground pepper
- Five-spice powder
- Ground Sichuan pepper
- Thirteen-spice powder
- Mala seasoning powder (麻辣鲜)
- White sesame seeds

## Calculation

- Dried chili flakes 60 grams
- Ground cumin 20 grams
- Ground pepper 10 grams
- Five-spice powder 15 grams
- Salt 20 grams
- Ground Sichuan pepper 15 grams
- Chicken bouillon powder 8 grams
- Thirteen-spice powder 5 grams
- Mala seasoning powder 5 grams
- White sesame seeds 30 grams

## Steps

1. Mix all the ingredients in a container and stir evenly.
2. Heat oil in the wok; use enough oil to cover all the ingredients in the container.
3. Pour in the hot oil in three additions, 1/3 each time, stirring as you go.
4. Finally add 10ml of sesame oil, 10ml of light soy sauce, 10ml of Sichuan pepper oil and 10ml of oyster sauce.

## Additional Notes

- The seasonings in the last step can be adjusted to your liking.
- Never pour in all the hot oil at once; it must be added in batches with stirring.
- The ingredients can be scaled down proportionally.
