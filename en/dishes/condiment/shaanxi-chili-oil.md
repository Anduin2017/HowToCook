# How to Make Shaanxi Chili Oil

![Shaanxi chili oil - preview image 1](../../../dishes/condiment/油泼辣子/口水鸡+油泼辣子.jpg)
![Shaanxi chili oil - preview image 2](../../../dishes/condiment/油泼辣子/油泼辣子.jpg)

A fragrant, spicy Shaanxi-style condiment (youpo lazi): glossy red oil interwoven with the aromas of sesame and garlic, spicy without being harsh, with a long finish. Chilies are rich in vitamin C and capsaicin, which stimulate the appetite and warm the body. You need to manage the oil temperature, which is a little challenging for beginners, but following the steps will get you there. It takes about 10 minutes in total and is the finishing touch for home-style cold dishes and noodles.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1854 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Garlic bulb
- Dried chili flakes
- Salt
- Toasted white sesame seeds
- Bird's eye chili
- Peanut oil (can be replaced with rapeseed oil)
- Small ceramic bowl
- Metal spoon
- Five-spice powder (optional)
- Black cardamom (caokou, optional)
- Green onion (optional)
- Star anise
- Sichuan peppercorns
- Bay leaves
- Angelica root (baizhi)
- Ginger slices (optional)
- Sugar
- White vinegar

## Calculation

- Garlic 1 bulb
- Dried chili flakes 100 grams
- Salt 5 grams
- Toasted white sesame seeds 15 grams
- Bird's eye chili 1
- Peanut oil 150 ml (can be replaced with rapeseed oil)
- Five-spice powder 10 grams (optional)
- Black cardamom 1 (optional)
- Green onion 3-5 stalks (optional)
- Other spices: star anise (1), Sichuan peppercorns (anywhere from 20-50, to taste), bay leaves (2-3), angelica root (2-3 slices), ginger slices (ginger about as thick as your thumb, sliced) (optional)
- Sugar 30 grams
- White vinegar 5 ml (about one small metal spoonful)

## Steps

1. Break off 2 `garlic cloves` from the bulb and peel them
2. Get out the cutting board and finely chop the `garlic cloves` and `bird's eye chili`
3. Get a bowl and pour in the `peanut oil`
4. When the oil is hot, add the `other spices` and `green onion`. When the spices turn dark, scoop them out and discard them
5. Pour the oil from the bowl into the wok and heat it for 2 minutes (if using rapeseed oil, until it smokes)
6. The bowl is now empty
7. Into the empty bowl, add the `dried chili flakes`, `white sesame seeds`, `minced garlic`, `bird's eye chili`, `salt`, `five-spice powder` and `black cardamom` as the "seasoning"
8. Turn off the heat and let the oil cool to `210` degrees Celsius
9. Pour the hot oil from the wok into the bowl and stir with the spoon (for extra heat, you can pour oil at `165` degrees Celsius into a second bowl with the same "seasoning", and mix the two at the end)
10. After pouring in the hot oil and stirring a little, add the white vinegar; it will start bubbling again. Keep stirring; the white vinegar boosts the aroma.
11. When the chili oil has cooled to warm, add the white sugar and MSG. The sugar mellows the spiciness so it isn't so harsh

## Additional Notes

- Adding five-spice powder and black cardamom as part of the "seasoning" increases the aroma and makes the chili oil more fragrant
