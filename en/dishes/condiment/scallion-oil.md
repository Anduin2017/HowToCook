# How to Make Scallion Oil

Scallion oil is a home-style seasoning oil that boosts aroma and savoriness, with a strong scallion fragrance and the complex umami of dried shrimp. It's great for mixing into fillings, dressing cold dishes, or finishing hot dishes with a glossy sheen. It mainly provides energy from fat, and aromatics like scallion and ginger contain plant compounds that help remove fishiness and whet the appetite. It's simple and beginner-friendly, usually taking about 30 minutes including prep and frying.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 2069 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Oil
- Scallion (large or small both work)
- Ginger
- Onion
- Cooking wine
- Cilantro (optional)
- Dried shrimp (kaiyang, optional)

## Calculation

- Oil 200g
- Scallion 80g
- Ginger 20g
- Cooking wine 10ml
- Onion 150g
- Dried shrimp 50g

## Steps

1. Soak the dried shrimp in 50-degree warm water, adding 10ml of cooking wine to remove fishiness. After 10 minutes, take them out and drain
2. Wash the scallion and cilantro, cut into 5cm segments, and pat dry
3. Cut the onion into shreds, boil in hot water in a pot for 5 minutes, then remove and drain
4. Peel and slice the ginger
5. Pour all the oil into the wok, add all the prepared ingredients above, and fry over medium-low heat for 20 minutes

## Additional Notes

- The leftover dried shrimp and scallion bits can also be eaten. You can strain them out of the scallion oil, or leave them in
- Dried shrimp greatly enhances the savory aroma of scallion oil and adds a sweet umami flavor
