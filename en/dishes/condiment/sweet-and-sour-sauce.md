# How to Make Sweet and Sour Sauce

This sauce is sweet, tangy and delicious, the classic source of flavor for Chinese sweet and sour dishes. It's simple to mix and the ratios are easy to remember, so beginners can handle it easily. It takes about 10 minutes from prep to finish. The sugar provides energy, and the vinegar helps whet the appetite and cut richness, instantly making home cooking more flavorful.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 146 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Water
- White sugar
- White vinegar / rice vinegar
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce

## Calculation

- Water (50ml)
- Light soy sauce (40ml)
- White sugar (30g)
- White vinegar (20ml)
- Cooking wine (10ml)

## Steps

1. Stir the seasonings together in a small bowl in the proportions above
2. After preparing the dish according to its own method, pour the sweet and sour sauce into the wok
3. Cook for 5-10 minutes, depending on the dish
4. Reduce the sauce over high heat to add thickness, aroma and gloss to the dish
