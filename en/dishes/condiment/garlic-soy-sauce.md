# How to Make Garlic Soy Sauce

Garlic soy sauce is a home-style dipping sauce with a strong garlic aroma, savory and rich with oil, often used to perk up dishes like white-cut chicken and dumplings. Garlic has antibacterial properties and sesame provides unsaturated fatty acids. It's extremely simple and very beginner-friendly, taking only about 5 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 177 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Garlic
- White sesame seeds
- Peanut oil
- Soy sauce
- Dipping sauce dish

## Calculation

- Garlic 2 cloves
- White sesame seeds 5 grams
- Peanut oil 15 ml
- Soy sauce 30 ml
- Dipping sauce dish 1

## Steps

1. Smash the garlic
2. Pour the soy sauce into the dipping dish
3. Heat the wok, add the peanut oil, and when the oil is very hot, add the smashed garlic and fry for half a minute
4. After half a minute, turn off the heat, pour the hot oil into the dipping dish, and stir with chopsticks
