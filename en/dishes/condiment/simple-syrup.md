# How to Make Simple Syrup

Homemade drink syrup: granulated sugar and water mixed and dissolved in a 1:1 ratio, making it easy to control the sweetness of cold drinks. It's the base recipe for mixing drinks. It's rich in carbohydrates for a quick energy boost. It's extremely simple and very beginner-friendly, taking just a few minutes, and keeps for a week in the refrigerator.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 400 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

Ingredients

- Granulated sugar
- Water

Tools

- Sealable container (a borosilicate glass reagent bottle is recommended; they're cheap)

## Calculation

Recommended ratio => sugar : water = 1 : 1. This makes it easy to control sugar amounts; for example, if you want 15 grams of sugar, measure out 30 grams of syrup

- Water 100 grams
- Granulated sugar 100 grams

## Steps

1. Mix the weighed sugar and drinking water evenly in the container
2. Seal the container and store it in the refrigerator

## Additional Notes

The water can be hot or cold. If you need it quickly, dissolve the sugar in hot water and then cool the syrup.

The syrup doesn't spoil easily; it usually keeps fine for a week in the refrigerator.
