# How to Make Youxu (Oil-Flour Paste)

Youxu (油酥) is a Chinese pastry ingredient, often used in flatbreads to make the finished bread distinctly layered, crispy outside and soft inside. It mainly provides carbohydrates and fat for energy. It's very simple and beginner-friendly, and takes only about 3 minutes to mix.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 111 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Flour
- Oil
- Salt

## Calculation

- Oil = (number of flatbreads to make * 10ml)
- Salt = (number of flatbreads to make / 2)g
- Flour = (number of flatbreads to make / 0.13)g

## Steps

1. Put the flour in a small bowl and add the salt
2. Pour in oil heated to 200 degrees
3. Stir with chopsticks into a smooth paste with no lumps.
