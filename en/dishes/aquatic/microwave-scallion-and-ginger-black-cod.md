# How to Make Microwave Scallion and Ginger Black Cod

Fish cooked in the microwave, brightened with scallion and ginger, and finished with a drizzle of hot oil that releases an amazing aroma. It is a home-style steamed fish dish with tender meat and a savory flavor, an adapted Cantonese method. Black cod is rich in high-quality protein and omega-3 fatty acids, which benefit the heart and brain. It is not complicated, but you need to manage cooking time and oil temperature, so it suits people with some cooking experience. It takes only about twenty minutes from prep to table.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 749 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

Ingredients:

- Black cod, skin on

Seasonings:

- Green onion
- Ginger
- Cooking wine
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Peanut oil

Tools:

- Zip-top bags

## Calculation

Per 2 portions:

- Black cod, skin on, 2 fillets, 450g (the star of this recipe; scale all seasonings to the actual weight of the cod)
- Green onion, white part, 25g.
- Green onion, green part, 10g.
- Ginger, 13g.
- Cooking wine, 5mL.
- Soy sauce, 25mL.
- Sesame oil, 2mL.
- Peanut oil, 50mL.

## Steps

1. Put each fillet in a separate zip-top bag and lay them skin-side down on a plate.
2. Julienne 25g of the white part of the green onion, and peel and julienne 10g of ginger. Mix them, divide into two halves, and place one half on each fillet inside its bag.
3. Pour 2.5mL of cooking wine into each bag.
4. Seal the bags, place them in the microwave and cook on medium power (800 watts) until the fish is *opaque and flakes easily* (about 3.5-5 minutes). Remove the fillets from the bags.
5. Discard the green onion and ginger.
6. Mix 25mL of soy sauce and 2mL of sesame oil and drizzle evenly over the two fillets.
7. Finely julienne 10g of the green part of the green onion, and peel and julienne 3g of ginger. Mix, divide into two portions and sprinkle over the fillets.
8. Heat 50mL of peanut oil in a small pot to 190℃.
9. Pour the hot oil over the fillets topped with green onion and serve immediately.

## Additional Notes

### Using Sea Bass, Tilapia, Halibut or Sole

| Fish     | Filleted? | Weight | Microwave time |
|----------|----------|------|-----------|
| Sea bass | Whole    | 450g | 6.5 minutes  |
| Tilapia  | Whole    | 800g | 6 minutes    |
| Halibut  | Fillet   | 170g | 2.25 minutes |
| Sole     | Fillet   | 170g | 1.5 minutes  |

### Other Variations

- For a stronger aroma, rub the scallion, ginger and cooking wine evenly over both sides of the fillets before microwaving.
