# How to Make Razor Clam Omelette

Razor clam omelette (蛏抱蛋, "razor clams hugging egg") is a traditional home-style dish from the Fuzhou area of Fujian. The clam meat is wrapped in beaten egg and fried until crispy outside and tender inside, with the savory aroma of onion and light soy sauce. Razor clams are rich in high-quality protein and trace elements such as zinc and iron, and paired with eggs they make a balanced dish. The difficulty is moderate: you need to control the frying heat, but the steps aren't complicated. It takes about 30 minutes to get it on the table.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1229 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Razor clams
* Eggs
* Cooking oil
* Onion
* Starch
* Light soy sauce
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Cooking wine

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Razor clams 200 g
* Eggs 2
* Cooking oil 100 ml
* Onion 0.25
* Starch 20 g
* Light soy sauce 5 ml
* Chicken bouillon powder 5 ml
* Cooking wine 5 ml

## Steps

1. Bring water to a boil, add the razor clams and boil for 2 minutes, then remove them, shell them, and put the meat in a large bowl
2. Add the onion, light soy sauce, cooking wine, chicken bouillon powder and starch to the bowl and mix thoroughly
3. Crack 2 eggs into the bowl and keep mixing
4. Heat oil in the wok, pour in the clam mixture, fry until golden on one side, then flip and keep frying

## Additional Notes

* More often, locals in Fuzhou use water chestnuts (to cut the richness) and potatoes (to absorb oil), but these weren't available during the pandemic, so pineapple was used instead
* Fujian cuisine leans sweet; if you're not used to it, you can leave out the white sugar
* You can tap the lean meat pieces with a spoon and listen to the sound to tell whether they are fried through
