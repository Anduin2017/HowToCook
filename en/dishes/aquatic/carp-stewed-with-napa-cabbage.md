# How to Make Carp Stewed with Napa Cabbage

![Carp stewed with napa cabbage](../../../dishes/aquatic/鲤鱼炖白菜/鲤鱼炖白菜.jpeg)

Carp stewed with napa cabbage is a fragrant, rich home-style stew: the fish is soft, tender and well-seasoned, and the cabbage is sweet and soaks up the broth. It's a typical northern Chinese flavor. Carp is rich in high-quality protein and unsaturated fatty acids, and the cabbage provides dietary fiber and vitamins for a balanced meal. It involves pan-frying the fish and stewing, which takes some skill but isn't complicated, suitable for people with basic cooking experience. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 792 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cooking oil
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Carp
- Napa cabbage hearts / baby napa cabbage
- Salt
- Dark soy sauce
- Light soy sauce
- Cassia bark
- Star anise
- Pixian doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Dried chili peppers (leave out if you don't eat spicy food)

## Calculation

Note: scale the seasonings to the size of the fish.

Per portion:

- Cooking oil: 10ml
- Ginger: 3 slices
- Garlic: 3 cloves (cut into chunks)
- Carp: 600g (1.2 jin, cleaned)
- Baby napa cabbage: 13 leaves (you can add more; it shrinks a lot when cooked)
- Salt: 5-8 grams
- Dark soy sauce: 3ml
- Light soy sauce: 6ml
- Cassia bark: 1 piece
- Star anise: 3
- Pixian doubanjiang: 20 grams
- Dried chili peppers: 4-6 (to taste)

## Steps

1. Clean the carp and score it (make several cuts in the body to help the flavor soak in)
2. Wash the baby napa cabbage and set it aside on a plate
3. Add oil to the wok. When the oil is hot, add "a little salt", "ginger", "garlic", "Pixian doubanjiang", "cassia bark" and "star anise" and fry until fragrant
4. Put the fish in the wok and pan-fry (3 minutes), flipping every (30 seconds)
5. Add "water" (to about the same level as the fish, or a little less), then add the "light soy sauce", "dark soy sauce" and "baby napa cabbage"
6. Stew on high heat for 15-20 minutes. When the liquid is nearly gone, add "salt" and serve

## Additional Notes

- You can also add some glass noodles. Soak them in cold/hot water until soft first, then add them together with the cabbage
