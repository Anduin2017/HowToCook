# How to Make Red-Braised Carp

Red-braised carp is a home-style Shandong dish. The fish is fried first and then braised: the skin is slightly chewy and the meat fresh and tender, with the richness of pork belly coming through the salty-sweet soy flavor, and a thick sauce that's great with rice. It is rich in high-quality protein and minerals such as calcium and phosphorus, which are nourishing. Frying the fish and balancing the seasoning takes a little patience, but the steps are clear and beginners can follow them. It takes about 1 hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1051 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type), ginger, garlic, dried chili peppers
- Oil, salt, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, Shanxi aged vinegar, oyster sauce, cooking wine, white sugar
- Carp, pork belly

## Calculation

Version for 2 people.

- Carp (about 1kg / 2 jin)
- Pork belly 100g
- Scallion 200g
- Ginger 80g
- Garlic 3-4 cloves
- Dried chili peppers 2
- White sugar 50g

## Steps

Note: the fish below has been given basic preparation by the seller when bought: scaled and gutted.

1. Wash the scallion, ginger, garlic and dried chilies separately.
2. Cut the white part of the scallion into segments about 4cm long, then split each segment into four.
3. Slice the ginger about 3mm thick.
4. Smash and mince one garlic clove; cut the rest in half.
5. Cut each dried chili into four pieces.
6. Slice the pork belly into pieces about 4cm*4cm.
7. Clean the fish.
8. Make several diagonal cuts in the thick meat along the back to help the flavor soak in
9. Pour a generous amount of oil into the wok and heat to 7/10 heat (just starting to smoke). Add the fish and fry for 1 minute until the skin firms up slightly, then remove and set aside (don't move the fish around as soon as it goes in; wait until it has fried for a bit before moving and flipping it). Pour out the frying oil, leaving a little in the wok
10. Heat the remaining oil and add the pork belly, frying until fragrant.
11. Add the dried chilies, scallion, ginger and halved garlic cloves and stir-fry for 1 minute.
12. Put the fried fish into the wok.
13. Pour in along the edge of the wok:

  - 50ml cooking wine
  - 50ml aged vinegar
  - 50ml premium light soy sauce (weijixian)
  - 20ml dark soy sauce for color
  - 5ml oyster sauce for savoriness
  - 5g salt
  - 50g white sugar
  - Water to cover the fish.

14. Turn to medium heat and bring to a boil.
15. Turn to low heat and braise slowly to let the flavors soak in.
16. After 15 minutes, remove the lid and pick out the scallion, ginger, garlic and dried chilies.
17. Turn to high heat to reduce the sauce. When 1/4 of the liquid remains, sprinkle on the minced garlic, turn off the heat and serve.
18. Red-braised carp is ready!

## Additional Notes

Pork belly is added to red-braised carp for the following reasons:

- When stir-fried, pork belly releases lard, and animal fat is more fragrant than vegetable oil.
- Pork belly fried until golden and then braised is also delicious, acting as a side dish.
- Fish itself is low in fat, so it lacks aroma; its strength is the texture of the meat. That's why lard is generally recommended for fish dishes, and if you want a pot of milky white, rich fish soup, lard is the best choice~
