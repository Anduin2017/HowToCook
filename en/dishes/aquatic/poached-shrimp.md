# How to Make Poached Shrimp

Poached shrimp (白灼虾) is a classic quick Cantonese dish. The shrimp is sweet, springy and tender, with its natural flavor intact. Shrimp is rich in high-quality protein and various minerals, and with a dipping sauce it's appetizing without being greasy. The method is simple and forgiving, very beginner-friendly, and takes only about 15 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 519 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Live shrimp
- Onion
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Green onion
- Cooking oil
- Soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Sesame seeds
- Oyster sauce
- Zhenjiang black vinegar

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

- Shrimp 250g * portions (1-2 portions recommended)
- Green onion 1 stalk
- Ginger 1 piece
- Onion 1
- Garlic 5-8 cloves
- Cooking oil 10-15ml
- Cooking wine 20 ml
- Soy sauce 10-15ml
- Sesame seeds 1 handful
- Black vinegar 10 ml
- Oyster sauce 10 ml

## Steps

1. Cut the onion into small pieces and slice the ginger. Spread them flat in a frying pan.
2. Rinse the live shrimp (deveining and trimming off the legs and antennae with scissors are optional), drain, and spread them over the onion and ginger in the pan.
3. Pour the cooking wine into the pan, cover, cook on medium heat for 1 minute, low heat for 5 minutes, then turn off the heat and leave for 5 minutes.
4. In parallel with the previous step, make the dipping sauce:

  - Chop the green onion and mince the garlic, add the soy sauce, sesame seeds and black vinegar, and stir.
  - Heat the oil and pour it over the dipping sauce.

5. Take the shrimp out of the pan and put them on a clean plate.

## Additional Notes

- Technical details:
  - Don't use high heat at the start, to avoid burning the bottom.
  - If your lid has a steam vent, adjust the time accordingly (consider adding 30 seconds on medium heat).
  - The dipping sauce is optional; it can also be plain vinegar. Fresh shrimp, nature's gift, tastes wonderful when there's no water to wash away and dilute its sweetness.
