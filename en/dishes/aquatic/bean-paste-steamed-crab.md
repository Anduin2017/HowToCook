# How to Make Bean Paste Steamed Crab

This dish is an autumn-only savory specialty from Pinghu in Zhejiang: the creamy crab roe is coated in savory bean paste, the crab meat is sweet, and the sauce over rice is unbeatable. It is rich in high-quality protein and trace elements. The difficulty is moderate, suitable for beginners with some basics, and it takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 468 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Crabs (freshwater crabs preferred, swimming crabs as a second choice)
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Rock sugar (optional)
- Dark soy sauce
- Rapeseed oil (unbleached rapeseed oil, commonly called "crude rapeseed oil" or "country rapeseed oil"; peanut oil as an alternative)
- Ketchup
- Cooking wine
- Mature ginger
- Green onion
- Egg (optional)
- Minced pork (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Crabs 500 g (about 3-4 medium freshwater crabs)
- Doubanjiang 30-50 g
- Rock sugar 0-15 g
- Dark soy sauce 15 ml
- Rapeseed oil 20 ml
- Ketchup 15 ml
- Cooking wine 5 ml
- Mature ginger 10 g
- Green onion 10 g
- Egg 1 (about 50 g)
- Minced pork 50 g
- Water 500 mL

## Steps

1. Scrub the crabs clean, then split them in half on the cutting board
2. Add the rapeseed oil to the wok, add the minced ginger and doubanjiang and fry until fragrant, then add the rock sugar and stir until it melts. Once small bubbles appear, scoop it out
3. Spread a layer of the sauce on a plate, then arrange the halved crabs neatly on top, cut side down
4. Add some green onion segments and ginger slices. It's recommended to crack an egg over it or spread minced pork on the bottom of the plate
5. Steam for 10-12 minutes

## Additional Notes

- For a savory version, use 50 g of doubanjiang; for a sweet version, use less doubanjiang and add rock sugar and ketchup
- Sprinkling some chopped green onion on after cooking makes it more fragrant and better looking
- Reference: [As the autumn wind rises, this delicious Pinghu dish is great with rice - Tide News](https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=2925971)
