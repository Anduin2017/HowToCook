# How to Make Butter Fried Shrimp

![Butter fried shrimp - preview image 1](../../../dishes/aquatic/黄油煎虾/黄油煎虾.jpg)

Butter fried shrimp is a richly flavored home-style seafood dish with fresh, tender, springy shrimp. Fresh shrimp is rich in high-quality protein and minerals such as calcium and zinc, and butter adds a creamy aroma and energy. It's relatively simple; you just need patience to prepare the live shrimp, and beginners can manage it smoothly. It takes about an hour in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 741 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh shrimp (kuruma prawns with firm meat are highly recommended; ordinary prawns also work)
- Butter (Anchor recommended, one small 7g portion per batch)
- Black peppercorns (in a grinder bottle)
- Light soy sauce
- Salt
- White sugar
- Rice wine

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Fresh shrimp 300g
- Butter 7g
- Black peppercorns about 15ml
- Cooking oil 45ml
- Light soy sauce 10ml
- Rice wine 5ml
- White sugar 10ml
- Salt 2.5ml

## Steps

1. Twist off the shrimp heads, pulling out the vein at the same time (if this doesn't work, you can remove the vein in the next step when butterflying). Cut open the back with scissors or slice it open with a knife, drain and set aside
2. Mix the sauce: in a small bowl, stir together all the light soy sauce, rice wine, white sugar and salt in the amounts above and set aside
3. Heat the wok over medium-high heat, add the cooking oil and wait 10 seconds for it to heat up
4. Put all the shrimp in the wok, start grinding black pepper evenly over them, and stir-fry
5. After the shrimp change color, add the butter. Once it has completely melted, pour in the sauce and keep stir-frying
6. Stir-fry on high heat for 15 seconds to reduce the sauce, then plate

## Additional Notes

- Butterflying the shrimp helps them absorb the flavor, but be very, very careful with the knife; beginners can easily cut themselves
