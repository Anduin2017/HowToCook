# How to Make Red-Braised Fish Head

Red-braised fish head is a classic home-style braised dish. The fish head is rich in gelatin, the meat is silky and tender, and the sauce is savory and slightly spicy, perfect with rice. Fish head is rich in high-quality protein, lecithin and unsaturated fatty acids, which nourish the skin and brain. Because you need to handle raw fish and chop through bone, it's somewhat harder, and complete beginners aren't advised to attempt it alone. The whole process (including marinating) takes about 2–2.5 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 333 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Note: if possible, prepare a separate cleaver. Supermarkets and markets sell cheap cleavers with thicker blades; a blade thickness of 5-7mm is best.
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type), ginger, garlic, cilantro, red finger chili (meirenjiao)
- Oil, salt, chicken bouillon powder, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, Shanxi aged vinegar, ground black pepper, cooking wine
- Star anise, dried chili peppers
- 1 fish head
- Note: fish heads sold at the market are generally of two kinds: silver carp and bighead carp. The former is cheaper; the latter is a little more expensive but tastes better!

## Calculation

Note: this dish only comes in a version for 2 people.

* 1 fish head
* Scallion 200g
* Ginger 80g
* Garlic 3-4 cloves
* Red finger chili 1/4
* Cilantro 4 stalks
* Star anise 2, dried chili peppers 5

## Steps

### Ingredient Prep

1. Wash the scallion, ginger, garlic, cilantro and red chili separately.
2. Just give the dried chilies and star anise a quick rinse.
3. Cut the scallion in half. Cut the back half (the white part) into segments about 4cm long. Cut the front half (the green part) into segments first, then split each segment into four.
4. Slice the ginger about 3mm thick.
5. Smash the garlic.
6. Take two stalks of cilantro, remove the roots and chop into 1.5cm pieces.
7. Cut the red chili into 3mm thick rings.
8. Cut each dried chili into four pieces.

### Marinating the Fish Head

1. Note: the "fish body" below refers to the fish meat that comes attached to the fish head when you buy it.
2. Scale the fish head and clean out any innards that weren't fully removed.
3. Chop off the fins and clean out the gills.
4. Chop through where the jaw joins the body. Chop the body into chunks and the head into four or six pieces.

  * Note: preparing fish is hard to fully describe in words; search for videos on preparing fish heads.

5. Rinse the chopped fish head, ideally washing off any blood remaining on the pieces.
6. Put the cleaned fish pieces in a basin and add 5g of salt, 10g of light soy sauce and 10g of cooking wine. Add the scallion (the chopped front half) and 1/3 of the ginger slices. Mix evenly and let stand for 1-2 hours.

### Final Steps

1. Add 30ml of oil and wait for the wok to heat up...
2. When the oil is hot, turn the heat down to low

  * If you don't understand why, see the section on stir-frying aromatics in [Learn Stir-frying and Pan-frying](../../tips/learn/stir-frying-and-pan-frying.md).

3. Add the ginger slices and stir-fry slowly until most of the juice in the ginger has been fried out and it turns golden.
4. Add the scallion segments and stir-fry until they turn slightly white.
5. Add the smashed garlic, star anise and dried chilies and stir-fry for 5 seconds.
6. Pour the marinated fish head into the wok and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes.
7. Pour in 500ml of water and add 2g of salt, 3g of chicken bouillon powder, 5g of light soy sauce, 3g of dark soy sauce, 5g of cooking wine, 2g of ground black pepper and 3g of aged vinegar.
8. Put two stalks of cilantro into the wok and cover.
9. Turn to high heat and bring to a boil.
10. Turn to medium heat and braise slowly to let the flavors soak in.
11. When the liquid has reduced by half, remove the lid.
12. Turn to high heat to reduce the sauce. When 1/3 of the liquid remains, turn off the heat and transfer to a small basin.
13. Note: pour the sauce from the wok evenly over the fish head. When plating, you can put the cilantro cooked in the wok on the bottom of the basin; this makes the finished dish look and taste better.
14. Place cilantro on top of the plated fish head, and put the red chili rings on top of the cilantro.
15. A red-braised fish head that looks, smells and tastes great is ready!
