# How to Make Steamed Sea Bass

Steamed sea bass is a classic Cantonese steamed dish. The fish is delicate and silky, with a light, fresh flavor that preserves as much of the sea bass's natural taste as possible. Sea bass is rich in high-quality protein and DHA, nutritious and easy to digest. This dish is simple and very beginner-friendly, taking only about thirty minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 385 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Sea bass (if you're afraid of killing fish, ask the vendor to do it)
- Green onion
- Ginger
- Cooking oil
- Seasoned soy sauce for fish
- Cooking wine
- Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Sea bass 1
- Green onion 3 stalks
- Ginger 1 piece
- Cooking oil 10-15ml
- Seasoned soy sauce for fish 10-15ml
- Cooking wine 10-15ml
- Salt 5-10g

## Steps

1. Slice and julienne the ginger. Cut the white part of the green onion into segments and julienne the green part. Soak the julienned aromatics in cold water and set aside.
2. Rinse the cleaned sea bass and pat it dry with paper towels. Make several cuts on each side, rub with salt to wash off the slime, then rub 10g of salt all over the inside and outside of the fish and marinate for at least 10 minutes.
3. Here is an extra way to cut and plate the sea bass: after this cut, the fish can stand upright while steaming, so it heats evenly and is easier to eat, with no need to flip it.
4. ![Cutting the fish](../../../dishes/aquatic/清蒸鲈鱼/改刀.jpg)
5. ![Steamed sea bass - preview image 2](../../../dishes/aquatic/清蒸鲈鱼/摆盘.jpg)
6. Stuff the belly with ginger and the white part of the green onion, and sprinkle more over the fish, using half of the prepared amount. On the steaming plate, use chopsticks to keep the fish raised off the plate
7. Heat the water until you can feel it's warm, then put the fish in
8. Steam over high heat for 10 minutes.
9. Transfer the steamed fish to a clean plate and remove the ginger and scallion on top
10. Pour 15ml of seasoned soy sauce for fish over the fish
11. Sprinkle fresh ginger and green onion shreds over the fish, heat 10ml of cooking oil in a pan until hot, and pour it over the fish. Serve

## Additional Notes

- Technical summary:
  - Anyone can make this dish. The key is timing, which depends on the size of the fish. Too long and the fish becomes tough, which really ruins the texture; too short and part of the fish won't be cooked. So steaming over high heat for under 10 minutes is generally best.
  - Remember to raise the fish off the plate with chopsticks when steaming. There are two benefits:
    - 1. During steaming, water drips onto the plate; if the fish sits directly in it, it will taste fishy.
    - 2. The fish heats evenly.
  - This dish is easy, and it tastes amazing
