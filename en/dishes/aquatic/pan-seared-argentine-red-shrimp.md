# How to Make Pan-Seared Argentine Red Shrimp

![Pan-seared Argentine red shrimp - preview image 1](../../../dishes/aquatic/干煎阿根廷红虾/干煎阿根廷红虾.jpg)

A delicious and tempting quick seafood dish. After pan-searing, the Argentine red shrimp meat is white and tender with a rich, sweet flavor, and the shells are crisp and fragrant. It is rich in trace elements such as iodine, phosphorus and astaxanthin, which help boost immunity, protect the heart and lower cholesterol. The difficulty is moderate: you need to master heat control and flipping, but the steps are clear and beginners can succeed by following them. It takes about 20 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 309 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Argentine red shrimp (frozen shrimp used here)
- Sea salt (grinder)
- Black pepper (grinder)
- White wine
- Light soy sauce
- Cilantro
- Lemon
- Onion
- Ginger
- Garlic

## Calculation

- Argentine red shrimp 2-3
- Sea salt 5g
- Black pepper (grinder)
- White wine 20ml
- Light soy sauce 1ml
- Cilantro 3 sprigs
- Lemon 1 slice
- Onion 10g
- Ginger 10g
- Garlic 10g

## Steps

1. Thaw the Argentine red shrimp. Ideally, move them from the freezer to the refrigerator 1 day in advance to thaw naturally; this better preserves flavor and texture. You can buy shrimp that are already butterflied and deveined to save a lot of time
2. Rinse the thawed shrimp, pat them dry and set aside. Make sure to drain them well; if you're in a hurry, blot them dry with paper towels
3. Slice the ginger and cut the onion into small squares. Wash the cilantro, separate the leaves from the stems, and chop the leaves. Crush the garlic and mince it
4. Heat the pan on high. Once hot, add two tablespoons of olive oil. When the oil is hot, add the ginger slices, onion pieces and cilantro stems and stir-fry
5. After about 1 minute, remove the ginger, onion and cilantro stems and discard them
6. Turn to medium-high heat and add the shrimp to sear. Make sure one full side of every shrimp is in contact with the pan. Sear for about 2 minutes, brushing each shrimp with a layer of oil at the same time
7. When the shells on the bottom are slightly browned, flip them, sprinkle in the minced garlic, and gently shake the pan so they heat evenly
8. After about 1 minute, add 20ml of white wine
9. Sear for another minute, then turn to medium-low heat and sprinkle evenly with salt and black pepper
10. Put one drop of light soy sauce on each shrimp
11. Sprinkle with the cilantro leaves and plate
12. Cut a slice of lemon and place it on the edge of the plate

## Additional Notes

- Lemon enhances the flavor of the shrimp but is rather sour; add it to taste, or leave it out
- Eat while hot
