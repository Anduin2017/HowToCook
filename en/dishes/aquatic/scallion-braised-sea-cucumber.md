# How to Make Scallion-Braised Sea Cucumber

![Scallion-braised sea cucumber - preview image 1](../../../dishes/aquatic/葱烧海参/葱烧海参.jpeg)

This is a classic home-style braised seafood dish from Shandong cuisine, featuring a rich scallion aroma and soft, well-seasoned sea cucumber, in a thick, glossy sauce that is savory with a sweet finish. Sea cucumber is rich in collagen and various trace elements, which are very nourishing. Overall the cooking isn't difficult, but you need to spend time in advance fully rehydrating dried sea cucumber. The actual braising takes only about thirty minutes, and beginners can succeed easily by following the steps.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 299 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Rehydrated sea cucumber ![Sea cucumber](../../../dishes/aquatic/葱烧海参/海参.jpeg)
- White part of a large scallion

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Rehydrated sea cucumbers (Arctic sea cucumber) 4
- White part of 1 large scallion
- Cooking oil 20-25ml
- Oyster sauce 20g
- Light soy sauce 5g
- White sugar 2g
- Starch 2g

## Steps

1. Cut the scallion whites into 1cm segments and set aside.
2. Cut the sea cucumbers into 1cm segments and set aside.
3. In an empty bowl, add 20g of oyster sauce, 10g of light soy sauce and 2g of white sugar and stir evenly. ![Sauce](../../../dishes/aquatic/葱烧海参/酱汁.jpeg)
4. In another empty bowl, mix starch and water to make a starch slurry for thickening.
5. Heat the wok and add 20ml - 25ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up.
6. Add the scallion whites and turn to *low heat*, being careful not to let them burn. Fry for about 3-5 minutes. ![Scallion whites](../../../dishes/aquatic/葱烧海参/葱白.jpeg)
7. Pick out the scallion whites with chopsticks and set them aside on a plate.
8. Pour in the prepared sauce and stir-fry until fragrant, **waiting 1 - 2 minutes**.
9. Add the cut sea cucumber and stir-fry for 1 minute
10. Add 100 ml of water, turn to medium-low heat, and **wait 5 minutes**
11. When the liquid in the wok is nearly gone, add the starch slurry and the scallion whites you set aside earlier
12. Once it *looks thick*, turn off the heat and plate ![Scallion-braised sea cucumber - preview image 5](../../../dishes/aquatic/葱烧海参/葱烧海参.jpeg)

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, keep an eye on the amount of liquid in the wok. If it's nearly dry, add the starch slurry right away.
