# How to Make Garlic Steamed Prawns

This traditional Cantonese dish is known for being fresh, fragrant, tender and smooth: the prawn meat is sweet and springy, with a strong garlic aroma. Sea prawns are rich in high-quality protein and trace elements such as calcium and selenium, and garlic helps kill bacteria and whet the appetite. The method is very simple: prepare the prawns, spread the sauce, steam, and pour over hot oil. Beginners can handle it easily, and it takes about 15 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 456 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Sea prawns
* Garlic sauce
* Cooking oil
* Light soy sauce

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Sea prawns 8
* Garlic sauce 50 g
* Cooking oil 20 ml
* Light soy sauce 5 ml

## Steps

1. Cut each prawn down the middle from the head with a knife, stopping 1 cm from the tail
2. Spread the garlic sauce down the middle of each prawn and place them on a plate
3. Pour hot water into the pot, put the plate in, and steam on high heat for 3 minutes
4. Heat the oil, pour it over the plate of prawns, and pour on the light soy sauce
