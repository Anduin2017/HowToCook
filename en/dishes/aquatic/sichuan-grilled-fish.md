# How to Make Sichuan Grilled Fish

This Sichuan-style grilled fish is numbing, spicy and fragrant, with fish that is charred outside and tender inside, and side vegetables that soak up the broth and go perfectly with rice. Grass carp is rich in high-quality protein, and paired with several vegetables it makes a balanced meal. It looks complicated, but following the steps it's actually not hard, making it suitable for beginners who want to try a showpiece dish. It takes about an hour from prep to table.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 2152 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Grass carp (have the vendor at the market or supermarket kill it and remove the unwanted organs)
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
- Cooking wine
- Ground white pepper
- Salt
- Garlic
- Cassia bark
- Star anise
- Bay leaves
- Green Sichuan peppercorns
- Dried chili segments
- Lantern chilies
- Hot pot soup base (any kind)
- Tofu skin sheets (qianzhang)
- Mung bean sprouts
- Onion
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Celery segments
- Roasted peanuts
- White sesame seeds
- Cilantro (tastes better with it, but optional depending on your taste)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Grass carp about 1.5kg (3 jin)
- Scallion half a stalk
- Cooking oil 20ml
- Cooking wine 10-15ml
- Salt 5-10g
- Ground white pepper 5g-10g
- Cassia bark 1 small piece
- Star anise 2
- Garlic cloves 8
- Bay leaves 2
- Green Sichuan peppercorns 1 small handful
- Dried chili segments 10
- Lantern chilies 4
- Celery 2 stalks, cut into segments
- Onion half
- Tofu skin 1 sheet

## Steps

1. Cut the grass carp (usually 1.5kg) open along the back. Make several cuts on both sides running down from the back, but don't cut through to the belly skin, or it won't hold its shape

  - You can pour hot water over the fish to wash off the slime, or keep scrubbing it with a brush under running water until it no longer feels slimy.

2. Put the fish in a container, add the cooking wine, 10g of ground white pepper and 5g of salt, rub evenly and marinate for 20 minutes.
3. Cut the half stalk of scallion into pieces, halve the garlic cloves, and put them in a container with the star anise, bay leaves and cassia bark
4. Cut the dried chili segments in half and put them in a container with the lantern chilies
5. Cut the celery into small segments
6. Blanch the bean sprouts
7. Blanch the tofu skin and cut it into shreds
8. Cut the onion into shreds.
9. Grill the fish

  - If you have an oven, brush the baking tray with oil, place the fish skin-side down, and bake until golden on both sides, then sprinkle with ground cumin
  - If you don't have an oven, heat the wok with oil, sprinkle 2g of salt around the sides of the wok, and add the grass carp to pan-fry. Don't rush to flip it right after it goes in; wait until one side has set before flipping. Fry until golden on both sides, sprinkle with ground cumin, and transfer to a plate.

10. Add 20ml of cooking oil to the wok. When the oil is hot, add the scallion, garlic, star anise and bay leaves and stir-fry until fragrant
11. Add half a packet of hot pot soup base and 15-20g of doubanjiang and stir-fry until the oil turns red
12. Season with 5g of white sugar, 10g of salt and 5ml of light soy sauce, then add water to the same level as the ingredients and bring to a boil
13. Add the celery, bean sprouts and tofu skin shreds in that order. They don't need to cook through; just scald them briefly, then spread the onion shreds on top and place the grilled fish on top
14. Add the dried chilies, lantern chilies and green Sichuan peppercorns
15. Heat oil in another pan, and when hot, pour it over the chilies you just added to release their aroma
16. Finally, sprinkle with roasted peanuts, chopped green onion, white sesame seeds and cilantro
17. Simmer for 5-6 minutes, and the dish is ready.

## Additional Notes

- Technical summary:
  - The amounts of salt, pepper, cumin, cooking oil, light soy sauce, white sugar and so on in this dish depend on your taste and the proportions of your ingredients; you don't need to follow the amounts above strictly.
