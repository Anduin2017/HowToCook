# How to Make Bream Braised with Tofu

![Bream braised with tofu](../../../dishes/aquatic/鳊鱼炖豆腐/鳊鱼炖豆腐.jpg)

Bream braised with tofu is a home-style red-braised dish: the fish is fresh, tender and well-seasoned, the tofu soaks up plenty of broth, and the flavor is savory with a little heat. Bream is rich in high-quality protein and unsaturated fatty acids, while tofu provides plant protein and calcium, making a balanced combination. The difficulty is moderate; frying the fish takes a little patience to keep it from sticking, but the steps are clear and beginners can give it a try. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 524 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Bream (you can ask the vendor to clean the fish)
- Firm tofu
- Ginger
- Green onion
- Garlic
- Dark soy sauce
- Cassia bark (optional)
- Rock sugar
- Dried chili peppers (leave out if you don't eat spicy food)
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- Salt
- Star anise (optional)
- Bay leaves (optional)
- Hot water

## Calculation

Note: these amounts serve about 2-3 people

Per portion:

- Bream: 550 grams
- Firm tofu: 400 grams
- Ginger: 5 slices
- Green onion: half a stalk (50 grams)
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Dark soy sauce: 2ml (leave it out if you don't like strong flavors)
- Cassia bark: 1 piece
- Rock sugar: 5 pieces
- Dried chili peppers: 4-6 (to taste)
- Cooking wine: 5ml
- Light soy sauce: 4ml
- Salt: 5-8 grams (to taste)
- Star anise: 1
- Bay leaves: 1-3
- Cooking oil: 10ml
- Hot water: 400 grams

## Steps

1. Score the bream, top with ginger slices and cooking wine and marinate for 5-10 minutes
2. Cut the firm tofu into cubes and put them in water until needed
3. Add oil to the wok. You can put a little salt in the wok to keep the fish from sticking. Pat the marinated fish dry with paper towels, put it in the wok and fry both sides
4. When both sides are done, push the fish toward the side of the wok a bit to leave space, and add the green onion, ginger, garlic, dried chilies, bay leaves and star anise to fry until fragrant
5. Once the aromatics are fragrant, add the cooking wine, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, rock sugar and cassia bark, then pour in hot water to the same level as the fish or a little less
6. After bringing to a boil on high heat, add the tofu against the sides of the wok, add the salt, and turn to low heat
7. Simmer on low heat for 10-15 minutes, then reduce the sauce a little on high heat and serve

## Additional Notes

- Fry each side of the fish for 2-4 minutes
- Use medium-low heat the whole time when frying the fish
- Adding some minced garlic right before serving makes it taste even better
