# How to Make Sichuan Boiled Fish

Sichuan boiled fish (水煮鱼) is a classic Sichuan dish that is numbing, spicy and fragrant, with silky tender fish. Basa fish is rich in high-quality protein and low in fat, and paired with seasonal vegetables it makes a balanced meal. The method is of medium difficulty and a bit challenging for beginners, but following the steps will get you there. It takes about 2 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 248 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Basa fish
- Vegetables (e.g. sliced potato / bean sprouts / cauliflower / lettuce / ...)
- Red oil doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Green Sichuan pepper oil
- Rapeseed oil
- Ground white pepper
- Garlic cloves
- Salt
- Sugar
- Measuring cup
- Kitchen scale (optional)
- Large stainless steel bowl

## Calculation

The amounts below serve 3 to 5 people.

- Basa fish 500g
- Vegetables (e.g. sliced potato / bean sprouts / cauliflower / lettuce / ...) any combination; 300g to 500g in total is recommended
- Red oil doubanjiang 40g (if you like it spicy and want more red oil, add another 10 to 20g)
- Fermented black beans 10g (optional)
- Green Sichuan pepper oil 10ml
- Rapeseed oil 25ml
- Ground white pepper 3g
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Salt 5g
- Sugar 2g

## Steps

1. Prep: if the basa is from the freezer, leave it at room temperature to thaw naturally for 5 hours before slicing.
2. Slicing: slice the basa into thin pieces, about 5cm long and 3cm wide.
3. [Marinating](../../tips/learn/marinating-meat.md): put the sliced basa in the large stainless steel bowl
4. Add 30g of doubanjiang, 3g of salt, 10ml of green Sichuan pepper oil and 3g of ground white pepper
5. Mix well by hand, then add 5ml of rapeseed oil at the end to seal in the flavor
6. Leave at room temperature for at least 30 minutes to absorb the flavor.
7. Vegetable prep: mince the garlic. Taking 300g of cauliflower and 200g of lettuce as an example, wash the cauliflower and lettuce.
8. Blanching and stir-frying: [blanch](../../tips/learn/blanching.md) the cauliflower in boiling water and set aside; wash the lettuce, let it dry, stir-fry until cooked (no oil needed) and set aside.
9. Frying the doubanjiang: heat the wok, add cold oil (20ml of rapeseed oil), add 10g of doubanjiang, 10g of fermented black beans (optional) and the minced garlic, and stir-fry slowly over **medium heat**.
10. Poaching the fish: add 150ml of hot water. As soon as it boils, add the marinated fish slices and gently stir so they separate in the water. Season with 2g of salt and 2g of sugar (adjust the salt to taste at this point). Once the water boils again, it's ready to serve.
11. Plating: put the cooked vegetables into a large bowl first, then place the hot fish slices on top of the vegetables, and pour the remaining hot broth from the wok over them!

## Additional Notes

- You can freely choose the combination and amount of vegetables at the bottom, but pay attention to each vegetable's characteristics. For example, if you use potatoes, cook the potato slices/chunks through (poke with a chopstick to check).
- Adjust the amount of red oil doubanjiang (spiciness) and salt to taste.
- When slicing the fish, first cut it into 5cm pieces perpendicular to the length of the fillet, then rotate 90 degrees and slice it thinly at an angle.
- When [marinating](../../tips/learn/marinating-meat.md), don't squeeze too hard.
