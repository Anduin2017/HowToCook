# How to Make Curry Crab Stir-fry

This is a home-style seafood dish with Thai-inspired flavor: rich curry wraps plump crab meat, with the aroma of crab roe and the smooth texture of coconut milk. Crab is rich in high-quality protein and various trace elements, which helps restore energy. The process is clear; you only need to pay attention to searing the roe and to tossing the sauce off the heat at the end. It is friendly to people with some cooking experience, and usually takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 815 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Mud crab (also called meat crab)
- Curry roux blocks (recommended: Lohui crab roe curry)
- Onion
- Coconut milk
- Eggs
- Potato starch (also called starch)
- Garlic

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

- Mud crab 1 (about 300g) * portions
- Curry roux 15g (one small block) * portions
- Coconut milk 100ml * portions
- Egg 1 * portions
- Onion 200g * portions
- Garlic 5 cloves * portions

## Steps

1. Lift off the crab's top shell and chop the crab in half. Gently crack the claws with the back of the knife. Dip the cut surfaces and the claws lightly in starch, not too much. Sprinkle 5g of starch into the top shell to cover the roe. Set aside
2. Finely chop the onion. Set aside
3. Mince the garlic. Set aside
4. Boil a kettle of water. Set aside
5. Heat the wok, add about 20ml of cooking oil, and wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
6. Gently place the crab cut-side down in the wok and sear for 20 seconds. This step mainly seals in the roe and the meat. Then flip and sear each side for 10 seconds. Remove the crab and set aside
7. Put the top shell in the wok and use a spoon to ladle hot oil from the wok into the shell to seal the roe inside. After 20 seconds, remove and set aside
8. Without washing the wok, add another 10ml of cooking oil and heat on high until it just starts to smoke. Add the minced garlic and chopped onion and stir-fry for 10 seconds
9. Add the curry roux and stir-fry until melted (10 seconds), then add the seared crab and toss evenly
10. Pour in 300ml of boiling water and simmer covered for 3 minutes.
11. After simmering, pour in the coconut milk and the egg white, turn off the heat, and keep tossing off the heat until the sauce thickens.
12. Serve
