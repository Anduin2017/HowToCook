# How to Make Sizzling Oil Eel Shreds

Sizzling oil eel shreds (响油鳝丝) is a classic home-style dish from the Jiangsu-Zhejiang-Shanghai region. The eel shreds are fresh, tender and silky, with a strong garlic aroma and a slightly sweet sauce. Hot lard is poured over at the end, releasing an irresistible aroma that goes perfectly with rice. Eel is rich in high-quality protein and DHA. This dish demands some knife skills and heat control; it is moderately difficult and beginners need a little practice. From prep to table it usually takes about 40 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 556 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eel shreds
- Garlic
- Minced ginger
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Salt
- White sugar
- Ground pepper
- Starch
- Minced garlic
- Chopped green onion
- Lard

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Eel shreds 400 g
- Garlic 40 g
- Minced ginger 20 g
- Cooking wine 13 g
- Light soy sauce 3 g
- Oyster sauce 2 g
- Dark soy sauce 2 g
- Salt 2 g
- White sugar 6-15 g
- Ground pepper 3.5-8 g
- Starch 10 g
- Water 50 g
- Minced garlic 40 g
- Chopped green onion 15 g
- Lard 20 g

## Steps

1. Cut the eel into three sections, then into thin shreds
2. Add 0.5 g of ground pepper and 3 g of cooking wine and mix well, then add 5 g of sesame oil to marinate
3. Coat the wok with hot oil, then pour it out
4. Add vegetable oil and heat to 6/10 heat (about 180°C)
5. Add half of the minced garlic and all of the minced ginger and stir-fry a few times
6. Add the eel shreds and stir-fry over medium heat for 30 seconds
7. Drizzle 10 g of cooking wine around the edge of the wok and stir-fry a few times
8. Add the light soy sauce and stir-fry evenly
9. Add the oyster sauce and dark soy sauce and stir-fry a few times
10. Add the salt, white sugar and 3 g of ground pepper and stir-fry evenly
11. Mix the starch and water into a slurry, pour it into the wok, and reduce until the sauce is thick
12. Transfer to a plate and sprinkle with the minced garlic and chopped green onion
13. Heat another pan, add the lard, heat it to 7/10 heat (about 210°C), and pour it over the eel shreds

## Additional Notes

- Don't wash the eel's blood off too thoroughly, or the eel will easily turn dark and smell bad
- You can ask the vendor to kill and clean the eel for you
- You can add a bit more ground pepper and white sugar; this helps remove fishiness and boost aroma
