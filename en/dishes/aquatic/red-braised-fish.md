# How to Make Red-Braised Fish

Red-braised fish is a classic dish full of home-style flavor: savory with a hint of sweetness, the fish tender and well-seasoned. It is a much-loved Chinese braised dish. Fish is rich in high-quality protein and unsaturated fatty acids, which are easily absorbed. Handling raw fish and pan-frying it involve a few more steps, which is a bit challenging for beginners, but following the recipe will get you there. From prep to plate it takes about 40 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 570 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Ginger, garlic cloves, dried chili peppers
- Oil, salt, cooking wine, vinegar, soy sauce, white sugar
- Fish

### Optional Extra Ingredients

Note that these amounts come from experience. Except for cilantro, more is not better.

- Oyster sauce
- Green onion
- Cilantro
- Bird's eye chili
- MSG (chicken bouillon powder)

## Calculation

* Fish: beginners should start with one medium-sized crucian carp. Score it in advance so it cooks through (otherwise the meat along the back can stay undercooked)
* Ginger shreds: slice 2-3 pieces from an ordinary piece of mature ginger, then cut into shreds
* Garlic 3-4 cloves, smashed, minced or sliced
* Dried chili peppers (to taste) 2-3, chopped
* Cilantro to taste
* Salt 10g; if the chilies are very hot, use a bit more
* Vinegar 5ml
* Soy sauce 5ml
* White sugar 10g
* Green onion 1-2 stalks, normally sprinkled as chopped green onion
* Bird's eye chili 1-2, or leave it out. From someone who's been there: at most 2, or it'll be so spicy you'll regret it the next day.
* MSG to taste; don't add too much, 5g is enough.
* Oyster sauce, 5g is enough, for the same reason as MSG

## Steps

### Ingredient Prep

1. Prepare the ginger and garlic and chop them
2. Chop the dried chilies and put them with the ginger and garlic

### Final Steps

1. Add 30-50ml of oil and wait for the wok to heat up...
2. Add the **fish, patted dry** (unless you want to get splattered with hot oil), then swirl the wok to pan-fry the fish in the hot oil. Be sure to use low heat during this process
3. Flip the fish and repeat the pan-frying above
4. Add the ginger, garlic and chilies and stir-fry until fragrant
5. Pour in the cooking wine, a bit more than usual. Be careful during this step; it will produce a lot of smoke
6. Pour in the vinegar (if you like vinegar, add a bit more)
7. Then add the white sugar and soy sauce (dark soy sauce)
8. Add cold water until it just covers the fish, then turn to medium heat and cover. After about 1 minute, flip the fish and cover again
9. After 3-4 minutes, add the salt, bird's eye chili and oyster sauce (MSG, chicken bouillon, etc.), then cover again; keep flipping the fish from time to time after this
10. When the sauce has reduced to just below the dorsal fin on the fish's back (or when there isn't much sauce left), turn to low heat, add the cilantro and chopped green onion, cover for 20 seconds, then turn off the heat
11. Serve
