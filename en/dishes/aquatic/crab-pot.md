# How to Make Crab Pot

This dish has a rich, savory sauce, sweet and slightly spicy crab meat, soft potatoes and chewy rice cakes, and the sauce is especially satisfying over rice. It's a showpiece dish perfect for gatherings. It is rich in high-quality protein and various trace elements. It is somewhat difficult: you need to handle live crab and control the heat and seasoning ratios, which is a bit challenging for beginners. The whole process takes about 1 hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 2111 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Meat crab (recommended in order of preference: Myanmar black crab, mud crab, swimming crab, Chinese mitten crab)
- Shrimp (optional)
- Potatoes
- Rice cakes (hard rice cakes recommended; fresh rice cakes also work)
- Onion
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Dried chili peppers
- Green bell pepper
- Red bell pepper
- Oyster sauce
- Hoisin sauce (optional)
- Soybean paste (optional)
- Sweet bean sauce (or barbecue sauce, optional)
- Ketchup (if you don't have any, crush half a tomato)
- Starch (corn starch or potato starch)
- Rock sugar
- Chicken bouillon powder
- Ground white pepper
- Cooking wine (can be replaced with Shaoxing wine)
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Beer
- Water

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for 3-4 people.

Per portion:

- Meat crab 500 g
- Shrimp 200 g
- Potatoes 450 g
- Rice cakes 200 g
- Onion 100 g
- Garlic 20 g (if adding shrimp, add another 10 g)
- Ginger 15 g
- Dried chili peppers 5 g
- Green bell pepper 30 g
- Red bell pepper 30 g
- Oyster sauce 20 g
- Cooking oil 30-50 ml (if adding shrimp, add another 10 ml; deep-frying needs an extra 500 mL)
- Hoisin sauce 15 g (substitute: 12 g oyster sauce + 3 g white sugar)
- Soybean paste 15 g (substitute: 12 ml light soy sauce + 2 ml dark soy sauce + 1 g white sugar)
- Sweet bean sauce 10 g (substitute: 5.5 g white sugar + 4 ml light soy sauce + 0.5 ml dark soy sauce)
- Ketchup 10 g
- Starch 40 g
- Rock sugar 10 g
- Chicken bouillon powder 3 g
- Ground white pepper 2 g (if adding shrimp, add another 1 g)
- Cooking wine 15 ml
- Light soy sauce 20 ml
- Dark soy sauce 5 ml
- Beer 200 ml (if adding shrimp, add another 50-100 ml)
- Water 800 ml (if adding shrimp, add another 200 ml)

## Steps

1. Cut the potatoes into 3 cm cubes, the green/red peppers into diamond-shaped pieces 4 cm on a side, the onion into 3 cm wide crescent wedges, and the rice cakes into 1 cm thick slices
2. Cut the crab into 50-80 g pieces, coat thinly in starch, and deep-fry in 180 °C oil for 1 minute, then remove
3. Put oil in the wok, fry the garlic, ginger and dried chilies until fragrant, then add all the sauces + rock sugar and stir-fry over low heat until the oil turns red. Be careful not to burn the bottom
4. Add the crab, potatoes, beer and water, bring to a boil, then turn to low heat and simmer for 12 minutes
5. Add the rice cakes and green and red peppers, turn to high heat and reduce until the sauce clings to the ingredients, then sprinkle with ground white pepper

## Additional Notes

- It's recommended to freeze the crab for 20 minutes to knock it out (don't freeze it solid!), or insert a chopstick through its mouthparts to destroy the nerves
- If using frozen crab, fully thawed crab legs fall off easily and the meat loses moisture. It's better to coat and fry them half-thawed, and extend the frying time to 2 minutes
- If your stove is low-powered, the oil temperature drops quickly once ingredients go in. Preheat the oil to 200 °C and fry in several batches
- Be sure to remove the crab's stomach (the triangular sac), gills and heart (the hexagonal white piece)
- Pat the starch-coated crab dry before frying; hot oil spatters easily when it meets water
- Never use dead crab (unless it was bought frozen, but not if it was thawed and not used promptly)
- Don't eat with persimmons or strong tea
- People with gout should eat with caution
- Soak the rice cake slices in cold water after cutting and rinse them before adding to the pot so they don't stick. Place them on top once added; if they sink to the bottom they'll easily burn
- If a crab pinches you or you get burned, rinse under cold water for 15 minutes first. Seek medical attention if it's serious, and definitely if the wound is deep
