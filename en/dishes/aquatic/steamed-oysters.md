# How to Make Steamed Oysters

Steamed oysters are a coastal home-style dish that keeps the oysters' natural flavor: the meat is tender and juicy with a light, briny savoriness, and the garlic and ginger bring out a fresh sweetness. Oysters are rich in high-quality protein and zinc, which can help boost immunity. It isn't complicated, but you need to be careful when lifting the hot lid and handling the oyster shells; beginners can manage it fine. From scrubbing to serving it takes only about twenty minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 184 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Oysters
* Green onion
* Garlic
* Ginger
* Soy sauce
* Brush

## Calculation

* Drinking water 1 liter
* Oysters 6
* Green onion 3 stalks
* Garlic 6 cloves
* Ginger 1 small piece
* Soy sauce 1 ml per oyster

## Steps

1. Scrub the oysters clean with a brush (use a toothbrush if you don't have one).
2. Put water in the steamer, place the steamer tray on top, lay the 6 oysters flat on the tray, and steam at 50% power for 3 minutes.
3. Holding a damp cloth in your right hand, lift the hot lid. Pry off and remove the top half of each oyster's shell, with the convex side down and the flat side up. Put 1 shred of ginger and 10g of minced garlic on each oyster.
4. Close the hot lid and steam at 100% power for 3.5 minutes.
5. Turn off the heat. Holding the cloth in your right hand, lift the hot lid and add 5ml of soy sauce to each.
6. Plate.
