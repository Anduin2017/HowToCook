# How to Make Oil-Braised Prawns

This Shandong cuisine classic coats whole prawns in a sauce that blends salty and sweet; the finished dish is glossy red, and the prawn meat is firm and springy. Prawns are rich in high-quality protein and astaxanthin and low in fat, making them a nutritious ingredient that won't weigh you down. You need to make scallion oil, trim the prawns, braise and reduce the sauce, which takes some control of heat and seasoning, so it's better suited to cooks with some basics. The whole process takes about 40 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 584 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Black tiger prawns or king prawns
- Scallion, ginger
- Cooking wine, salt, rock sugar, vegetable oil

## Calculation

Version for 1 person.

- Prawns 10
- Sichuan peppercorns 5g
- Scallion 50g
- Ginger 20g
- Shaoxing wine 30g
- Salt 3g
- Rock sugar 10g
- Vegetable oil

## Steps

1. Cut off the head spike down to the base, trim off the antennae and legs, remove the sand sac, butterfly the back and remove the vein. Rinse and set aside
2. Fry the aromatic oil

  - When the oil reaches 3/10 heat, add the Sichuan peppercorns. When the oil is hot, take it off the heat and add the scallion and ginger (ideally without letting the oil change color). When the scallion turns slightly yellow, strain the oil (if you make too much scallion oil, keep it for tossing noodles).

3. Add oil to the pan, arrange the prawns neatly, and once both sides change color, gently press the prawn heads

  - Add the ginger rice (ginger cut into fine granules)
  - Shaoxing wine 30g
  - Water, two small bowls
  - Salt 3g
  - Rock sugar 10g

4. Bring to a boil over high heat, then turn to low heat and braise covered (don't add more liquid halfway, and don't lift the lid)
5. When the shells are glossy and the prawns are curled, take them out and arrange them on a plate
6. Reduce the sauce (strain it, pour it back into the pan and reduce until thick, adding the scallion oil) until about 1/4 of the liquid remains.
7. Pour the sauce over
8. Done
