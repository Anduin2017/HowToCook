# How to Make Sweet and Spicy Air Fryer Whole Wings

These sweet and spicy whole wings are cooked in an air fryer, with slightly charred skin and juicy meat, and a popular sweet-with-a-kick flavor. Chicken is rich in high-quality protein, and air frying instead of deep frying greatly reduces the fat, making it lighter to eat. The process is clear and straightforward—just marinate in advance and flip once halfway—so it's fairly beginner-friendly. It takes about 3 hours from prep to plate, but most of that is marinating time; the actual work isn't much.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 775 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Air fryer
- Whole chicken wings
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Garlic powder
- Ground pepper
- Sugar
- Sweet paprika
- Chili powder
- Oyster sauce
- Water
- Oil
- Aluminum foil pan
- Plastic wrap

## Calculation

One portion is a meal for one person; scale proportionally for more people.

One portion:

- Whole chicken wings 4
- Light soy sauce 45ml
- Dark soy sauce 15ml
- Garlic powder 10g
- Ground pepper 5g
- Sugar 10g
- Sweet paprika 10g
- Chili powder 5g
- Oyster sauce 15ml
- Water 20ml
- Oil 10ml

## Steps

1. Take out 4 fresh whole chicken wings and make a cut through the skin between the two bones of the midjoint with a knife, one cut on each side
2. Put the 4 wings in a bowl and add 45ml of light soy sauce, 15ml of dark soy sauce, 10g of garlic powder, 5g of ground pepper, 10g of sugar, 10g of sweet paprika, 5g of chili powder, 15ml of oyster sauce, 20ml of water and 10ml of oil
3. Use a spoon to spread the sauce evenly over the wings, especially into the cuts in the midjoints; this takes about 3 minutes
4. Cover the bowl of marinating wings with plastic wrap and let it rest in the refrigerator for 120 minutes
5. Take out the wings, put the 4 wings in the foil pan, and pour the remaining sauce from the bowl evenly over them
6. Put the foil pan in the air fryer basket and roast at 200 degrees Celsius for 25 minutes
7. Open the air fryer, carefully take out the foil pan, and flip the wings
8. Roast at 200 degrees Celsius for another 25 minutes
9. Take them out and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- The wings in this example are mildly spicy. For more heat, increase the chili powder, but not above 30g
- "Whole chicken wings" means wings that include the tip, midjoint and drumette
- These come out slightly charred. If you don't like a dark brown look, don't pour the leftover sauce into the foil pan, and roast only 20 minutes per side
- Judge the actual results based on your air fryer's power
- Sweet paprika adds color and flavor; it isn't spicy on its own
- You can control the marinating time freely; between 60 and 180 minutes is recommended
- The foil pan is very hot right out of the air fryer; pick it up with tongs or use something heat-insulating like a damp cloth
