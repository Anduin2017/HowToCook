# How to Make Cantonese Sweet and Sour Pork

Gulou rou (咕噜肉) is a classic Cantonese home-style dish: sweet, sour and appetizing, crispy outside and tender inside, with pineapple's fruity aroma making the pork especially refreshing rather than heavy. Pork provides high-quality protein, while green pepper and pineapple add vitamins and dietary fiber. The tricky part is controlling the heat when frying the pork, which is a little challenging for beginners, but following the steps will get you there. From marinating to plating it takes about 40 minutes, a great quick dish to go with rice for the family.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 673 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork shoulder butt (meitou)
- Green bell pepper
- Canned pineapple slices
- Salt
- Ketchup
- White vinegar
- Minced garlic
- Light soy sauce
- Potato starch
- Granulated sugar

## Calculation

Per portion:

- 1 tablespoon = 15ml
- 1 teaspoon = 5ml

- Pork shoulder butt 100g
- Green bell pepper 25g
- Canned pineapple slices 75g
- Salt 1/4 teaspoon
- Ketchup 4 tablespoons
- White vinegar 2 teaspoons
- Minced garlic 1 tablespoon
- Light soy sauce 1/2 teaspoon
- Potato starch 2 1/2 teaspoons
- Granulated sugar 2 tablespoons
- Water 200 ml

## Steps

1. Rinse the pork shoulder (100 grams), pat dry with paper towels, and cut the pork into pieces slightly smaller than you want the finished pieces to be.
2. Marinate the pork with salt (1/2 teaspoon) for 20 minutes.
3. Chop the green bell pepper (25 grams).
4. Cut the pineapple slices (75 grams) into pieces.
5. In a bowl, add ketchup (4 tablespoons), white vinegar (2 teaspoons), minced garlic (1 tablespoon), light soy sauce (½ teaspoon), potato starch (2½ teaspoons), sugar (2 tablespoons), salt (¼ teaspoon) and water (200 ml), and mix into a sauce.
6. Coat the pork pieces in potato starch (6 tablespoons).
7. Heat oil (500 ml) over medium heat.
8. Fry the pork pieces in the wok over medium heat for 5 minutes, then remove.
9. Add the pork pieces back and fry again over high heat for 1 minute.
10. Add oil (1 teaspoon) and the sauce and heat over medium heat for 3 minutes.
11. Add the green pepper and pineapple and heat over high heat for 2 minutes.
12. Toss the fried pork pieces in the sauce until evenly coated, and it's done.

## Additional Notes

- For more texture, add egg to the starch coating; it fries up crispier and more fragrant
