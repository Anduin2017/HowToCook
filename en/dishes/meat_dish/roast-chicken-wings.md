# How to Make Roast Chicken Wings

Marinated chicken wing midjoints roasted at high heat come out with slightly charred skin and tender, juicy meat, with the savory flavor of black pepper and soy sauce. They're rich in high-quality protein and collagen. The difficulty is moderate and beginners can handle it easily; it takes about 1 hour from prep to out of the oven. You can also add honey or five-spice powder to taste for extra flavor.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 187 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken wing midjoints
- Salt
- Ground black pepper
- Soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Oven

## Calculation

- Chicken wings: 3-4 per person
- Salt: 2 g/person
- Ground black pepper: 1 g/person
- Soy sauce: 3 ml/person
- Cooking wine: 3 ml/person

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Preparing the Wings

1. Put the chicken wings in a bowl
2. Add the salt, ground black pepper, soy sauce and cooking wine and marinate for 30-40 minutes

### Roasting

1. Preheat the oven to 200℃
2. Spread the marinated wings evenly on a baking tray
3. Put the tray on the middle rack of the oven and roast for 15-20 minutes
4. Take out the tray, flip the wings, and roast for another 15-20 minutes until cooked through

## Additional Notes

- When marinating, you can add honey, soy sauce, ginger-garlic powder, five-spice powder and so on as seasonings for extra flavor and aroma
- When roasting, line the bottom of the baking tray with foil to make cleanup easier
