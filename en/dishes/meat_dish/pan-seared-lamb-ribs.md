# How to Make Pan-Seared Lamb Ribs

A dish that's crispy outside, tender inside and richly aromatic: the ribs are first simmered with spices, then seared into a golden, crispy crust. It's full of flavor without being heavy, a showpiece dish suitable for both family meals and guests. Lamb is rich in high-quality protein and minerals such as iron and zinc, which help warm and nourish the body. There are a few more steps, but as long as you follow the process, beginners can pull it off. With a pressure cooker it takes about 1 hour in total; with an ordinary pot, about 1.5 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1850 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Lamb ribs
- Cassia bark
- Sichuan peppercorns
- Star anise
- Angelica root (baizhi)
- Bay leaves
- Salt
- Cooking wine
- Chili powder
- Whole cumin seeds
- Barbecue seasoning
- Ground cumin
- White sesame seeds
- Sugar
- Pressure cooker
- Wok / frying pan

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 3 - 4 people.

Per portion:

- Lamb ribs 750g (choose ones with some fat)
- Cassia bark 2g
- Sichuan peppercorns 5g
- Star anise 1
- Angelica root 6g
- Bay leaves 5
- Salt 25g (Note: if you plan to drink the broth from simmering the ribs, leave the salt out of the simmering step and add it in the final searing step)
- Cooking wine 10ml
- Chili powder 5g
- Whole cumin seeds 2g
- Barbecue seasoning 2g
- Ground cumin 2g
- White sesame seeds 2g
- Sugar 1g

## Steps

1. Cut the lamb ribs into pieces following the bones.
2. Put cold water in the wok, add all the rib pieces, and once the water boils, blanch for another 2 - 3 minutes, then remove and set aside.
3. Put the blanched ribs, cassia bark, Sichuan peppercorns, star anise, angelica root, bay leaves and cooking wine in a pot (pressure cooker or ordinary pot).
4. Add drinking water to cover all the ingredients. If using a pressure cooker, cook for 30 minutes once it reaches pressure. If using an ordinary pot, simmer for 80 minutes.
5. Take out the simmered ribs, drain them, and put them in a dry wok / frying pan with no oil or water.
6. Sear over medium-high heat for 6 - 7 minutes until the fat has rendered out of the ribs and *the surface is golden and crispy*.
7. Keep the rendered lamb fat in the pan, add the chili powder, whole cumin, barbecue seasoning, ground cumin and white sesame seeds, and toss evenly.
8. Add the sugar and salt (if you didn't add it during simmering) and toss evenly.
9. Once evenly coated, turn off the heat and plate.

## Additional Notes

- Sichuan peppercorns and angelica root are the key seasonings in this dish and must not be left out.
- Don't simmer too long; ribs that are too soft will fall apart when seared.
- Using the fat rendered from the ribs themselves to fry the seasonings gives better flavor.
- Blanching, simmering and searing produce a lot of lamb fat. Don't pour it straight down the drain, to prevent clogs.
