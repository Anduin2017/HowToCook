# How to Make Wugang Blood Duck

![Blood duck (extra spicy)](../../../dishes/meat_dish/血浆鸭/血浆鸭%28特辣%29.jpg)

![Blood duck (mild)](../../../dishes/meat_dish/血浆鸭/血浆鸭%28微辣%29.jpg)

Blood duck (xuejiang ya) is a famous traditional dish from Wugang in Hunan: the duck is fragrant, crisp and tender, and after soaking up the vinegared duck blood, even the ginger and chilies become sweet and mild. Duck meat and duck blood provide high-quality protein and iron, good for nourishing the blood. There are quite a few steps and you need to watch the heat and keep stir-frying, but following the steps, beginners can finish it in about 2 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 2482 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh young duck
- Fresh duck blood (when slaughtering the duck, collect the blood with vinegar and stir clockwise with chopsticks to prevent clotting)
- Ginger
- Garlic cloves
- Green onion
- Chili peppers
- Alcohol (baijiu, beer or rice wine all work)
- Light soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Salt
- Chicken bouillon powder

## Calculation

Per portion (2-4 servings recommended):

- Fresh young duck 2000g
- Fresh duck blood 250ml
- Ginger 6 slices (add 1-3 more slices if you like it spicier)
- Garlic 6 cloves
- Green onion 2 stalks, chopped
- Chili peppers 1000g (depending on how spicy you like it, choose green chilies, long thin chilies, red finger chilies, etc.; if you like it hot, add 5-8 bird's eye chilies or facing-heaven chilies, chopped)
- Alcohol (choose one):
  - High-proof baijiu 50ml + water 150ml
  - Beer 200ml
  - Rice wine 200ml
- Light soy sauce 10ml
- Cooking wine 30ml
- Salt 8g
- Chicken bouillon powder 5g

## Steps

1. Cut the fresh duck into pieces about 3cm, and add cooking wine and ginger slices to draw out the blood.
2. Heat the wok, add about 100ml of cooking oil, and once the oil is very hot over high heat, add the marinated duck and keep stir-frying.
3. When the duck has completely changed color (visibly whitened), add the alcohol and 200ml of boiling water, just enough to cover the duck. Cover and cook over medium heat for 15 minutes.
4. Once the water boils, open the lid, add the ginger and garlic, stir-fry once through, cover and keep heating for 10 minutes.
5. Open the lid, add the chilies and keep stir-frying. When the chilies have visibly softened, add the fresh duck blood. Now you must keep stir-frying to make sure every piece of duck and every piece of chili is coated in blood (this is the essence of blood duck).
6. Stir-fry until the duck blood has visibly turned black, add the salt, chicken bouillon and green onion (if you like mountain pepper oil, you can add 3-6 drops now), and toss once or twice more.
7. Plate and serve.

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, watch the liquid level and adjust the heat to keep it from boiling dry and sticking.
- When adding the duck blood, keep stir-frying and stirring to keep it from clumping and setting into lumps.
