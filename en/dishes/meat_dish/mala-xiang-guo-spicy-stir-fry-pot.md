# How to Make Mala Xiang Guo (Spicy Stir-fry Pot)

A richly numbing and spicy Sichuan-style home dish (mala xiang guo) that brings together vegetables, meat and soy products, satisfyingly spicy with layers of flavor. The mix of meat and vegetables provides high-quality protein and various vitamins for complete nutrition. The steps are clear, but you need to master blanching and stir-frying heat, which is a bit challenging for beginners. It takes about 30 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1050 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Leafy greens (bok choy, Indian lettuce, spinach)
- Boneless meat (pork, beef, chicken, fish balls, ham sausage)
- Dried tofu sheets
- Beijing mala instant noodles
- Dried chili peppers

## Calculation

These amounts are for about 3 people.

- Leafy greens 455 grams in total, with bok choy, Indian lettuce and spinach in whatever proportion you like
- Boneless meat 430 grams in total, with pork, beef, chicken, fish balls and ham sausage in whatever proportion you like
- Dried tofu sheets 152 grams
- Beijing mala instant noodles 1 pack
- Dried chili peppers 5 grams
- Mala xiang guo seasoning 110 grams

The proportions of greens, meat, dried tofu and instant noodles above are recommendations; if you have favorite ingredients, redistribute as you like.

## Steps

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil (enough to cover the ingredients in steps 2-4).
2. Add the greens to the boiling water, blanch for 5 minutes, then take them out and set aside.
3. Add the boneless meat to the boiling water, blanch for 4 minutes, then take it out and set aside.
4. Add the Beijing mala instant noodles to the boiling water, cook for 2 minutes, then take them out and set aside.
5. Pour out the water, dry the pot, add 105 grams of cooking oil, and heat on high for 30 seconds.
6. Add the mala xiang guo seasoning and stir-fry for 20 seconds.
7. Add the dried chilies and stir-fry for 10 seconds.
8. Add the blanched greens, turn to medium heat, and stir-fry for 3 minutes.
9. Add the blanched meat and stir-fry for 3 minutes.
10. Add the cooked instant noodles and toss with chopsticks for 1 minute.
11. Turn off the heat and serve.

## Additional Notes

About heat levels:

- High heat is usually about 240 degrees Celsius; on an induction cooktop, about 2 kilowatts of input power.
- Medium heat is usually about 120 degrees Celsius; on an induction cooktop, about 1.4 kilowatts of input power.
