# How to Make Spicy Chicken Feet Pot

![Spicy chicken feet pot](../../../dishes/meat_dish/香辣鸡爪煲/result2.jpg)

Spicy and rich with a springy texture, this is a common home-style dish that goes great with rice. Chicken feet are rich in collagen, which helps nourish the skin and strengthen tendons and bones. You need to trim the claws first, and if using whole chicken feet you also need to debone them, which is a little challenging for beginners, but patiently following the steps will get you there. From blanching to slow simmering to the final stir-fry, it takes about 45 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 986 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken feet
- Green onion
- Ginger
- Cooking wine
- Bay leaves
- Star anise
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Garlic
- Bird's eye chili
- Chili powder (optional)
- Oyster sauce
- Five-spice powder
- Salt
- Chicken bouillon powder

## Calculation

- Chicken feet 500g (1 jin)
- Bay leaves 3
- Star anise 3
- Bird's eye chili 6
- Minced ginger 10g
- Minced garlic 10g
- Green onion 2 stalks
- Oyster sauce 3g

## Steps

1. Cut the claws off the chicken feet. If you bought only the foot pads, just cut them in half. If they're whole chicken feet, debone them. Rinse clean with water.
2. Put the chicken feet in cold water with green onion, ginger and cooking wine and blanch. When the water boils, skim off the foam.
3. Add the bay leaves, star anise, light soy sauce and dark soy sauce, cover, and simmer over low heat for thirty minutes.
  ![Simmering chicken feet](../../../dishes/meat_dish/香辣鸡爪煲/result1.jpg)
4. Take out the chicken feet, saving a bowl of the broth.
5. Heat oil in the wok and fry the minced ginger, minced garlic and bird's eye chilies over low heat until fragrant; if you can handle the heat, add some chili powder. Add the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, five-spice powder and salt and fry until you smell the savory sauce.
6. Add the chicken feet, season with a little salt, stir-fry for a minute or two, then pour in the broth, stirring as you cook.
7. Add chicken bouillon for savoriness, sprinkle in green onion segments, mix well, and serve.

Note: if you want to add side vegetables, add them after frying the sauce base and before adding the chicken feet.
