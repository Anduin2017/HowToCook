# How to Make Country-Style Beer Duck

Duck braised with beer gives a rich sauce and meat that is savory, fragrant and tender, with a light beer aroma—a home-style dish with a distinctive flavor. Duck is nourishing, rich in protein, B vitamins and various minerals, and braising it with beer helps it absorb the flavors. The dish has a few more steps, but they aren't complicated; beginners can usually finish it in about 1 hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1324 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Duck
- Beer
- A wok at least 32 cm across (a small wok makes stir-frying hard)
- Green chili peppers
- Red chili pepper
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Bird's eye chili
- Garlic sprouts
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
- Black cardamom (caoguo)
- Cassia bark
- Star anise
- Bay leaves
- Dried chili peppers

## Calculation

- Duck (half a duck, 1 kg; ask the vendor to chop it into small pieces)
- Beer 1000 ml (you can buy two 500 ml cans)
- Green chili peppers 2, red chili pepper 1 (anywhere from 10 to 15 cm long) (cut into 2 cm segments or slices)

### Aromatics

- Garlic 4 cloves, smashed
- Ginger 3 cm piece, smashed
- Bird's eye chili 3, each cut into two pieces (leave out if you don't eat spicy food)
- Garlic sprouts 2 stalks, cut into segments
- Scallion 2 stalks: 1 cut into segments, 1 set aside

### Spices

- Black cardamom 2, smashed and seeds removed
- Cassia bark, one small 4 cm piece
- Star anise 3
- Bay leaves 3
- Dried chili peppers 6 (leave out if you don't eat spicy food)

## Steps

### Blanching the Duck to Remove Fishiness and Blood

1. Rinse the duck and put it in the pot
2. Add water to cover the duck
3. Add 20 ml of cooking wine
4. Add the 1 scallion you set aside
5. Add 2 cm of the smashed ginger
6. Turn on the heat and bring to a boil
7. Skim off the foam
8. Take out the duck, rinse it clean with water and set aside

### Cooking

1. Wash the wok, heat it, and add 60ml of peanut oil
2. When the oil reaches 60 degrees, add a handful of Sichuan peppercorns (30)
3. Add the duck and stir-fry for 4 minutes

  - After 2 minutes, add all the spices
  - At 3 minutes, add all the aromatics (ginger, garlic, bird's eye chili)

4. Add 1000 ml of beer
5. Braise the duck for 30 minutes

  - At 10 minutes, add salt (3 grams), light soy sauce (10 ml) and dark soy sauce (5 ml)
  - At 20 minutes, add all the peppers (green and red chili segments)
  - At 29 minutes, add the garlic sprout segments and scallion segments
  - Stir-fry for 1 minute

6. Serve

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, watch the liquid level. If it drops below 2/3 of the ingredients, add hot water or more beer.
