# How to Make Xinjiang Big Plate Chicken

![Big plate chicken](../../../dishes/meat_dish/新疆大盘鸡/大盘鸡.jpeg)

Xinjiang big plate chicken (dapanji) is a richly flavored, colorful Xinjiang home-style dish: tender, juicy chicken and soft potatoes that soak up the sauce, numbing and spicy with a slightly sweet finish. It's rich in high-quality protein and carbohydrates, and bell peppers add vitamin C. You need to make caramel and manage the heat when reducing the sauce, which is a bit challenging for beginners, but it's not hard if you follow the steps carefully. It takes about 1 hour overall.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1108 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Sichuan peppercorns, bay leaves, xiangguo spice, dried long chilies (xianjiao), garlic, scallion
- Oil, salt, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, cooking wine (beer works), white sugar
- Chicken (thigh meat is best), potatoes, green bell pepper and sweet red pepper (optional, but they add nice color)

## Calculation

For 2 people

- Meat from two pistol-cut chicken legs (about 1kg)
- Potatoes, 2 medium: 750g
- Scallion 100g
- Green bell pepper and sweet red pepper, one each, 50g each
- White sugar 20g
- Dried long chilies 5
- Garlic 4 cloves
- Oil 50g

## Steps

1. Chop the meat into chunks first, soak in water + salt for 5 minutes to remove blood and gaminess, then drain
2. Wash the scallion, garlic, chilies, potatoes and so on, and peel the potatoes
3. Cut the scallion whites into 4cm long segments, and cut the bell peppers and chilies into chunks
4. Roll-cut the potatoes, i.e. cut once, roll the potato a little, cut again; each piece should be about 4cm*4cm
5. Make the caramel: add the oil to the wok, then the sugar, and stir back and forth with a spatula until the sugar melts and turns golden brown. Then add the drained chicken and stir-fry to color it
6. Add the Sichuan peppercorns, bay leaves, xiangguo spice, dried long chilies and so on and stir-fry
7. Add 5g of salt, 7ml of light soy sauce, 10g of oyster sauce and 100g of cooking wine, then pour in 1 liter of water. Beer can be used instead of cooking wine
8. Turn to medium heat and bring the water to a boil, then turn to medium-low and simmer slowly to let the flavors soak in
9. When the liquid has reduced until the chicken is about to be exposed, place the potatoes on top: be careful not to stir the potatoes in, just lay them on top, otherwise if they get stirred to the bottom they'll stick. Keep simmering with the lid on, and after a while add the scallion, green bell pepper and sweet red pepper and keep simmering.
10. When the sauce has mostly reduced, you can turn everything over so the potatoes soak up the sauce, then turn off the heat and serve.

**Also: this dish is great with noodles. Buy thick dough strips, press them flat, then pull them by hand into wide noodles—they must be wide noodles. Once cooked, put them in the big plate chicken and mix with the sauce. Delicious!**
