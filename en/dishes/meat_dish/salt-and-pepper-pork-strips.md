# How to Make Salt and Pepper Pork Strips

Salt and pepper pork strips are a classic Shanghai home-style dish, crispy outside and tender inside, savory and well-seasoned. Pork chops provide high-quality protein and essential amino acids for energy. There are a few more steps, but it's not hard if you follow the process, so beginners can try it boldly. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1290 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork chops
- Egg
- Salt
- Salt and pepper seasoning (jiaoyan)
- Scallion-ginger water
- Flour
- Starch
- Custard powder (adds color and aroma; leave it out if you don't have it)
- Water
- Oil

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for 1 - 2 people.

- Pork chops 4 (about 360 grams)
- Egg 1, 50 g
- Salt 1 g
- Salt and pepper seasoning 10 g
- Scallion-ginger water 100 ml
- Flour 80 g
- Starch 80 g
- Custard powder 2-3 g (adds color and aroma; leave it out if you don't have it)
- Water 10 g
- Oil 10 g

## Steps

1. Wash the pork chops, remove the bones, pound them loose with the flat of the knife, cut into thick slices, then into thick strips.
2. Add the salt and pepper seasoning and mix. Once it becomes sticky, add the scallion-ginger water **in several additions**, then marinate in the refrigerator for 20 minutes.
3. Make the batter: add 80 g of flour, 20 g of starch (note: 20 g of starch, keep the remaining 60 g for later), 2 - 3 g of custard powder and 1 g of salt.
4. Crack in an egg and stir, then add 100 g of water in several additions, then add 10 g of oil, and stir repeatedly until the batter is completely smooth and slightly thick.
5. Take the remaining 60 g of starch, take out the pork strips, coat them in a layer of starch, then in the batter.
6. Add enough oil to the wok to cover the ingredients and heat to about 150 ℃ - 160 ℃. Add the pork strips and fry until light golden, then remove. The strips may stick together when they first go in; don't touch them. Once they've set, you can turn them with chopsticks and they'll separate.
7. When the oil is back up to 150 ℃ - 160 ℃, fry the strips again until golden, then remove.
8. Sprinkle with salt and pepper seasoning, toss evenly, and serve.

## Additional Notes

- You can use breadcrumbs instead of batter, but the texture is very different.
- The batter can also be used for frying chicken legs, fish and so on.
