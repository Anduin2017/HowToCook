# How to Make Pork with Smoked Tofu Strips

Pork with smoked tofu strips (xianggan rousi) is a savory home-style stir-fry that goes great with rice: tender pork strips, chewy smoked tofu and crisp green peppers. It's rich in both animal and plant protein, and the green peppers add vitamins. The difficulty is moderate; beginners can master it with a little practice. It takes about 30 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 472 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork tenderloin (you can buy pre-cut, pre-weighed pork strips at the supermarket)
* Smoked tofu (xianggan)
* Salt
* Light soy sauce
* Starch
* Garlic
* Green peppers
* Chicken bouillon powder

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

* Smoked tofu = portions * 75g
* Number of green peppers = portions * 5
* Pork = portions * 100g
* Salt = portions * 3g
* Light soy sauce = portions * 5ml
* Starch = portions * 5g
* Garlic = portions * 5g (about 3 cloves)
* Chicken bouillon powder = 2g

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Prep

1. Mix the `pork strips` (if you don't have pre-cut strips, cut them yourself) evenly with light soy sauce (3ml) and starch, and set aside.
2. Wash the `green peppers` and `roll-cut` them. Set aside.
3. Slice the `garlic` crosswise, and cut the `smoked tofu` into strips.
4. Mix the `starch` with water (10ml) and stir well.

### Cooking

1. In a clean wok, add 15ml of oil and add the pork strips without waiting for the oil to heat, slowly separating them. As soon as the pork is cooked, take it out, leaving the oil in the wok.
2. Add the garlic slices and smoked tofu to the wok, add 2ml of light soy sauce, and stir-fry evenly.
3. After 2-3 minutes, depending on your heat, add the green peppers and stir-fry together.
4. After 1 minute, add the pork strips and mix.
5. Pour in the starch slurry to thicken, add 3g of salt and 2g of chicken bouillon, stir-fry for 2-3 minutes and serve.
6. Done.

Note: different cookware and stoves affect stir-frying times. If you're not sure whether it's cooked, taste it.

## Additional Notes

**For peppers, choose green peppers—luosi peppers are the optimal solution. If you like it spicy, finely chop some bird's eye chilies and add them with the green peppers!**
