# How to Make Hunan Home-Style Red-Braised Pork

![Hunan home-style red-braised pork](../../../dishes/meat_dish/湖南家常红烧肉/湖南家常红烧肉.jpeg)

Hunan home-style red-braised pork is a home-style dish full of Hunan flavor: after slow braising, the pork belly is rich without being greasy, melt-in-the-mouth soft, savory with a slight heat and sweet finish. It's rich in high-quality protein and fat and provides plenty of energy. It takes some patience and is a bit challenging for beginners, but it's not hard to master if you follow the steps. It takes about 1 hour in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1348 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Skin-on pork belly
- Dried bird's eye chilies 🌶 (to taste)
- Ginger
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Bay leaves
- Cassia bark
- Star anise
- Rock sugar
- Cooking wine
- Salt
- Rock sugar (to seal in fat and add color)
- Cooking oil
- Boiling water

## Calculation

One portion is just enough for 2 people.

- Pork belly: 500g
- Cooking oil: 10g
- Bay leaves 5
- Ginger 3 slices
- Cassia bark 1 small piece
- Rock sugar 6 pieces
- Cooking wine 20 grams
- Light soy sauce 5 grams
- Dark soy sauce 2 grams
- Salt 2 grams
- Star anise 3

## Steps

1. Rinse the skin-on pork belly and put it in a pot of cold water with 2~3 slices of ginger to remove the gamy smell. Bring to a boil, take it out, and rinse off the foam with cold water
2. Cut the pork belly into 1.5cm*1.5cm cubes
3. Heat the wok, add oil, add the rock sugar and stir over low heat until it turns caramel-colored, then add the pork and stir-fry over medium heat to color it
4. Add the prepared ginger slices, star anise, cassia bark, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, cooking wine, dried bird's eye chilies and salt, stir-fry over low heat for 1 minute, then add boiling water to cover the meat
5. Cover and bring to a boil over medium heat, then turn to low heat and braise slowly for 30 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes to prevent sticking
6. When about a third of the liquid remains, turn to medium heat to reduce the sauce, and serve.
