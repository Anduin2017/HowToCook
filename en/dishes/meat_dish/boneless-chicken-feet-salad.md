# How to Make Boneless Chicken Feet Salad

![Finished boneless chicken feet](../../../dishes/meat_dish/无骨鸡爪/无骨鸡爪.jpg)
Tangy, spicy and appetizing with a springy texture, this is a home-style cold snack. Chicken feet are rich in collagen, which is good for the skin. Deboning is tedious and laborious, so the difficulty is high and it's not ideal for beginners. The whole process takes about 8 hours 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 815 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken feet
- Ginger
- Cooking wine
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
- Garlic
- Bird's eye chili
- Onion
- Light soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Black vinegar (Shanxi aged vinegar recommended)
- White sugar
- Salt
- Sichuan pepper oil
- Cilantro
- Lemon

## Calculation

One portion is just enough for 2-3 people.

- Chicken feet 1kg
- Ginger 4 slices
- Cooking wine 65g
- Scallion 3 segments (5cm each)
- Garlic 10 cloves
- Bird's eye chili: 4 for mild, 6 for medium, 12 for hot (hot recommended)
- Onion (half)
- Light soy sauce 75g = 15g * 5
- Oyster sauce 30g = 15g * 2
- Black vinegar (aged vinegar recommended) 50g
- White sugar 10g
- Salt 3g
- Sichuan pepper oil 10ml
- Cilantro 3 stalks
- Lemons 2 (adjust the sourness one lemon at a time)

## Steps

### Preparing the Chicken Feet

1. Use scissors / a knife to cut off all the claw parts of the chicken feet, **including the meat and bone under the claws, so no claw is left at all**
2. Rinse them clean with water and set aside
3. Put the `chicken feet` in a large pot to remove the gamy smell
4. Add the `scallion`, `cooking wine` and `ginger`
5. Add water to cover the `chicken feet`
6. Bring to a boil over high heat **(you can skim off the foam along the way)**
7. Once the water boils **(100 degrees, boiling)**, wait 10 minutes
8. Turn off the heat, take them out, drain, rinse clean, and put them in a basin

### Deboning

This step can be skipped; it takes about 2 hours

1. Put them in the refrigerator **freezer** for 20 minutes
2. Move them all to the non-freezer part of the refrigerator, and take them out in batches **(10 per batch)** to debone
3. Starting from the tip of each toe, slit every toe open with a knife **down to the back of the foot**
4. Then slit from the back of the foot all the way down the leg
5. Bend each toe joint **like cracking your knuckles**
6. Hold the tip of each toe and push inward, doing the same for every toe, first pushing to the palm/back of the foot
7. Once the skin has come off every toe, push from the palm down toward the leg until the whole thing comes off
8. Put them in a bowl and set aside

### Mixing the Marinade

1. Chop the `bird's eye chilies` into evenly sized small pieces
2. Chop the `garlic`, `onion` and `cilantro`
3. Cut the `lemons` in half and squeeze the juice into the container with the chicken feet
4. Pour `all` the seasonings into the container with the chicken feet, along with the `bird's eye chilies`, `garlic`, `onion` and `cilantro`
5. Mix evenly by hand
6. Put in the refrigerator overnight (6 hours)
7. Once mixed, put everything in with the prepared chicken feet

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, watch the water level as it boils. If it drops below 3/4 of the ingredients, add hot water to cover them.
