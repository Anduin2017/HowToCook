# How to Make Sichuan Boiled Beef

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 1](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr1.jpg)

Sichuan boiled beef (shuizhu niurou) is a classic numbing-spicy flavor of Sichuan cuisine: silky tender beef in a glossy red broth, spicy, hot and savory. Beef is rich in high-quality protein and iron, and bean sprouts add dietary fiber. It isn't too hard for beginners, but pay attention to the heat and be careful when pouring the hot oil. It takes about forty minutes overall.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 431 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Beef
- Bean sprouts
- Egg
- Cilantro
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Cooking wine
- Starch
- Dried chili powder
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Red chili pepper
- Oyster sauce

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

This is one portion:

- Beef 300g
- Bean sprouts 100g
- Egg 1
- Cilantro 5 stalks
- Doubanjiang 10g
- Cooking wine 10ml
- Starch 15g
- Dried chili powder 5g
- Ginger 20g
- Garlic 3 cloves
- Red chili pepper 1
- Oyster sauce 8g

## Steps

1. Rinse the beef and slice it.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 2](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr2.jpg)

2. Add 15g of shredded ginger, 1 egg, 15g of starch, 8g of oyster sauce and 10ml of cooking wine, mix well, and marinate for 15 minutes.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 3](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr3.jpg)

3. Wash and chop the cilantro.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 4](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr4.jpg)

4. Pour oil into the wok and add the doubanjiang, 5g of shredded ginger and sliced garlic.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 5](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr5.jpg)

5. Pour in boiling water and cook into a red broth.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 6](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr6.jpg)

6. Wash the bean sprouts, remove the root tails, and blanch in boiling water until cooked.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 7](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr7.jpg)

7. Spread the bean sprouts on the bottom of a bowl.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 8](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr8.jpg)

8. Put the beef slices into the red broth one at a time, and remove them once cooked.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 9](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr9.jpg)

9. Lay the beef over the bean sprouts and sprinkle with the cilantro stems.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 10](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr10.jpg)

10. Sprinkle with the cilantro leaves, chili powder and chili rings.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 11](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr11.jpg)

11. Heat oil in another pan and pour the hot oil over the dish. Done.

![Sichuan boiled beef - preview image 12](../../../dishes/meat_dish/水煮牛肉/sznr12.jpg)
