# How to Make Cumin Beef

Cumin beef is a spicy, tender home-style stir-fry, where the rich flavor of cumin blends perfectly with smooth beef and crisp green pepper. Beef is rich in high-quality protein and iron, and green pepper adds vitamins for balanced nutrition. It requires some knife skills and heat control, so it suits cooks with some experience. Including marinating and prep, it's on the table in about 45 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 612 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Beef tenderloin or beef shoulder
* Green bell pepper
* Cumin (whole seeds > ground)
* Bird's eye chili
* Light soy sauce
* Starch
* Oil
* Salt
* Green onion
* Mortar and pestle (optional)

## Calculation

* Beef: 250 g/person
* Green bell pepper: 2/person, 100g each
* Cumin: 20 g/person
* Bird's eye chili: 3/person, 5g each
* Light soy sauce: 20 ml/person
* Starch: 10g/person
* Oil: 15ml/person
* Salt: 3 g/person
* Green onion: 1 stalk/person, 20g each

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use, adjusting to taste.

## Steps

1. First chop the bird's eye chilies and pound them with the cumin seeds in the mortar into coarse granules; this gives more flavor. If you're short on time, you can skip pounding
2. Cut the tops off the green peppers and remove the seeds (leave them in if you like it spicy), then cut into strips. Cut the green onion into segments.
3. Thaw the beef in advance, rinse it, let it dry or pat dry with paper towels, and slice it along the grain
4. Then marinate the beef: add light soy sauce, starch and oil, mix evenly, and let rest for 30 minutes. For marinating methods, also see [Learn Marinating](../../tips/learn/marinating-meat.md)
5. Heat the wok and add oil, add the green onion, and once fragrant, add the marinated beef and stir-fry
6. Once the beef changes color, add the cumin-chili mixture evenly and stir-fry until cooked
7. Then add the green pepper strips, and once they're just cooked, add the salt
8. Stir-fry on high 🔥 for 1 minute, turn off the heat and toss for another 30 seconds so everything heats evenly, then serve
