# How to Make Pork Stir-fried with Green Peppers and Potatoes

![Pork stir-fried with green peppers and potatoes](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/49046468/205808925-b0ab8f98-0325-4136-8094-3f2ae8c547d5.jpg)

Pork stir-fried with green peppers and potatoes is a home-style stir-fry pairing meat and vegetables: fresh, fragrant and crisp-tender with a rich soy aroma, and especially good with rice. Potatoes are rich in carbohydrates and vitamin C, green peppers provide plenty of vitamin C and dietary fiber, and with the high-quality protein in pork, it's nutritionally balanced. The method isn't complicated and is fairly beginner-friendly, taking only about 1 hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1288 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Green peppers
- Potatoes
- Pork (pork belly)
- Green onion
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Salt
- Soy sauce
- Potato starch

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Green peppers 2 (about 200g total)
- Potatoes 2 (about 300g total)
- Pork 200g
- Green onion 1 stalk (about 10g)
- Ginger 1 piece (about 5g)
- Garlic 3 cloves (about 12g)
- Salt 7g
- Soy sauce 6-10ml
- Cooking oil 10-15ml
- Potato starch 5g
- Water 15g

## Steps

1. Remove the stems from the green peppers and cut into small pieces, peel the potatoes and cut into 2mm thin slices, cut the pork into 4mm thin slices, cut the green onion into 3mm pieces, and peel, smash and mince the ginger and garlic. Mix the potato starch with about 15g of water into a slurry.
2. Heat oil in the wok to 7/10 heat, add the pork slices, and slowly stir-fry until no longer red. Add about 3ml of soy sauce, toss so the pork is evenly colored, and add about 2g of salt.
3. Bring the oil down to 5/10 heat, add the green onion, ginger and garlic and stir-fry for 5 seconds, then add the potato slices. Bring the oil up to 7/10 heat and stir-fry evenly, adding about 5ml of soy sauce and 2g of salt, until the potatoes are just cooked and slightly browned on the surface.
4. Bring the oil up to 8/10 heat, add the green peppers, and stir-fry over high heat until you get "wok hei" (white smoke rises). Keep tossing evenly for 1 minute to color, then finally pour the starch slurry around the edge of the wok and drop to 4/10 heat to thicken.
5. Once it *looks thick*, turn off the heat and plate
