# How to Make Pan-Fried Pork Belly Lettuce Wraps

![Pan-fried pork belly](../../../dishes/meat_dish/香煎五花肉/香煎五花肉.jpg)

Pan-fried pork belly is a quick, home-style meat dish: pork belly slices are fried until fragrant and slightly crispy, rich without being greasy, then wrapped in crisp lettuce leaves with savory doubanjiang for a rich flavor that also cuts the fat. Pork is rich in high-quality protein and fat for quick energy, while lettuce provides dietary fiber and vitamins for more balanced nutrition. This dish suits people with some cooking experience; beginners can handle it easily as long as they watch the heat and guard against oil spatters. It takes about half an hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 2985 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork belly strip (recommended dimensions 20cm\*6cm\*5cm)
- Lettuce
- Soy sauce, salt, MSG, cooking wine, ginger and garlic, oil, doubanjiang

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Pork belly strip (recommended dimensions 20cm\*6cm\*5cm)
- Lettuce 1 head
- Cooking oil 5ml

## Steps

1. Slice the pork belly along its long side, each slice 1mm-1.5mm thick. Set aside
2. Put the sliced pork belly in a bowl and add 8g of soy sauce, 1g of salt, 1g of MSG, 10g of cooking wine, two slices of ginger and two smashed garlic cloves, and marinate for 10 minutes
3. Pull the lettuce leaves off by hand and wash them. Set aside
4. Heat the wok and pour in 5ml of cooking oil. When the oil just starts to smoke, add the pork belly. Fry one side until golden brown, then flip and do the same on the other side.
5. Take out the pork belly and plate it.
6. Spread doubanjiang on a lettuce leaf, wrap up the pork belly, and eat

## Additional Notes

- Be careful of spattering oil while cooking
