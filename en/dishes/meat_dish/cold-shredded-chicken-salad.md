# How to Make Cold Shredded Chicken Salad

![Finished cold shredded chicken salad](../../../dishes/meat_dish/凉拌鸡丝/凉拌鸡丝.jpg)
A low-oil, low-calorie home-style cold dish: the shredded chicken is tender and well-seasoned, and the sesame oil and black vinegar blend into a slightly numbing, slightly tangy refreshing flavor, great for cutting richness in summer. Chicken breast is rich in high-quality protein and low in fat. It's simple; beginners can make it easily, and it takes only about 30 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 293 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken breast (fresh or frozen)
- Sesame oil (Sichuan pepper oil)
- Light soy sauce
- Zhenjiang black vinegar
- White sugar
- Salt
- Cooking wine
- Ginger
- Cooled boiled water

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Chicken breast 200 grams
- Sesame oil 5 ml
- Light soy sauce 4 ml
- Black vinegar 4 ml
- White sugar 3 grams
- Salt 2 grams
- Ginger 20 grams

## Steps

1. Slice the ginger and set aside
2. Pour 4 liters of water into the pot
3. Add the chicken breast and ginger slices
4. Pour in 20 ml of cooking wine
5. Bring the water to a boil over high heat, uncovered
6. Once boiling, turn to medium heat and skim off the foam with a spoon
7. Keep boiling for **5-7** minutes: 5 minutes for unfrozen chicken, 7 minutes for frozen
8. The size of the chicken breast affects cooking time. Poke a chopstick into the chicken; if it goes in easily, the chicken is cooked. If not, cook longer

![Cold shredded chicken - poaching](../../../dishes/meat_dish/凉拌鸡丝/凉拌鸡丝_焯水.jpg)

9. Rinse the chicken breast with cooled boiled water to bring it down to room temperature
10. Tear the chicken breast into fine shreds along the grain

![Cold shredded chicken - shreds](../../../dishes/meat_dish/凉拌鸡丝/凉拌鸡丝_撕.jpg)

11. Get a bowl
12. Add the prepared sesame oil, light soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar and salt to the bowl
13. Stir the sauce until the sugar and salt have dissolved as much as possible
14. Pour the sauce over the shredded chicken and mix well

## Additional Notes

- This dish was made during quarantine with whatever was on hand. If you can, add cilantro, green onion and so on for flavor; if you like it spicy, add chili oil
