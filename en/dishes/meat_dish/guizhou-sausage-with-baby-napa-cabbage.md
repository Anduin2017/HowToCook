# How to Make Guizhou Sausage with Baby Napa Cabbage

![Guizhou sausage with baby napa cabbage](../../../dishes/meat_dish/黔式腊肠娃娃菜/黔式腊肠娃娃菜.jpg)

This dish is savory and delicious, combining the mellow aroma of Guizhou-style cured sausage with the sweetness of baby napa cabbage—a rare non-spicy flavor in southwestern Chinese cuisine that suits everyone. Baby napa cabbage is rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, and with the protein and fat of the sausage it's nutritionally balanced. It's extremely simple, with no need to master heat or add extra seasoning, and takes only 15 minutes from prep to plate—perfect for beginners and busy days.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 1030 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Guizhou-style cured sausage
- Baby napa cabbage

## Calculation

For 1 person:

- Guizhou-style cured sausage 200g
- Baby napa cabbage 300g
- Water 750ml

## Steps

1. Put 750ml of water in the pot and bring to a boil
2. Add the sausage and time 13 minutes
3. Add the baby napa cabbage and time 2 minutes
4. Turn off the heat and take out the sausage and cabbage
5. Cut the cabbage into segments and slice the sausage, then plate

## Additional Notes

- Depending on the size of your pot, use enough water to cover the ingredients—more is better than less
- If you don't have a knife, you can leave the cabbage and sausage uncut
