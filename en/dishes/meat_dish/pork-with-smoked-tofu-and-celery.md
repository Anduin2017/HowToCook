# How to Make Pork with Smoked Tofu and Celery

![Pork with smoked tofu and celery](../../../dishes/meat_dish/香干芹菜炒肉/香干芹菜炒肉.jpg)

Pork with smoked tofu and celery is a home-style stir-fry: chewy dried tofu, crisp celery and tender pork, savory and slightly spicy. Celery is rich in dietary fiber, while dried tofu and pork provide high-quality plant and animal protein. It's simple and beginner-friendly, taking only about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 741 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Dried tofu (doufu gan)
- Chinese celery / celery
- Pork
- Garlic
- Chili peppers: green or red both work
- Sichuan peppercorns: optional
- Salt
- Chicken bouillon powder: optional
- Dark soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

Note: these amounts are for about 2 people.

Per portion:

- Dried tofu: 150g
- Chinese celery: 4 stalks
- Pork: 200g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Chili peppers: 4
- Sichuan peppercorns: 6 (leave them out if you don't like them, or use Sichuan pepper water)
- Salt: 5g
- Chicken bouillon powder: 3g
- Dark soy sauce: 8ml
- Oyster sauce: 5ml
- Cooking oil: 10-15ml

## Steps

1. Remove the celery leaves and cut the stalks into strips no longer than 4cm. Set aside
2. Cut the smoked tofu into strips about as wide as your little finger. Set aside
3. Slice or mince the garlic, either works. Set aside
4. Cut the chilies into rings or diagonal strips, either works. Set aside
5. Heat the wok and add 10ml - 15ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
6. Add the Sichuan peppercorns and garlic and fry until fragrant (if you like ginger, you can also add some ginger slices/shreds)
7. Add the pork and stir-fry until it changes color, then add 8ml of dark soy sauce for color and toss evenly (if you have doubanjiang, you can add 3ml and stir-fry together)
8. Add the smoked tofu and stir-fry evenly (about 2 minutes)
9. Add the chilies and stir-fry evenly (about 1-2 minutes)
10. Add the celery and 5g of salt and stir-fry for 1 minute
11. Add 3g of chicken bouillon and 5ml of oyster sauce, stir-fry evenly, and serve

## Additional Notes

- If you couldn't find Chinese celery, or your celery is fairly thick, consider blanching it: put it in boiling water for 1 minute, then rinse with cold water (to keep it crisp)
- If your dried tofu is fairly hard and dense, you can also blanch it after cutting into strips
