# How to Make Hunan Stir-fried Beef

![Finished Hunan stir-fried beef](../../../dishes/meat_dish/小炒黄牛肉/小炒黄牛肉.jpg)

This Hunan-style stir-fry uses intense high heat to bring out the tenderness of the beef and the aroma of the chilies: fresh, spicy and punchy, with crisp, tender celery—very Hunan in flavor. Beef tenderloin provides high-quality protein and easily absorbed iron, and celery adds dietary fiber for balanced nutrition. Prep and cooking are simple and clear, all done in a quick high-heat stir-fry, which is very beginner-friendly. It takes about an hour from cutting to plating.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 619 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Beef tenderloin
- Celery
- Bird's eye chili
- Pickled wild chilies (yeshanjiao)
- Cilantro

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Beef tenderloin 400g
- Celery 200g
- Bird's eye chili 30g
- Pickled wild chilies 30g
- Cilantro 30g
- Cooking oil 15ml
- Soy sauce 6ml

## Steps

1. Cut the beef tenderloin into thin slices no more than 3cm wide and 3mm thick, add 6ml of soy sauce, mix by hand and set aside
2. Cut the celery into pieces no longer than 5cm and set aside
3. Cut the bird's eye chilies into thin strips and set aside
4. Chop the pickled wild chilies into small pieces and set aside
5. Cut the cilantro into pieces no longer than 3cm and set aside
6. Heat the wok, add 15ml of cooking oil, and wait 30 seconds on high heat for the oil to heat up
7. Add the bird's eye chilies and pickled wild chilies and fry until fragrant
8. Add the beef and celery, then **stir-fry on high heat for 1 minute**
9. Turn off the heat, sprinkle with cilantro, and plate

## Additional Notes

- Use high heat the entire time
