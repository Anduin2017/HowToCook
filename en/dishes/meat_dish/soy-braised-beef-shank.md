# How to Make Soy-Braised Beef Shank

![Soy-braised beef shank](../../../dishes/meat_dish/酱牛肉/酱牛肉.jpg)

Soy-braised beef (jiang niurou) is a northern Chinese home-style cold meat dish with a rich soy aroma and a firm texture that holds together, with plenty of chew. Beef shank is rich in high-quality protein and various amino acids, and slow braising makes it easier to digest. The process isn't complicated, but it includes marinating, slow braising and a long chill, so beginners will need some patience. It takes about 10 hours from start to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 2265 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Beef shank
- Bay leaves
- Ginger
- Green onion
- Dark soy sauce
- Cassia bark
- Rock sugar
- Sichuan peppercorns
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- Salt
- Star anise
- Soybean paste

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Beef shank 2000 grams
- Bay leaf 1
- Ginger 3 slices
- Green onion half a stalk
- Dark soy sauce 15ml
- Cassia bark 1 piece
- Rock sugar 7-8 pieces
- Sichuan peppercorns 15
- Cooking wine 30ml
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Salt 8 grams
- Star anise 4
- Soybean paste 15ml

## Steps

1. Rinse the beef shank and soak in water for 1 hour to draw out the blood
2. Poke holes all over the surface of the beef with a toothpick or fork, add the cooking wine, Sichuan peppercorns and ginger slices, wrap the container tightly with plastic wrap, and marinate for 4-6 hours
3. Cut the beef shank into chunks about 8cm, no larger than 10cm
4. Put the beef in a pot, add cold water to cover, and start timing when the water boils, skimming off the foam. After 3 minutes, turn off the heat, take out the beef and rinse with warm water
5. Put the rinsed beef shank in a clay pot or stockpot, add water to cover, turn on high heat, and add all the remaining seasonings except the salt
6. Once boiling, turn from high to low heat and keep cooking for 90 minutes, then add the salt
7. After adding the salt, keep cooking on low heat for 90 minutes (Note: check the water level every 30 minutes; it should be at least 80% of the height of the beef)
8. After 180 minutes of cooking, take out the beef shank, wrap it tightly in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for several hours
9. Take it out, slice it across the grain, and serve. It's recommended to store the rest of the beef shank unsliced in the refrigerator

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, watch the water level as it boils. If it drops below 2/3 of the ingredients, add hot water to cover them (check about every 30 minutes).
- Wrapping the beef tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerating it for several hours makes the slices firm so they don't fall apart.
- Poking holes in the surface of the beef with a toothpick or fork breaks up the membrane and helps the flavor soak in.
