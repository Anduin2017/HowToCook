# How to Make Stuffed Winter Melon Rolls

![Finished stuffed winter melon rolls](../../../dishes/meat_dish/冬瓜酿肉/冬瓜酿肉成品.jpg)

Stuffed winter melon is a home-style steamed dish pairing meat and vegetable. It's fresh, tender and silky, the savory pork filling blending with the light sweetness of winter melon, refreshing and not greasy. Minced pork provides high-quality protein and essential amino acids, and winter melon helps clear heat, promote urination and hydrate. It's simple; even beginners can handle it with a little practice rolling the meat. It takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 578 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Winter melon
- Minced pork
- Egg
- Green onion
- Minced scallion and ginger
- Ground pepper
- Light soy sauce
- Starch

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Minced pork 300g
- Egg 1 (optional; people not used to it may find it a bit eggy-smelling)
- Winter melon 200g
- Chopped green onion (one stalk, about 20g)
- Ground pepper 5g
- Light soy sauce 10ml
- Starch 5g
- Starch slurry 25g (25g starch, 50ml water)
- Minced scallion and ginger (3-4 slices of ginger, about 30g; just use the white part of the green onion above)
- Salt 20g

## Steps

1. Peel the winter melon and cut into slices 25cm long and 3cm thick

![Winter melon shape](../../../dishes/meat_dish/冬瓜酿肉/冬瓜形状.jpg)

2. Put the cut winter melon in a bowl, add 15g of salt, rub it evenly over the melon, and let it sit for 10 minutes
3. While the melon sits, put the minced pork in another bowl with the minced scallion and ginger, 5g of salt, 5g of starch, ground pepper and light soy sauce
4. Stir the pork mixture clockwise with chopsticks until the ingredients no longer show obvious color contrast (about 2 minutes)
5. Rinse the salted winter melon (it will have softened) three times with water

![Salted winter melon](../../../dishes/meat_dish/冬瓜酿肉/腌制好的冬瓜.jpg)

6. Take 1 slice of winter melon, roll it up, and stuff the meat inside

![Rolling the meat](../../../dishes/meat_dish/冬瓜酿肉/卷肉.jpg)

7. Place the rolls on a plate, arranged around the edge

![Stuffed winter melon rolls - preview image 5](../../../dishes/meat_dish/冬瓜酿肉/摆盘.jpg)

8. Crack 1 egg into the circle in the middle

![Cracking the egg](../../../dishes/meat_dish/冬瓜酿肉/打鸡蛋.jpg)

9. Put the plate in an ordinary wok once the water is boiling, cover with the lid, and steam for 15 minutes
10. Remove the lid and take out the stuffed winter melon
11. Pour the liquid from the plate into the wok, add the starch slurry and 50ml of water, and bring to a boil
12. Pour it over the stuffed winter melon rolls

## Additional Notes

- When taking out the stuffed winter melon, be careful: the plate is very hot.
