# How to Make Xiangqi Rice-Flour Steamed Duck

![Xiangqi rice-flour steamed duck](../../../dishes/meat_dish/湘祁米夫鸭/湘祁米夫鸭.jpg)

A traditional specialty from the Qiyang and Qidong area of Hunan (mifu ya): the duck is stir-fried, then coated in a mix of rice flours and steamed, giving a soft, sticky texture that's savory and full of flavor with a long finish. Duck is rich in high-quality protein and B vitamins, and the rice flour adds energy for balanced nutrition. You need to stir-fry it first and then steam it in a pressure cooker, which is a little tricky and suits people with some cooking experience. It takes about 1 hour from prep to plate; if you use an old duck, the steaming time must be extended.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1332 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Duck (must be freshly slaughtered)
- Glutinous rice flour
- Rice flour
- Steamed meat rice powder (zhengrou fen)
- Fine chili powder (add if you eat spicy food)
- Ground white pepper
- Pork belly (optional)
- Ginger and garlic
- Salt
- Cooking oil
- Boiling water

## Calculation

Normally for 3-4 people

- Duck: 1000g
- Glutinous rice flour: 100g
- Rice flour: 300g
- Steamed meat rice powder: 50g
- Fine chili powder: 50g
- Ground white pepper: 5g
- Pork belly: 50g
- Ginger and garlic: 20g
- Salt: 10g
- Cooking oil: 10g
- Boiling water: 100g

## Steps

1. Mix the glutinous rice flour, rice flour, steamed meat rice powder, fine chili powder, 5 grams of salt and ground white pepper together evenly
2. Ask a friendly vendor to chop the duck into pieces for steaming; slice the ginger, peel the garlic, and slice the pork belly
3. Heat the wok, add cold oil, stir-fry the pork belly to render out its fat, then add the cooking oil and heat, and add the duck to stir-fry
4. Stir-fry the duck until the skin is browned, then add the ginger, garlic and salt and keep stir-frying until fragrant
5. Turn the heat down, add the rice flour mixture and stir-fry so the duck is evenly coated, then add the boiling water a little at a time, stir-frying as you add
6. Once the duck and rice flour feel moist, scoop into a bowl and steam in a pressure cooker with water for 20-25 minutes
7. Sprinkle with chopped green onion before serving, and enjoy

## Additional Notes

This is a dish from my hometown.
Any breed of duck works; I bought a mallard here, for 24 yuan.
Steam for 20 minutes; for an old duck, steam for over an hour.
In the first step, rice flour is the main powder, glutinous rice flour adds a soft, sticky texture, steamed meat rice powder adds five-spice flavor, and chili powder and white pepper add complex aroma.
