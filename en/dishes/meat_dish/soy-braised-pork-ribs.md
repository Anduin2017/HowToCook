# How to Make Soy-Braised Pork Ribs

Soy-braised pork ribs are soy-red in color, with tender meat, a rich bony aroma, and a thick, savory sauce that's salty with a touch of sweetness—a classic home-style dish. Pork ribs are rich in high-quality protein and calcium for balanced nutrition. It involves making caramel and fairly long braising, which is a bit challenging for beginners, but following the steps you can master it. It takes about 1 hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1351 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork ribs
* Five-spice powder
* Cooking wine
* Cooking oil
* Granulated sugar
* Dark soy sauce
* Light soy sauce
* Oyster sauce
* Bird's eye chili
* Garlic
* Ginger

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Pork ribs 300 g
* Five-spice powder 20 g
* Cooking wine 10 ml
* Cooking oil 30 ml
* Granulated sugar 15 g
* Dark soy sauce 10 ml
* Light soy sauce 10 ml
* Oyster sauce 5 ml
* Bird's eye chili 1
* Garlic 2 cloves
* Ginger 2 slices

## Steps

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add the ribs, ginger slices and cooking wine. Once it boils, skim off the white foam with a spoon, and after 2-3 minutes take the ribs out
2. Rinse the ribs in cold water 2-3 times
3. Heat the wok over low heat, add the cooking oil and sugar, and stir gently until the sugar syrup turns yellow
4. Add the ribs and stir-fry for 30 s, then add the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, five-spice powder, garlic and bird's eye chili and stir-fry for 30 s, then add water to cover the ribs
5. Boil over high heat for 30 minutes, add the dark soy sauce for color, and cook for another 10 minutes
6. Plate and serve

## Additional Notes

* For more flavor, consider adding doubanjiang, ketchup, etc.
* Adjust the high-heat boiling time to your situation
* While the ribs are cooking, you can add 2-3 eggs to cook together for simple braised eggs
