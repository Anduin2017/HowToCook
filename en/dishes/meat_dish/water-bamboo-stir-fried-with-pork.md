# How to Make Water Bamboo Stir-fried with Pork

Water bamboo stir-fried with pork is a quick home-style dish, fresh, tender and crisp, savory and well-balanced, a typical Jiangnan home-style flavor. Water bamboo (jiaobai) is rich in dietary fiber and various vitamins, and paired with lean pork for high-quality protein, it balances meat and vegetables. The difficulty is medium; beginners can master it with a little practice. It takes about 20 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 515 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Water bamboo (jiaobai)
* Lean pork
* Starch
* Cooking oil
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Ginger
* Garlic
* Cooking wine
* Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Water bamboo 2 stalks
* Lean pork 100 g
* Starch 15 g
* Cooking oil 30 ml
* Chicken bouillon powder 5 g
* Ginger 1 slice
* Garlic 1 clove
* Cooking wine 5 ml
* Salt 2 g

## Steps

### Prep

1. Slice the water bamboo, each slice 0.5 cm thick
2. Cut the lean pork into strips 0.3-0.5 cm thick, and mix with cooking wine, starch, salt and water
3. Slice the ginger and mince the garlic

### Cooking

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add the water bamboo, boil for 60-90 s, then remove and drain
2. Heat the wok, add 15 ml of oil, add the pork, stir-fry for 60 s, then remove
3. Heat the wok, add 15 ml of oil, add the ginger and garlic and stir-fry for 30 s, then add the water bamboo and keep stir-frying for 30 s
4. Add the pork back and stir-fry for 60 s, add 20 ml of water, add the salt and chicken bouillon, stir-fry for 60 s, and serve

## Additional Notes

* In the last step, you can consider using oyster sauce and light soy sauce instead of salt for color
