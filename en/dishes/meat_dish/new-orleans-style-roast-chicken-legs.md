# How to Make New Orleans-Style Roast Chicken Legs

New Orleans-style roast chicken legs are a simple, easy dish, rich in protein and iron. It takes about 3 hours in total, of which about 20 minutes is hands-on time.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 955 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Oven
- Baking tray
- Aluminum foil
- Pistol-cut chicken legs (whole leg with part of the back, shaped like a pistol)
- New Orleans roast wing seasoning (recommended brand: McCormick)
- Seasoned soy sauce (recommended brand: Rihe "low-salt rice noodle roll soy sauce")

## Calculation

One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Pistol-cut chicken legs 2 (each about 200g, about 400g in total)
- New Orleans roast wing seasoning 15 - 20 g
- Water ≥30 mL

## Steps

1. Thaw the chicken legs and rinse them.
2. Score the chicken legs: on the "barrel" of each pistol-cut leg, make 4-6 cuts with a small knife parallel to the "barrel", cutting as deep as possible.
3. Take a pot and **add 15 - 20 g of New Orleans roast wing seasoning powder, 5 g of salt and ≥30 mL of water**.
4. Put the scored chicken legs in the pot and mix well.
5. Put the whole pot in the refrigerator and **chill for 2 - 4 hours**; the longer, the more flavorful.
6. Put a sheet of aluminum foil on the baking tray, take out the chicken legs, and place them skin side down (don't tear the foil). **Discard the remaining liquid in the pot.**
7. Put the tray on the upper-middle rack of the oven and bake with these settings:
_**Top 230 ℃, bottom 230 ℃, top and bottom heat, 20 minute timer**_
8. Take it out and pour the fat from the tray into a large bowl to set aside. (Don't tear the foil)
9. Flip all the chicken legs.
10. Put the tray on the top rack of the oven and bake with these settings:
_**Top 150 ℃, bottom 230 ℃, top and bottom heat, 20 minute timer**_
11. Take it out and pour the fat from the tray into the large bowl. (Don't tear the foil)
12. Put the tray on the top rack of the oven and bake with these settings:
_**Top 60 ℃, bottom 60 ℃, top and bottom heat, 15-20 minute timer**_
13. Take it out and pour the fat from the tray into the large bowl. (Don't tear the foil)
14. Take a small bowl and **add the fat from the large bowl and the seasoned soy sauce in a volume ratio between 10:1 and 2:1**. The more soy sauce, the saltier; the exact amount depends on your taste.
15. Hold a chicken leg by the end and dip it in the liquid in the small bowl; use a napkin or gloves to protect against the grease. You can now enjoy your New Orleans-style roast chicken legs straight off the foil-lined tray.
If the liquid in the small bowl runs out partway, repeat the previous step.

## Additional Notes

- You can take the pot out halfway through chilling and stir. Don't chill for too long.
- This guide is based on an oven with _**25 L capacity and 1400 W power**_.
If your oven is lower/higher powered, lengthen/shorten the timers accordingly, but make sure the chicken is cooked through.
