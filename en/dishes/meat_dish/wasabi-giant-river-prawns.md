# How to Make Wasabi Giant River Prawns

![Finished wasabi giant river prawns](../../../dishes/meat_dish/芥末罗氏虾/芥末罗氏虾成品.jpg)

This dish is fresh, fragrant and slightly spicy with a strong wasabi flavor and firm, springy prawn meat—a quick home-style seafood dish. Prawns are rich in high-quality protein and calcium and low in fat, and wasabi whets the appetite and cuts the richness. It's medium difficulty; as long as you mix the sauce and control the heat following the steps, beginners can pick it up quickly. It takes about 30 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 579 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Giant river prawns
- Garlic
- Wasabi
- Light soy sauce
- Potato starch
- Ground pepper
- White sugar
- Oyster sauce
- Salt
- Bird's eye chili (leave out if you don't eat spicy food, or replace with red bell pepper)
- Butter
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

Per portion (about 2 servings):

- Giant river prawns 250g
- Garlic 1-2 bulbs
- Wasabi 20g, for the sauce
- Potato starch 10g, for the sauce
- Light soy sauce 30g, for the sauce
- Ground pepper 5g, for the sauce
- White sugar 3g, for the sauce
- Oyster sauce 15g, for the sauce
- Salt 3g, for the sauce
- Bird's eye chili 1-2
- Butter 20g
- Cooking oil 80ml

## Steps

1. **Preparing the prawns**:
   - Cut the prawns open along the back and remove the vein and sand sac. You can also cut them open from the belly; they'll puff open when fried and look nicer
   - Rinse with water, drain, and dust with starch if you like (optional)

2. **Garlic and sauce**:
   - Mince 2 garlic bulbs
   - Make the sauce: add the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, ground pepper and salt, squeeze in wasabi to taste, dilute with water, then stir in the starch

3. **Cooking**:
   - Heat the wok and pour in cooking oil, about enough to cover the bottom
   - Add the drained prawns and fry slowly
   - Once the prawn oil has been fried out (lots of bubbles appear in the wok), add the prepared minced garlic and bird's eye chili
   - When the garlic is fragrant, add the butter
   - Once the butter melts, toss evenly and add the prepared sauce
   - Cover and braise for 2 minutes until the sauce is thick, then serve

## Additional Notes

- **Precautions**:
  1. Don't fry the butter too long once it's in the wok; as soon as it melts and is tossed evenly, add the sauce
