# How to Make Simple Red-Braised Pork

This home-style favorite is glossy and reddish, soft and tender, rich without being greasy, with a deep soy aroma—especially satisfying with rice. Pork belly provides high-quality protein and essential fatty acids, and with quail eggs or tofu skin it's even more nutritious. It's quite beginner-friendly and takes about 1.5 hours from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 2775 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Note: if possible, sharpen your knife as much as you can.
- Main ingredients: `pork belly`, `eggs` (optional), `tofu skin` (optional)
- Supporting ingredients: `ginger`, `rock sugar`, `light soy sauce`, `dark soy sauce`, `cooking wine`, `bay leaves`, `star anise`, `salt`, `water`, `green onion` (remember to use boiling water)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2-3 people.

If only 1 person is eating, consider halving the ingredients.

- Pork belly: about 1.5~2kg (3~4 jin)
- Ginger: 6 slices
- Rock sugar: 15 grams (about 7 pieces)
- Light soy sauce: 10ml
- Dark soy sauce: 15ml
- Cooking wine: 5ml
- Boiling water: enough to cover the ingredients, 600ml-900ml
- Bay leaves: 3
- Star anise: 2
- Quail eggs (optional; if you don't have quail eggs, use the same weight of chicken eggs): 0-2
- Tofu skin (optional): 0-80g
- Salt: 2-3g

## Steps

### Preparing the Ingredients

1. Cut the `pork belly` into large pieces (about 4.5cm; it's easier to cut after freezing for half an hour to an hour)
2. Cut the `tofu skin` into 2cm wide strips
3. Slice the `ginger` (each slice about 3mm thick)
4. Bring the `water` to a boil
5. Boil the `quail eggs` and poke holes in them with a `fork`/`toothpick` (as many as possible, so they absorb flavor)
6. Take the white part of the `scallion` (the `scallion white`)

### Cooking

1. Put the cut `pork belly` in a pot of cold water, add the cooking wine, scallion and ginger, and boil for 15 minutes to remove the blood and gaminess
2. Put two slices of `ginger` in the wok for flavor
3. Turn on medium-low heat and add the `pork belly` directly—no oil needed. Sear all six sides of each piece until the fat renders
4. Pour off the rendered fat and save it. Push the `pork belly` to one side, add 15g of `rock sugar`, and stir until the `rock sugar` melts;
5. Once melted, stir-fry the pork with the sugar until coated and colored, then add

  - `light soy sauce` 10ml
  - `dark soy sauce` 15ml
  - `cooking wine` 5ml
  - and stir-fry until colored;

6. Add the `boiling water` and braise for 40 minutes (if your knife skills are poor and the pieces are too big, braise longer), adding

  - `ginger` 2 slices
  - `bay leaves` 3
  - `star anise` 2

7. Cover and bring to a boil, then add the boiled, poked `quail eggs` and the `tofu skin`, turn to medium-low heat, and wait 40 minutes. (Stir occasionally to prevent sticking);
8. Remove the lid, and when the sauce is almost gone, turn to high heat to reduce it (remember: never reduce it completely dry);
9. Add 2-3g of `salt`, toss, and serve.
