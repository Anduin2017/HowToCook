# How to Make Crispy Air Fryer Fried Chicken

Air fryer fried chicken is a home-style meat dish that's marinated first and then fried: a crispy crust, juicy inside, with seasonings you can easily switch to suit your taste. Chicken wings are rich in high-quality protein and fat, good as a main course or a snack. Hands-on time is about 55-70 minutes (with a single coating; about 70-90 minutes with a double coating). For the best results, marinate overnight in the refrigerator (prepare a day ahead and fry the next day).

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 700 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh chicken wing midjoints
- Light soy sauce
- Garlic powder
- Paprika (sweet red pepper powder)
- Onion powder
- Ground black pepper
- All-purpose flour
- Starch (corn starch or potato starch)
- Cooking oil (for spraying or brushing)
- Air fryer
- Yogurt or milk (optional, makes the meat more tender)
- Egg (optional, helps form a fuller crust)
- Baking powder (optional, helps form a crispier crust)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. This recipe is written for 1 portion (6 wing midjoints, about 300g), which fits in a single layer in most air fryers.

1 portion:

- Wing midjoints 6 (about 300g)
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Yogurt or milk 5ml (optional)
- Egg 1 (optional, for the wet coating)
- Garlic powder 13g
- Paprika 10g
- Onion powder 5g
- Ground black pepper 5g
- All-purpose flour 60g
- Starch 30g
- Baking powder 1g
- Cooking oil 5ml

## Steps

### Marinating the Wings (the Night Before)

1. Rinse the wing midjoints and pat dry, then make 2 cuts on each side of every wing so the flavor soaks in.
2. Mix the marinade: 15ml of light soy sauce, 8g of garlic powder, 5g of paprika, 3g of onion powder, 3g of ground black pepper, and 5ml of milk or Greek yogurt (optional, makes the chicken more tender), stirred evenly.
3. Put the marinade and wings together in a large zip-top bag, mix and massage thoroughly, and leave in the refrigerator overnight.

### Preparing the Coating

4. Dry coating: mix 60g of all-purpose flour, 30g of starch, 5g of garlic powder, 5g of paprika, 2g of onion powder, 2g of ground black pepper and 1g of baking powder (optional, makes the crust crispier) evenly.
5. Wet coating (optional): beat 1 egg thoroughly with 5ml of milk or water.

### Coating

6. Dry coating: shake excess liquid off the wings, press them gently into the dry coating, shake off the excess, and let them rest for 10 minutes.
7. Wet coating (optional): if you want a fuller crust, add a layer of wet coating, then repeat the dry coating step.
    - Dip the rested, dry-coated wings into the wet coating, then gently shake off the excess.
    - Put them in the dry coating again, press gently, and shake off the excess.
    - Let them rest for 20 minutes (a bit longer if you can; the crust becomes sturdier and less likely to fall off).

### Frying

8. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C for 5 minutes.
9. Brush the basket with a thin layer of oil, place the wings skin side down, and spray or brush them with a thin layer of oil.
10. Set the air fryer to 180°C and fry for 10 minutes first.
11. Open the basket, flip the wings, spray or brush the surface with another thin layer of oil, and fry at 180°C for another 6 minutes.
12. Check the color of the skin, and poke the thickest part with a toothpick to see whether any pink juices come out.
13. If needed, fry at 200°C for another 1-2 minutes to crisp and brown (you can iterate: fry 1 minute, check, then decide whether to continue).

## Additional Notes

- This recipe also works for bone-in chicken legs; just adjust the amounts and frying time accordingly.
- Adjust the garlic powder, paprika and onion powder to taste.
- For a spicy version: add 3g of chili powder to the dry coating, or sprinkle 2g of chili flakes on after frying.
- For a cumin version: sprinkle 2g of ground cumin and 2g of white sesame seeds on after frying.
- Don't stack the wings; the touching surfaces won't heat enough and the crust won't be crispy enough.
