# How to Make Lamb Brisket and Bean Curd Stick Pot

This is a traditional clay pot dish from Guangdong: lamb and dried bean curd sticks (zhizhu) seasoned with chu hou sauce and fermented bean curd, rich and savory, luscious without being greasy, warming body and soul, and especially good in the cold of winter. Lamb is rich in protein and iron and is warming and nourishing, while bean curd sticks provide high-quality plant protein, a nutritionally balanced pairing. There are quite a few prep and braising steps, but heat control throughout isn't complicated, and beginners can succeed by following the steps in order. It takes about two and a half hours from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 2391 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Lamb brisket
- Dried bean curd sticks (fuzhu)
- Chu hou sauce
- Fermented bean curd (white)
- Red fermented bean curd (nanru)
- Dark soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Oyster sauce
- Water
- Rock sugar
- Green onion segments
- Ginger slices
- Shiitake mushrooms
- Onion or shallots
- Garlic cloves
- Bay leaves
- Star anise
- Cassia bark
- Other sides such as water chestnuts, potatoes or radish can be added according to your preference

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Lamb brisket 500g
- Fried bean curd sticks 30g-50g
- Chu hou sauce 30g
- Fermented bean curd 40g
- Red fermented bean curd 35g
- Dark soy sauce 5ml
- Chili oil 5ml
- Water 500ml
- Rock sugar 20g
- Granulated sugar 10g
- Green onion 5 stalks
- Ginger 6-8 slices
- Shiitake mushrooms 7-8
- Onion 1, or shallots 4 - 5
- Garlic 7-8 cloves
- Bay leaf 1
- Star anise 4-5
- Cassia bark 10g

## Steps

1. Prep: soak the shiitake mushrooms 2 - 3 hours in advance until soft. Soak the bean curd sticks 30 minutes in advance until soft
2. Sauce 1: mix the red fermented bean curd, chu hou sauce, 20g of fermented bean curd and dark soy sauce evenly in a small bowl
3. Sauce 2: mix 20g of fermented bean curd, the granulated sugar and chili oil evenly in a small bowl
4. Remove the stems from the soaked shiitakes
5. Cut the soaked bean curd sticks into 5cm pieces and squeeze out the water
6. Peel the onion and cut into shreds. You can use peeled shallots instead for even better flavor.
7. Cut the green onion into segments about 5cm long
8. Put the lamb in a pot with 2 - 3 ginger slices and cold water, bring to a boil over high heat, then turn off the heat
9. Take out the lamb and put it in a prepared basin of cold water to cool, which makes it chewier
10. Heat the wok, add cold oil, and fry 4 - 5 ginger slices, the onion/shallots, the white parts of the green onion and 7 - 8 garlic cloves until fragrant
11. Add the cooled lamb and stir with chopsticks for about 2 - 5 minutes until golden
12. Add sauce 1 and stir-fry for about 2 minutes until evenly colored
13. Pour in water until it just covers the ingredients
14. Add the shiitakes, rock sugar, bay leaf, star anise and cassia bark
15. Cover and braise over low heat for 90 minutes
16. Open the lid, add the bean curd sticks, cover and cook over medium heat for 20 minutes
17. Open the lid, add sauce 2 and stir well
18. Turn off the heat, and add the green parts of the green onion or cilantro before serving

## Additional Notes

- This is a Cantonese dish, and authentic versions often add water chestnuts. Since everyone has their own preferences, you can swap in potatoes, radish or other ingredients to taste
