# How to Make Northeastern Pig-Killing Stew

Shazhucai ("pig-killing dish") is a classic stew from northeastern China, tangy, savory and warming. Suancai (fermented napa cabbage) is rich in lactic acid bacteria and dietary fiber, pork ribs provide high-quality protein and calcium, and blood sausage adds iron. You need patience when cooking the blood sausage so it doesn't burst, which is a bit of a challenge for kitchen beginners. It takes about an hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1657 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Blood sausage
- Suancai (fermented napa cabbage)
- Pork ribs
- Cooking wine
- Chili peppers
- Bay leaves
- Star anise
- Green onion knot
- Sesame oil
- Rapeseed oil
- Garlic

## Calculation

This dish is suitable for 2-3 people to share. Each portion should contain:

- Blood sausage 200 grams
- Suancai 500 grams
- Pork ribs 400 grams
- Cooking wine 10 grams
- Garlic cloves 5
- Ginger powder 5 grams
- Dried chili peppers 5
- Bay leaves 2
- Star anise 1
- Green onion knot 1
- Sesame oil 10 grams
- Rapeseed oil 10 grams
- Salt 5 grams
- Dipping sauce: chili oil 5 grams, light soy sauce 10 grams, minced garlic 5 grams, sesame oil 2 grams.

## Steps

1. Poke lots of small holes in the blood sausage with a toothpick, then simmer it in water over low heat for ten minutes. Don't let the water boil—keep it at 80 degrees—or the blood sausage will burst very easily.
2. Cut the cooked blood sausage into pieces and set aside.
3. Blanch the ribs with cooking wine, drain and set aside.
4. Put the rapeseed oil in the wok and fry the garlic cloves, dried chilies and ginger powder until fragrant.
5. Add the ribs and stir-fry until golden on the surface.
6. Rinse the suancai and wring out the water, add it to the wok with the sesame oil and stir-fry. Sesame oil helps remove the sourness and makes the suancai more fragrant. Stir-fry on high heat for two minutes.
7. Add 600 ml of hot water.
8. Transfer to an electric pressure cooker and add the bay leaves, star anise, green onion knot and salt.
9. Pressure cook on the "rich flavor" setting for 40 minutes.
10. When time is up, release the pressure and open the lid. Add the blood sausage and goji berries, cover and let sit for two minutes. The blood sausage is already cooked and doesn't need further heating.
11. Pour into a bowl, mix the dipping sauce according to the list above, and dig in.
