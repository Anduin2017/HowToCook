# How to Make Chu Hou Beef Brisket

![Finished chu hou beef brisket](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/柱候牛腩.jpeg)
![Chu hou beef brisket with rice](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/柱候牛腩配米饭.jpeg)

This is a classic Cantonese dish with a rich meaty aroma and a glossy red color; brisket with tendon becomes tender and full of flavor, with a rich texture. It's rich in high-quality protein and collagen, nourishing and warming. It takes some patience, but a pressure cooker greatly shortens the time, making it fairly beginner-friendly. Ordinary braising takes about 3 hours; with a pressure cooker it takes about 1 hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 980 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Braising pot, pressure cooker (optional, but extremely recommended!)
- Beef brisket (preferably "hang naam", the part with tendon)
  ![Brisket cut](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/牛腩部位.jpeg)
- Chu hou sauce (the core sauce), Pixian doubanjiang, red fermented bean curd, char siu sauce (optional), oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, light soy sauce
- Huadiao wine, baijiu (Chinese liquor)
- Bay leaves, Sichuan peppercorns, star anise, dried chili peppers, cloves, licorice root, dried chili peppers, bird's eye chili (optional), ginger, garlic

## Calculation

The following is one portion for two people:

- Beef brisket 500-600g
- Ginger 30g
- Garlic half a bulb
- Bird's eye chili 1 (adjust to taste)
- Bay leaves 2
- Sichuan peppercorns 0.5g
- Star anise 2
- Dried chili peppers 3 (adjust to taste)
- Cloves 3
- Licorice root 2 slices
- Red fermented bean curd 2 cubes
- Pixian doubanjiang 15g
- Rock sugar 10g
- Huadiao wine 10g
- Baijiu 20g
- Chu hou sauce 50g
- Oyster sauce 20g
- Dark soy sauce 5g
- Light soy sauce 60g
- Char siu sauce 20g

## Steps

1. Prep the supporting ingredients first:

  - Bowl 1: cut the ginger into 1.5cm\*1.5cm pieces, peel half a bulb of garlic, and if you have bird's eye chili, cut it into 1cm segments
    ![Bowl 1](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/碗1.jpeg)
  - Bowl 2: add 2 bay leaves, 0.5g of Sichuan peppercorns, 2 star anise, 3 dried chilies, 3 cloves and 2 slices of licorice root
    ![Bowl 2](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/碗2.jpeg)
  - Bowl 3: add 2 cubes of red fermented bean curd
    ![Bowl 3](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/碗3.jpeg)
  - Bowl 4: add 15g of doubanjiang
    ![Bowl 4](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/碗4.jpeg)
  - Bowl 5: add 10g of rock sugar
    ![Bowl 5](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/碗5.jpeg)
  - Bowl 6: pour in 10g of Huadiao wine and 10g of baijiu
    ![Bowl 6](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/碗6.jpeg)
  - Bowl 7: add 50g of chu hou sauce, 20g of oyster sauce, 20g of char siu sauce, 5g of dark soy sauce and 60g of light soy sauce
    ![Bowl 7](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/碗7.jpeg)

2. No need to cut the beef; put it straight into cold water and blanch over high heat, taking it out when the water boils
  ![Blanching the brisket](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/牛腩焯水.jpeg)
3. Rinse the impurities off the surface of the beef, cut it into large 4cm\*4cm\*4cm chunks, drain and set aside in a bowl
  ![Cutting the brisket](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/牛腩切块.jpeg)
4. Over high heat, heat the wok with oil, add bowl 1 (ginger, garlic, bird's eye chili) and fry until fragrant
5. Over medium-low heat, add bowl 2 (spices) and stir-fry evenly, about 30 seconds
6. Over medium-low heat, add bowl 3 (red fermented bean curd) and mash it with the spatula
7. Over medium-low heat, add bowl 4 (doubanjiang) and stir-fry evenly, about 30 seconds
8. Over medium-low heat, add bowl 5 (rock sugar) and stir-fry until melted
9. Over medium-low heat, add the brisket and stir-fry until the beef is colored
10. Over high heat, drizzle bowl 6 (alcohol) around the edge of the wok and stir-fry quickly until the surface of the beef is slightly browned
11. Pour in bowl 7 (sauces) and stir-fry quickly, watching the liquid at the bottom, until it's bubbling constantly and the sauce has thickened slightly
  ![The brisket is ready to braise](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/牛腩此时可开始炖煮.jpeg)
12. Transfer everything in the wok to a braising pot or pressure cooker and add water to cover
  ![Brisket in the pot](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/牛腩入锅.jpeg)
13. Choose the braising time according to your pot:

  - a. Ordinary braising pot: simmer over low heat for 3 hours
  - b. Pressure cooker: pressure cook for 40 minutes

14. When time is up, open the lid and adjust the seasoning. If it's not salty enough, add salt or light soy sauce (add a little at a time, tasting until it's right); if not sweet enough, add sugar the same way
15. Once seasoned, you can take the brisket out first
16. If you want radish or potatoes, peel and cut them into 2cm thick slices and cook them in the pot for 10 - 15 minutes (or until they reach the texture you like); with a pressure cooker, pressure cook for 5 minutes
  ![Sliced potatoes](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/土豆切片.jpeg)
17. Once cooked, take out the radish and potatoes and put them with the brisket
18. Strain the sauce over the bowl
  ![Straining the sauce](../../../dishes/meat_dish/柱候牛腩/过滤汤汁.jpeg)
