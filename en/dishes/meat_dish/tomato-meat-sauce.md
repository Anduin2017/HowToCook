# How to Make Tomato Meat Sauce

This Italian-style sauce is rich and delicious, with vegetables and beef giving it plenty of texture. It's rich in protein and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. The process is a bit involved and needs some cooking basics, such as frying the beef until crumbly and simmering with controlled heat. It takes about an hour from prep to finish, and you can make extra to freeze and use whenever you need it.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1955 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Ground beef
- Garlic cloves
- Carrot
- Celery
- Onion
- Olive oil
- Sugar
- Salt
- Ground pepper
- Ketchup
- Milk
- Dried basil or thyme (optional)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Ground beef 500g
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Carrot half
- Celery 1 stalk
- Onion half
- Olive oil
- Sugar 2g
- Salt 10g
- Ground black pepper 5g
- Ketchup 300g
- Milk 300ml

## Steps

1. Finely chop the carrot, celery and onion, and slice the garlic.
2. Add 10ml of olive oil, and when hot, add the vegetables and stir-fry over high heat until they start to brown slightly, then remove.
3. Add another 10ml of oil to the pan, add the garlic and stir-fry for 10 seconds, then add the ground beef, sugar, salt, pepper and herbs and fry until the beef is crisp (crumbly).
4. Add the cooked vegetables and ketchup and keep stir-frying until evenly mixed.
5. Slowly pour in the milk in several additions and simmer over medium-low heat for 30 minutes. Done.

## Additional Notes

- **Since ketchup brands vary in salt content, use less salt when frying the beef and taste while simmering before adjusting the seasoning**
- **Stir while the sauce simmers so it doesn't stick. Once the sauce thickens, it's ready!**
- To taste, you can replace the ground beef with half ground pork and half ground beef, and the milk with chicken stock or drinking water.
