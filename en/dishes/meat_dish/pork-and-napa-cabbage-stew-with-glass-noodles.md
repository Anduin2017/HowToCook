# How to Make Pork and Napa Cabbage Stew with Glass Noodles

Pork, napa cabbage and glass noodle stew is a classic home-style stew from northeastern China: the broth is rich and savory, the pork belly tender, the glass noodles smooth and chewy after soaking up the meaty flavor, and the napa cabbage sweet and refreshing. The dish balances meat and vegetables, providing protein, vitamins and carbohydrates, nutritious and filling. It takes some patience, especially preparing the noodles and managing the stewing heat, which is a bit challenging for beginners, but following the steps it's hard to fail. It's on the table in about 45 minutes from start to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1880 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork belly
- Napa cabbage
- Dried potato starch noodles
- Thirteen-spice powder
- Chicken bouillon powder
- Salt
- Dark soy sauce
- Light soy sauce

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Pork belly 300g
- Napa cabbage 500g
- Dried potato starch noodles 50g
- Thirteen-spice powder 10g
- Chicken bouillon powder 5g
- Salt 15g
- Dark soy sauce 5ml
- Light soy sauce 5ml

## Steps

1. Boil water in a pot, add the dried noodles once it boils, cook for 5 minutes, then pour them out with the water into a container, cover, and leave them to keep soaking (do this first; you can do the following steps in the meantime)
2. Slice the pork belly 3mm thick and set aside
3. Separate the tender napa cabbage leaves from the stems and cut each into pieces, keeping them in 2 separate portions. Set aside
4. Heat the wok and add 10ml - 15ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
5. Add the pork belly and keep stir-frying until it changes color
6. Add the dark soy sauce and stir-fry for **1 minute** to color the pork
7. Add the cabbage stems, salt and light soy sauce and stir-fry for a minute (if it sticks, splash in 10ml of water)
8. Add water to cover all the ingredients, add the chicken bouillon and thirteen-spice powder. Once boiling, turn the heat down and **wait 20 minutes**
9. Drain the noodles, cut into short lengths, and set aside in a bowl
10. Add the tender cabbage leaves, stir well, then place the noodles on top of the vegetables, cover and cook for another **5 minutes**
11. Taste, turn off the heat, and reduce the sauce

## Additional Notes

- Dried potato starch noodles take a long time to cook, so do them first and work on the other steps in the meantime
- In the last step, taste it. If it's bland or not cooked yet, cover and keep cooking a while longer
