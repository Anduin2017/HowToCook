# How to Make Hunan Chili Pork Stir-fry

Xiaochao rou (小炒肉) is a classic spicy Hunan home-style dish that goes great with rice: pork belly slices are seared fragrant without being greasy, then stir-fried with fresh chilies, fermented black beans and doubanjiang for a rich savory aroma. It's rich in high-quality protein and fat, and chilies provide plenty of vitamin C, which whets the appetite and warms the body. The difficulty is medium, suitable for beginners with some basics; pay attention to marinating the pork and controlling the heat. It takes about 1 hour from prep to plate, including 30 minutes of marinating and about 15 minutes of stir-frying.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 2873 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork belly
- Facing-heaven chilies (chaotianjiao)
- Bird's eye chili
- Fermented black beans (douchi)
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Dark soy sauce
- Starch
- Salt
- Green onion
- Garlic

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Pork belly 500g
- Facing-heaven chilies 4
- Bird's eye chili 4
- Fermented black beans 10g, ±5g to taste
- Doubanjiang 10g, ±5g to taste
- Dark soy sauce 10ml
- Starch 10g
- Salt 1-2g
- Green onion 0.5-1 stalk
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Cooking oil 15ml

## Steps

1. Slice the pork belly
2. Put the pork in a container, add the starch, dark soy sauce and salt, mix and marinate for half an hour
3. Cut the green onion into segments
4. Cut the bird's eye chilies and facing-heaven chilies on the diagonal
5. Heat the wok and add oil
6. When the oil is hot, add the pork belly and stir-fry. Once it changes color, take it out
7. Add the garlic to the wok and fry until fragrant, add the fermented black beans, and stir-fry evenly
8. Add the doubanjiang and stir-fry evenly
9. Add the fried pork belly back and keep stir-frying evenly
10. Add the bird's eye chilies, facing-heaven chilies and green onion segments and stir-fry for 40 seconds
11. Serve.
