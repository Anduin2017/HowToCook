# How to Make Yuxiang Shredded Pork

Yuxiang shredded pork ("fish-fragrant" pork, yuxiang rousi) is a classic Sichuan dish: sweet, tangy and slightly spicy, with tender pork strips, carrots, green peppers and wood ear mushrooms for varied textures. It's rich in protein and various vitamins, a balanced mix of meat and vegetables. It's somewhat difficult: you need to mix the yuxiang sauce well and master the stir-frying heat, so beginners should follow the steps strictly. It takes about 40 minutes (not including rehydrating the wood ear mushrooms).

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 421 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork tenderloin 200g
* Carrot 100g
* Green pepper 100g
* Wood ear mushrooms (dried) 5g
* Light soy sauce 10ml
* Cooking wine 5ml
* Egg white 1
* Starch 10g
* Vinegar 15ml
* White sugar 10g
* Salt 5g
* Ginger 20g
* Green onion 20g
* Garlic 2 cloves
* Doubanjiang (chili bean paste) 15g

## Calculation

Note: this dish only comes in a version for 1-2 people.

## Steps

### Making the Marinade and Sauce

1. Make the `marinade` by mixing:

  * Light soy sauce 5ml
  * Cooking wine 5ml
  * Starch 5g
  * Water 20ml
  * Egg white 1

2. Make the `sauce` by mixing:

  * Light soy sauce 5ml
  * Vinegar 15ml
  * White sugar 10 grams
  * Salt 1 gram
  * Starch 5g
  * Water 20ml

### Preparing the Ingredients

1. Marinate the pork tenderloin in the `marinade` for 15-30 minutes. Be sure to massage it in evenly.
2. Soak the dried wood ear mushrooms for 4 hours, rinse, and cut into small pieces.
3. Wash the green pepper, remove the stem, and cut into strips.
4. Wash the carrot, cut into strips, and [blanch](../../tips/learn/blanching.md) the carrot strips.
5. Mince the ginger and garlic.
6. Cut the green onion into 5mm pieces.

### Cooking

1. Heat the wok and add 15ml of oil.
2. Add the marinated pork and quickly separate it until it turns white, then take it out.
3. Heat the wok and add 5ml of oil.
4. Add all the `green onion`, `ginger`, `garlic` and `doubanjiang`.
5. Add all the `carrot` and stir-fry for 20s, then add the green pepper and wood ear mushrooms and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
6. Add the `cooked pork` and toss quickly and evenly. Be careful not to stir-fry for more than 20 seconds.
7. Pour in the `sauce` and toss quickly and evenly. Be careful not to stir-fry for more than 15 seconds.
8. Turn off the heat and plate.
