# How to Make Scallion-Braised Chicken Thighs

Scallion-braised chicken thighs is a home-style dish that goes great with rice and needs just two core ingredients: chicken thighs and scallion. The chicken is browned outside and tender inside with a strong scallion aroma. It's simple, all done in one pan, and beginners can get it right the first time. It's high in protein and especially good with rice, perfect for a quick weeknight dinner.

Estimated difficulty: ★★
Estimated calories: 850 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken legs 2 (about 500g)
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type) 1 stalk
- Green onions 3-4 stalks
- Light soy sauce (Haitian Golden Label recommended)
- Dark soy sauce (for color, optional)
- Oyster sauce (optional)
- Granulated sugar
- Cooking wine or Huadiao wine
- Salt
- Ground white pepper
- Cooking oil
- Ginger 3-4 slices (optional, mainly to remove gaminess and add aroma)
- Sichuan peppercorns 10 (about 1g, optional, adds a numbing aroma)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Chicken legs 2 (about 350g of meat after deboning)
- Scallion 1 stalk (about 100g)
- Green onions 3-4 stalks (about 30g)
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Dark soy sauce 5ml (for color; if you don't have any, use all light soy sauce)
- Oyster sauce 10ml (optional; if you don't have any, add 5ml more light soy sauce)
- Granulated sugar 5g
- Cooking wine 10ml
- Salt 2-3g
- Ground white pepper 1g
- Cooking oil 15-20ml

## Steps

1. Debone the chicken legs: insert scissors from the thick end of the leg and cut the meat open along the bone, snip twice more along the bone to separate the meat, and finally pull out the whole bone. This is safer than deboning with a knife.
2. Cut the deboned chicken into 2-3cm cubes.
3. Cut the scallion into thick slices (about 0.5cm) and the green onions into 1cm segments.
4. Add 10ml of cooking wine, 5ml of light soy sauce, 2g of salt and 1g of ground white pepper to the chicken, mix by hand, and marinate for 10 minutes.
5. In a small bowl, mix the sauce: 10ml of light soy sauce + 5ml of dark soy sauce + 10ml of oyster sauce + 5g of sugar. Stir well and set aside.
6. Heat the wok and add 15-20ml of cooking oil.
7. Add the ginger slices and Sichuan peppercorns and fry over low heat until the ginger is slightly browned and the peppercorns darken.
8. Scoop out the ginger and peppercorns with a slotted spoon and discard them, keeping the oil.
9. Over medium-high heat, spread the marinated chicken in a single layer in the wok and don't rush to move it.
10. Wait 2-3 minutes for the bottom of the chicken to form a browned crust, then stir-fry until all sides have changed color and the chicken is fully cooked.
11. Add the scallion slices and stir-fry over medium-high heat for 1 minute to release the scallion aroma.
12. Pour in the prepared sauce, toss evenly, and cover.
13. Wait 2 minutes for the sauce and scallion aroma to fully infuse the chicken.
14. Remove the lid, add the green onion segments and 2g of toasted white sesame seeds (optional), toss for 5-10 seconds, turn off the heat and serve.

## Additional Notes

### Deboning Chicken Legs (Scissors Method)

1. Rinse the chicken legs and pat dry with paper towels.
2. Find the end of the bone at the thicker end of the leg.
3. With the tip of the scissors against the bone, cut through the outer skin and meat, cutting along one side of the bone all the way down.
4. Turn the leg over and continue cutting along the other side of the bone.
5. Use your fingers to separate where the meat connects to the bone.
6. Snip through the joint and pull out the whole bone.

The whole process doesn't need a knife; it's much safer than the traditional "slice open and debone" method, and beginners can get it right the first time.

### About "Only Scallion and Chicken"

The core of this dish is scallion and chicken, but those two alone lack depth. The ginger, Sichuan peppercorns, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce and white sesame seeds used in the video all make the scallion and chicken flavors more rounded.

- **Minimal version**: scallion + chicken + salt + light soy sauce + sugar works, but the scallion aroma will be rather one-note.
- **Recommended version**: add ginger, Sichuan peppercorns and oyster sauce for a much richer aroma.
- **Perfect version**: add a little dark soy sauce for color + toasted white sesame seeds for garnish, taking both looks and taste up a notch.

### Storage and Reheating

1. Refrigerated: seal and keep in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
2. Frozen: let cool, portion and freeze for up to 1 month.
3. Reheating: microwave on medium for 2 minutes, or stir-fry in a wok with a splash of water.
