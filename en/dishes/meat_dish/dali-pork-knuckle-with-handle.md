# How to Make Dali Pork Knuckle with Handle

Pork knuckle "with handle" (daiba zhouzi) is a famous traditional dish from Dali in Shaanxi, named because the trotter looks like a handle. The finished skin is gelatinous, sticky and soft, and the meat is tender and falling apart without being greasy. It's eaten dipped in sweet bean sauce with scallion segments, savory and rich. It's rich in collagen and high-quality protein, nourishing and good for the skin. It requires deboning, shaping and a long steam, which is challenging for beginners; the whole process takes about 3.5 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 1037 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

### Ingredients

- Front pork knuckle with trotter attached
- Red fermented bean curd
- Sweet bean sauce
- Fine salt
- Red (dark) soy sauce
- White (light) soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Garlic slices
- Minced ginger
- Star anise
- Cassia bark
- Scallion

### Tools

- Small hatchet (for breaking the bone)
- Sterile cheesecloth (big enough to cover the knuckle)

## Calculation

Per portion: for three people

- Front pork knuckle with trotter: one (about 1250 grams / 2.5 jin)
- Red fermented bean curd: 1 cube = 10 grams
- Sweet bean sauce: 150 grams
- Fine salt: 15 grams
- Red soy sauce: 35 grams
- White soy sauce: 25 grams
- Cooking wine: 25 grams
- Garlic slices: 50 grams
- Minced ginger: 10 grams
- Star anise: 3
- Cassia bark: 5 grams
- Scallion: 200 grams

## Steps

1. Scrape and wash the knuckle clean, and place it on the board with the knuckle end facing out, the "handle" (trotter) facing in, and the skin side down.
2. With a knife, cut the skin open down the middle along the leg bone from the knuckle end to the trotter, and cut the meat away from both sides of the leg bone (freeing it on three sides) while leaving the bottom of the bone attached to the meat, so the bone is exposed. Then break both leg bones in the middle with the back of the knife (better use the hatchet).
3. Boil the knuckle in a pot until about 70% cooked (normal on the outside, pinkish inside), then take it out, wipe it dry with a clean cloth, and while hot, brush the skin with red soy sauce.
4. Take a steaming pot and put the star anise and cassia bark on the bottom. First snap the joint of the trotter by hand without damaging the skin, then put the knuckle into the pot skin side down. Depending on the shape of the knuckle, curl the trotter along the side of the pot so it forms a round shape.
5. Sprinkle with the salt, cover the meat with the sterile cheesecloth, then spread the sweet bean sauce (50 grams), scallion (75 grams), red fermented bean curd, red soy sauce, white soy sauce, ginger, garlic and so on over the cheesecloth. Steam over high heat for about three hours (until very tender).
6. When done, take it out, remove the cheesecloth, turn it out onto a plate, and pick out the star anise. Serve with scallion segments and a small dish of sweet bean sauce on the side (or spread the sweet bean sauce over the knuckle and serve with a small dish of scallion segments).

## Additional Notes

- Judge "70% cooked" by the actual situation; if possible, ask an experienced elder in your family.
- Put water in the bottom half of the steamer and the knuckle in the top half, and add more water as needed during steaming so it doesn't boil dry.
