# How to Make Red-Braised Pig Trotters

![Red-braised pig trotters](../../../dishes/meat_dish/红烧猪蹄/红烧猪蹄.jpg)

Red-braised pig trotters are a classic home-style dish with a rich sauce and soft, sticky meat, savory and sweet, and excellent with rice. Pig trotters are rich in collagen and protein. This dish is a bit challenging for beginners, but a pressure cooker simplifies the process, and it's easier with some cooking experience. It takes about 1 hour from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 639 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pig trotters
- Bay leaves
- Ginger
- Green onion
- Dark soy sauce
- Cassia bark
- Rock sugar
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- Salt
- Star anise
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

One portion is just enough for 3-4 people.

- Pig trotters: 2~3
- Cooking oil: 30ml
- Bay leaves 2
- Ginger 5 slices
- Green onion half a stalk
- Cassia bark 1 piece
- Rock sugar 7-8 pieces
- Cooking wine 30 ml
- Light soy sauce 20 ml
- Dark soy sauce 20 ml
- Salt 8 grams
- Star anise 4

## Steps

### Cooking

1. Put the trotters (chopped by a friendly vendor) in a pot of cold water, add 20 ml of cooking wine plus green onion and ginger, and boil for 15 minutes to remove the blood and gaminess
2. Heat the wok, add cold oil—30ml of cooking oil—add 7-8 pieces of rock sugar, turn on low heat and cook into caramel, pressing the rock sugar with the spatula to break it up, about 2 minutes
3. Once caramelized, add the blanched trotters and stir-fry over low heat until all the trotters are lightly golden on both sides
4. Add 2 bay leaves, 1 piece of cassia bark, 4 star anise, 20 ml of light soy sauce, 20 ml of dark soy sauce, 10 ml of cooking wine, 3 slices of ginger and 8 grams of salt, turn to medium heat and keep stir-frying for 1 minute
5. Add boiling or cold water to cover the trotters, cover, bring to a boil over high heat, then turn off the heat
6. Transfer everything to a pressure cooker and cook for 15 minutes (if you don't have a pressure cooker, just braise in the pot, bringing it to a boil over high heat and then simmering over low heat)
7. After 15 minutes, pour everything from the pressure cooker back into the wok and reduce the sauce over high heat. Taste it with chopsticks now; if it's bland, add 2~3g of salt
8. The reducing time depends on how much liquid is in the wok, usually 30 seconds is enough. Leaving a bit more is fine—red-braised trotter sauce is great with rice too
