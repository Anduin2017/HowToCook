# How to Make Coca-Cola Chicken Wings

Coca-Cola chicken wings are glossy red, tender and smooth, with a balanced sweet and savory flavor—a creative dish popular on family tables. Chicken wings are rich in high-quality protein and fat, and cooking them in cola makes the meat even more tender. You need to master the heat for pan-frying and reducing the sauce, which is a bit challenging for beginners, but following the steps will get you there. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 960 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Chicken wing midjoints
* Cola
* White sugar
* Light soy sauce
* Salt
* Ginger
* Cooking wine or beer
* Green onion

## Calculation

For 1 plate:

* Chicken wings 10 ～ 12
* Cola 500ml
* White sugar 10 grams
* Light soy sauce 15 grams
* Dark soy sauce 3 grams
* Salt 2 grams
* Ginger 2 slices
* Cooking wine 20 ml
* Green onion tied in a knot

## Steps

1. Put the wings in a pot and cover with cold water. Add 1 slice of ginger and 10 ～ 20 ml of cooking wine. Bring to a boil over high heat (about 2 minutes), skim off the foam, and drain

  * This step is for chilled wings; freshly bought wings can be scored and marinated in light soy sauce directly.

2. Take out the wings and make two cuts on each side with a knife. Marinate the wings in about 10 grams of light soy sauce for 10 minutes (just enough to fully coat the surface of the wings)
3. Heat oil in the wok again over low heat, fry the remaining ginger slice until fragrant, then add the marinated wings. Fry until golden, then flip (until both sides are golden). Toss the wings with a spatula and stir-fry them with the ginger 4～5 times (to keep the wings and ginger from sticking).
4. When the wings are golden, pour in cola to cover them, bring the cola to a boil over high heat, then skim off the floating black foam (which contains blood). Now add the green onion knot.
5. Seasoning: add 2 grams of salt, 10 grams of white sugar and 3 grams of light soy sauce (you can use a little dark soy sauce for color, 3 grams).
6. When the green onion knot turns yellow, remove it along with the ginger slices, and keep simmering the wings over medium heat.
7. When the cola has reduced to a syrup that forms threads, turn to low heat so the sauce clings firmly to the wings. Serve and plate.

## Additional Notes

* Frying the ginger until fragrant also helps keep the wings from sticking to the wok.
* When reducing the sauce at the end, don't use too high a heat, or it may taste bitter.
* This dish is on the sweet side.
