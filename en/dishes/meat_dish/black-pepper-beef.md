# How to Make Black Pepper Beef

![Finished black pepper beef](../../../dishes/meat_dish/黑椒牛柳/黑椒牛柳.jpg)

Black pepper beef is a hugely popular home-style stir-fry with a strong black pepper flavor and tender beef strips, paired with sweet, crisp onion and bell peppers—a balance of meat and vegetables that suits all ages. Beef is rich in high-quality protein and iron, and bell peppers and onion add various vitamins. This dish is very beginner-friendly and is usually done within an hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 216 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Beef (beef tenderloin or steak)
- Onion
- Bell pepper (red/yellow)
- Starch
- Black pepper (ground)
- Black pepper marinade
- Salt
- Peanut oil

Note: the ground pepper and the marinade can substitute for each other; for exact amounts, follow the instructions on the package you bought.

A non-stick pan is recommended.

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2-3 people.

Total:

- Beef = portions * 100 grams (adjust to the diners' appetites and your pan size)
- Onion = portions * 1/12 (i.e. about 1/4 for 3 people)
- Bell pepper = portions * 1/12 (i.e. about 1/4 for 3 people)
- Salt = portions * 1 gram
- Starch = portions * 3 grams
- Black pepper marinade = marinate in proportion according to the product instructions
- Ground black pepper = portions * 1 gram (in practice, just sprinkle)
- Peanut oil = portions * 10ml (in practice the amount of oil varies with the amount of food; if you change the amount of beef, adjust proportionally)

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. Cut the beef into strips, preferably no longer than 8 cm, about 5-10 mm thick and about 1 cm wide (not strict)
2. Marinate the beef with the marinade, mix evenly and let it rest. Follow the product instructions for amount and time; you can marinate longer but not shorter.
3. If using a liquid marinade, sprinkle a layer of ground black pepper three minutes before the end of marinating, then add the starch, mix evenly again, and let rest for 20 minutes.
4. Turn on the heat, heat the wok, and add the peanut oil.
5. When you see a wisp of smoke from the oil, add the beef and stir-fry.
6. On medium-high heat, stir-fry for 2 minutes until the outside of the beef changes color (i.e. no obvious blood color; any parts with blood color haven't been stir-fried enough) (watch out for spattering oil here).
7. Add the onion and bell pepper and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
8. Add the salt, sprinkle on another portion of ground black pepper, and stir-fry for 30 seconds until evenly mixed.
9. When the onion has softened, turn off the heat and plate.
