# How to Make Rice Flour Steamed Pork

Rice flour steamed pork (fenzheng rou) is a classic Chinese steamed dish with a rich aroma and a soft, tender texture, a widely loved home-style flavor. Pork belly provides high-quality protein and fat, and with potatoes or other root vegetables it's nutritionally balanced. This dish suits people with some cooking experience and takes about 90 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1606 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork belly 500g (with alternating fat and lean)
- Steamed meat rice powder 100g (Lee Kum Kee recommended, or homemade)
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Dark soy sauce 10ml
- Cooking wine 15ml
- Pixian doubanjiang 10g (optional)
- Minced ginger 10g
- Minced garlic 10g
- Granulated sugar 5g
- Potatoes 300g (or pumpkin 300g, as the base layer)
- Water (for the steamer) 2000ml

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 3 people.

Per portion:

- Pork belly 250g
- Steamed meat rice powder 50g
- Light soy sauce 7.5ml
- Dark soy sauce 5ml
- Cooking wine 7.5ml
- Pixian doubanjiang 5g (optional)
- Minced ginger 5g
- Minced garlic 5g
- Granulated sugar 2.5g
- Potatoes or pumpkin 150g
- Water for the steamer 1000ml

## Steps

1. Rinse the pork belly and cut into slices about 5cm long, 3cm wide and 0.5cm thick
2. Mince the ginger and garlic finely, with pieces no bigger than 1mm
3. In a large bowl, add the sliced pork belly, 15ml of light soy sauce, 10ml of dark soy sauce, 15ml of cooking wine, 10g of Pixian doubanjiang, 10g of minced ginger, 10g of minced garlic and 5g of sugar
4. Mix evenly with chopsticks, cover with plastic wrap, and marinate at room temperature (20°C - 25°C) for 30 minutes
5. After marinating, add 100g of steamed meat rice powder and keep tossing for 2 minutes, making sure every slice is evenly coated
6. Peel the potatoes and cut into slices 0.8cm thick and about 5cm x 5cm in area, 300g in total
7. Line the bottom of a 20cm deep bowl with the potato slices, overlapping as little as possible
8. Spread the coated pork belly evenly over the potato slices and press down
9. Add 2000ml of water to the steamer and heat until the water is bubbling continuously (100°C)
10. Put the bowl of ingredients in the steamer and cover
11. Steam over medium heat for 60 minutes (keep the heat strong enough to maintain a steady boil, about 600W)
12. When time is up, poke a chopstick into the center of the pork; if it goes through easily with no noticeable resistance, it's cooked
13. If not, keep steaming for 10 - 15 minutes until the pork is very tender and the fat has seeped out
14. Take it out, plate, and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- To make your own rice powder, toast 150g of rice in a pan over medium-low heat until lightly yellow (about 8 minutes), add 1g of dried chilies, 1g of Sichuan peppercorns and 0.5g of star anise, toast until fragrant, grind in a blender, and sift
- If the water level drops below 1cm while steaming, immediately add 500ml of hot water (above 90°C) to keep the steam going
