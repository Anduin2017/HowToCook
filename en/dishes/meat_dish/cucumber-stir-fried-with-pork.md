# How to Make Cucumber Stir-fried with Pork

This is a refreshing, crisp, quick home-style dish, savory and well-balanced, with garlic aroma and a slight heat. It's rich in protein and various vitamins, which help cool you down and hydrate. It's fairly beginner-friendly; just watch the heat and you'll succeed. It takes about 25 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 550 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Cucumber
* Lean pork
* Cooking oil
* Light soy sauce
* Salt
* Garlic
* Bird's eye chili

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

* Cucumber = 100 grams * portions
* Pork = 50 grams * portions
* Oil = 50 grams * portions
* Salt = 10 grams * portions
* Soy sauce = 5 grams * portions
* Garlic = 2 cloves * portions
* Bird's eye chili = 1 * portions

## Steps

1. Slice the lean pork, put it in a bowl, add 10 grams of cooking oil and the light soy sauce, mix well, and marinate for 10 minutes
2. Cut 5 cm off both ends of the cucumber, and slice the rest diagonally into 0.5 cm thin slices
3. Put the cucumber in a bowl, sprinkle with 8 grams of salt, mix well, and let it sit for 5 minutes
4. Peel the garlic, crush it, and mince. Set aside
5. Remove the stems from the bird's eye chilies and cut them into even 0.5 cm pieces
6. Heat the wok, add 40 grams of oil, and when the oil is smoking, add the minced garlic and chilies and toss 5 times
7. Add the marinated pork and stir-fry until cooked and changed color
8. Add the cucumber and 2 grams of salt, toss evenly over high heat for half a minute, and serve

  * If you can't get a strong enough flame, it may be a little underdone. Consider increasing to 50 seconds.
