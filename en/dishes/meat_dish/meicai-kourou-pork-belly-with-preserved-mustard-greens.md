# How to Make Meicai Kourou (Pork Belly with Preserved Mustard Greens)

This is a famous traditional Hakka dish: the finished dish is glossy and soy-red with a thick sauce, and the pork belly is rich without being greasy, soft and mellow. Pork belly is rich in high-quality protein and fatty acids, and the preserved mustard greens (meigancai) provide dietary fiber. There are quite a few steps, and you need to master boiling, frying and steaming, which is a challenge for beginners. From prep to plate, including the time to soak the mustard greens, it takes about 2.5 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 3756 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork belly
* Preserved mustard greens (meicai)
* Five-spice powder
* Cooking oil
* Granulated sugar
* Dark soy sauce
* Light soy sauce
* Bird's eye chili
* Minced garlic
* Salt
* Chicken bouillon powder

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Pork belly 200 g
* Preserved mustard greens 30 g
* Five-spice powder 2 g
* Cooking oil 300 ml
* Granulated sugar 5 g
* Dark soy sauce 30 ml
* Light soy sauce 20 ml
* Bird's eye chili 1
* Minced garlic 10 g
* Salt 2 g
* Chicken bouillon powder 2 g

## Steps

1. Soak the preserved mustard greens in water for 1 hour
2. Pour 50 ml of cooking oil into the wok, put the whole piece of pork belly in skin side down for 1 minute, then take it out and scrape the skin [optional]
3. Add boiling water to the pot, add the pork belly, boil over high heat for 20 minutes (until a chopstick can pierce it), then take it out
4. Rub the surface of the pork belly evenly with dark soy sauce, five-spice powder and sugar, and let it sit for 15 minutes
5. Heat oil in the wok, add the pork belly, and fry over medium heat until golden on both sides (3-5 minutes)
6. Heat oil in the wok, add the mustard greens, bird's eye chili, minced garlic, chicken bouillon and salt, and stir-fry until the greens are dry
7. Slice the pork belly (0.5-1 cm thick), arrange in a large bowl, and spread the mustard greens on top
8. Steam over medium heat for 45 minutes
9. Place a plate upside down over the bowl and flip the pork belly out onto the plate

## Additional Notes

* Discovered while making this: crispy-skin pork belly is amazing Orz~
* If you don't like it sweet, leave out the sugar; it doesn't affect the main process
* The second-to-last step's time can be adjusted to your preference
* Stir-frying the mustard greens dry is so they absorb fat later (my guess)
