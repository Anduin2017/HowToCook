# How to Make Braised Noodles with Lamb Ribs

![Braised noodles with lamb ribs](../../../dishes/meat_dish/羊排焖面/羊排焖面.jpg)
Braised noodles with lamb ribs is a hearty northern Chinese home-style dish with a rich meaty aroma and chewy noodles: the lamb ribs are tender and the wide noodles soak up the sauce, making every bite satisfying. With meat, noodles and vegetables, one meal covers the protein, carbohydrates and fat your body needs. You need to braise the lamb ribs first and then make and braise the noodles, so there are a few more steps, better suited to people with some cooking experience. It takes about an hour and a half in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1616 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Skin-on lamb ribs
- Green peppers
- Sweet peppers
- Green peppers
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
- Sichuan peppercorns
- Dried chili peppers
- Ginger
- Granulated sugar
- Dark soy sauce

## Calculation

1 serving * 2 meals = 2 servings

- Skin-on lamb ribs 500g
- Green peppers and sweet peppers, 2 each

## Steps

1. Put the lamb in cold water and blanch it. When the water boils, skim off the blood foam and take out the lamb.
2. Slice the ginger (4 slices) and put it in a bowl with the dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns.
3. Add oil to the wok (a bit more is fine).
4. When the oil is hot, add the sugar and stir-fry the lamb until caramelized.
5. Once the lamb's moisture has been fried off, add salt, dark soy sauce and the prepared seasonings.
6. Add water to cover the lamb, bring to a boil over high heat, keep boiling for 10 minutes, then braise over low heat for 30 minutes.
7. Meanwhile, you can make the dough. The amounts and method are explained in the Additional Notes *(Note 1).
8. Add the green peppers, sweet peppers, scallion and dough sheets and stir-fry.
9. Once evenly mixed, serve.

*Note 1: you can use store-bought noodles instead, but try to choose wide noodles.

## Additional Notes

(Dough mini-guide: ingredients)

- All-purpose flour 300 g
- Salt 3 g
- Water 180 ml

(Dough mini-guide: method)

- Mix the dry ingredients evenly, add water while mixing and stirring, then knead the dough until smooth
- Let the dough rest (10 minutes)
- Knead again until smooth
- Let it rest (5 minutes)
- Roll the dough into a log and cut it into 6 to 8 small pieces
- Brush with oil and wait 5 minutes
- Roll each piece out into a sheet
- Add to the wok and braise for 4 minutes (one sheet at a time)
