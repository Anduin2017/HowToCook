# How to Make Garlic Scapes Stir-fried with Pork

Garlic scapes stir-fried with pork is a northern Chinese home-style dish: savory, crisp and tender, with a strong garlic aroma. Garlic scapes are rich in dietary fiber and vitamins, and pork belly provides protein for a balanced combination of meat and vegetables. It's simple with easy prep, so beginners can pick it up quickly. It takes only about 10 minutes from cutting to plating.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 258 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Thin pork belly slices
- Garlic scapes
- Salt
- Light soy sauce
- Cooking oil
- Garlic cloves

## Calculation

Version for 1 person

- Garlic scapes 1 bunch (each bunch about 190g)
- Thin pork belly slices 4 (about 20g)
- Cooking oil 10ml
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Salt 2g

## Steps

1. Cut the garlic scapes into 5cm pieces and set aside
2. Cut the pork belly into 5mm * 5cm strips and set aside
3. Smash and mince the garlic cloves and set aside
4. Heat the wok and add 10ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
5. Add the minced garlic and stir-fry over medium heat for **10 seconds** until fragrant
6. Add the pork belly and 5ml of light soy sauce and stir-fry over medium heat for **30 seconds** until the pork is cooked and colored
7. Add the garlic scapes and 10ml of light soy sauce and stir-fry for **30 seconds**
8. Add 20g of water and stir-fry over medium heat for **5 minutes** until the garlic scapes soften slightly
9. Finally add 2g of salt, stir-fry over medium heat for **30 seconds**, and plate

## Additional Notes

- Taste before adding the salt and decide for yourself whether to add more or less
- Thin pork belly slices are used because they're easy to cut and absorb flavor without marinating
