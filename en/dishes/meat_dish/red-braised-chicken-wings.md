# How to Make Red-Braised Chicken Wings

Red-braised chicken wings are a home-style dish that goes great with rice: savory with a touch of sweetness, glossy red, with tender meat that slides off the bone. Chicken wings are rich in high-quality protein and collagen that are easily absorbed. The difficulty is moderate; beginners can master it by following the steps. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1016 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh chicken wings (midjoints recommended; the meat is more tender)
- Ginger slices (fresh ginger recommended for better removal of gaminess)
- Scallion segments (the white part of large scallions recommended for a stronger aroma)
- Garlic
- Bay leaves
- Star anise
- Dried chili peppers (optional; leave out if you don't eat spicy food)
- Rock sugar
- Shaoxing wine or cooking wine (either one; Shaoxing wine has a mellower flavor)
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Salt
- White sugar
- Ground pepper (ground white pepper recommended; it's less pungent)
- White sesame seeds (toasted)
- Chopped green onion (for garnish)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make; one portion is enough for 3-4 people.

Per portion:

- Fresh chicken wings 500g (about 8-10; each wing about 50-60g)
- Ginger 4-5 slices (about 10g, sliced about 2mm thick)
- Scallion 2-3 segments (about 15g, each about 3cm long)
- Garlic 3-4 cloves (about 15g)
- Bay leaf 1
- Star anise 1
- Dried chili peppers 2 (optional, about 5g; leave out if you don't eat spicy food)
- Rock sugar 15g (granulated rock sugar)
- Shaoxing wine or cooking wine 35ml
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Dark soy sauce 5ml
- Salt 3g
- White sugar 2g
- Ground pepper 1g
- White sesame seeds 3g
- Chopped green onion 5g
- Cooking oil 10-15ml

## Steps

1. Rinse the wings. Make three cuts in the thick part of each wing and one cut on the back, so the flavor soaks in.
2. If the wings aren't fresh, soak them in ginger water for 10-20 minutes after scoring.
3. Prepare 10g of ginger slices (3-4 slices) and 15g of scallion segments (2-3 segments); smash 3-4 garlic cloves (about 15g) with a knife (no need to mince); and set out 1 bay leaf, 1 star anise and 2 dried chilies (optional).
4. Put the prepared wings in a pot with cold water to cover, add 20ml of Shaoxing wine or cooking wine and 2 slices of ginger (about 5g), and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, keep on high heat for another 2-3 minutes and skim off the foam.
5. Take out the wings, rinse them twice with warm water to thoroughly remove the blood foam, drain and set aside.
6. In another wok, turn on the heat and add 10-15ml of cooking oil.
7. When the oil reaches 5/10 heat, add 15g of rock sugar and cook over high heat until it has completely melted. Once it melts and changes color, turn to low heat and keep stirring until the rock sugar is jujube red, then immediately add the drained wings and stir-fry quickly for 1-2 minutes so every wing is evenly coated in caramel.
8. Add the prepared bay leaf, star anise, dried chilies (optional), the remaining 2 slices of ginger (about 5g), 15g of scallion segments and the smashed garlic, and keep stir-frying for 30 seconds until the spices are fully fragrant.
9. Drizzle 15ml of cooking wine or Shaoxing wine and 15ml of light soy sauce around the edge of the wok and stir-fry quickly for 10 seconds.
10. Add warm water (about 50-60℃), just enough to slightly cover all the wings.
11. Add 5ml of dark soy sauce, 3g of salt, 2g of white sugar and 1g of ground pepper, and stir gently with a ladle.
12. Cover and cook over medium heat for 7-8 minutes.
13. Remove the lid and pick out the bay leaf, star anise, ginger slices, scallion segments and garlic with chopsticks and discard them.
14. Turn to high heat and keep stir-frying until the sauce is thick (about 1-2 minutes).
15. Turn off the heat, transfer the wings to a plate, and pour the remaining thick sauce from the wok over them.
16. Finally sprinkle with 3g of white sesame seeds and 5g of chopped green onion. Done.

## Additional Notes

- If it smells gamy, double the amount of spices.
