# How to Make Fermented Bean Curd Pork

This is a classic Jiangnan home-style dish: pork belly braised with red fermented bean curd, glossy red like amber, with meat so tender it melts in your mouth, and layers of salty-sweet wine and bean aromas unfolding on your tongue. It's rich in high-quality protein and B vitamins and is easier to digest after slow braising. The process requires patience with the heat and reducing the sauce, which is a bit challenging for beginners. It takes about 1 hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 1190 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Skin-on pork belly
- Red fermented bean curd (Xianheng brand rose fermented bean curd recommended)
- Rock sugar
- Dark soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Green onion (optional)
- Ginger
- Water
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for about 2 people.

Per portion:

- Skin-on pork belly 400 g
- Red fermented bean curd 30 g (about 2 cubes)
- Fermented bean curd brine 15 ml
- Rock sugar 25 g
- Dark soy sauce 5 ml
- Cooking wine 15 ml
- Green onion whites 15 g
- Chopped green onion 5 g
- Ginger 10 g
- Water 500 ml
- Cooking oil 10 ml

## Steps

1. Cut the pork belly into 2.5 cm cubes
2. Put cold water with cooking wine and ginger slices in a pot, add the pork, turn on the heat and slowly bring to a boil, then take the pork out
3. Mash the rose fermented bean curd with its brine into a paste, and add the rock sugar and dark soy sauce to make a sauce
4. Heat the wok, add cold oil, fry the green onion and ginger until fragrant, add the pork and fry until golden
5. Pour in the fermented bean curd sauce, stir well, add hot water, and braise
6. Simmer slowly over low heat for 40 minutes
7. Reduce the sauce over high heat, and turn off the heat when the sauce forms thick "fish-eye" bubbles

## Additional Notes

- Single-crystal rock sugar is recommended
- Fermented bean curd is salty on its own, so no extra salt is needed
- When reducing over high heat, keep shaking the wok to prevent sticking
