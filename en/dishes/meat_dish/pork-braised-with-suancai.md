# How to Make Pork Braised with Suancai

Pork braised with suancai is a northern Chinese home-style stew: tangy and rich, with tender pork belly and flavorful ribs. It mainly provides protein, and suancai (fermented napa cabbage) also brings beneficial lactic acid bacteria. The steps are clear and beginners can follow them in order, but it needs patient slow braising, taking about 3 hours from start to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 1817 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork belly or pork ribs
* Northeastern suancai (fermented napa cabbage)
* Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
* Ginger
* Garlic
* Salt
* Light soy sauce
* Five-spice powder
* Cooking wine
* Star anise

## Calculation

For 3-5 people:

* Pork ribs or pork belly (total) 1500 grams
* Northeastern suancai 1000 grams
* Scallion 1 stalk
* Ginger 100 grams
* Garlic 4 cloves
* Salt 10 grams
* Light soy sauce 15 grams
* Five-spice powder 10 grams
* Cooking wine 20 ml
* Star anise 2

## Steps

1. Cut the scallion into segments; cut 50 grams of ginger into pieces and mince 50 grams; mince the garlic. Set aside.
2. Shred all the suancai and rinse it 2 ～ 3 times with water. Set aside.
   - The number of rinses depends on your taste: if you like it sour, rinse 2 times; if you don't like sourness, rinse 3 ～ 4 times. Don't skip rinsing, or it will be too sour.
3. Put the ribs and pork belly in a pot and add cold water to cover. Add all the scallion segments, 50 grams of ginger pieces and 20 ml of cooking wine. Bring to a boil over high heat, then wait 5 minutes. Turn off the heat, take out the ribs and pork belly, and rinse off the foam with cold water. Set aside
4. Slice or cube the boiled pork belly and set aside.
5. Wash the pot and dry it (otherwise the oil will spatter when added).
6. Add oil to the pot, turn on medium heat, add the minced ginger and garlic and fry until fragrant, then add the pork belly and ribs. Fry the pork belly and ribs until golden, add 10 grams of five-spice powder and 15 grams of light soy sauce, and toss with a spatula for 1 ～ 2 minutes.
7. Add the rinsed suancai to the pot and stir-fry for 3 minutes.
8. Pour in purified water to just cover the ingredients, add 2 star anise, turn to high heat until the water boils. Turn to medium heat, cover and braise.
9. Wait 1.5 ～ 2 hours, until the pork belly is very tender (a chopstick pierces it easily)
10. Remove the lid, turn to high heat to reduce the liquid, tossing the ingredients until the remaining liquid only covers the bottom of the pot, then turn to low heat and get ready to season.
11. Seasoning: add 10 grams of salt and stir well.
12. Turn off the heat and serve.

## Additional Notes

- When reducing on high heat, be careful not to burn the bottom; toss occasionally to check the liquid level.
- Seasoning tip: when adding salt at the end, add a little at a time, stir and taste, until it's to your liking
- This dish has the distinctive flavor of suancai
