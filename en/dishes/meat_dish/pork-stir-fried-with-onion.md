# How to Make Pork Stir-fried with Onion

This is a home-style stir-fry, savory with a touch of sweetness and a rich soy aroma. The onion becomes mild once softened, and paired with tender pork slices it goes perfectly with rice. Pork provides high-quality protein and B vitamins, and onion contains dietary fiber and various antioxidants that whet the appetite and aid digestion. It's simple; beginners can make it easily, and it takes only about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 979 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Onion
- Sliced pork
- Ketchup
- Sesame oil

## Calculation

Per portion:

Ingredients

- Onion 1 (it's the star; if you love onion, add another half to one)
- Pork (250g)
- Garlic (3 cloves)

Seasonings

- Cooking oil (15ml)
- Black pepper (1.25g)
- Soy sauce (30ml)
- Sugar (15g)
- Sesame oil (5ml)
- Ketchup (15ml)
- Cooking wine (15ml)

Note: consider buying [teaspoons and tablespoons](https://www.sohu.com/a/127293243_570657) to measure ingredients precisely.

## Steps

1. Slice the onion, prepare the pork, smash the garlic, and mix the seasonings above. Set aside
2. Pour 1 tablespoon of cooking oil into the wok (wait 10 seconds for it to heat up), and add the pork
3. Once it changes color, add the garlic and fry until fragrant, then take it out and set aside
4. In the same wok, stir-fry the onion for 3~4 minutes, then add the seasonings and stir well
5. Add the pork you set aside and stir-fry until cooked through
6. Once the pork is cooked, stir-fry for another 1 or 2 minutes and serve

## Additional Notes

- Use sliced pork shoulder or pre-cut pork strips, as you prefer
