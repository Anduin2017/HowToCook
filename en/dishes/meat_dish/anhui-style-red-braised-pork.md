# How to Make Anhui-Style Red-Braised Pork

Anhui-style red-braised pork is a classic Huizhou home-style dish, glossy red and rich without being greasy, soft yet bouncy, with savory and sweet flavors blended together. Made mainly from pork belly, it's rich in high-quality protein and fat and provides plenty of energy. Cooking it requires mastering the caramel and the heat, which is a little challenging. It takes about 45 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 3847 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork belly
* Granulated sugar
* Cooking oil
* Light soy sauce
* Oyster sauce
* Dark soy sauce
* Ginger slices
* Garlic
* Cooking wine
* Green onion
* Five-spice powder
* Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Pork belly 300 g
* Granulated sugar 100 g
* Cooking oil 200 g
* Light soy sauce 10 ml
* Oyster sauce 5 ml
* Dark soy sauce 5 ml
* Ginger 2 slices
* Garlic 3 cloves
* Cooking wine 100 ml
* Green onion 1 stalk
* Five-spice powder 10 g
* Salt 10 g

## Steps

1. Cut the pork belly into 2-3 cm pieces
2. Add 150 ml of cooking oil to the wok, add the pork belly and fry for 2 minutes, then add the salt and stir-fry the pork; after 2 minutes, take it out
3. Add 50 ml of cooking oil to the wok, add the sugar, and stir until it turns coffee-colored
4. Add the pork belly and stir-fry for 30 s, then add the ginger slices and garlic and stir-fry for 30 s
5. Add the cooking wine, five-spice powder and green onion, add water to cover the pork, cover, and cook for 10 minutes
6. Add the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and oyster sauce and cook over medium heat for 20 minutes
7. Remove the lid, reduce the sauce over high heat, and plate

## Additional Notes

* Use the whole green onion; no need to chop it
* The purpose of adding salt in step 2 is to lock in the fat of the pork belly
* While making this, some of the pork was boiled first and cooked together with the unboiled pork in later steps; the unboiled pork turned out bouncier
