# How to Make Huangmen Braised Chicken

Huangmen ji (braised chicken) is a home-style dish made mainly with chicken legs, shiitake mushrooms and green peppers: a rich sauce, tender chicken, savory with a touch of sweetness, and very good with rice. It's rich in high-quality protein and mushroom polysaccharides. Making the caramel is a little challenging, but you can substitute dark soy sauce; overall it's not difficult, and beginners can master it smoothly. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 766 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken legs
- Shiitake mushrooms (dried are best)
- Green peppers
- Ginger slices
- Dried chili peppers
- Salt
- Cooking wine
- Ground white pepper
- White sugar
- Soy sauce
- MSG
- Potato (optional)

## Calculation

- Chicken legs = 2
- Shiitake mushrooms (dried are best) = 5
- Green peppers = 2
- Ginger = 2 slices
- Dried chili peppers = 5 or 6
- Salt = 10g
- Cooking wine = 10ml
- Ground white pepper = 5g
- White sugar = 5g
- Soy sauce = 5ml
- Potato = 1 (optional; makes the sauce thicker)

## Steps

### Prep

1. Rinse the chicken legs and chop into **4cm** pieces
2. Slice the ginger and cut the dried chilies into **small rings**
3. Slice the shiitakes and cut the green peppers into long, thin **horseshoe shapes**. If using dried shiitakes, rinse off the dust, soak them overnight, and save the soaking water
4. If using potato, **roll-cut** it into pieces about the same size as the chicken

### Cooking

1. Make the caramel: add oil to the wok, and while the oil is still cold, add the sugar (**this is a bit tricky; beginners can skip straight to frying the chicken and use dark soy sauce instead**)
2. Heat slowly over low heat. As the oil temperature rises, the sugar starts to melt and turns a fairly dark brown (stir constantly to prevent burning)
3. Quickly add the chicken, turn to high heat, and stir-fry fast! Splash in the cooking wine and keep stir-frying for a moment
4. Add the ginger slices and dried chilies
5. Add the soy sauce and stir-fry evenly
6. Pour in the shiitake soaking water or plain water, enough to cover the chicken
7. Add the shiitake slices, ground white pepper, salt and potato
8. After stirring evenly, cover and braise over medium-low heat for **15-20 minutes**; if you can, transfer to a clay pot
9. When the chicken is tender and the sauce is thick (don't reduce it too dry), finally add the green peppers
10. Add the MSG, toss evenly, and turn off the heat! The green peppers only need to be just cooked; don't stir-fry them too long
