# How to Make Yuxiang Eggplant

![Yuxiang eggplant - preview image 1](../../../dishes/meat_dish/鱼香茄子/yxqz1.jpg)

Yuxiang eggplant ("fish-fragrant" eggplant) is a classic Sichuan home-style dish: sweet, tangy and slightly spicy, with soft eggplant that soaks up the sauce—great with rice. Eggplant is rich in dietary fiber and various vitamins, and minced meat adds protein. The difficulty is medium; you need to master frying the eggplant and seasoning, but beginners can succeed with a little practice. It takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 579 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggplant
- Minced meat
- Salt
- Sugar
- MSG
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Vinegar
- Starch slurry
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for one person.

- Eggplants 2
- Minced meat 20g
- Salt 3-5g
- Sugar 5-10g
- MSG 5g
- Light soy sauce 10ml
- Dark soy sauce 5ml
- Vinegar 10ml
- Starch slurry 100ml
- Doubanjiang 20-30g
- Green onion, ginger, garlic, bird's eye chili (to taste)

## Steps

1. Cut the eggplant into strips.

    ![Yuxiang eggplant - preview image 2](../../../dishes/meat_dish/鱼香茄子/yxqz2.jpg)

2. Mince the meat, chop the green onion, ginger and garlic, and dice the bird's eye chilies.

    ![Yuxiang eggplant - preview image 3](../../../dishes/meat_dish/鱼香茄子/yxqz3.jpg)

3. Mix the yuxiang sauce: in a bowl, stir together the salt, MSG, sugar, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, vinegar and starch slurry.
4. Pour 300ml of oil into the wok and turn on low heat (low heat is easier to control). When the oil reaches 7/10 heat (about 40 seconds on low, when smoke appears), add the eggplant and fry for two minutes. When the edges of the eggplant turn slightly yellow, take it out. Save the excess oil for future stir-fries.

    ![Yuxiang eggplant - preview image 4](../../../dishes/meat_dish/鱼香茄子/yxqz4.jpg)

5. Leave 15-30ml of oil in the wok, add the minced meat and stir-fry until it turns white, then take it out.

    ![Yuxiang eggplant - preview image 5](../../../dishes/meat_dish/鱼香茄子/yxqz5.jpg)

6. Add 15-30ml of oil to the wok, add the doubanjiang, green onion whites, ginger and garlic and fry until fragrant, then add the minced meat back and stir-fry evenly.

    ![Yuxiang eggplant - preview image 6](../../../dishes/meat_dish/鱼香茄子/yxqz6.jpg)

7. Add 80-150ml of water (about 80% of the height of the eggplant), add the eggplant and the sauce, stir-fry over high heat to let the flavors soak in and reduce the sauce. Finally add the green onion, toss evenly, and serve.

    ![Yuxiang eggplant - preview image 7](../../../dishes/meat_dish/鱼香茄子/yxqz7.jpg)

## Additional Notes

- You can peel the eggplant if you like, and add more minced meat.
