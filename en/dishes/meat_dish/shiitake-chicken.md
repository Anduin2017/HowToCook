# How to Make Shiitake Chicken

![Shiitake chicken](../../../dishes/meat_dish/香菇滑鸡/香菇滑鸡.jpg)

Shiitake chicken (xianggu hua ji) is a home-style dish made mainly with chicken thighs and shiitake mushrooms. After stir-frying and braising, the chicken is silky and tender, with the unique earthy aroma of shiitakes and a rich, savory sauce—a Cantonese flavor loved by all ages. Chicken is rich in high-quality protein and various amino acids, and shiitakes contain lentinan and dietary fiber, which help boost immunity and aid digestion. This dish needs some knife skills and heat control, which is a bit challenging for beginners. It takes about 1 hour overall.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 608 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Large chicken legs
- Dried shiitake mushrooms
- Ginger
- Green onion
- Garlic

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Large chicken legs 2
- Dried shiitake mushrooms 5
- Ginger 2 slices
- Green onion 2 stalks
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Warm water (30-40 ℃) 150ml
- Cooking wine 15ml
- Light soy sauce 30ml
- Salt 1.5g
- Dark soy sauce 15ml
- Sugar 15ml
- Sesame oil 5ml

## Steps

1. Rehydrate the dried shiitakes in warm water
2. Cut the ginger into small pieces, the green onion into segments, and halve the garlic into small pieces
3. Debone the chicken legs (you can leave the bone in; it just slightly hurts a programmer's eating efficiency) and cut into small pieces
4. Quarter the rehydrated shiitakes, saving the soaking water
5. Blanch the chicken for 1 minute to remove blood foam and impurities
6. Add 15ml of cooking wine, 15ml of light soy sauce, 1.5g of salt and 15ml of dark soy sauce to the chicken and mix by hand
7. At 3/10 oil heat, add the chicken and stir-fry until golden, then take it out
8. Leave some oil in the wok, add the green onion, ginger and garlic and fry until fragrant, add the shiitakes and toss over high heat
9. After about 20 seconds you'll smell the shiitakes. Now add the fried chicken, the shiitake soaking water (all of it—**what this programmer considers the soul of the dish**), 15ml of sugar and 30ml of light soy sauce
10. Turn to medium heat, uncovered, and bubble for 2 minutes to thicken the sauce. Drizzle with 5ml of sesame oil, sprinkle with chopped green onion, turn off the heat and plate

## Additional Notes

- Deboning chicken legs is a bit complicated; here's my experience for reference:
  - Place the chicken leg straight on the board with **the bone pointing toward the programmer**, and cut from the top all the way to the bottom. Cut deep, making sure you hit the bone
  - Push the meat apart to both sides with your hands
  - Use the tip of the knife to separate the meat still stuck to the bone
  - Turn the leg over, insert the knife tight against the bone, and cut the connection points between bone and meat
  - Use the tip of the knife to cut the last bits still attached
