# How to Make Shanxi Guoyou Rou

Guoyou rou ("passed-through-oil pork") is a famous traditional Shanxi dish: tender, silky pork slices and crisp garlic scapes, savory and slightly tangy with the mellow aroma of aged vinegar—a typical local home-style flavor. Pork tenderloin provides high-quality protein, and with wood ear mushrooms, garlic scapes and onion it's nutritionally balanced. This dish requires some skill with heat and velveting, so it's better suited to people with some cooking experience. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to plate.

## Steps

1. Rehydrate the wood ear mushrooms in advance; if you're in a hurry, use hot water.
2. Slice the pork tenderloin and put it in a bowl. Add 20ml of light soy sauce, cooking wine and ground Sichuan pepper, crack in an egg, and mix evenly with your own little hands. Add the starch (sweet potato starch recommended) and mix well, pour in 300ml of cooking oil to seal the coating, and marinate for 15 minutes.
3. Cut the garlic scapes into segments about 3cm long and the onion into diamond-shaped pieces. Set aside.
4. Heat the wok with plenty of oil. At 5/10 heat, add the marinated pork slices, separating them, then remove and drain.
5. Pour the excess oil out of the wok, leaving 10ml for stir-frying, and heat to 7/10 heat
6. Add the scallion, ginger and garlic and fry until fragrant. First add the garlic scapes and stir-fry until just cooked, then add the wood ear mushrooms and onion, add light soy sauce and ground Sichuan pepper, toss a few times, and add the fried pork slices and stir-fry
7. Add 10g of salt, and just before serving add 10ml of vinegar and the chicken bouillon. Plate.

## Additional Notes

- When frying the pork slices, they only need to be lightly browned on the surface; watch the heat.
- You can also add a thin starch slurry before serving for a better texture.
