# How to Make Tomato Beef Brisket

Tomato beef brisket has a thick, sweet-and-tangy sauce and soft, mellow beef—an appetizing home-style dish that goes great with rice. It's rich in protein, vitamins and lycopene for balanced nutrition. It's somewhat challenging for beginners; you need to master the heat for braising the meat and the technique for cooking the tomatoes down into a sauce. Beginners generally need about 90 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 1569 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Tomatoes
* Beef brisket
* Gas stove (for peeling the tomatoes)
* Pressure cooker / clay pot / ordinary aluminum pot (iron pot)
* Two 2cm green onion segments, two ginger slices, 10g each of chopped green onion and ginger
* Light soy sauce, ground white pepper, white sugar, cooking wine / Shaoxing wine, three small pieces of star anise
* Beef brisket (choose pieces with alternating fat and lean for better texture)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Tomatoes 3-4 (about 200g each)
- Beef brisket 500g
- Cooking oil 20-30ml

## Steps

1. Cut the brisket into strips, then into 2cm cubes. Put them in cold water, bring to a boil, and boil for 2 minutes to remove the blood, then take them out and rinse clean
2. In another pot, bring 2L of water to a boil, add two 2cm green onion segments, two ginger slices, the star anise and 5-10ml of cooking wine / Shaoxing wine, add the blanched beef, cover and braise (1 hour in a clay pot, 45 minutes on the meat setting of a pressure cooker). It's done when a chopstick easily pokes through
3. Peel the tomatoes: score a cross on the top of each tomato down to its middle, push chopsticks / a fork in through the stem end, and roast over low heat on the gas stove, turning as you go. Take it off often to check, and once the skin lifts, peel it off and cut into pieces—the smaller the better

  - Be careful not to burn yourself when peeling; peeled tomatoes are very slippery, so cut slowly and safely

4. Heat oil in the wok to 7/10 heat, add 10g each of green onion and ginger, add the tomatoes, and stir-fry thoroughly until the tomatoes release their red color. Add the cooked brisket and its broth, just enough to cover the beef
5. Season with salt, sugar and light soy sauce to taste, and cover
6. Once boiling, keep cooking over high heat for 3-5 minutes
7. When the tomato sauce is moderately thick, turn off the heat, sprinkle with chopped green onion, and plate

## Additional Notes

- Be careful with fire, be careful with fire, be careful with fire
- When braising meat in a clay pot / aluminum pot, turn to medium-low / low heat once the water boils. For pressure cookers, see [Learn to Use a Pressure Cooker](../../tips/learn/pressure-cooking.md)
- The tomato peeling method in this tutorial is the fastest I've tried so far
- Absolutely no ketchup and minimal seasonings, to keep the natural flavor of the ingredients as much as possible
