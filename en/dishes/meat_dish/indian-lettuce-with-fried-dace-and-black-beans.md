# How to Make Indian Lettuce with Fried Dace and Black Beans

![Indian lettuce with fried dace and black beans](../../../dishes/meat_dish/豆豉鲮鱼油麦菜/豆豉鲮鱼油麦菜成品.jpg)

Indian lettuce with fried dace and black beans is a quick, savory home-style stir-fry that goes great with rice, inspired by Cantonese canned fish dishes: crisp Indian lettuce (youmaicai) and crispy, fragrant dace. It pairs meat and vegetables and is rich in protein and dietary fiber. It's simple and very beginner-friendly, usually done in fifteen minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 416 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Indian lettuce (youmaicai)
- Canned fried dace with salted black beans (Kam Chuk brand)
- Garlic
- Light soy sauce
- White sugar
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

Per portion (about 2 servings):

- Indian lettuce 500g
- Canned dace 1 can (about 250g)
- Garlic 4 cloves
- Light soy sauce 20g
- White sugar 3g
- Cooking oil 15ml

## Steps

1. **Preparing the ingredients**:
   - Wash the Indian lettuce and cut into segments
   - Open the can of dace, remove the main bones from the fish, and cut into small pieces. Set aside
   - Mince the garlic

2. **Cooking**:
   - Heat the wok and add cooking oil. When the oil is hot, add the washed lettuce and stir-fry until wilted and watery, then take it out and pour off the liquid
   - Reheat the wok, add the oil from the can of dace, and fry the minced garlic until fragrant
   - Once fragrant, add the black beans from the can and toss, then add the chopped dace and toss again
   - Once evenly mixed, add the lettuce, and season with light soy sauce and sugar
   - After seasoning, toss briefly and plate

## Additional Notes

- **Precautions**:
  1. The canned dace is already seasoned; with the light soy sauce it's flavorful enough, so no salt is needed
