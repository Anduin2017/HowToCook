# How to Make Bacon Mac and Cheese

![Baked version](../../../dishes/meat_dish/奶酪培根通心粉/oven.jpg)
![Non-baked version](../../../dishes/meat_dish/奶酪培根通心粉/onepot.png)

Bacon mac and cheese is a classic American home-style dish: rich and creamy, with cheese coating springy macaroni and crispy bacon for savory satisfaction. It's rich in protein and calcium, but high in calories and fat, so enjoy it occasionally. It takes some patience; mastering a basic white sauce and the heat is a bit challenging for beginners, so the overall difficulty is medium. Without baking it's on the table in about 30 minutes; if you bake it, you need extra time to preheat and bake, about 1 hour in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 728 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Macaroni
- Cheese
- Meat
- Onion
- Butter
- Flour
- Milk
- Garlic

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

- Macaroni 100-125g
- Cheese 40-55g, plus an extra 25g in strips if baking
- Bacon or other meat 100-125g
- Onion 25g-40g, chopped
- Butter 15g
- Flour 10g
- Milk 125ml
- Garlic half a clove, minced

## Steps

1. Grate the cheese
2. Cut the onion into strips
3. Boil the macaroni in lightly salted water for 6 minutes
4. **Medium heat**
5. Put the butter in the pan and wait for it to melt
6. Add the onion
7. When the onion has softened, add the garlic
8. When the garlic is fragrant, add the meat and wait 5 seconds
9. **Low heat**
10. Add the milk in four additions, stirring for 5 seconds after each before adding the next
11. Add the flour and stir thoroughly
12. Add the cheese and stir until smooth
13. Stir the macaroni into the cheese sauce
14. If you're not baking it, it's ready to eat
15. **Baking:**
16. Preheat the oven to 180°C
17. Spread the extra cheese over the macaroni
18. Once the oven has reached 180°C, put the macaroni in
19. Bake until golden on top, about 24 minutes

## Additional Notes

This dish is extremely unhealthy, with about 1300 kcal and 80g of fat. Don't eat it often.
