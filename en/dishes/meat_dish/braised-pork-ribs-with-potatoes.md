# How to Make Braised Pork Ribs with Potatoes

The ribs are so tender they fall off the bone, the potatoes are soft and full of flavor, and the sauce is rich, savory, slightly sweet and slightly spicy—a typical northern Chinese home-style braise. It's rich in protein, calcium and carbohydrates for balanced nutrition. It's a bit demanding, as you need to master the caramel color and the heat, but the steps are clear and beginners can give it a try. It takes about 1.5 hours in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 991 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Pork spare ribs
* Potatoes
* Ginger
* Green onion
* Cooking wine
* White sugar
* Dried chili peppers

* Sichuan peppercorns
* Cassia bark
* Light soy sauce
* Dark soy sauce
* Oyster sauce
* Soybean paste
* Salt
* Cooking oil

## Calculation

Note: the parameters below are a precise configuration for a standard 2-3 servings.

* Pork spare ribs = 750g
* Potatoes = 300g
* Ginger = 30g (split into two: 15g for blanching, 15g for stir-frying)
* Green onion = 25g (split into two: 15g tied in a knot, 10g chopped)
* Cooking wine = 25ml (split into two: 15ml for blanching, 10ml for stir-frying)
* White sugar = 10g
* Dried chili peppers = 5g
* Star anise = 1g (about 1-2)
* Sichuan peppercorns = 1g (about 10)
* Cassia bark = 1g (one small piece)
* Light soy sauce = 10ml
* Dark soy sauce = 5ml
* Oyster sauce = 5ml
* Soybean paste = 5g
* Salt = 3g (for seasoning at the end while reducing)
* Boiling water = 700ml
* Cooking oil = 20ml

## Steps

### Preprocessing: Cutting Ingredients and Preventing Oxidation

1. **Potatoes:** peel 300g of potatoes and roll-cut them into irregular chunks. **Put them in a bowl of water immediately after cutting** to keep out oxygen and prevent the surfaces from oxidizing and darkening. Set aside.
2. **Green onion and ginger:** slice all 30g of ginger into thin slices (split into two portions of 15g). Prepare the 25g of green onion: tie 15g into a knot and finely chop the remaining 10g. Set aside.

### Preprocessing: Packaging Seasonings and Async Tasks

1. **Package the sauce bowl (one-click call):** in a small bowl, add 10ml of light soy sauce, 5ml of dark soy sauce, 5ml of oyster sauce and 5ml of soybean paste in order, stir thoroughly, and place within easy reach of the stove.
2. **Package the spice dish (one-click call):** on a small dish, put 5g of dried chilies, 1g of star anise (about 1-2), 1g of Sichuan peppercorns (about 10), 1g of cassia bark (one small piece), and the 15g of ginger slices you just cut. Set aside.
3. **Start the async water job:** switch on the electric kettle and start boiling 700ml of water. (So the boiling water is ready just as the stir-frying finishes.)

### Initial Processing: Blanching and Drying the Ribs

1. **Cold-water blanching:** put the 750g of ribs in cold water with the 15g green onion knot, 15g of ginger slices and 15ml of cooking wine. Bring to a boil over high heat, then keep boiling for 2 minutes to force out the blood foam.
2. **Wash hot, not cold:** take out the ribs and **you must use hot water** to wash the remaining foam off the surface (cold water makes the meat contract sharply and toughen).
3. **Absolute dehydration (anti-spatter):** the washed ribs **must be thoroughly patted dry with paper towels**, then plated and set beside the wok (to prevent violent spattering when they go into the hot caramel later).

### Core Stir-fry: Caramel Color and Aromatics (High-Concurrency Phase)

1. **Make the caramel color:** heat the wok, add 20ml of cooking oil. Add 10g of white sugar and stir constantly over medium-low heat until the sugar has completely melted, bubbles, and turns a reddish-brown caramel color.
2. **Color and sear:** quickly add the 750g of thoroughly dried ribs and stir-fry over medium-high heat. Sear for about 2-3 minutes until the ribs are lightly golden on both sides and evenly coated in caramel.
3. **One-click aromatics:** clear a small space in the wok and **pour in the [spice dish] directly**. Fry in the hot oil at the bottom of the wok for about 10 seconds to bring out the fat-soluble aromas of the spices.
4. **One-click seasoning:** drizzle the remaining 10ml of cooking wine around the edge of the hot wok for "wok aroma". Immediately **pour in the [sauce bowl] directly** and stir-fry for about 30 seconds, so the sauce fully coats the ribs and releases a rich savory aroma.

### Braising and Reducing: Glazing and Serving

1. **Add water and braise:** the kettle has now boiled; add the 700ml of boiling water (enough to cover the ribs) and bring to a boil over high heat. Then cover and braise over low heat for 40 minutes.
2. **Add the potatoes:** after 40 minutes, remove the lid, drain the 300g of potatoes from their water, and add them to the wok. Cover again and keep braising over low heat for 15-20 minutes, until a chopstick easily pierces the potato chunks.
3. **Season and reduce:** remove the lid and add 3g of salt (taste the sauce now and adjust to taste). Turn to high heat and toss to reduce the sauce, about 3-5 minutes, until the sauce is thick and evenly coats the ribs and potatoes. Turn off the heat.
4. **Garnish and serve:** plate, and sprinkle the 10g of chopped green onion evenly on top as a UI decoration.
