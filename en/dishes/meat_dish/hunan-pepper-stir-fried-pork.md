# How to Make Hunan Pepper Stir-fried Pork

Pepper stir-fried pork (lajiao chao rou) is a home-style dish full of Hunan flavor: the peppers are dry-fried until fragrant and slightly charred, the pork slices tender and well-seasoned, savory and slightly spicy—great with rice. Pork is rich in high-quality protein and B vitamins, and green peppers provide plenty of vitamin C. This dish requires mastering the heat for dry-frying and quick stir-frying, suitable for people with some cooking experience. It takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 425 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Green peppers (if you like spicy food, Hangzhou peppers or luosi peppers are recommended; if not, use long sweet peppers or bell peppers)
* Lean pork
* Salt
* Light soy sauce
* Oyster sauce
* Garlic
* Ginger
* Soy sauce (optional)
* Fermented black beans (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

* Number of green peppers = portions * 3.
* Pork = portions * 200g.
* Salt = portions * 3g.
* Light soy sauce = portions * 3ml.
* Oyster sauce = portions * 3ml.
* Garlic = portions * 5g.
* Ginger = portions * 5g.
* Soy sauce = portions * 2ml.
* Fermented black beans = portions * 3g.

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. Wash the `green peppers`, remove the `stems` and `seeds`, then `roll-cut` them and set aside.
2. Smash the `garlic` with a knife, then cut crosswise into `pieces`, and mince the `ginger`.
3. Slice the `lean pork` (cut along the grain, i.e. with the knife parallel to the grain of the meat, so the slices show grain lines like the character "川").
4. Rinse the sliced `pork`, put it in an empty bowl, add the calculated `light soy sauce`, `oyster sauce` and `salt`, mix evenly and marinate for 10 minutes.
5. Heat the wok without oil, add the `cut peppers`, and dry-fry over high heat until blistered like tiger skin, then add 2g of `salt`, keep stir-frying for 1 minute, and remove.
6. Without washing the wok, heat it over high heat, add portions * 8ml of `oil`, wait 30s, then add the `garlic` and `ginger` and stir-fry for 15s.
7. Add the marinated `pork` and stir-fry for 2 minutes, then add the dry-fried `peppers` and stir-fry for 1 minute.
8. Add the `fermented black beans` to taste, and finally the `soy sauce`, and keep stir-frying for 30s.
9. Serve and plate.

## Additional Notes

**Only use green peppers—luosi peppers are the optimal solution. Don't use other kinds of chili peppers!**
