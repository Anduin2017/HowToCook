# How to Make Beer Duck

![Finished beer duck](../../../dishes/meat_dish/啤酒鸭/啤酒鸭.jpg)

This is a home-style dish with a distinctive flavor: the duck is savory and tender with a light beer aroma, warming without being heavy, fragrant without being greasy. Duck is rich in protein, and beer removes gaminess and adds aroma, making the combination especially flavorful. It's beginner-friendly and takes about 1 hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 170 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Duck
- Beer
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Rock sugar
- Dried chili peppers
- Cooking wine
- Salt
- Chicken bouillon powder
- Cloves
- Star anise
- Bay leaves
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Half a duck (about 1kg)
- Beer 800ml
- Light soy sauce 10-15ml
- Dark soy sauce 5-10ml
- Ginger 5 slices
- Garlic 12 cloves
- Rock sugar 10g
- Dried chili peppers 5
- Cooking wine 30ml
- Salt 8g
- Chicken bouillon powder 5g
- Cloves 4
- Star anise 3
- Bay leaves 3
- Doubanjiang 20g

## Steps

1. Cut the duck into 3 cm pieces. Put the duck in a pot of cold water with ginger slices and cooking wine and blanch it once, then take it out, drain and set aside.
2. Heat the wok, add about 100ml of cooking oil, and once the oil is very hot over high heat, add the duck and stir-fry until browned.
3. When the duck has completely changed color (visibly whitened), push it to one side of the wok, add the doubanjiang and sugar, and stir-fry over low heat until fragrant and caramelized.
4. Add the cloves, star anise, bay leaves, dried chilies, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and garlic and stir-fry until fragrant.
5. Pour in beer to cover the duck, add the salt and chicken bouillon powder, then braise the duck over medium heat for 30 minutes (if your teeth aren't great, braise for another 5 minutes).
6. Plate and serve.

## Additional Notes

- There must be enough beer to cover all the duck, and a light, crisp beer is best.
