# How to Make Stir-fried Chicken Livers

![Stir-fried chicken livers](../../../dishes/meat_dish/小炒鸡肝/成品.jpg)

This chicken liver stir-fry has a strong garlic aroma and a tender texture with a little toasty cumin, a home-style stir-fry. Chicken liver is rich in iron, vitamin A and high-quality protein, which help nourish the blood and support eyesight. You need to master blanching to remove the off-flavors, which is a little challenging for beginners. It takes about forty minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 760 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Raw chicken livers
- Garlic sprouts (the green shoots of young garlic plants; called "green garlic" in some places, just to clarify.)
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type), ginger, cooking wine
- Salt, chicken bouillon powder (MSG), five-spice powder (thirteen-spice), ground pepper
- Barbecue seasoning or ground cumin (optional)
- Sesame seeds (optional)
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make.
One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Raw chicken livers 5 (about 800g)
- Garlic sprouts (about 200g; add more if you like them)
- Scallion 150g (split into two: 100g cut into segments, 50g sliced)
- Ginger 120g (split into two: 70g sliced, 50g diced)
- Cooking wine 30ml
- Salt 5g
- Chicken bouillon powder 5g (or MSG)
- Five-spice powder 5g (or thirteen-spice)
- Ground pepper 5g
- Barbecue seasoning or ground cumin 10g (optional)
- Sesame seeds 5g (optional)
- Cooking oil 30ml

## Steps

1. Rinse the chicken livers and set aside
2. Wash the garlic sprouts, cut into segments, and set aside
3. Wash the scallion; cut 100g into segments and slice 50g. Set aside
4. Wash the ginger; slice 70g and dice 50g. Set aside
5. Part one: blanching

  - Put the cleaned livers in a pot, add cold water to cover, and add the 100g of scallion segments, 70g of ginger slices and 30ml of cooking wine
  - Turn on high heat. When the water boils, turn to medium heat, skim off the foam, and **wait 5-10 minutes**
  - Turn off the heat, take out the livers, rinse in cold water and clean thoroughly
  - Once clean, slice them and set aside

6. Part two: stir-frying

  - Turn on medium heat. Add 30ml of cooking oil to the wok and wait 10 seconds to let it heat up
  - Add the 50g of sliced scallion and 50g of diced ginger and stir-fry until fragrant
  - Once fragrant, add the sliced livers and stir-fry until slightly browned
  - Add the garlic sprouts, stir-fry for 30 seconds, and once just cooked, turn to low heat
  - Add the seasonings
    - Salt 5g
    - Chicken bouillon powder (MSG) 5g
    - Five-spice powder (thirteen-spice) 5g
    - Ground pepper 5g
    - Barbecue seasoning or ground cumin 10g (optional)
  - Turn to high heat, stir-fry quickly for 30 seconds so nothing burns, then turn off the heat
  - Transfer to a plate. You can sprinkle 5g of sesame seeds on top as decoration; if you don't like them or don't have any, leave them out.

## Additional Notes

- When blanching, watch the water level as it boils and don't let it boil over, to avoid danger.
- After blanching, rinse the livers in cold water—ice water is even better—for a better texture.
