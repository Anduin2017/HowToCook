# How to Make Pork Skin Aspic

![Finished pork skin aspic](../../../dishes/meat_dish/猪皮冻/猪皮冻.jpg)

Pork skin aspic (zhupi dong) is a traditional cold dish commonly served at Lunar New Year's Eve dinner in northern China: crystal clear, smooth and springy, especially good with drinks. Pork skin is rich in collagen and is said to be good for the skin. This dish is a bit harder: it takes patience to remove the gamy smell from the pork skin and to simmer it for a long time. It takes about 24 hours in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1630 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork skin
- Star anise, Sichuan peppercorns, angelica root, cassia bark, cloves, bay leaves, fennel seeds

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Main ingredients: pork skin 1kg, water 4kg
* Seasonings: salt 13g, MSG 10g, chicken bouillon powder 15g, light soy sauce 50ml, dark soy sauce 20ml, green onion 10g, green onion 10g, ginger slices 20g
* Spice bag: star anise 10g, Sichuan peppercorns 5g

## Steps

1. Chop the pork skin into pieces no larger than 10cm and soak in water for 12 hours. Then put it in a pot of cold water, add 10g of ginger and 50ml of cooking wine, blanch for 5-10 minutes, then take it out and put it in cold water
2. Put the blanched pork skin on the board and scrape off all the white fat on the inside, then cut into strips no wider than 3mm and put them in a basin
3. Add 20g of white vinegar and 5g of salt, scrub hard for 3 minutes, then rinse clean with water. At this point the pork skin has basically no gamy smell left
4. Add 4kg of water to the pot, add the pork skin, 10g of green onion, 10g of ginger slices, 10g of star anise and 5g of Sichuan peppercorns. Bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer over low heat for 90 minutes until the skin is very soft
5. Season with 8g of salt, 10g of MSG, 15g of chicken bouillon, 50ml of light soy sauce and 20ml of dark soy sauce, pour into a dish, pick out the green onion, ginger and star anise, and let it cool until it sets like jelly
6. Refrigerate. To serve, cut into small cubes or thick slices

## Additional Notes

- Important: if you put the pork skin straight into the pot to boil, it's "ruined"—this is the biggest mistake. You must keep scrubbing the pork skin with white vinegar and salt to make sure the finished aspic is crystal clear with no gamy smell
