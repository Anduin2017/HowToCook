# How to Make Steamed Pork Patty with Egg

Steamed pork patty with egg is a savory, tender traditional home-style dish: the pork patty soaks up the sauce, with an egg nestled in the middle, soft and great with rice. It's rich in high-quality protein and various amino acids, good for restoring energy. It's very simple and very friendly to kitchen beginners, taking only about 20 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1124 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Minced pork
- Eggs
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- Ground white pepper
- Sesame oil

## Calculation

- Minced pork 300g
- Eggs 2
- Cooking wine 10ml
- Light soy sauce 20ml
- Ground white pepper 5g
- Sesame oil 10-15ml

## Steps

1. In a bowl, add the minced pork, cooking wine, light soy sauce, ground white pepper, egg and sesame oil, and mix well.
2. Spread the seasoned pork on a plate, make a hollow in the middle with a spoon, and crack 1 egg into the hollow.
3. Add water to the pot to 1/4 of its height. When the water boils, put the plate in, cover, and steam for 15 minutes.

## Additional Notes

- When taking out the steamed pork patty, be careful: the plate is very hot.
