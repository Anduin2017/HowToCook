# How to Make Ginger Stir-fried Chicken

![Ginger stir-fried chicken](../../../dishes/meat_dish/姜炒鸡/姜炒鸡.jpg)

Ginger stir-fried chicken is a Hunan-style dish that goes great with rice: the chicken is dry-fragrant and well-seasoned, with a strong ginger aroma, spicy without being harsh. Chicken is rich in high-quality protein, and ginger helps warm the stomach and stimulate the appetite. It's simple; beginners can master it easily. It takes about 1 hour from marinating to plating.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1539 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken
- Ginger
- Beer
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Salt
- Bird's eye chili
- Red finger chili (meirenla)
- Pickled chilies
- Garlic

## Calculation

- Chicken = half (free-range chicken is best, about 650g)
- Cooking oil = 50ml (camellia oil is best; otherwise use rapeseed oil)
- Ginger = 250g
- Beer = half a bottle, 250ml
- Light soy sauce = 20ml
- Dark soy sauce = 10ml
- Salt = 3g
- Bird's eye chili = 0-5 (0-50g) (adjust to your spice preference)
- Red finger chili = 0-5 (0-50g) (substitute bird's eye chili if you don't have any)
- Pickled chilies = 5 (50g)
- Garlic = 3 bulbs (50g)

## Steps

1. Chop the chicken into pieces about 1cm in size, rinse, drain, then marinate in light soy sauce and cooking wine for 30 minutes
2. Heat the wok on high until it just starts to smoke, add the cooking oil, and wait 5 seconds
3. Add the ginger slices, turn to medium heat and stir-fry for 30 seconds
4. Add the chicken and stir-fry for 3 minutes, frying off the moisture and rendering out the chicken fat
5. Add all the chopped chilies and garlic cloves, add the salt and dark soy sauce, and keep stir-frying for 30 seconds
6. Pour in the beer and braise over medium-low heat for 2 minutes
7. Reduce the sauce over high heat and plate
