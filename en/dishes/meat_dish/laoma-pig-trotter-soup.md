# How to Make Laoma Pig Trotter Soup

![Result 3](../../../dishes/meat_dish/老妈蹄花/result3.jpg)

Laoma tihua (Mom's pig trotter soup) has a milky white broth and trotters so tender they fall off the bone, with a delicate texture; served with a tangy, spicy dipping sauce it's very appetizing—a classic Sichuan home-style dish. Pig trotters are rich in collagen, and white kidney beans add a creamy texture for a nutritious dish. It requires a pressure cooker and soaking in advance, which is a bit challenging for beginners, but it's not hard to master if you follow the steps. Including soaking and pressure cooking, it takes about 2 hours in total (not counting soaking the white kidney beans overnight).

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1425 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pig trotters (preferably front trotters: more meat and tendon, less bone)
- Green onion
- Ginger
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- Angelica root (baizhi)
- Dong quai (danggui, optional)
- Chicken bouillon powder
- Salt
- Garlic
- Bird's eye chili
- Ground white pepper
- Light soy sauce
- Zhenjiang black vinegar
- Sichuan pepper oil
- Shaanxi chili oil (youpo lazi, optional)
- White kidney beans (use kelp if you don't have any)

## Calculation

- Pig trotters: 3
- White kidney beans: 200g
- Dong quai: 2g
- Ground white pepper: 5g
- Ginger slices: 30g
- Dong quai: 2g
- Minced garlic: 8g
- Chicken bouillon powder: 2g
- Light soy sauce: 25g
- Chopped green onion: 10g

## Steps

### Cooking

1. Soak 200g of white kidney beans in water the night before
2. Prepare the front trotters: when buying, ask the butcher to split them down the middle, use a blowtorch to remove the hair follicles, then wash them at home
3. Put the trotters, scallion segments, ginger slices and cooking wine in a pot of cold water, blanch for ten minutes, skim off the foam, then take them out, rinse clean and set aside
4. Put the trotters, dong quai, angelica root, ground white pepper and ginger slices in the pressure cooker. Once it reaches pressure, cook for thirty minutes, then add the white kidney beans and cook for another ten minutes. At this point, if the broth is milky white, congratulations—you did it right (if you need to add water partway, only add hot water)

![Laoma pig trotter soup - preview image 2](../../../dishes/meat_dish/老妈蹄花/result1.jpg)

5. Open the lid and season with salt, chicken bouillon and chopped green onion
6. Make the soul sauce: combine green onion, garlic, bird's eye chili, ground white pepper, light soy sauce, black vinegar, chili oil, Sichuan pepper oil and some of the trotter broth
  ![Laoma pig trotter soup - preview image 3](../../../dishes/meat_dish/老妈蹄花/result2.jpg)
7. Pour on the soul sauce and dig in
