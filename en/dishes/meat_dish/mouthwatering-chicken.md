# How to Make Mouthwatering Chicken

![Mouthwatering chicken](../../../dishes/meat_dish/口水鸡/口水鸡.jpg)

Mouthwatering chicken (koushui ji) is a classic Sichuan cold dish: chicken coated in red chili oil, chilled and springy, numbing, spicy and savory, appetizing and refreshing in summer. Chicken is rich in high-quality protein, and the crushed peanuts and spices add energy and flavor. The difficulty is medium; you need to watch the timing when poaching the chicken and the seasoning of the chili oil, so it suits cooks with some experience. From prep, poaching and cooling to saucing, it takes about 1.5 hours.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1392 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Half a chicken
- Chili powder
- Sichuan peppercorns
- Peanuts
- Scallion, ginger, garlic
- Sichuan peppercorns
- White sugar
- Light soy sauce
- Vinegar
- MSG

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Cooking oil   20ml
- Chicken     half (500g)
- Chili powder   20g
- Sichuan peppercorns     30 (20g)
- Peanuts     10 (30g)
- Green onion     2 stalks (50g)
- Ginger       1 small piece (20g)
- Garlic       2 cloves (10g)
- White sugar     5g
- Light soy sauce     5ml
- Vinegar       5ml
- MSG     5g
- Ground Sichuan pepper   5g
- Cilantro     5g

## Steps

1. Slice the ginger, and set aside 1 green onion and 15 Sichuan peppercorns
2. Wash the chicken and put it in a pot with water to cover. Add the ginger slices, green onion and Sichuan peppercorns, and bring to a boil over high heat.
3. Once boiling, turn to medium-low heat for 20 minutes, then turn off the heat
4. Take out the chicken and put it in ice water until it's ice cold
5. Take out the chicken, cut into pieces, arrange on a plate and set aside
6. Heat the wok over low heat, add the peanuts, and roast until the skins crack. (Keep tossing so they don't burn)
7. Cut one green onion into segments, smash the garlic into minced garlic, take 15 Sichuan peppercorns, and peel and chop the peanuts.
8. Heat oil in the wok, add the green onion segments, Sichuan peppercorns and half the minced garlic, and fry until fragrant
9. When the oil reaches 8/10 heat, turn off the heat and strain out the hot oil
10. Pour the hot oil into a bowl with the chili powder, stir, and strain out the red oil
11. Add the remaining minced garlic, light soy sauce, vinegar, salt, MSG, sugar, sesame oil and ground Sichuan pepper to the red oil. Mix well and let cool
12. Sprinkle the crushed peanuts over the chicken, pour the red oil over the cut chicken, and sprinkle with cilantro. Plate up
