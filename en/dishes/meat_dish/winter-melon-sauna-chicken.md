# How to Make Winter Melon Sauna Chicken

Winter melon sauna chicken is a light steamed dish: winter melon on the bottom soaks up the chicken juices, cordyceps flowers add savory depth, and the chicken is tender and silky—just steam it once the steamer is going. It's low in calories and high in protein, simple to make, and takes about 35 minutes from prep to plate, great for everyday home cooking.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 680 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken
- Shredded ginger
- Light soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Salt
- Cooking oil
- Winter melon
- Cordyceps flowers

## Calculation

Per portion (one portion is just enough for 2 people):

- Chicken 400 g (cut into pieces)
- Winter melon 300 g (thinly sliced)
- Cordyceps flowers 30 g (rinsed and soaked until soft in advance)
- Shredded ginger 10 g
- Light soy sauce 15 ml
- Oyster sauce 10 g
- Salt 3 g
- Cooking oil 10 ml

## Steps

1. Cut the chicken into pieces, add the shredded ginger, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt and cooking oil, rub in evenly, and marinate for 20 minutes
2. Thinly slice the winter melon and lay it on the bottom of a plate
3. Spread a layer of the soaked cordyceps flowers on top
4. Lay the marinated chicken on top of the cordyceps flowers
5. Bring water to a boil, put the plate in the steamer, and once steam is rising, steam for 15 minutes, then serve

## Additional Notes

- Thinly sliced winter melon on the bottom cooks through and absorbs flavor more easily, and soaks up all the savory chicken juices
- Rinse and soak the cordyceps flowers in advance and layer them between the winter melon and chicken, so the savory flavor seeps both up and down
