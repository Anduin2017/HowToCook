# How to Make Curry Beef Slices

![Finished curry beef slices](../../../dishes/meat_dish/咖喱肥牛/咖喱肥牛.jpg)

Curry beef slices is a rich home-style dish that goes perfectly with rice: the spiciness of curry blends with the richness of thinly sliced fatty beef, along with potatoes, carrots and other vegetables, for a soft, mellow texture. It's rich in protein, vitamins and calcium, a balanced mix of meat and vegetables. It's simple and beginner-friendly, usually on the table in 40 minutes, and great for packed lunches.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1821 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Bay leaf
- Whole milk (Weigang fresh milk recommended)
- Onion
- Carrot
- Potato
- Thinly sliced fatty beef rolls (feiniu)
- Curry roux blocks

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Bay leaf 1
- Whole milk 50ml
- Onion 100g
- Carrot 150g
- Potato 200g (quite filling; adjust as you like)
- Fatty beef rolls 300g (add more if you love meat)
- Curry roux 2 blocks (about 100g)

## Steps

1. Cut the onion into strips and the carrot and potato into chunks. Set aside
2. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add the beef rolls when it boils, skim off the blood foam, then drain the beef and set aside
3. Heat the wok, add 10ml - 15ml of cooking oil, and **wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up**
4. Add the onion and stir-fry until soft and translucent
5. Add the potato and carrot and **stir-fry for 2 minutes**
6. Add cold water to just cover all the ingredients
7. Add the bay leaf and curry roux to the wok and cover. **Once the water boils, turn the heat down and wait until the potato and carrot chunks are very soft (check with chopsticks)**
8. Add the beef rolls and milk, cover, and simmer on low for another 2-3 minutes, then serve (stir with a spoon gently so the beef slices don't break apart)

## Additional Notes

- No need to crush the curry roux; just put the whole blocks in.
- While cooking, stir the ingredients so the seasoning is evenly distributed.
