# How to Make Italian Roast Chicken

Italian roast chicken is a home-style oven dish with Mediterranean flavor. Chicken thighs are marinated in garlicky olive oil and roasted until the skin is slightly charred and the inside is tender and juicy, finished with lemon juice and parsley for a fresh, light taste. Chicken is rich in high-quality protein and olive oil provides unsaturated fatty acids for balanced nutrition. The difficulty is moderate—you need to preheat the oven—and beginners can follow the steps smoothly. It takes about fifty minutes in total from marinating to out of the oven.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 315 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken thighs
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Olive oil
- Garlic
- Lemon juice
- Parsley

## Calculation

- Chicken thighs: usually 1-2 per person
- Salt: 2 g/person
- Black pepper: 0.5 g/person
- Olive oil: 5 ml/person
- Garlic: 1 clove/person
- Lemon juice: 5ml/person
- Parsley: 1 sprig/person

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Preparing the Chicken

1. Rub the chicken thighs with salt, black pepper, olive oil and minced garlic
2. Put them in an oven preheated to 180 degrees and roast for 30-40 minutes or until cooked through

### Preparing the Garnish

1. Finely chop the parsley and set aside
2. Squeeze the lemon juice and set aside

### Finishing

1. Take out the roasted chicken and drizzle with lemon juice
2. Sprinkle with chopped parsley

## Additional Notes

- You can drizzle the chicken with honey or another barbecue sauce while roasting for extra flavor
- Preheat the oven to 180 degrees; the roasting time depends on the size and thickness of the chicken. Make sure the chicken is cooked through
- Chicken must be fully cooked before eating. Eating undercooked chicken may cause food poisoning and bacterial infections such as salmonella and Shigella flexneri.
