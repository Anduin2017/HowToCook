# How to Make Lamb Ribs Stewed with Radish

This soupy dish is fresh and not greasy: the lamb is tender and flavorful, and the radish soaks up the broth to become sweet and soft. It's a common warming home-style dish in northern China in winter. Lamb is rich in high-quality protein and iron, and radish contains vitamins and dietary fiber; together they warm the stomach, drive out the cold and aid digestion. Although it involves blanching to remove gaminess and two rounds of stewing, the steps are clear and easy to follow, and beginners can manage it. It takes about two hours overall, or about one hour with a pressure cooker.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1259 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Lamb ribs
- White radish (daikon)
- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
- Sichuan peppercorns
- Angelica root (baizhi, optional)
- Ginger
- Cooking wine or Shaoxing wine
- Salt
- Rock sugar
- Water

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Lamb ribs 400g
- White radish 1
- Scallion 1 stalk
- Sichuan peppercorns 10
- Ginger 10g; buy a piece of ginger and cut about 4 slices from the middle
- Cooking wine or Shaoxing wine 30ml-40ml
- Salt 10g
- Rock sugar 2-4 pieces
- Water: enough to cover the ingredients, about 1000ml

## Steps

1. Peel the radish and roll-cut into large 3-5cm chunks. Set aside
2. Ask the vendor to cut the lamb ribs when you buy them, since home knives generally can't cut through them. Set aside
3. Put the lamb in cold water, add half the cooking wine and half the scallion and ginger, and boil for 10 minutes to remove the blood and gaminess. (Optional) Skim off the blood foam that appears during blanching with a spoon
4. In another pot of cold water, add the cut radish and half the rock sugar. Once the water boils, cook for 5 minutes to remove the radish's sharpness
5. Take out the blanched lamb ribs and put them in the pressure cooker. Add water to cover all the ingredients, then add about another 300ml
6. Put the remaining scallion, ginger and cooking wine, the Sichuan peppercorns, rock sugar, angelica root (optional) and salt in the pot. Cover, start timing once it reaches pressure, and stew over medium heat for about 15 minutes.

  - Without a pressure cooker, use an ordinary pot and stew for about 40 minutes to 1 hour.

7. Turn off the heat, wait for the pressure cooker to release all its pressure, open the lid, add the blanched radish, season with 3-10g of salt or water, and taste the broth for saltiness
8. Turn the heat back on to medium: in the pressure cooker, stew for another 10 minutes once it reaches pressure; in an ordinary pot, cover and stew for another 20 minutes
9. Turn off the heat and serve

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, watch the water level as it boils. If it drops below 2/3 of the ingredients, add hot water to cover them.
