# How to Make Mapo Tofu

![Mapo tofu - preview image 1](../../../dishes/meat_dish/麻婆豆腐/1.jpeg)

This spicy, silky tofu dish uses salted duck egg instead of the traditional doubanjiang: savory with a slight numbing heat, especially appetizing with rice. It's rich in high-quality protein and trace elements such as calcium and iron. The difficulty is moderate and beginner-friendly, taking about 40 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 476 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Silken tofu (Qingmei brand recommended)
- Fruit knife
- Salted duck egg (Liufu brand recommended—this is the soul of the dish)
- Pork belly (supermarket ground pork also works)
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Bird's eye chili (optional if you don't eat spicy food)
- Spicy chili sauce (Guangle brand recommended)
- Sichuan peppercorns
- Salt
- Soy sauce (premium "weijixian" soy sauce)

## Calculation

- 1 box of silken tofu
- 1 salted duck egg
- 20-30g of pork belly
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 2 slices of ginger
- 5 bird's eye chilies
- 5g of garlic chili sauce
- 20 Sichuan peppercorns
- 3g of salt
- 10g of soy sauce

## Steps

1. Mince the garlic and ginger. Set aside
2. Cut the bird's eye chilies into rings. Set aside
3. Mince the pork belly (skip if you bought ground pork)
4. Add half the salt and the premium soy sauce to the minced pork, mix well, and set aside
5. Cut the duck egg in half lengthwise with a cleaver (be careful), remove the yolk (you must remove it, or it will taste fishy), and crumble the remaining white into pieces about 2 mm * 2 mm—no need to make them too fine. Set aside
6. Open the tofu package and use the fruit knife to cut the tofu in the box into pieces about 2.5 cm * 3 cm. Set aside
7. Heat the wok and add 10ml - 15ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
8. Turn to low heat, add the garlic, ginger, chili rings, Sichuan peppercorns, salted duck egg and garlic chili sauce, and stir-fry for 20 seconds until fragrant
9. Turn to medium heat, add the minced pork, and stir-fry for about 1 minute until it changes color
10. Turn to low heat, add the tofu, and sprinkle the remaining salt and premium soy sauce evenly over it
11. Pour boiling water in from the edge of the wok (otherwise the tofu breaks easily), just enough to cover the tofu
12. Turn to high heat; as soon as the water boils, switch to medium heat and wait about 10 minutes
13. When only 1/5 of the water remains and the tofu has taken on color, turn off the heat and plate

## Additional Notes

- Keep an eye on it the whole time to prevent burning
