# How to Make Sweet and Sour Pork Tenderloin

Sweet and sour pork tenderloin (tangcu liji) is rich, sweet and tangy, crispy outside and tender inside, a widely loved home-style flavor found in many regional cuisines. Pork tenderloin provides high-quality protein, but the batter, deep-frying and sweet and sour sauce make it fairly high in calories overall. You need to master marinating, double-frying and the heat for the sauce, which is a bit challenging for beginners; prep everything in advance and do it all in one go. It takes about 45 minutes to 1 hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1573 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork tenderloin
- Vinegar
- White sugar
- Starch
- Egg
- Light soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Oyster sauce
- Ketchup
- Ground white pepper
- Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Pork tenderloin 500g
- Vinegar 10g
- White sugar 30g
- Starch 50g
- Egg 50g
- Light soy sauce 10ml
- Cooking wine 20g
- Oyster sauce 10g
- Ketchup 30ml
- Ground white pepper 5g
- Salt 10g

## Steps

1. Marinate the pork: cut the tenderloin into thick slices and pound with the back of a knife to loosen the meat. Cut into strips about as thick as a finger, add the cooking wine, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt, ground white pepper and one egg, mix with your hands, and marinate for at least 20 minutes.
2. Make the sauce: ketchup + 10g of vinegar + 30g of sugar + 150ml of water, stirred until the sugar dissolves. Set aside.
3. Coat: coat all the pieces first before frying, so you won't be scrambling while frying. Put the starch in a large bowl and coat every strip of pork thoroughly.
4. Fry: add the tenderloin when the oil is at 160 degrees Celsius. You can test with a dry chopstick in the oil; when small bubbles form around it, the oil is ready.
5. Fry until the surface is lightly golden, then remove, using medium heat throughout. Then wait for the oil to reach 200 degrees Celsius and fry the tenderloin again for just 40 seconds; the coating will become very crispy. Remove immediately.
6. Sauce: in another wok, add a little oil, pour in the prepared sauce, cook until it bubbles, add the fried tenderloin and toss so every strip is coated in sauce.
7. Toss the fried tenderloin in the sauce, turn off the heat, and serve.

## Additional Notes

- Fry the tenderloin a few times and watch the heat, or you won't get the crispy-outside, tender-inside result!
