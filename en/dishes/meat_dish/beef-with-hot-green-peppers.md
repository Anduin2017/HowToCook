# How to Make Beef with Hot Green Peppers

A home-style stir-fry that's savory and slightly spicy, with tender, well-seasoned beef and crisp, refreshing hot green peppers. Beef provides high-quality protein and iron, and the peppers are rich in vitamin C, a nutritionally balanced combination. Marinating takes some patience, but the steps are clear, beginners can handle it, and the difficulty is moderate. It takes about 1 hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 449 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Beef
* Green onion, ginger, garlic
* Hot green peppers
* Soy sauce
* Salt
* Sugar

## Calculation

* Beef: 250 g/person
* Green onion: 0.5 stalk/person
* Ginger: 1 piece/person
* Salt: 3 g/person
* Sugar: 1.5 g/person
* Soy sauce: 9 ml/person

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Preparing the Vegetables

1. Mince the garlic into a paste
2. Cut the green onion into segments
3. Slice the ginger
4. Cut the hot peppers into segments

### Preparing the Beef

1. Put the beef in a bowl
2. Add the ginger, salt, soy sauce and sugar and marinate for 30-40 minutes
3. You can remove the ginger after marinating

### Cooking

1. Put cold oil in the wok and heat until the occasional bubble appears
2. Add the minced garlic
3. When the garlic turns golden, add the hot peppers
4. When the pepper skins wrinkle slightly, add the marinated beef and stir-fry
5. Before the beef is cooked through, add the green onion and keep stir-frying
6. Stir-fry until the beef is cooked, then turn off the heat and serve

## Additional Notes

* You can add baking soda to the marinade to make the beef more tender, in a beef-to-baking-soda ratio of 500g : 3g.
* Cooking wine can replace the ginger slices in the marinade, at 3 g/person
