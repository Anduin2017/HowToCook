# How to Make Lean Pork with Potato Slices

![Lean pork with potato slices - preview image 1](../../../dishes/meat_dish/瘦肉土豆片/瘦肉土豆片.jpg)

A quick home-style stir-fry with tender pork slices and soft yet slightly firm potato slices that soak up the flavor, plus the fresh aroma of garlic sprouts—typical home cooking. Lean pork is rich in high-quality protein and iron, and potatoes provide carbohydrates and dietary fiber for a balanced combination. The process is simple and clear, but you need to pay attention to knife skills and heat, so it suits beginners with some basics. It takes about 1 hour from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 585 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Lean pork
- Potatoes
- Garlic sprouts
- Starch (corn starch or other starch)
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Salt

## Calculation

Version for 1 person

- Lean pork 200g
- Potatoes 200g
- Garlic sprouts 2 stalks (about 20 g)
- Starch 5g
- Light soy sauce 10g
- Dark soy sauce 3g
- Salt 2g

## Steps

1. Peel the potatoes, cut in half, then into slices about 2mm thick. Set aside
2. Wash the garlic sprouts and cut into segments about 1cm long. Set aside
3. Wash the lean pork and cut into slices about 2mm thick. Put in a bowl, add 5g of starch, 5g of light soy sauce and 3g of dark soy sauce, and marinate for ten minutes. Set aside
4. While the pork marinates, bring a pot of water to a boil and blanch the potato slices for about 5 minutes
5. Heat the wok and add 10ml - 15ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
6. Add the pork and stir-fry until it changes color, then add the garlic sprouts and stir-fry together for about 20 seconds
7. Add the potatoes, keep stir-frying, and add 2g of salt and 5g of light soy sauce
8. Stir-fry for about 3 minutes, then plate

## Additional Notes

- Don't blanch the potato slices too long, or they'll get too soft
- Mix the pork well when marinating
