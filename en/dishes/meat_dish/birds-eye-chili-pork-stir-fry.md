# How to Make Bird's Eye Chili Pork Stir-fry

Bird's eye chili pork stir-fry is spicy and satisfying, a typical southwestern Chinese dish that goes great with rice: the tenderness of the pork paired with the sharp heat of bird's eye chilies really stimulates the appetite. Pork is rich in protein and fat, and chilies provide plenty of vitamin C and boost the appetite. You need to master frying the aromatics and the heat, but the steps are clear and beginners can pick it up easily. It takes about 25 minutes. Note: this dish is very spicy, not recommended for people who prefer mild food.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 620 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Bird's eye chili
* Peanut oil
* Pork belly / lean pork
* Salt
* Light soy sauce
* Oyster sauce
* Garlic
* Ginger
* Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
* Chicken bouillon powder
* White sugar

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Bird's eye chilies 20, adjust to taste
- Peanut oil 20ml
- Pork belly / lean pork 200g
- Salt 1-2g
- Light soy sauce 10ml
- Oyster sauce 10ml
- Ginger and garlic 50g
- Doubanjiang 10g, adjust to taste
- Chicken bouillon powder 10g
- White sugar 5g

## Steps

1. Wash the bird's eye chilies and cut them on the diagonal into fairly large pieces
2. Cut the pork into strips or slices as you prefer, add the seasonings (light soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt) and marinate for 5 minutes
3. Heat the wok, add oil, stir-fry the pork first and take it out
4. Fry the ginger and garlic until fragrant, add the doubanjiang and stir-fry, add the cut chilies, then add the pork back, stir-fry briefly, and add a little light soy sauce, chicken bouillon, salt and sugar and stir-fry

## Additional Notes

**Adjust the seasonings to your liking. You can also add dark soy sauce; it makes it more fragrant!**
