# How to Make Lu Braised Meats

This is classic Chinese lu wei (master-stock braised food): the meat is tender and well-seasoned with a rich aroma, and the braising liquid can be reused again and again, getting more fragrant with age. It's great as a cold dish, with drinks, or with a staple. It's rich in high-quality protein and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. It takes a little patience, but the steps are clear and fairly beginner-friendly. Soaking out the blood, making the caramel color and pressure-cooking take about 1 hour; then let it soak overnight so the flavor fully penetrates, and slice it the next day to enjoy.

## Ingredients and Tools

- Lu spice packet (available at supermarkets)
- Soybean paste
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Oyster sauce
- Red fermented bean curd (nanru)
- Onion
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Dried chili peppers
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Salt
- White sugar (yellow rock sugar is best, for the caramel color)
- Beer
- Beef shank (or other meat)
- Pressure cooker
- Strainer

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion serves about 2-3 people.

Per portion:

- Lu spice packet 1 (about 10g)
- Soybean paste 15ml
- Doubanjiang 15ml
- Oyster sauce 15ml
- Red fermented bean curd 15ml
- Onion half (about 100g)
- Ginger 30g
- Garlic 40g
- Dried chili peppers 10g
- Light soy sauce 120ml
- Dark soy sauce 60ml
- Salt 10-15g
- White sugar 30g (for the caramel color)
- Beer 1 can (330ml)
- Beef shank 500g
- Water, enough (to cover all the meat)

## Steps

It's recommended to read the [caramel color](../condiment/simple-caramel-color.md) tutorial first

1. Soak the beef shank in cold water for at least 3 hours in advance to draw out the blood

### Preparing the Braising Liquid

1. Make the caramel color: add 30g of white sugar to the pot, heat over low heat until the sugar completely melts and turns tan brown, add 150ml of hot water, stir well and set aside
2. Cut the onion into chunks, smash the ginger and garlic, and break the dried chilies
3. Add plenty of water to the pot, add the lu spice packet, onion, ginger, garlic and dried chilies, and bring to a boil over high heat
4. Add 15ml each of soybean paste, doubanjiang, oyster sauce and red fermented bean curd, and stir well
5. Pour in the prepared caramel color and mix well
6. Add 120ml of light soy sauce and 60ml of dark soy sauce and stir well
7. Season with 10-15g of salt
8. Pour in 1 can of beer (330ml) and bring to a boil again

### Braising the Beef

1. Blanch the beef shank in a pot for 2-3 minutes, then remove it and rinse the foam off the surface with hot water
2. Put the blanched beef shank into the boiling braising liquid, making sure the liquid covers all the meat
3. Close the pressure cooker lid, and once it reaches pressure, cook for another 25-30 minutes
4. When done, don't open the lid; let it cool naturally and soak overnight (this makes it more flavorful)
5. Take out the braised meat and refrigerate it so it firms up
6. Slice before eating; eat as is or dress it as a cold dish

### Keeping the Braising Liquid

1. To reuse the braising liquid: after each batch, strain the liquid to remove all solids, bring it back to a boil to sterilize, cool, and refrigerate or freeze. When using it again, add seasonings again in the original proportions. (Add water depending on how much liquid is left)
2. Stored properly, braising liquid can be used for a very long time, and gets more fragrant with age.
3. **Important**: if braising vegetables, you must take a separate portion of the liquid for them, and liquid used for vegetables must not be reused.

### Cold Dish Dressing

1. Mix minced garlic, chopped green onion, white sesame seeds and chili powder in a 1:1:1:1 ratio, adding bird's eye chili to taste. Add a little sesame oil or green Sichuan pepper oil to hot vegetable oil and pour it over the seasoning in several additions, then add 10ml each of light soy sauce, vinegar and oyster sauce, 5ml of sugar, MSG/chicken bouillon, and finally 15ml of braising liquid, and mix well.
2. For the cold dish, you can add sides such as smashed cucumber, wood ear mushrooms, fried peanuts and cilantro.

## Additional Notes

- You can take some of the braising liquid to make beef noodle soup: use it as the broth for plain boiled noodles and add your favorite vegetables. It tastes amazing.
