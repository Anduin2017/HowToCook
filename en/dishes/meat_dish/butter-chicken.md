# How to Make Butter Chicken

This is a classic curry dish from India: boneless chicken thighs are first roasted and then simmered in a sauce of tomatoes, butter and cream, giving a rich, silky, sweet-tangy, mildly spicy dish with a distinctive blend of spices. Chicken, yogurt and cashews provide plenty of protein and healthy fats, and tomatoes and spices add some vitamins and antioxidants. It's somewhat challenging, requiring control of heat and balance of spices, and including marinating it takes about 1 hour 45 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 641 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken thighs (boneless)
- Yogurt
- Butter
- Heavy cream
- Tomatoes
- Onion
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Cashews (for thickening)
- Turmeric powder
- Red chili powder (Kashmiri red chili powder is best: deep red but not very hot)
- Garam masala
- Ground coriander
- Ground cumin
- Cardamom powder
- Dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) — optional but strongly recommended
- Sugar
- Salt
- Cooking oil
- Wok
- Blender / food processor

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 3-4 people.

Per portion:

**For marinating the chicken:**

- Chicken thighs 500g (boneless, cut into large pieces)
- Yogurt 80ml
- Ginger 10g, grated
- Garlic 4 cloves (about 12g), grated
- Red chili powder 5g
- Turmeric powder 3g
- Salt 3g
- Lemon juice 15ml
- Cooking oil 15ml

**For the sauce:**

- Tomatoes 5 (about 500g), cut into chunks
- Onion 1 (about 100g), cut into chunks
- Cashews 30g
- Ginger 10g
- Garlic 4 cloves
- Butter 50g
- Heavy cream 100ml
- Red chili powder 5g (Kashmiri is best)
- Ground coriander 5g
- Ground cumin 3g
- Garam masala 3g
- Cardamom powder 2g
- Dried fenugreek leaves 3g (crushed by hand)
- Sugar 10g
- Salt 5g
- Water 100ml

## Steps

### Marinating the Chicken (1 Hour Ahead)

1. In a large bowl, mix the yogurt, grated ginger, grated garlic, red chili powder, turmeric, salt and lemon juice
2. Add the chicken pieces and coat thoroughly in the marinade
3. Cover with plastic wrap and marinate in the refrigerator for **at least 1 hour**

### Searing the Chicken

1. Pour 15ml of cooking oil into a frying pan and heat over high heat
2. Put the marinated chicken pieces in the pan (don't pour in the marinade)
3. Sear over high heat until *golden brown on both sides* (about 3 minutes per side); the chicken doesn't need to be fully cooked
4. Take out and set aside

### Making the Sauce Base

1. In a pot, put the tomato chunks, onion chunks, cashews, ginger, garlic and 100ml of water
2. Bring to a boil over high heat, then turn to medium and **cook for 15 minutes**, until *everything is completely soft*
3. Let cool slightly, then blend into a *smooth, silky purée*
4. Strain through a sieve to remove the solids, giving a smooth sauce. Set aside

### Simmering

1. Put 50g of butter in the wok and melt over medium heat
2. Pour in the strained tomato sauce
3. Add 5g of red chili powder, 5g of ground coriander, 3g of ground cumin and 5g of salt
4. Stir-fry for **3-5 minutes**, until *the sauce darkens and oil rises to the surface*
5. Add the seared chicken and mix evenly
6. Add 10g of sugar (to balance the tomatoes' acidity) and stir
7. Pour in 100ml of heavy cream and stir well
8. Simmer over low heat for **10-12 minutes**, until the chicken is fully cooked and has absorbed the sauce
9. Add 3g of garam masala and 2g of cardamom powder
10. Sprinkle in 3g of dried fenugreek leaves (crush them by hand as you add them)
11. Stir well and turn off the heat
12. Plate, and drizzle with a little extra cream as garnish if you like

## Additional Notes

- Dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) are the key ingredient for this dish's unique flavor, giving it a special herbal aroma. You can buy them at Indian grocery stores or online.
- Kashmiri red chili powder gives a brighter red color with less heat. If you can't find it, use half the amount of regular red chili powder plus 3g of sweet paprika.
- Cashews make the sauce thicker and silkier. If you're allergic to nuts, replace them with the same amount of heavy cream.
- This dish is usually eaten with naan or basmati rice.
- Butter chicken is considered one of India's greatest contributions to world cuisine, and it's near the top of almost every Indian restaurant menu around the world.
