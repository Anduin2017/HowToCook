# How to Make Ginger Scallion Shredded Chicken

Ginger scallion chicken (jiang cong lao ji) is a classic Cantonese home-style dish: the chicken is tender and juicy, with a strong aroma of scallion and ginger, savory and well-balanced, perfect with rice. Chicken thigh provides high-quality protein, and ginger and scallion warm the body and stimulate the appetite. It mainly involves steaming and shredding by hand, with clear steps, so it's fairly easy for beginners. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 549 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken legs
- Salt-baked chicken seasoning powder
- Green onion, ginger

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Chicken legs 4, about 400g
- Salt-baked chicken seasoning powder 5g
- Ginger 50g
- Green onion 1 stalk
- Salt 5g
- Sugar 5g
- Oil 35ml

## Steps

1. Rinse the four chicken legs and put them in a bowl
2. Add the salt-baked chicken seasoning powder and 5ml of oil to the bowl and mix well
3. Let the chicken marinate for 15 minutes, meanwhile preparing the steamer and bringing the water to a boil
4. After marinating, put the chicken legs in the steamer once the water is boiling and steam for 20 minutes
5. Cut the ginger to your taste into 1) grated ginger, 2) shreds, or 3) small dice
6. Cut the green onion into 0.5cm pieces
7. Put the green onion and ginger in a dipping bowl and add the salt and sugar
8. Heat the remaining oil in another pan to 6/10 to 7/10 heat
9. Pour the hot oil into the bowl of green onion and ginger
10. Once the chicken legs are steamed, tear them into shreds; they don't need to be very fine, about 1cm thick is fine
11. Pour the ginger-scallion oil over the shredded chicken and mix well
