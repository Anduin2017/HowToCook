## Steps

<!-- Describe the entire cooking process in detail here. -->
<!-- Imprecise words are not allowed, such as `some`, `a little`, `a moderate amount`, `as appropriate`. -->
<!-- If a step uses only part of an ingredient rather than all of it, say so. Otherwise it means the whole ingredient. For example, "potatoes" here means "all of the prepared potatoes". -->

1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into large chunks no bigger than 4cm. Set aside
2. Chop the curry roux to increase its surface area so it dissolves faster. Set aside
3. Heat the wok and add 10ml - 15ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
4. Add the potatoes and keep stir-frying until they *soften* (check with chopsticks)<!-- Do not introduce ingredients that were not listed above when describing the process. -->
5. Add water to cover all the ingredients. Once boiling, turn the heat down and **wait 15 - 20 minutes** <!-- For ingredients whose amount can be adjusted to taste, give a suggested range -->
6. Turn off the heat, add the curry and stir until the curry melts <!-- Every waiting step must give a `formula for the wait time` or a `criterion for when the step is done` -->
7. Turn the heat back on and **stir slowly for 10 minutes** so it doesn't burn on the bottom
8. Once it *looks thick*, turn off the heat and plate
