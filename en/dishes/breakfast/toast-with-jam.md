# How to Make Toast with Jam

This is an ultra-minimal quick breakfast: lightly crisp toast sandwiching sweet jam, crispy outside and soft inside, easy to take on the go. It's a typical convenient Western-style light meal. It mainly provides carbohydrates and sugar for a quick morning energy boost. There's practically no barrier to entry, perfect for busy beginners who aren't good at cooking. It takes only about 2 minutes from start to finish, needs no stove, and leaves almost nothing to wash.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 223 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh sliced bread
- Jam
- Toaster

## Calculation

- 2 slices of bread
- Enough jam to cover one side of one slice

## Steps

1. Put the bread in the toaster
2. Set the level; it will pop up automatically when time is up
3. After two minutes, the toast is done and pops up
4. Take out one slice, spread it with jam, and cover it with the other slice
5. Wrap it in a napkin and eat it on the go, or eat it before you head out

Done in two minutes, very simple and very tasty, perfect for programmers. It's quick, creates no extra waste, and doesn't require washing any tools.

## Additional Notes

A toaster generally costs under 100 yuan. Buy sliced bread at the supermarket downstairs or have it delivered via a grocery app; a pack of eight slices is usually about 10 yuan. It has a short shelf life and is very clean and hygienic.
