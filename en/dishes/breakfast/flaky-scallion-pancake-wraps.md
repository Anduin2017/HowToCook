# How to Make Flaky Scallion Pancake Wraps

Shouzhuabing (手抓饼, "hand-grabbed pancake") is crispy outside, soft inside and distinctly layered, a much-loved home-style flour-based dish. The dough is made with partly scalded flour, making it supple and chewy, and paired with egg and vegetables it's a balanced meal. It's very beginner-friendly, taking only about 40 minutes from mixing the dough to serving, perfect for busy mornings.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 1050 kcal

---

## Ingredients and Tools

- All-purpose flour  
- Boiling water  
- Cold water  
- Cooking oil  
- Salt  
- Egg  
- Lettuce  
- Ham  
- Cheese slice

---

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1~2 people.

Total (per portion):

- Flour 200 grams  
- Boiling water 100 ml  
- Cold water 50 ml  
- Cooking oil 15 ml  
- Salt 3 grams  
- Egg 1  
- Lettuce 30 grams  
- Ham 30 grams  
- Cheese slice 1

---

## Steps

1. Put the flour in a bowl, add the boiling water and stir until shaggy, then add the cold water and knead into a smooth dough. Cover with a damp cloth and let rest for 20 minutes.  
2. Divide the dough into pieces of about 100 grams each, roll into balls, then roll out into thin sheets.  
3. Brush the surface evenly with cooking oil, sprinkle with salt, roll up into a snail shape, and let rest for 10 minutes.  
4. Roll the dough out again into a thin pancake of even thickness.  
5. Pour oil into a hot pan and fry over low heat until both sides are golden and puffed.  
6. Top the cooked pancake with fried egg, lettuce, ham, cheese slice and other fillings in turn, then roll it up.

---

## Additional Notes

- Mixing the dough with both boiling and cold water helps make the pancake more supple.  
- Rest the dough for at least 20 minutes, or it will be hard to roll thin.  
- Adjust the fillings to taste; keep the total weight of fillings under 100 grams.  
- Leftover uncooked pancake dough keeps in the refrigerator for 24 hours; let it come to room temperature and roll it out again before using.
