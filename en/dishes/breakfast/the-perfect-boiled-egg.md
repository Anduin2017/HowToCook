# How to Make the Perfect Boiled Egg

![Perfect boiled egg](https://img-s-msn-com.akamaized.net/tenant/amp/entityid/AA1yBdnK.img?w=768&h=512&m=6)

This egg is made with a periodic boiling method developed by scientists: the yolk is creamy and smooth, the white sets evenly, and the texture has distinct layers. It retains more nutrients, with 23% more polyphenols than traditional boiling, and is rich in protein. It is fairly demanding, requiring precise temperature control and repeated transfers. It takes about 35 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 86 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh eggs (AA grade recommended)
- A pot of 100°C boiling water (diameter ≥ 15cm)
- A pot of 30°C warm water (diameter ≥ 15cm)
- Timer
- Slotted spoon

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Egg 1 (about 60g)
- 100°C boiling water 1500ml
- 30°C warm water 1500ml

## Steps

1. Prepare two pots of water: keep pot A at 100°C boiling, and pot B at 30°C warm
2. Use the slotted spoon to put the egg into pot A and start the timer
3. Transfer the egg to the other pot precisely **every 2 minutes**
4. Repeat the transfer 16 times in total (32 minutes total)
5. After the last transfer, let it sit in pot B for 30 seconds
6. Immediately put it into ice water (0 degrees Celsius) to stop the cooking (for 30 seconds)
7. When peeling, start from the air cell at the blunt end and peel the membrane along the long axis

## Additional Notes

- Key parameters:
  - Yolk center temperature: 67±1°C
  - Egg white layer temperatures:
    - Outer layer: 100°C→87°C
    - Middle layer: 87°C→55°C
    - Inner layer: 55°C→30°C
- Nutritional advantage: 23% more polyphenols than traditional boiling
