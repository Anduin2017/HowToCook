# How to Make Scotch Eggs

![Simplified Scotch eggs](../../../dishes/breakfast/苏格兰蛋/egg1.png)

This simplified Scotch egg is crispy on the outside with a soft, jammy yolk inside, combining the creaminess of cheese with the smoky flavor of bacon. It's a creative snack based on a British recipe. The egg provides high-quality protein, the cheese adds calcium, and the scallion pancake wrapper makes it filling enough for a meal. It's not complicated; beginners can handle it easily, and it takes only about 20 to 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 599 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Frozen flaky pancake sheets (shouzhuabing)
- Cheese
- Bacon
- Air fryer or a pot of oil

## Calculation

> One portion

- Egg 50g (about 1)
- Flaky pancake sheets 1-2 (depending on egg size)
- Cheese slices 1-2
- Bacon slices 1-2

## Steps

1. Put the eggs in cold water, bring to a boil, cook for 3 minutes, then remove
2. Put the eggs in ice water; this makes peeling faster and cleaner
3. Wrap each egg in a cheese slice
4. Wrap each egg in a bacon slice
5. Cut off both ends of the pancake sheet and wrap the egg in the remaining rectangle
6. Fry in oil at 6/10 heat (the oil surface shimmers, there's light smoke, and bubbles form around a chopstick dipped into it) until golden
7. Or air fry at 160 degrees for 15 minutes
8. Cut open and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- Cheese slices, bacon and flaky pancake sheets can all be bought online (e.g. Taobao)
