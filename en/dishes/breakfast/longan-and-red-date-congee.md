# How to Make Longan and Red Date Congee

A sweet, fragrant and soft traditional Chinese congee. Glutinous rice is cooked together with red dates (jujubes) and longan, which bring their own natural sweetness, warming the body and stomach. In traditional Chinese food therapy it is said to nourish the blood, calm the mind, support brain health and nourish the heart and spleen. It's very easy; beginners can master it, and it takes about 70 minutes from prep until the house smells of congee.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 617 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Glutinous rice (or regular rice)
- Red dates (jujubes)
- Longan

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Glutinous rice 100g
- Red dates 15
- Longan 15

## Steps

1. Peel the longan and take out the flesh, rinse it twice with water, and soak in a bowl for 10 minutes
2. Rinse the red dates twice with water and soak in a bowl for 10 minutes
3. Put the glutinous rice in the rice cooker, rinse it twice with water, then add 2000ml of water
4. Add the longan and red dates to the rice cooker
5. Start the rice cooker on its rice cooking mode; the congee is ready in 1 hour

## Additional Notes

If you prefer your congee thinner or thicker, increase or decrease the amount of water. The congee comes out sweet; you can control the sweetness through the number of longans you add.
