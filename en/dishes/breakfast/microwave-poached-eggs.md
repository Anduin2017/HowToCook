# How to Make Microwave Poached Eggs

Microwave poached eggs are a convenient home-style quick dish: the whites are tender and smooth, the yolks soft, with the light aroma of sesame oil. Eggs are rich in high-quality protein and lecithin. It's extremely simple; beginners can master it easily, and it takes only about 2 minutes from start to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 169 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Sesame oil
- Salt

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just right as a side for 1 person's breakfast.

Per portion:

- Eggs 2
- Drinking water 35ml
- Sesame oil 3ml
- Salt 0.8g

## Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a small bowl and poke 2 holes in every yolk with chopsticks, so they don't make a mess of the microwave when heated
2. Then pour room-temperature drinking water into the bowl
3. Then add the salt
4. Finally add the sesame oil
5. Put the bowl in the microwave and heat on high for 80 seconds
6. When the time is up, take it out using a cloth to protect your hand

## Additional Notes

- To further prevent the yolks and whites from spattering, cover the bowl with a lid before microwaving so the microwave doesn't get dirty
