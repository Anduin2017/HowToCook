# How to Make Steamed Egg Custard

This home-style dish is known for its pudding-smooth texture and delicate, fresh flavor. It's popular in both northern and southern China; in the north it's affectionately called "鸡蛋糕儿" (little egg cake). Eggs are rich in high-quality protein and lecithin, a convenient way to get daily nutrition. You only need to master the egg-to-water ratio and the trick of steaming slowly over low heat. It's very beginner-friendly and usually takes about 15 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 143 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh eggs
- Hot water
- Aluminum foil or plastic wrap

## Calculation

- Eggs 2
- Salt 2g
- Hot water 260ml

## Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them
2. In another container, pour warm water (20~30 degrees) equal to 1.5 times the volume of the egg (half an eggshell of water counts as 0.5 times), and dissolve the salt in the water
3. Pour the salt water into the beaten egg and stir in one direction only (clockwise or counterclockwise) until evenly mixed. Skim off and discard any bubbles; straining gives an even better texture.
4. Wrap the bowl of egg mixture with aluminum foil (or cover it with a plate) and place it in a pot with a lid and about 3cm of water in it
5. Bring the water to a boil over medium heat, then turn to the lowest heat and steam for another 4 minutes

## Additional Notes

- It can usually be eaten after cooling slightly, for the ultimate texture that preserves the eggs' natural aroma
- You can also add seasoned soy sauce for fish, chopped green onion and sesame oil as toppings.
