# How to Make American Scrambled Eggs

This is a fluffy, tender American-style home egg dish. Adding milk makes the curds fine and the texture creamy, and it's rich in high-quality protein and calcium. It's simple and beginner-friendly, taking only about 20 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 258 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Whole milk / cream
- Butter
- Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Eggs 3
- Whole milk / cream 10g
- Butter 5 grams
- Salt 1 gram

## Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a large bowl, add the salt and whisk until frothy, then let rest for 15 minutes
2. Cut the butter into small pieces and put it in the pan, pour in the eggs, turn on low heat and stir constantly
3. As soon as the butter melts, quickly stir the eggs, breaking them into fine curds. Turn off the heat before the eggs are mostly set
4. Add the milk and stir for 15 seconds until the eggs are moist and creamy, then plate

## Additional Notes

- For something more substantial, in the last step you can add sautéed diced tomato, diced onion, diced bacon, small cubes of cheese and so on (anything that won't release more water).
- Use a frying pan (non-stick).
- Since many people cooking for one only scramble one or two eggs, a smaller pan may make it easier to stir evenly.
