# How to Make Oat and Egg Pancake

Oat and egg pancake is a quick, nutritious breakfast with a soft, tender texture, the grainy aroma of oats and the richness of egg and milk, in a simple home style. It's rich in protein, carbohydrates and dietary fiber, providing plenty of energy, and is especially good for fitness enthusiasts and weekday mornings. It's simple and beginner-friendly, usually taking just over ten minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 535 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Oats
- Milk 50-100g, just enough to stir the oats into a thick mixture
- Optionally add 50g of vegetables to taste, such as spinach.

## Calculation

- Two eggs, or two egg whites and one yolk.
- Plain dry rolled oats 50g (about the same amount as one egg)
- Milk, one carton, about 250ml
- A handful of chopped leafy vegetables

## Steps

1. Mix the milk and dry oats until evenly combined and thick.
2. Beat the eggs until uniform in color.
3. Pour the beaten eggs into the oat-milk mixture and keep stirring until thick and even.
4. Coat the frying pan evenly with a layer of butter.
5. Add the mixture and spread it into a pancake.
6. Cook over low heat for two to three minutes. If you want to add vegetables, add the chopped leaves during cooking.
7. Flip and cook for another two minutes.
8. Serve, with the remaining milk as part of breakfast.

## Additional Notes

- If you prefer it savory, add salt, pepper and other seasonings when beating the eggs.
- Use low heat when frying to keep the oats from burning.
