# How to Make Tuna Mayo Sandwich

This Western-style breakfast sandwich mixes water-packed tuna with mayonnaise and Russian pickle brine: savory and slightly tangy, creamy in texture, and especially filling with crispy toasted bread. It's rich in high-quality protein and nutrients such as Omega-3, for a quick energy boost. It's very simple: just mix the filling and heat it in a sandwich maker. Beginners can easily get it done in 5 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 569 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Canned tuna in water (tuna in oil isn't recommended; it's too greasy)
- Square sandwich bread slices
- Mayonnaise
- Russian pickle brine
- Cheese slice (optional)
- Ham slice (optional)
- Sandwich maker

## Calculation

- Tuna in water 65g
- Square bread slices 2
- Mayonnaise 50 mL
- Russian pickle brine 10-15mL (adjust to taste)

## Steps

1. Put the tuna, mayonnaise and Russian pickle brine in a bowl and stir with a spoon, breaking up the tuna chunks until the whole mixture is a paste. Set aside
2. Place 1 slice of bread in the sandwich maker
3. Spread the tuna mixture on the bread, about 10-15ml recommended
4. Cover with the other slice of bread, press down the sandwich maker, and turn it on
5. When the sandwich maker stops heating automatically, plate and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- The tuna mixture can be made the night before and kept in the refrigerator
- Don't keep the tuna mixture refrigerated for more than a week, and be sure to cover it with plastic wrap
- Don't plug in the sandwich maker before pressing it down, to avoid safety issues
