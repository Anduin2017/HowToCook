# How to Make Air Fryer Toast

Air fryer toast is an ultra-minimal, quick staple: crispy outside, soft inside, with a pure wheaty aroma, especially good when you're trying to lose fat. It's rich in dietary fiber and plant protein and keeps you full for a long time. There's no barrier to entry; just put the bread slices in the air fryer. It's extremely beginner-friendly and takes only about five minutes from start to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 254 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Sliced bread
- Air fryer

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Sliced bread (two slices)

## Steps

1. Take two slices of bread (whole grain bread recommended)
2. Place the slices **upright** in the air fryer
3. Bake at 200°C for 5 minutes
4. Take them out and enjoy
