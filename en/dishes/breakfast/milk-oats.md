# How to Make Milk Oats

A convenient, nutritious high-protein, high-fiber breakfast that cleverly combines the smoothness of milk, the grainy aroma of oats and a tender fried egg. It's warm, mild, easy to digest and filling. The process is simple and direct, extremely beginner-friendly, and takes about three minutes in total. Pair it with fruit and vegetables for extra freshness.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 421 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Milk (pasteurized milk tastes better)
- Oats
- Eggs

## Calculation

- 🥛 Milk 280ml/per
- 🍳 Egg 1/per
- 🍚 Oats 40g/per

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Standard Method for Oats

1. Pour the milk into a breakfast mug (cold is fine)
2. Prepare 200ml of water. If it's drinkable straight from the tap/dispenser, add the oats directly; otherwise boil it first, then add the oats
3. 2 minutes after the water boils, the oats are done
4. Scoop out the cooked oats and add them to the milk (try not to pour the oat cooking water into the milk; it affects the taste)

### Quick Method for Oats

1. Use quick-cooking oats instead of regular oats
2. Pour the milk into a container with the quick oats and stir
3. Put the mixture in the microwave
4. Microwave on medium power for 4 minutes

### Fried Egg Method

1. Heat the pan, add a thin layer of oil, and when the oil is hot, fry the egg, 20s per side. Consider seasoning it (3g of pepper salt, optional)
2. Turn off the heat and plate

Cooking takes about 3 minutes. Everything ends up at room temperature, so it's very easy to eat, usually finished in 2 minutes.

> 🥑 Even better with fruit, vegetables or soda crackers

## Additional Notes

- Microwave power varies between models, so the power level can't be stated precisely
- Don't fill the container more than 50% with the mixture, or it is very likely to boil over during heating
- Cooking in a glass is not recommended, for the same reason
