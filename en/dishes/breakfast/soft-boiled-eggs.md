# How to Make Soft-Boiled Eggs

This is a home-style egg dish that's easy to make with an ordinary electric pot. The yolk is runny, and the texture is tender, smooth and rich. Each egg provides about 6 grams of high-quality protein, which is especially good for people who work out. The process is simple and clear; as long as you get the heating and resting times right, beginners can pick it up quickly. It takes only about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 72 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Electric pot
- Water
- Stopwatch (optional)

## Calculation

- 1 or more eggs (as many as your electric pot can hold)
- Cold water to cover the eggs by about 2 cm

## Steps

1. Put the eggs in the electric pot. The eggs must not be stacked; they should all sit on the bottom with room to move
2. Pour in cold water to cover the eggs by about 2 cm
3. With the lid off, heat at maximum power until the water starts bubbling (about 85 - 95 degrees, a gentle simmer, not a full boil)
4. Turn off the heat, cover, and let the eggs sit.

  - For a runny yolk in the center, let sit for 6 minutes
  - For a fully cooked, crumbly yolk, let sit for 10 minutes

5. Drain, rinse the eggs under cold water for about 1 minute, then peel and eat.

## Additional Notes

If you find the eggs not cooked enough, add more water according to your preference.

**Warning** Soft-boiled eggs carry a risk of salmonella infection. Letting them sit for less than 5 minutes is not recommended.
