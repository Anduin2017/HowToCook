# How to Make Microwave Sunny-Side Up Egg

A sunny-side up egg made quickly in the microwave is a home-style breakfast. It is smooth and tender, with set whites and a slightly runny yolk, a typical quick dish. Eggs are rich in high-quality protein and various vitamins and are easy to digest. It's very simple and beginner-friendly. With a microwave that has power levels it takes just 3 minutes; with one that doesn't, it takes only a few minutes of repeated heating.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 113 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Salt
- Oil
- A microwave with adjustable power levels, or one without (see Additional Notes for definitions and how to tell)
- Chopsticks or toothpicks

## Calculation

- Use 1 egg.
- Use 1 g of salt per egg.
- Use 5 mL of oil per egg.

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Microwave with Adjustable Power

1. Prepare a small bowl, pour in the oil calculated above, sprinkle in the salt and stir evenly. Tilt the bowl so the oil coats its surface.
2. Crack an egg into the small bowl.
3. Poke holes in the surface of the yolk: 5 with a toothpick or 1 with a chopstick.
4. Put it in the microwave on medium power for 3 minutes.

### Microwave without Adjustable Power

1. Prepare a small bowl, pour in the oil calculated above, sprinkle in the salt and stir evenly. Tilt the bowl so the oil coats its surface.
2. Crack an egg into the small bowl.
3. Poke holes in the surface of the yolk: 5 with a toothpick or 1 with a chopstick.
4. Put it in the microwave for 1 minute.
5. while (egg is NOT mostly solid) microwave(30s);

## Additional Notes

while (egg's doneness does NOT match your taste) microwave(1 minute);

- Microwave without adjustable power:
  - Definition: a microwave where you can't control the power, only the time.
  - How to tell: if you can't find words like low, medium or high on the microwave's control panel, it's a microwave without adjustable power
- Microwave with adjustable power:
  - Definition: a microwave where you can control both the power and the time.
  - How to tell: if you can find words like low, medium or high on the microwave's control panel, it's a microwave with adjustable power
