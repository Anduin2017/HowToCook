# How to Make Boiled Corn

Boiled corn is a quick home-style dish that's sweet and tender. Fresh corn is boiled with some of its husk on to keep its natural aroma, and a little salt and sugar enhance the flavor. It's rich in dietary fiber, vitamins and carbohydrates, and keeps you full. It's extremely simple; beginners can handle it easily, and it takes only about 15 minutes from prep to table.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 108 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh corn
- A pot big enough for the corn
- Water
- Salt
- Sugar (optional)

## Calculation

- One ear of corn with husk
- Water to cover the corn by about half a finger joint
- When boiling the corn, start with lightly salted water, about 2 grams of salt per 50ml of water
- Add sugar or not, to taste (optional)

## Steps

1. Peel off the outer husk of the fresh corn, leaving some of the husk on, and put it in the pot
2. Add water to cover the corn by about half a finger joint, then add the salt and sugar
3. Once the water boils, turn to low heat, cover and keep cooking for 15-20 minutes; cooking the corn a little longer won't hurt.
4. When cooked, drain and let it cool a bit before eating.
