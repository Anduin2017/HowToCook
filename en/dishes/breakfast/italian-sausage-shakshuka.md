# How to Make Italian Sausage Shakshuka

Italian sausage shakshuka is a rich, savory Mediterranean-style dish: soft-yolked eggs nestled in a sweet and tangy tomato meat sauce, with crusty bread for dipping. The main ingredients provide high-quality protein, while tomatoes, bell peppers and onion add vitamins and dietary fiber. It isn't hard; you just need to watch the heat while the eggs cook. It's on the table in about 30 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 668 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Italian pork sausage (casings removed), chorizo, or diced luncheon meat
- Barilla Napoletana pasta sauce
- Eggs
- Bell pepper (green or red)
- Onion
- Olive oil
- Parmesan cheese (optional)
- Fresh parsley (optional)
- Red pepper flakes (optional)
- Bread (sourdough, ciabatta or thick-cut toast recommended)
- Wide frying pan (with lid)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Meat (sausage/luncheon meat): 100g - 150g
- Barilla Napoletana pasta sauce: 1 jar (400g, provides the basic tomato, onion and herb flavor)
- Eggs: 3 - 4
- Bell pepper: half (thinly sliced)
- Onion: half (finely diced)
- Olive oil: 10ml - 15ml (for sautéing)

## Steps

1. Remove the casings from the Italian sausage and set aside; finely dice the onion; thinly slice the bell pepper.
2. Pour 10ml - 15ml of olive oil into the wide frying pan and heat over medium heat.
3. Add the prepared sausage or luncheon meat and fry until the edges are golden and crispy (if using raw sausage, break it up with a spoon as it browns).
4. Add the diced onion and sliced bell pepper and stir-fry with the meat for 4 - 5 minutes, until the vegetables soften and the onion is translucent.
5. Pour in the Barilla Napoletana sauce and stir well. Turn the heat to medium-low and keep at a gentle simmer for 2 - 3 minutes. (If the sauce looks too thick, add 15ml of water.)
6. Use the back of a spoon to make 3 - 4 small "wells" in the sauce.
7. Carefully crack an egg into each well.
8. Cover the pan and let the steam cook the egg whites. Wait 3 - 5 minutes. **Note: check frequently**. This step is done when the whites are opaque and set but the yolks still wobble when you gently shake the pan (soft-yolked).
9. Turn off the heat. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan, fresh parsley or red pepper flakes to taste.
10. Bring the pan straight to the table and serve with crusty bread for dipping into the yolks and sauce.

## Additional Notes

- **Choosing the meat:**
  - **Italian sausage**: gives the most authentic flavor (look for sausages with fennel seeds in the ingredients).
  - **Luncheon meat**: brings a salty, nostalgic flavor, and the surface fries up slightly crisp.
  - **Chorizo**: if you like a smoky, spicy flavor, this is the best choice, and it turns the oil an appealing red.
