# How to Make Egg Sandwich

This is a simple, quick Western-style light meal: bread sandwiching creamy egg salad and savory bacon, soft and tender with a toasty aroma. Eggs are rich in high-quality protein, and with bacon and butter it gives a quick energy boost. It's very easy; kitchen beginners can handle it, and it takes only about 10 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 560 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Egg
- Sandwich bread
- Bacon
- Butter
- Mayonnaise
- Salt
- Black pepper

## Calculation

- Egg 1
- Bread 2 slices
- Bacon 2 slices
- Butter 10 g
- Mayonnaise 20g
- Salt 1g
- Black pepper 2g

## Steps

1. Cut the crusts off the bread and set aside
2. Hard-boil the egg and mash it
3. Mix the egg, mayonnaise, salt and black pepper
4. Add the butter to the pan and fry the bacon until cooked
5. Assemble: put the egg salad and bacon between the two slices of bread
6. Cut the square sandwich into triangles and plate

## Additional Notes

- You can also add cheese, pickles and so on
