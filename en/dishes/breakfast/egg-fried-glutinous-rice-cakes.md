# How to Make Egg-Fried Glutinous Rice Cakes

A simple, quick snack: slightly crispy outside, soft and chewy inside, coated in egg and sweetness, a home-style flavor from southern China. The glutinous rice cakes (ciba) provide plenty of carbohydrates and the egg adds protein. It's fairly high in calories but very filling. It's extremely simple; beginners can handle it easily, and it takes only about ten minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 379 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Glutinous rice cakes (ciba)
- White sugar or brown sugar

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Glutinous rice cakes, two pieces
- Brown sugar 10g (8g - 15g recommended)
- Egg 1
- Cooking oil 10-15ml
- Salt 2g

## Steps

1. Cut the rice cakes into small rectangles so they're easier to fry
2. Crack an egg into a bowl, beat it, and add 2g of salt
3. Put the small rice cake pieces into the beaten egg one by one, until both sides are coated
4. Pour 10ml of vegetable oil into the pan, add the coated rice cake pieces, and fry slowly over low heat until soft.
5. Slowly pour the remaining beaten egg over the rice cakes
6. Flip the rice cakes with chopsticks or a spoon, and fry back and forth until golden, then dig in

## Additional Notes

- When adding the rice cakes, space them apart so they don't stick together
- The amount of egg depends on the amount of rice cake
- The amount of sugar is up to your taste
