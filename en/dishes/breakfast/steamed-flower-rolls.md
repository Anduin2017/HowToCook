# How to Make Steamed Flower Rolls

This is a traditional northern Chinese flour-based food, soft and tasty with a light wheaty aroma. Steaming frozen flower rolls (huajuan) is a quick way to get carbohydrates and dietary fiber. It's extremely simple; even cooking beginners can master it. It takes only half an hour in total, making it an ideal quick breakfast.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 400 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Frozen flower rolls
- Round plate
- Steaming rack
- Water 400ml

## Calculation

- Frozen flower rolls 5 (3 is enough for a smaller appetite) (available at supermarkets and on grocery apps)
- Round plate, 28cm in diameter
- Steaming rack, 20cm in diameter
- Water 400ml

## Steps

1. Take 5 flower rolls out of the package
2. Lay the rolls flat on the plate, trying not to overlap them
3. Pour 400ml of water into the pot, put the steaming rack in, place the plate of rolls on the rack, and cover with the lid.
4. Heat on high until the water boils.
5. Turn to medium heat and steam for 15 minutes
6. Remove the lid and feel the surface temperature of the rolls with your hand. If they aren't hot enough, cover and keep heating; otherwise turn off the heat and take them out.
7. Take out the plate and let cool to 50 degrees before eating

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, make sure the water in the pot doesn't drop below 50ml.
