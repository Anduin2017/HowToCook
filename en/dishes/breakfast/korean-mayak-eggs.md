# How to Make Korean Mayak Eggs

A rich, sweet and spicy Korean soft-boiled egg side dish (mayak gyeran, "drug eggs" for how addictive they are). The sauce coats the whites, the eggs are silky in the mouth, with a slightly spicy finish. Eggs provide high-quality protein and lecithin, nutritious and filling. It's extremely simple; beginners can make it easily. From boiling the eggs to chilling them until flavorful takes about an hour and a half.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 669 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Korean soy sauce (or light soy sauce)
- Japanese mirin
- White sugar
- Sesame oil
- Green onion
- Chili peppers (bird's eye chili or green/red chili)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. The amounts below are one batch of sauce, enough to marinate 5 - 8 eggs.

Per portion:

- Eggs 5 - 8
- Soy sauce (or light soy sauce) 4 tablespoons
- Water 4 tablespoons
- Mirin 2 tablespoons
- White sugar 2 tablespoons
- Sesame oil 5ml
- Green onion 15g (chopped)
- Chili peppers 15g (cut into rings)

## Steps

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add the eggs once it's boiling, and **boil for 7 minutes** (for perfectly soft-boiled eggs).
2. While the eggs boil, make the sauce: pour 4 tablespoons of soy sauce, 4 tablespoons of water, 2 tablespoons of mirin and 2 tablespoons of white sugar into a small pot.
3. Heat the sauce to a boil to cook off the alcohol in the mirin, then turn off the heat and let it cool.
4. Add the chili rings to the cooled sauce.
5. Take out the boiled eggs, cool them in ice water, then peel them.
6. Put the peeled eggs in the sauce, drizzle 5ml of sesame oil over the top, and sprinkle with chopped green onion.
7. Put in the refrigerator and **chill for 1 - 2 hours** so the eggs fully absorb the flavor.

## Additional Notes

- You can fine-tune the sauce ratio to taste. If you like it sweeter, add more sugar.
- The leftover sauce is delicious and can be used directly over rice.
