# How to Make Microwave Mug Cake

A quick microwave cake that is soft, fluffy and sweet, a home dessert that even complete beginners can make easily. It's based on egg and flour, with butter adding energy and rich aroma and sugar giving a quick boost; adding nuts or fruit adds dietary fiber and vitamins. You only need a few ingredients and one minute on high in the microwave. From prep to tasting takes as little as 2 minutes; a beginner can get it done in about 20 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 308 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Microwave
- Microwave-safe container
- Butter
- Flour
- Baking powder (without it, it tastes like a pancake)
- Egg

## Calculation

- Egg 🥚 1
- Flour 🍚 15g
- Baking powder 🍚 2.5g
- White (brown) sugar 🍬 10g
- Salt 🧂 1g

(Optional flavors)

- Coffee powder ☕
- Chocolate 🍫
- Oats 🍿
- Milk 🥛
- Nuts 🥜
- Cookie crumbs 🍪
- Banana 🍌
- Anything that won't turn it into a culinary disaster 🍆

## Steps

1. Add the following ingredients, making sure not to fill the container more than 3/4 full

  - Scoop butter about the size of half an egg into the container and **heat in the microwave for 15 seconds** until melted
  - Melt/mash the **chocolate/banana** into **bits/a paste**
  - Crack in an egg, beat and mix
  - 15g of white (brown) sugar (add more if you have a sweet tooth) (see note 4)
  - Add 1g of salt (**use your judgment if the flavor ingredients you add later contain salt**)
  - Add 2.5g of baking powder
  - Add 15g of flour (gluten content doesn't matter much)
  - **Add any flavor ingredients you like!** (Dry ingredients like nuts and cookies don't go in yet)
  - Stir until it looks like thick yogurt with no dry flour visible
  - Put any optional dry ingredients (not including instant drink powders) on top

2. Give yourself a compliment 🥰
3. Microwave (high) for **1 minute** (until it puffs up into a fluffy cake)
4. Take out the mug (hot hot hot!!↑), post a photo on social media, and dig in

## Additional Notes

- You can repeat the steps before complimenting yourself several times to make a bigger cake
- Baking powder makes the cake fluffier. Most are aluminum-free and cost a few yuan a pack; buy one to keep at home
- In the **Add any flavor ingredients you like!** step, if you add **liquid** (such as milk), make sure you can still stir it into a **thick yogurt-like** consistency in the next step. Add it a little at a time to avoid too much moisture, which makes the cake soggy and flat
- **Don't fill the container more than 3/4 full!!**
