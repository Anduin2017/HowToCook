# How to Make Pan-Fried Dumplings

Pan-fried dumplings have golden, crispy bottoms and fresh, juicy fillings, a home-style flour-based dish common on northern Chinese tables. Made with frozen dumplings, they're rich in carbohydrates and protein, simple and quick. Estimated at two stars, very beginner-friendly, they can be on the table in about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 769 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Dumplings (frozen boiled dumplings)
- Sesame seeds, chopped green onion (cut into segments) or other toppings

## Calculation

Per portion:

- One bag of dumplings (according to your appetite, about 10 - 15)

## Steps

1. Take out a frying pan (non-stick is best)
2. Add 10ml - 15 ml of cooking oil
3. Turn on the heat and add the dumplings (spread them out as evenly as possible; don't stack them)
4. Immediately add water until it reaches half the average height of the dumplings
5. Cover with the lid (the stove should be on high heat at this point)
6. Wait 8 - 10 minutes
7. When only 2mm of water is left in the pan, turn to medium heat and start frying
8. Once all the water has evaporated, shake the pan so the dumplings heat evenly
9. Add black sesame seeds and green onion and cover for another 10s
10. After 1 - 2 minutes, lift out a dumpling and check the bottom. If it has a golden, crispy crust, remove them immediately

## Additional Notes

- While cooking, keep an eye on the pan the whole time. Never get distracted playing on your phone.
