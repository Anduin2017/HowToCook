# How to Make Egg Fried Rice

A home-style egg fried rice: golden egg coating separate, fluffy grains of rice, with ham, carrot and cucumber for varied textures, savory and well-seasoned. Eggs provide high-quality protein, carrots add vitamins, and carbohydrates bring plenty of energy. There are a few more steps, but beginners can succeed by following the process. It takes about 25 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 853 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Cold cooked rice
* Eggs
* Ham sausage
* Cucumber
* Carrot
* Oil
* Salt
* Ground pepper
* Light soy sauce
* Green onion
* Dengying shredded beef / luncheon meat / Chinese sausage / braised pork... or other cooked meat (optional)

For the rice, see [Rice Cooker Rice](./rice-cooker-rice.md).

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1-2 people.

Total:

* Cold cooked rice (portions*500ml)
* Eggs (portions*1.5, integer-divided by 1, i.e. rounded down)
* Ham sausage (portions*2)
* Cucumber (optional, portions*30g)
* Carrot (optional, portions*30g)
* Oil (portions*12ml)
* Salt (portions\*4g - portions*6g)
* Ground pepper (portions*8g)
* Green onion (portions*1 stalk)
* Light soy sauce (portions*10ml)

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. Break the rice into small pieces with a spatula in advance
2. Slice or dice the ham sausage, carrot, cucumber and so on as you like
3. If you have cooked meat at home, prepare it too for better flavor
4. Separate the whites and yolks into two large bowls and beat each. Note: don't add salt at this step.
5. Heat the wok on high until it smokes, add cooking oil, add the egg whites, and once mostly set, take them out and set aside.
6. If there's enough oil left, add the yolks directly; if not, add more oil and heat it on high again
7. Once mostly set, turn to medium-low heat, add the ham sausage, cooked meat, carrot, cucumber and other ingredients, and stir-fry for 10 seconds (until fragrant)
8. Add the egg whites back, stir-fry for 5s, then quickly add the rice and stir-fry on high heat, so every grain of rice gets coated in egg.
9. While stir-frying, break up the clumps of rice; this takes a while. Once all the rice is broken up, stir-fry evenly
10. Turn to low heat and add the salt, ground pepper and light soy sauce
11. Keep stir-frying evenly; when you see some grains of rice "jumping" in the wok, it's about done
12. Finally add the green onion and stir-fry for 10s
13. Turn off the heat and serve in bowls

## Additional Notes

* Technical summary
  - Day-old cold rice is best for fried rice. If you don't have any, you can cook fresh rice (you can cool it right away in the refrigerator), but you'll need to stir-fry longer to get the "separate grains" texture.
  - "Separate grains" really means frying the rice dry. Day-old cold rice is used because it has already lost some moisture overnight.
