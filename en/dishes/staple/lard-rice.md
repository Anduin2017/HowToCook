# How to Make Lard Rice

This is a simple mixed rice full of southern Chinese home-style charm: grains of rice coated in melted lard, with the savory saltiness of soy sauce and the fresh aroma of chopped green onion—a comforting choice for many kids with a poor appetite. It mainly provides quick carbohydrates and fat for energy, but it's high in cholesterol, so don't eat it often. There's no barrier at all; even if you can't cook, you can make it easily. With cooked rice, a fragrant bowl is ready in just a few minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 420 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cooked rice (japonica rice recommended)
- Lard
- Light soy sauce (Lee Kum Kee or Haitian recommended)
- Dark soy sauce
- Oyster sauce (optional)
- Chopped green onion (optional)
- Pork cracklings (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Cooked rice 200 g
- Lard 15 g (reduce to 10 g if adding oyster sauce)
- Light soy sauce 5 ml (reduce to 3 ml if adding oyster sauce)
- Dark soy sauce 2 ml
- Oyster sauce 8 g
- Chopped green onion 0-12 g (5 g to start)
- Pork cracklings 5 g

## Steps

1. Put the lard in the bottom of the bowl
2. Add the rice
3. Drizzle with soy sauce and oyster sauce
4. Sprinkle with chopped green onion and pork cracklings
5. Mix well

## Additional Notes

- Eat with lean meat and vegetables for balanced nutrition
- High in cholesterol; not recommended for frequent eating
