# How to Make Rice Cooker Rice

A universally known basic staple, with a pure rice aroma and soft texture, firmer or moister depending on how much water you use. It's rich in carbohydrates for a quick energy boost and goes with all kinds of dishes. It's extremely simple; beginners can pick it up easily. It takes about forty minutes from rinsing the rice to finishing the rest.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 442 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Rice cooker
* Jiangnan rice (long-grain) or northern Chinese rice (short-grain)
* Water

## Calculation

* One person can usually eat 100ml-200ml of rice.

Fingertip method for measuring water (a precise way to determine the rice-to-water ratio)

* Jiangnan rice: with the rice and water in the rice cooker pot, when your index finger touches the rice, the water should just cover the first joint of your index finger plus 1/4 of the way to the second joint (about 2.6 cm).
* Northern rice: with the rice and water in the rice cooker pot, when your index finger touches the rice, the water should just cover the first joint of your index finger (about 2 cm).

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. Rinse the rice
2. Put the rice and water in the rice cooker.
3. Plug in the rice cooker and start the cooking mode. Wait about 30 minutes.
4. Wait for the rice cooker to switch to keep-warm mode automatically.
5. Let the rice rest in the rice cooker for 10-15 minutes.
6. Serve the rice.

## Additional Notes

- Technical summary
  - Whatever the container, the "fingertip method" for measuring water gives you good rice.
  - The amount of water can seriously affect the texture of a good bowl of rice.
  - Southern China generally eats Jiangnan rice and northern China generally eats northern rice. Jiangnan rice is slender and long, while northern rice is short and round.
  - Different varieties of rice and different amounts of water dramatically affect the texture.
- For more information, see your rice cooker's manual.
