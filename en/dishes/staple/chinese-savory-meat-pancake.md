# How to Make Chinese Savory Meat Pancake

This pancake has a slightly crisp outside and a soft inside with a savory filling—a home-style flour-based dish. It's rich in carbohydrates and protein, and carrot and egg add extra nutrition. The difficulty is medium; beginners need to master the heat, but the steps are clear and easy to follow. It takes about 1 hour from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1265 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Flour (not self-rising)
* Minced meat
* Oil
* Salt
* Sugar
* Potato starch
* Soy sauce
* Flavor seasonings (such as chicken bouillon, cumin, pepper salt; optional)
* Garlic
* Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
* Egg (optional)
* Carrot (optional)
* Frying pan
* Wok (the same frying pan can be used instead)

## Calculation

Each portion contains:

* Flour 200g
* Minced meat 50g
* Oil 30ml
* Salt 3g
* Sugar 5g
* Potato starch 10g
* Soy sauce 5g
* Flavor seasonings 3g
* Garlic 2 cloves
* Scallion 1/4 stalk (the leafy part)
* Egg (optional, 1)

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

### Preparing the Ingredients

1. Take the minced meat (thawed), add 1/2 of each of the seasonings above (oil, salt, sugar, soy sauce, flavor seasonings) and all of the starch, mix well, and marinate for 30 minutes.
2. Put the flour in a bowl, add the egg, the remaining 1/2 of each seasoning, and water equal to 1/2 of the flour (so the flour is fairly thick but can still flow), and mix well.
3. Mince the garlic.
4. Cut the scallion into segments.
5. Finely mince the carrot (it's for the filling, so make it as fine as possible; chop randomly)

### Cooking

1. Heat the wok, add cold oil—a generous amount.
2. When the oil is hot, add the minced garlic and fry until fragrant.
3. Add the marinated minced meat and stir-fry until just cooked.
4. Add the minced carrot to the meat and stir-fry together until the oil turns golden (this extracts the carrot's flavor).
5. Turn off the heat. Let cool for 2 minutes.
6. Pour the fried meat into the raw batter and mix well.
7. Turn the heat back on and coat the bottom of the frying pan with oil.
8. Turn to low heat, pour the batter into the pan and spread evenly. Keep it from getting too thick. You can lift the pan and let the batter flow across the bottom to do this.
9. While the surface of the pancake is still liquid, sprinkle on the scallion segments.
10. Keep on low heat until the bottom has set.
11. Flip the pancake and keep frying on low heat until the other side has set.
12. Then fry each side for another 20 seconds.
13. Turn off the heat and serve.

## Additional Notes

- After adding the meat and carrot to the batter, mix as evenly as possible so the flavor spreads throughout the pancake.
- The meat and carrot filling can be replaced with any kind of cooked filling.
