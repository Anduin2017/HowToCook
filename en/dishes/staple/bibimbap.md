# How to Make Bibimbap

![Bibimbap](../../../dishes/staple/韩式拌饭/韩式拌饭.png)

Bibimbap is a colorful Korean home-style dish with rich textures: a sweet-spicy sauce coats rice, vegetables, beef and a runny egg, and once mixed, every bite is satisfying. The variety of vegetables provides vitamins and dietary fiber, and beef and egg add high-quality protein for balanced nutrition. It's simple, though there's a fair bit of vegetable prep; beginners can manage it calmly. It takes about 45 minutes from prep to table.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1080 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cooked rice
- Egg
- Hot pot beef rolls (thinly sliced beef)
- Bean sprouts
- Mushrooms
- Carrot
- Zucchini
- Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- Sprite
- Sesame seeds
- Sesame oil

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Cooked rice 1 bowl (400g)
- Egg 1
- Hot pot beef rolls 6 rolls, 60g
- Bean sprouts 1 handful, 80g
- Mushrooms 50g
- Carrot 1/4
- Zucchini 50g
- Gochujang 25ml
- Sprite 2 bottle caps, 20ml
- Sesame seeds 10g
- Sesame oil 20ml
- Light soy sauce 15ml

## Steps

1. Wash and julienne the vegetables, stir-fry in the pan until soft, then take them out
2. Bring water to a boil and blanch the beef rolls; they just need to be cooked through, about three minutes, then scoop them out
3. Fry a [soft-boiled-style egg](../breakfast/soft-boiled-eggs.md)
4. Put the [rice](./rice-cooker-rice.md) in a bowl, then invert it into a large bowl
5. Arrange the prepared vegetables and beef in a circle on top of the rice, with the egg in the middle
6. Make the sauce

  - 10ml of gochujang (sweet-spicy) + 5ml of light soy sauce + two bottle caps of Sprite (if you're losing weight, use Sprite Zero) + 10g of sesame seeds + 5ml of sesame oil
  - Stir the sauce; you can add more light soy sauce and salt

7. Pour the sauce over the arranged bowl

## Additional Notes

- This is a very simple dish, though the process is a bit tedious, but mixing the vegetables, meat, rice and sauce together and eating it is very satisfying
- If you care about presentation, use a clay pot instead of an ordinary bowl
