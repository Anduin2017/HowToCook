# How to Make Scallion Oil Noodles

Scallion oil noodles are a classic Shanghai home-style noodle dish, famous for their distinctive scallion oil aroma, quick and easy to make. They're rich in carbohydrates and fat for a quick energy boost. Beginners can usually finish them in just 20 minutes, perfect for a quick everyday dinner.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 355 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Dried noodles
- Green onions
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- White sugar

## Calculation

You can make several portions of scallion oil sauce at once and use it over several meals. Below are the base amounts for the sauce and the amount of sauce needed per portion of noodles.

Scallion oil sauce base (enough for about 3-4 portions):

- Green onions 100 g
- Cooking oil 100 ml
- Light soy sauce 60 ml
- Dark soy sauce 20 ml
- White sugar 15 g

Per portion of noodles:

- Dried noodles 80 g (about the same as 150 g of fresh noodles)
- Scallion oil sauce 15 ml

## Steps

### Making the Scallion Oil Sauce

1. Wash the green onions and cut into long segments (about 5-7 cm). You can separate the whites and greens.
2. Heat 100 ml of cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the white segments first and fry until lightly golden.
3. Add the green segments, turn to low heat, and keep frying.
4. Keep the heat low and patiently fry for about **15-20 minutes**, until the scallions are dark golden and crispy.
5. Scoop out the crispy scallions (leaving the scallion oil in the pan).
6. To the scallion oil in the pan, add 60 ml of light soy sauce, 20 ml of dark soy sauce and 15 g of sugar. Heat over low heat and stir for about **1 minute**, until the sugar dissolves and the sauce is well mixed. Turn off the heat immediately. Pour the scallion oil sauce into a container, let it cool, and seal.

### Cooking the Noodles (Per Portion)

1. Take 80 g of dried noodles.
2. Bring 1000 ml of drinking water to a boil in a pot over high heat.
3. Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions until done (usually **3-8 minutes**; follow the package).
4. Drain the cooked noodles and put them in a bowl.

### Mixing (Per Portion)

1. Add 15 ml of the scallion oil sauce to the bowl of noodles.
2. You can also add some of the crispy fried scallions (optional).
3. Mix quickly with chopsticks and enjoy.

## Additional Notes

- When frying the scallion oil, keep the heat low and be patient, so the scallion aroma is fully released.
- The finished scallion oil sauce keeps in the refrigerator for a while; just take some out the next time you make noodles.
