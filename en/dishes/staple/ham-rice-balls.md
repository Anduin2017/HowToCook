# How to Make Ham Rice Balls

![Ham rice balls](../../../dishes/staple/火腿饭团/饭团.png)
A simple, quick Japanese-style rice ball: savory ham with mayonnaise and seaweed for a varied, filling texture. It's rich in carbohydrates, protein and vitamins for a quick energy boost. The difficulty is extremely low; beginners can make it easily. It takes only about 1 hour from cooking the rice to finishing.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 665 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Ham
- Rice
- Water
- Frozen green peas (optional)
- Frozen corn kernels (optional)
- Shredded seaweed (optional)
- Your favorite mayonnaise (Japanese mayo recommended!)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Ham (100g)
- Rice (125g)
- Water (90ml)
- Frozen green peas (30g)
- Frozen corn kernels (30g)
- Shredded seaweed (10g)
- Your favorite mayonnaise (20g)
- Cooking oil 10-15ml

## Steps

1. Put the rice and water in the rice cooker, press the rice mode, and wait for it to finish
2. Put the frozen corn and peas in a pot with water to cover, and after it boils, wait 2 minutes, then scoop them out.
3. Cut the ham into 1cm cubes
4. Meanwhile, add 10ml of cooking oil and stir-fry the ham until browned
5. Put the rice, ham, shredded seaweed, peas, corn and mayonnaise in a bowl and mix well
6. Plate (if you have a plate)

## Additional Notes

- Adjust the amount of mayonnaise to taste.
- Try mixing different mayonnaises.
- Reference: Xiaohongshu - "Ganfan xuanshou you kun le"
