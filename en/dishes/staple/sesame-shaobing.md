# How to Make Sesame Shaobing

![Sesame shaobing - preview image 1](../../../dishes/staple/烧饼/芝麻烧饼.jpg)
Sesame shaobing is a well-known northern Chinese flatbread: the crust is coated in fragrant, crunchy sesame seeds, and the inside is distinctly layered, soft and pleasant. Flour provides plenty of carbohydrates, and sesame seeds are rich in calcium and unsaturated fatty acids. The process includes kneading dough and making youxu (oil-flour paste), but it's not complicated, and beginners can make it by following along. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 282 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Flour
- Yeast
- White sugar
- Thirteen-spice powder
- Cooking oil
- Warm water (40℃)

## Calculation

The recipe makes 7 flatbreads

## Steps

1. Dough: 300 grams of flour, 3 grams of yeast, 3 grams of sugar, 180 grams of warm water and 20 grams of cooking oil; let the dough rest for 10 minutes
2. Youxu: in a small bowl, mix 30 grams of flour, 2 grams of salt, 4 grams of thirteen-spice powder and 20 grams of cooking oil, then let it stand
3. Shaping: roll the dough into a rectangle, spread with the youxu, roll up from one end, and cut into 7 pieces. Fold each in half and gather it closed with the web of your hand. Dip first in water, then in white sesame seeds, and roll into a small round flatbread
4. Cooking: preheat the electric griddle and add cold oil (enough to cover the bottom). Put the rolled breads on the griddle, brush a little oil evenly over the tops, close the lid, select the flatbread setting, and take them out when you hear the ding

## Additional Notes

- If you don't have an electric griddle, a frying pan works too
- If you don't have sesame seeds, you can leave them out; it's just as tasty
