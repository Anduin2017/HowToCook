# How to Make Laobing (Layered Flatbread)

This home-style laobing is distinctly layered, crispy outside and soft inside, with the toasty aroma of youxu (oil-flour paste)—a common staple on northern Chinese tables. It mainly provides carbohydrates and energy, and goes well with other dishes. It takes a bit of technique, especially rolling in the paste and rolling out the bread, but following the steps it's not hard, and beginners can master it. It takes about 1 hour from mixing the dough to finished bread.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1185 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Oil
* Flour
* Electric griddle (electric pancake pan)

## Calculation

Note: this staple doesn't come in smaller or larger versions; the amount chosen here is the most suitable for beginners:

* Flour = 400g
* Hot water = 130ml (80 degrees)
* Cold water = 130ml

## Steps

1. Put 400g of flour in a basin. Mix half with cold water and half with hot water, stirring into shaggy flakes, then knead by hand into a ball. Seal with plastic wrap and let the dough rest for 40 minutes
2. When there are 10 minutes left in the resting time, see the youxu recipe in [this tip](../condiment/youxu-oil-flour-paste.md) (hot-oil youxu works better)
3. No need to knead the rested dough; just press it a little, and cut it into four pieces with one horizontal and one vertical cut.
4. Roll each into a ball, then roll it out into a round about the size of the electric griddle, take 1/4 of the youxu, and spread it evenly over the surface
5. Cut along a radius of the round, roll it up from the outside edge into a cone, and pinch the end of the cone closed to keep the youxu from leaking out.
6. Press down on the tip of the cone to flatten it, then roll it out again into a round about the size of the griddle (about 3mm thick)
7. Preheat the electric griddle and brush with cold oil (hot pan, cold oil). Put the rolled bread on the griddle, brush a little oil evenly over the top too (to lock in moisture), and close the lid
8. Cook on high for one minute, open the lid, flip the bread, and cook for another minute
9. Repeat the above until all the breads are cooked
