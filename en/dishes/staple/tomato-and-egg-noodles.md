# How to Make Tomato and Egg Noodles

Tomato and egg noodles are a quick home-style noodle dish: sweet, tangy and savory, with a rich sauce and chewy noodles—an everyday staple with northern Chinese flavor. Eggs provide high-quality protein, tomatoes are rich in lycopene and vitamins, and with the noodles it's nutritionally balanced. It's simple with clear steps, beginner-friendly, and takes only about 20 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 654 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Dried noodles, or fresh noodles also work
- One tomato
- Salt
- Eggs
- Green onion
- Soy sauce, oyster sauce or chicken bouillon powder
- White sugar (to balance the tomato's acidity; not needed if the tomato isn't sour)
- Green bell pepper (not long thin chilies)
- Sesame oil

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. The amounts below are just enough for 1 person.

Total:

- Dried noodles 1 bundle (according to appetite) 50-100g
- Tomato 1, about 200g.
- Eggs 1~2
- Salt 5g
- Oyster sauce 5g or chicken bouillon 3g
- White sugar 2g
- Soy sauce 5-8g
- Cooking oil 20g
- Sesame oil 5g

## Steps

### Preparing the Ingredients

Prepping ingredients is very important. Whatever you cook, prep in advance so you won't be flustered while cooking, and cooking becomes a pleasant process. This... is probably why restaurant kitchens need a dedicated prep station

1. Wash the green onion and chop it
2. Cut the tomato into chunks; if you're not sure how, search it up first~
3. Cut the green pepper into diamond-shaped pieces
4. Crack the raw eggs into a small bowl and beat. If the eggs smell a bit eggy, add 2g of white vinegar to remove the smell

![Prepped ingredients](../../../dishes/staple/西红柿鸡蛋挂面/pretreatFood.jpg)

### Cooking the Eggs

1. Heat the wok and add 15~20g of cooking oil. Tender scrambled eggs need a bit more oil, and it leaves some oil for frying the tomato afterward
2. When the oil reaches 7/10 heat (you can feel the heat with your palm about 10cm above it), pour in the egg and quickly scramble it
3. Once the egg has set with no liquid egg left at all, transfer to a small bowl and set aside, leaving some oil in the wok

![Scrambled eggs](../../../dishes/staple/西红柿鸡蛋挂面/fryEgg.jpg)

### Making the Tomato-Egg Topping

1. With the oil left in the wok, add the green onion whites and minced garlic and fry until fragrant
2. Add the tomato chunks and green pepper and fry until the tomatoes release some juice
3. Quickly add 5g of soy sauce and 2g of sugar
4. Stir-fry for ten-odd seconds, then add a bowl of water (just about enough to cover the tomatoes)
5. Once boiling, add the scrambled eggs and 5g of oyster sauce or 2g of chicken bouillon for savoriness
6. Reduce over medium-low heat, stirring to prevent sticking. Once reduced to the state in the picture below, add the remaining green parts of the green onion and some sesame oil (optional). The topping is done

![Tomato-egg topping](../../../dishes/staple/西红柿鸡蛋挂面/tomato.jpg)

### Finally, Cooking the Noodles

1. No need to wash the wok; just add 500ml of water
2. Bring to a boil and add the noodles; once they soften, add 100ml of cold water
3. When it boils again, if the noodles float, add another 100ml of cold water
4. When it boils, check whether the edges of the noodles look translucent; if so, they're cooked
5. Scoop the noodles into the bowl with the topping and mix

![Tomato and egg noodles](../../../dishes/staple/西红柿鸡蛋挂面/tomatoNoodle.jpg)

## Additional Notes

- Adding 2g of black pepper to the beaten egg makes it taste better
- Adding 2g of ground white pepper while cooking the topping boosts the spicy aroma
- You can cook the noodles in one go, but adding water several times makes the noodles chewier and less sticky. The trick is to add cold water each time it boils, two or three times
