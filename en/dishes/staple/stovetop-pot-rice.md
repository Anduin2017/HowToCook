# How to Make Stovetop Pot Rice

This is a plain, unpretentious home-style staple: soft, pleasant rice made in an ordinary pot. White rice mainly provides carbohydrates for a quick energy boost. It's extremely simple; beginners only need to keep an eye on the heat to master it. It takes about 25 minutes from adding the rice to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 418 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Northern Chinese rice (short-grain)
- Water
- Heavy-bottomed pot + a tight-fitting lid (so not much steam escapes during cooking)

## Calculation

- Rice: 100ml-200ml/person
- Water: 2 times the volume of the rice

## Steps

1. Rinse the rice
2. Put the rice and water in the pot
3. Bring to a boil over high heat
4. **Stir the bottom to prevent sticking**
5. Cover and cook over **low heat** for 10-15 minutes (depending on how soft you like it). Don't lift the lid during this time
6. Turn off the heat and let it rest for 5 minutes
7. Enjoy :)
