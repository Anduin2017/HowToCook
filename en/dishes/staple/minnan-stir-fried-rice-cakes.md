# How to Make Minnan Stir-fried Rice Cakes

This is a southern Fujian (Minnan) style staple: soft, chewy rice cakes with the toasty aroma of scallion oil, home-style and filling. Rice cakes made from rice flour provide plenty of carbohydrates, and with eggs and greens they add protein and vitamins for balanced nutrition. It's simple and beginner-friendly, taking about 30 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 960 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Rice cakes / baiguo (any shape)
- Green onion
- Seasonings: soy sauce, salt
- (Optional): eggs, leafy greens

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

- Rice cakes 250 g * portions
- Green onion 2 stalks * portions
- Cooking oil 50 ml
- Soy sauce 15 ml
- Salt 1-2g * portions, to taste.

## Steps

1. Bring water to a boil and cook the rice cakes until done. Keep them in a bowl of water so they don't stick together, then scoop out and set aside.
2. Chop the green onion (keeping the whites and greens separate), and cut the greens into short pieces. Set aside.
3. (Optional) Make scrambled eggs; see [Tomato and Egg Stir-fry](../vegetable_dish/tomato-and-egg-stir-fry.md).
4. Heat the wok and add 30ml of cooking oil.
5. Add the green onion whites and fry until most of them are golden brown and fragrant, then pour out the scallion oil and set aside.
6. Reheat the wok and add 20ml of cooking oil.
7. Add all the extras (eggs, greens, etc.) and stir-fry evenly.
8. Drain the rice cakes and add them to the wok.
9. Add the soy sauce and salt and stir-fry evenly.
10. Turn off the heat, add the scallion oil, stir-fry evenly, and plate.

## Additional Notes

- Add whatever extras you like, such as lean pork
- Stir while boiling the rice cakes so they don't stick to the pot
