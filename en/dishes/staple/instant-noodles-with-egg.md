# How to Make Instant Noodles with Egg

Instant noodles with egg is a convenient quick staple: smooth noodles and a rich broth, good for filling up anytime. It mainly provides carbohydrates and protein for a quick energy boost. It's extremely simple—just boil water, cook the noodles, and crack in an egg to cook—so beginners can master it easily. It takes about 10 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 550 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Instant noodles
- Egg
- Water

## Calculation

- One person; keeps an adult from feeling hungry for about 3 to 4 hours.

Per portion:

- Instant noodles 1 pack
- Water 550ml-1000ml, depending on your pot—enough to fully submerge the noodles.
- Egg 1

## Steps

1. Bring the water to a boil (no strict requirement on heat; using hot water is faster)
2. Put the noodle block in the pot
3. Add the seasoning packets that came with the noodles
4. Gently stir the noodles with chopsticks so the seasoning dissolves and the noodle block is fully soaked and heated
5. Cover and wait about 1 minute for the water to boil again
6. Crack the egg into the pot
7. Wait about 3 to 4 minutes, and it's done

## Additional Notes

- "Egg" in this guide includes but is not limited to: raw eggs, boiled eggs, braised eggs, etc.
- Instant noodles can be bought at the convenience store downstairs
- Add seasonings and other ingredients as you like, including but not limited to: ham sausage, lettuce, shredded pork, latiao, dried fish, shrimp, chicken drumsticks
