# How to Make Steamed Braised Noodles (Zheng Lu Mian)

This is a classic home-style noodle dish from southern Henan, combining meat and vegetables: the noodles soak up the sauce, soft yet chewy, savory and full of flavor. Pork belly provides high-quality protein and fat, and celery is rich in dietary fiber. It's simple and easy to learn; beginners can handle it calmly. It takes about an hour overall.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1563 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pork belly
- Celery
- Fresh noodles (must be the **thinnest** kind; if your local market doesn't have them, see how to make [braised noodles](./braised-noodles-with-green-beans.md))
- Green onion, ginger, garlic
- Cooking oil (peanut oil is best)
- Light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, cooking wine, salt, five-spice powder
- Steamer, with steaming tray
- Wok

The following ingredients are optional for those with some cooking experience or extra energy

- Sichuan peppercorns
- Dried red chilies
- Green peppers

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

- Celery: two medium stalks
- Pork belly 350g
- Noodles 500g
- Scallion 10cm
- Garlic 5 cloves
- Ginger slices 20g
- Green peppers 2
- Dried red chilies 3
- Sichuan peppercorns 20
- Salt 10g
- Five-spice powder 5g
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Dark soy sauce 10ml

## Steps

### Prep

1. Remove the skin from the pork and cut into thin `2 cm * 6 cm * 0.5 cm` slices. Set aside
2. Remove the celery leaves and the bottom 2cm, split down the middle, and cut into 2cm segments. Set aside
3. Peel and dice the garlic, cut the green onion into 0.2cm thin slices, and julienne the ginger. Set aside

### Pre-cooking

1. Heat the wok until smoking, add 3ml of cooking oil to coat it, then pour the oil out
2. Add fresh cooking oil, then the pork slices, green onion, ginger, garlic and dried red chilies, and stir-fry for 1 minute, tossing steadily and continuously
3. Add the cooking wine, light soy sauce and dark soy sauce and stir-fry for another minute
4. Add 500ml of hot water. Cover and braise for 3 minutes
5. Add the celery and green peppers, season with salt and five-spice powder, cover and braise for another 3 minutes, then turn off the heat
6. Add 1000ml of water to the steamer, bring to a boil until steaming, spread the noodles flat on the steaming tray, and steam for 15 minutes
7. Take out the steamed noodles, spread them out on the board with chopsticks and your iron hands, and let them cool to room temperature

### Final Cooking

1. Put the noodles into the wok with the vegetables and mix: hold chopsticks in one hand and the spatula in the other, flipping the vegetables over onto the noodles, until all the noodles are evenly colored
2. Spread the mixed noodles on the steaming tray again and steam for another 10 minutes, then turn off the heat

## Additional Notes

- Technical summary
  - When cutting the pork, you can ask the market vendor to help. In my experience, the success rate is highest if you ask after it's been cut but before you pay
  - The first time you cook, prep all your ingredients in advance to avoid scrambling; once you're practiced, you can run steps concurrently
  - Both times you steam the noodles, spread them out as much as possible so they don't clump together after steaming. If the noodles are too long you can break them, making mixing and serving easier
  - Be gentle when taking out the steamed noodles, so the tray doesn't tip and the noodles don't touch the water
  - Goes even better with beer!
