# How to Make Sesame Oil Noodles

Sesame oil noodles have a rich sesame oil aroma, simply seasoned with dark soy sauce and pepper, savory and well-balanced—a time- and money-saving home-style noodle dish. The noodles provide plenty of carbohydrates, and sesame oil contains unsaturated fatty acids and vitamin E, providing energy for the body. There's no difficulty at all; even kitchen beginners can make it easily, and it takes only about ten minutes from boiling water to mixing.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 451 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Air-dried quick-cooking noodles / any brand of quick-cooking noodles (no seasoning needed)
* Sesame oil
* Ground pepper
* Dark soy sauce
* Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Water 1 liter
* Quick-cooking noodles 1 block
* Sesame oil 15ml
* Dark soy sauce 10 grams
* Salt 30 grams (optional; not all of these 30g of salt is eaten)
* Ground pepper 10 grams
* Light soy sauce 5 grams (optional)

## Steps

1. Pour the water into the pot and bring to a boil (if you like springy noodles, add 30 grams of salt to the water; noodles cooked in salted water come out springier)
2. Put the noodles in the pot for 3 minutes (or follow the cooking time for your brand of noodles)
3. When the noodles start to separate, stir so they heat evenly
4. Drain the noodles and put them in a bowl
5. Add the sesame oil, dark soy sauce, ground pepper and light soy sauce (optional) according to the amounts above
6. Mix well with chopsticks
7. A simple, money-saving, lazy sesame oil noodle dish is done
