# How to Make Noodle Soup

Noodle soup is a freely customizable home-style staple: a light broth and smooth noodles that go with all kinds of meat and vegetables, flavored rich or light as you like. It's rich in carbohydrates and high-quality protein, and with vegetables it adds vitamins and dietary fiber—balanced and filling. It's very simple, needs no cooking skills, and beginners can handle it easily. It usually takes about 30 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 565 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Noodle ingredient: handmade noodles, thin dragon-whisker noodles, mianyu (noodle fish), instant noodle blocks, glass noodles of any kind, or any other noodle you like.
- Vegetables and toppings: a mix of meat and vegetables is recommended; choose whatever you like and wash it. For example:
  - Meat like beef, lamb, fish or shrimp (raw or cooked)
  - Eggs: chicken, duck, goose, ostrich...
  - Soy products like tofu cubes, tofu sticks or tofu skin
  - Lettuce, spinach, Indian lettuce
  - Vegetables like green peppers, tomatoes and carrots.

## Calculation

- Noodles: one instant-noodle-block's worth per person, anywhere from 70-230g.
- Cold water: enough to submerge the noodles, generally 200 - 400 ml
- Vegetables and toppings: roughly the same volume as the noodles
  - Leafy greens' volume can be ignored

## Steps

1. Cut the vegetables and toppings into pieces no larger than 4cm on a side so they cook easily
2. If you have raw meat, put it in cold water first, cover, and bring to a boil. Skim off the blood foam on top, turn off the heat, and take out the half-cooked meat to set aside
3. Bring the water to a boil over high heat, then turn to medium
4. Add the harder-to-cook ingredients first (e.g. the half-cooked meat and mushrooms). To make sure they cook through, time 10 minutes after it boils; for especially large, slow-cooking pieces, add another 5 minutes.
5. Add the noodles and stir so they're fully in contact with the broth, keeping it at a gentle boil for 5 minutes. White foam tends to form after adding the noodles; lift the lid to vent or take it off.
6. Add the quick-cooking ingredients like leafy greens, stir so they're submerged, and cook for 2-5 minutes
7. Turn off the heat, add salt, pepper, sesame oil or whatever seasonings you like, stir, and serve

## Additional Notes

- Change the process however you like; experimenting leads to pleasant surprises
- If you're not sure how much salt to add, add a little at a time to avoid overdoing it

If you follow this guide and find problems or possible improvements to the process, please open an Issue or Pull request.
