# How to Make Air Fryer Teriyaki Chicken Rice

This Japanese teriyaki-style chicken rice has a rich sweet-savory sauce, a lightly crisp, browned top and a tender, juicy inside—great with rice. Chicken and eggs provide plenty of high-quality protein, though the amounts of soy sauce and sugar are high, so it's better as an occasional treat. It's simple—just mix the ingredients and let the air fryer do the rest—so beginners can handle it easily. It takes about 1 hour from prep to serving.

## Ingredients and Tools

- Ritz crackers
- Soy sauce
- Sugar (white sugar)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Ground chicken 900g
- Soy sauce 100-125ml
- Sugar 60-65g
- White vinegar 30-35ml
- Ritz crackers (salty crackers can substitute) 16 (48g)
- Eggs 2

## Steps

1. Mix the soy sauce, sugar and vinegar together and stir into a sauce. Set aside
2. In another bowl, add the chicken, eggs, 1/2 of the sauce and the crushed Ritz crackers. Mix well
3. Line the air fryer with a foil bowl, add the meatloaf mixture, and pour the remaining sauce evenly on top
4. Air fry at **350°F (177°C)** for **40 minutes**. Best eaten over rice
5. Once it *looks golden and crispy*, take it out, cut into pieces and plate
