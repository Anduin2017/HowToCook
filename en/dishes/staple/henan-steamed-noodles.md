# How to Make Henan Steamed Noodles

![Henan steamed noodles - preview image 1](../../../dishes/staple/河南蒸面条/河南蒸面条.png)

Henan steamed noodles are a home-style snack from Henan: the noodles are steamed, then stir-fried, then steamed again, making them rich, well-seasoned, chewy and smooth. Pork belly and garlic scapes provide plenty of protein and dietary fiber, and with the noodles it's a balanced meal. There are a few more steps, but beginners can succeed by doing them in order. It takes about 40 minutes in total from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 3066 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Dried noodles (round ones recommended)
- Pork belly
- Garlic scapes
- Green onion + ginger + garlic + cooking wine
- Salt + chicken bouillon powder + thirteen-spice powder
- Light soy sauce + dark soy sauce + oyster sauce
- Sesame oil
- Oil + wok + kitchen knife + spatula
- Steaming rack
- An extra basin

## Calculation

The amounts below are just enough for one person's staple for one meal! Adjust to your actual needs!

Per portion:

- Dried noodles 300g
- Pork belly 350g
- Garlic scapes 150g
- Cooking oil 10-15ml
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Dark soy sauce 10ml
- Oyster sauce 5ml
- Salt 2g
- Chicken bouillon powder 2g
- Thirteen-spice powder 1g
- Green onion 10g
- Ginger 5g
- Garlic 10g
- Cooking wine 5ml
- Sesame oil 5ml

## Steps

### First Steaming

1. Fill the wok 70% with water, bring to a boil, and put on the steaming rack
2. Spread the noodles out evenly, drizzle with 5ml of oil and spread it around, and steam for 15 minutes
3. Take out the noodles and the steaming rack, set aside, and pour out the water in the wok

### Cutting the Vegetables

1. Pork belly: cut into 2mm thick slices
2. Garlic scapes: cut into 3cm segments
3. Green onion: cut into 0.2cm thin slices
4. Ginger: cut into fine 1mm x 1mm x 3cm shreds
5. Garlic: smash on the board and mince to 1mm pieces

### Stir-frying

1. Heat the wok until dry and add 3ml of cooking oil
2. Holding the handle, swirl the wok so the oil coats 2/3 of it
3. Over medium heat, add the pork slices and stir-fry for 1 minute
4. Add the green onion, ginger, garlic and cooking wine and keep stir-frying for 1 minute
5. Add the garlic scape segments and stir-fry for 1 minute
6. Season: add the dark soy sauce, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt, chicken bouillon and thirteen-spice powder and stir-fry for 1 minute
7. Add 500ML of water to cover the vegetables and simmer for 1 minute
8. Add the steamed noodles and keep stirring for 3 minutes, until all the noodles are evenly colored, then turn off the heat
9. Pour all the mixed noodles and vegetables into the extra basin

### Second Steaming

1. Fill the wok 70% with cold water, put on the steaming rack, and spread the mixed noodles and vegetables evenly on it
2. Once the water boils, steam on high heat for 15 minutes, then take it out
3. Drizzle with 10g of sesame oil and enjoy

## Additional Notes

Noodle thickness and texture:

- Thin, round ones (most noodles in the supermarket are this kind; chewier)
- Wide, flat ones (softer)
