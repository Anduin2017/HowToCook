# How to Make Bonito Seaweed Corn Rice

![Bonito seaweed corn rice - preview image 1](../../../dishes/staple/鲣鱼海苔玉米饭/米饭.jpg)

This is a savory Japanese-style light meal: fluffy, separate grains of rice combined with the savory flavor of bonito, the crispness of seaweed and the sweet crunch of corn. Bonito is rich in high-quality protein and DHA, and with the minerals in seaweed and the dietary fiber in corn it's nutritionally balanced. It's very simple; beginners can make it easily. Hands-on time is only 5 minutes; including cooking the rice, it's ready in about 40 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 383 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Required: northeastern Chinese rice (Arawana brand, 30 yuan for 5kg, is fine; a Mijia IH rice cooker is recommended—the tutorial used a small Mijia rice cooker. **Using a pressure cooker for rice is not recommended**)
- Required: bonito seaweed flakes (available on JD and Taobao; search for "Japanese rice seasoning", i.e. furikake)
- Required: corn kernels (search Taobao for "ready-to-eat corn kernels")

## Calculation

- Bonito seaweed flakes 20g
- Corn kernels 80g/bag

## Steps

1. Serve the rice, add the corn kernels, and mix
2. Add the bonito seaweed flakes

## Additional Notes

- When cooking northeastern rice in the small Mijia rice cooker, water just 3 mm above the rice is enough; with too much water, the grains won't come out separate
