# How to Make Buckwheat Noodles with Chili Sauce

Buckwheat noodles with chili sauce are sweet, tangy, cool and smooth—a simple, quick home-style cold noodle dish. Buckwheat noodles are rich in dietary fiber and protein, and cucumber and carrot add freshness and vitamins for balanced nutrition. Just boil the noodles, cut the vegetables and mix in Lao Gan Ma—a few steps and you're done. It's very beginner-friendly and usually takes about 20 minutes. It tastes even better chilled.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 475 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Buckwheat noodles (soba)
* Cucumber
* Carrot
* Lao Gan Ma chili crisp

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Buckwheat noodles 100 g
* Cucumber 0.5
* Carrot 0.5
* Lao Gan Ma 20 ml

## Steps

1. Put the buckwheat noodles in cold water and boil until cooked, 8-10 minutes, then drain and set aside
2. Cut the cucumber and carrot into small strips
3. Put the noodles, cucumber and carrot on a plate, top with Lao Gan Ma, and mix

## Additional Notes

* After draining, chilling the noodles in the refrigerator makes them taste even better
* Choose other toppings as you like, such as shrimp or minced meat
* Choose other sauces as you like, such as peanut butter or sesame paste
* Buckwheat noodles have almost the same calories as other noodles, so they aren't a good staple for losing fat
