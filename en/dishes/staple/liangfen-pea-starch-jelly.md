# How to Make Liangfen (Pea Starch Jelly)

![Liangfen - preview image 1](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf1.jpg)

Liangfen is a traditional snack from Sichuan: cold, smooth and slippery, tangy and spicy, perfect for beating the summer heat. The main ingredient is pea starch, rich in carbohydrates for a quick energy boost. The process isn't complicated, but it requires patient stirring while cooking and a long chill to set, so the difficulty is moderate for beginners. It takes about 3-4 hours from cooking to finishing, most of which is waiting while it chills; the hands-on work is only about half an hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 539 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pea starch
- Garlic
- Bird's eye chili
- Chili powder
- Soy sauce
- Vinegar
- White sugar
- Chicken bouillon powder
- Salt
- Crushed peanuts
- Cilantro

## Calculation

The ingredients below are for one person; decide how many portions to make before you start. One portion is just enough for one person.

- Pea starch  100g
- Garlic   3 cloves
- Bird's eye chili  3
- Chili powder  10g
- Soy sauce   10ml
- Vinegar   10ml
- White sugar  3ml
- Chicken bouillon powder  3g
- Salt  3g
- Crushed peanuts  5g
- Cilantro  5g

## Steps

1. Prepare the ingredients.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 2](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf2.jpg)

2. Mix 100 grams each of pea starch and water.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 3](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf3.jpg)

3. Pour 600g of water into a pot, bring to a boil over high heat, then turn to low heat.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 4](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf4.jpg)

4. Pour in the starch slurry, stirring constantly as you pour, until thick and even in color.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 5](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf5.jpg)

5. Brush a thin layer of cooking oil inside a container.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 6](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf6.jpg)

6. Pour the cooked starch into the container and refrigerate for 2-4 hours.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 7](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf7.jpg)

7. Take it out, unmold, and cut into strips.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 8](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf8.jpg)

8. Mince the garlic and bird's eye chilies, add 10g of chili powder and 5g of crushed peanuts, pour hot oil over and stir well.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 9](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf9.jpg)

9. Then add 10ml of soy sauce, 10ml of vinegar, 5g of sugar, 3g of chicken bouillon and 3g of salt and stir well.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 10](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf10.jpg)

10. Pour the seasoning over the liangfen and sprinkle with cilantro.

    ![Liangfen - preview image 11](../../../dishes/staple/凉粉/lf11.jpg)
