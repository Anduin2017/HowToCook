# How to Make Mini Tangyuan in Fermented Rice Soup

Mini tangyuan in fermented rice (laozao) soup is a sweet, soft, chewy traditional Chinese dessert: the slight tang of fermented rice blends with the smoothness of glutinous rice, warming the stomach and soothing the soul. It's rich in carbohydrates and a little B vitamins for a quick energy boost, and is said to help circulation. There's no barrier at all; it's especially beginner-friendly, taking only about 15 minutes from boiling water to serving—a home-style sweet soup you can make anytime.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 957 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Mini tangyuan (small glutinous rice balls)
* Laozao (fermented sweet rice)
* White sugar
* Goji berries (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1-2 people.

Total:

* Water 300 ml * portions
* Mini tangyuan 250 grams * portions
* Laozao 50 grams * portions
* Goji berries 5 * portions
* White sugar

## Steps

1. Pour the water into a pot and bring to a boil
2. Add the mini tangyuan and cook for 8 minutes
3. Add the laozao and goji berries and cook for another 2 minutes
4. Ladle into bowls, add sugar to taste, and stir well
5. Eat

## Additional Notes

* Adjust the amount of sugar to taste
* For a thicker soup, use up to 100 ml less water
* If you like softer goji berries, cook them together with the tangyuan
