# How to Make Omurice

Ketchup fried rice wrapped in a tender, smooth omelette, with rich textures, sweet and tangy and appetizing—a classic Japanese home-style dish. It's rich in protein, carbohydrates and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. Wrapping the omelette takes a bit of technique, but the overall difficulty is moderate and beginners can try it. It takes about 25 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 695 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs (free-range eggs recommended for better flavor)
- Onion
- Carrot
- Corn kernels
- Green peas (optional)
- Ham sausage or chicken breast
- Cooked rice
- Ketchup
- Cooking oil (vegetable oil recommended)
- Milk (optional, makes the omelette more tender)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Eggs 2
- Onion 30g
- Carrot 30g
- Ham sausage or chicken breast 50g
- Corn and peas 30g in total
- Cooked rice 200g
- Ketchup 20ml
- Cooking oil 15ml
- Milk 10ml (mixed with the eggs)

## Steps

1. Dice the onion, carrot and ham sausage or chicken breast. Set aside
2. Heat the pan, add 10ml of cooking oil, and wait 10 seconds for it to heat up
3. First add the diced onion and stir-fry for 1 minute until fragrant, then add the carrot, corn and peas and stir-fry for another 2 minutes
4. Add the diced ham sausage or chicken breast and stir-fry until it changes color
5. Add the rice and break it up, then add 20ml of ketchup and stir-fry evenly. The fried rice is done; set aside
6. Beat the eggs and stir in 10ml of milk
7. Add 5ml of cooking oil to the pan, pour in the egg, and gently swirl the pan so it spreads evenly
8. Heat on low; when the surface of the egg is half set, put the fried rice in the center
9. Use the spatula to fold the omelette over the rice into an oval shape
10. Gently slide it onto a plate with the spatula, tidy the shape, and decorate the top with a little ketchup

## Additional Notes

- Day-old rice won't stick to the pan as easily and tastes better.
- Adding milk to the beaten eggs makes the omelette more tender and smooth.
- Keep the heat low when making the omelette so the bottom doesn't burn.
- If you're not good at wrapping, you can just put the fried rice on a plate and lay the omelette on top.
