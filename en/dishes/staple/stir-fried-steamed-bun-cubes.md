# How to Make Stir-fried Steamed Bun Cubes

Stir-fried mo (chao mo) is a savory, crispy home-style flour-based dish with northern Chinese flavor. Cubes of steamed bun are fried until golden, crisp outside and soft inside, with cumin and five-spice powder making it even more flavorful. You can add egg for protein and green onion for freshness, for balanced nutrition. The difficulty is moderate, beginners can pick it up quickly, and it's ready in about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 767 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Steamed buns (mantou; day-old and slightly hard is better)
* Salt
* Oil
* Ground cumin
* Five-spice powder
* Green onion
* Eggs (optional)

## Calculation

* Steamed buns 2 (day-old and slightly hard is better)
* Salt 3g
* Oil 20ml (peanut oil or sesame oil is better)
* Ground cumin 3g
* Chili powder 3g
* Five-spice powder 3g
* Green onion 2 stalks
* Eggs (optional, 2)

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. Cut the steamed buns into small cubes or slices.
2. If using eggs, crack them into a bowl and beat (you can add 1g each of salt and five-spice powder, or leave them out and add them while frying).
3. Pour the egg over the bun pieces and mix well; don't use too much egg.
4. Heat the wok on high and add the cooking oil (if you're worried about damaging a stainless steel wok, add the oil first and heat it until hot)
5. Add the bun cubes and stir-fry evenly.
6. Turn the heat down and fry until the bun cubes are golden.
7. Add the salt, pepper and five-spice powder.
8. Finally add the chopped green onion and toss a few times.
9. Turn off the heat and serve.

## Additional Notes

- Control the heat while frying to keep it from burning
- If the heat is too high, turn it down, and drizzle oil or sprinkle water around the edge of the wok—spread it out rather than pouring it all in one spot
- Add other spices to taste (such as Nande seasoning)
- If you like it crispy, turn the heat to the lowest, stir-fry a bit longer, and after turning off the heat let it sit in the hot wok a while before serving for extra crunch
- You can also add extras like minced garlic or garlic sprouts to taste, fried separately until fragrant and then mixed in
