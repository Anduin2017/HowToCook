# How to Make Chive Pockets

Chive pockets (jiucai hezi) are a traditional Chinese pastry with a golden, crispy crust and a fresh, savory, juicy filling. Garlic chives paired with shrimp and egg are rich in vitamins and high-quality protein. They're simple and beginner-friendly, taking about 2.5 hours from mixing the dough to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 794 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Garlic chives
- Shrimp
- Eggs
- Sesame oil
- Salt
- Flour

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Garlic chives 500g
- Shrimp 100g
- Eggs 3
- Sesame oil 10ml
- Salt 5g
- Flour 250g

## Steps

1. Put the flour in a large bowl, add water, mix into a smooth dough, and let it rest for 30 minutes.
2. Wash and chop the garlic chives, add the beaten eggs and 5g of salt, and mix well.
3. Divide the dough into small pieces, roll into thin rounds, and fill with the chives, shrimp and egg.
4. Heat a pan, add cooking oil, add the filled pockets, and fry until golden on both sides, about 3-4 minutes.
5. Plate, let cool slightly, and enjoy.

## Additional Notes

- Add other ingredients like dried tofu to taste.
- Watch the heat when frying, so they're not burnt outside and raw inside.
