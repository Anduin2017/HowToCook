# How to Make Cola Fried Rice

This dish uses cola instead of sugar for seasoning, giving a distinctive caramelized, slightly sweet flavor, with a rich sauce coating every grain of rice—a creative home-style fried rice. Eggs and ham sausage provide high-quality protein and energy, and with rice as the staple it's balanced and filling. The steps are intuitive and suitable for beginners; it's usually on the table in about 20 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 913 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cooked rice
- Cola (regular or sugar-free)
- Eggs
- Ham sausage (or luncheon meat, optional)
- Oil
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Doubanjiang (optional)
- Chopped green onion
- Ground pepper (white or black)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Cooked rice 200 g
- Cola 160 ml
- Eggs 3
- Ham sausage 20-100 g
- Oil 25 ml
- Light soy sauce 15 ml
- Dark soy sauce 7.5 ml (add another 5 ml if using sugar-free cola, and another 5 ml if not using doubanjiang)
- Doubanjiang 7.5 ml
- Oyster sauce 5 ml
- Chopped green onion 5 g
- Ground pepper 1 g

## Steps

1. Heat the pan, add 25 ml of oil, add two eggs, and fry until the bottoms are completely set
2. Flip and fry until both sides are completely set
3. Turn off the heat, take out the eggs, cut them into small pieces of 2-5 cm² with scissors, and put them back in the pan (or just break them up with the spatula)
4. Turn the heat back on, pour in the cola, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, doubanjiang and oyster sauce, and stir well
5. Heat until 1/3 of the liquid remains, then add the rice and ham sausage and stir-fry
6. Once evenly mixed, turn to low heat, make a well in the center of the rice, crack in 1 egg, cover, and cook for 2 minutes
7. Open the lid and stir-fry until the third egg is cooked through, sprinkle with chopped green onion and pepper, and serve

## Additional Notes

- Regular (sugared) cola tastes better
- If you like it drier, stir-fry a bit longer
- If you find it a hassle, put all 3 eggs in at the first step and skip making a well for the extra egg
