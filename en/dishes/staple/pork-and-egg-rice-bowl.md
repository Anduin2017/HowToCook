# How to Make Pork and Egg Rice Bowl

This dish has a rich savory sauce, balanced salty-sweet flavor, browned minced pork and tender eggs—very satisfying over hot rice, a quick home-style dish. It's rich in high-quality protein and essential amino acids for a quick energy boost. It's simple and intuitive; beginners can pick it up easily, and it takes only about fifteen minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1822 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cooked rice
- Eggs
- Minced pork
- Dark soy sauce
- Light soy sauce
- Vinegar
- Shallot oil (optional)
- Green onion
- Oil
- Sugar

## Calculation

One portion is a meal for one person; scale proportionally for more people, but keep the size of your pan in mind.

One portion:

- Cooked rice 240g
- Eggs 4
- Minced pork 300g
- Dark soy sauce 10ml
- Light soy sauce 25ml
- Vinegar 20ml
- Shallot oil (optional) 10g
- Green onion 10g
- Oil 30ml
- Sugar 15g

## Steps

1. Cook the rice; usually one cup of the measuring cup that comes with the rice is 240g
2. Add 30ml of oil to the pan
3. Add the minced pork and fry over medium heat until lightly browned on both sides
4. Crack the eggs into the pan without breaking them up, and cover
5. Mix a sauce: in a bowl, stir together the dark soy sauce, light soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and shallot oil in the amounts listed
6. Open the lid, pour the sauce into the pan, and wait three minutes
7. Turn off the heat and put the pork and eggs on top of the rice
8. Safety check, then enjoy your rice bowl

## Additional Notes

- If you like it sweeter, add another 10g of sugar
- Be sure to turn off the gas after cooking to avoid danger
