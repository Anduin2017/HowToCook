# How to Make Stir-fried Liangfen

![Finished stir-fried liangfen](../../../dishes/staple/炒凉粉/chaoliangfen.jpg)

Stir-fried liangfen (starch jelly) is a specialty snack from Shanxi and Shaanxi: smooth and tender, savory and slightly spicy, with garlic and soy bean paste aromas. Liangfen is mainly made of starch and provides plenty of carbohydrates for a quick energy boost. The difficulty is moderate, suitable for beginners with some basics. It takes about 15 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 567 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Liangfen (starch jelly)
- Corn oil
- Garlic
- Green onion
- Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Salt
- Thirteen-spice powder
- Medium-coarse chili flakes
- Mineral water

## Calculation

The ingredients below are for one person; decide how many portions to make before you start. One portion is just enough for one person.

- Liangfen 500g
- Corn oil 10ml
- Minced garlic 10g
- Green onion 15g
- Doubanjiang 15g
- Light soy sauce 20ml
- Dark soy sauce 10ml
- Salt 5g
- Thirteen-spice powder 5g
- Medium-coarse chili flakes 15g
- Mineral water 20ml

## Steps

1. Cut the liangfen into mahjong-tile-sized cubes
2. Over low heat, add oil to the wok. When it's slightly hot, add the minced garlic and fry until fragrant, then add the doubanjiang and fry until the oil turns red
3. Add the liangfen cubes and stir-fry for 10 seconds
4. Add the light soy sauce for flavor and dark soy sauce for color, stir-fry evenly, then add the chili flakes and keep stir-frying evenly
5. Add the salt and thirteen-spice powder and stir-fry for 10 seconds
6. Add the prepared mineral water and stir-fry for another 10 seconds. When the sauce has thickened, turn off the heat and plate
7. Sprinkle with chopped green onion. Done

  ![Finished stir-fried liangfen](../../../dishes/staple/炒凉粉/chaoliangfen.jpg)

## Additional Notes

- When choosing liangfen, pea starch liangfen is best; if that's not available, sweet potato starch or other kinds also work
