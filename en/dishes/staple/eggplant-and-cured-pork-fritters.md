# How to Make Eggplant and Cured Pork Fritters

![Finished eggplant and cured pork fritters](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/茄子肉煎饼.jpg)

This home-style fritter combines the savory saltiness of cured pork with soft eggplant: crispy outside and soft inside, a rice-flour staple full of rustic flavor. It's rich in carbohydrates and high-quality protein, and eggplant adds dietary fiber and vitamins, a balanced mix of meat and vegetables. The difficulty is medium—you need to get the batter consistency and frying heat right—but beginners can succeed by following the steps. It takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 904 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Rice flour (flour ground from rice)
* Wheat flour
* Egg
* Cooked cured pork (larou)
* Eggplant (buy the long kind; the rounder the better)
* Cooking oil
* Salt

## Calculation

Makes 1 portion; 1 portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

* Rice flour 250g
* Wheat flour 50g
* Egg 1
* Cooked cured pork 100g
* Eggplant 1 (about 10-15cm long)
* Cooking oil 10-15ml
* Salt 1-2g

## Steps

1. Peel the eggplant and slice it, and slice the cured pork. Set aside

![Eggplant and pork slices](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/1茄片肉片.jpg)

2. Add 250g of rice flour (flour ground from rice), 50g of wheat flour and 1 egg to a basin in that order

![Rice flour 250g](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/2米粉250g.jpg)

![Rice flour, wheat flour and egg](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/3米粉面粉鸡蛋.jpg)

3. Stir with chopsticks while adding water (**the water adjusts the consistency**) to mix the rice flour, wheat flour and egg into a batter. Stop adding water when the batter clings to the eggplant and pork slices without dripping off, then put all the eggplant and pork slices in the batter and coat thoroughly

![Mixing](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/4混合.jpg)

4. Add **10-30ml** of cooking oil to a frying pan and turn on low heat

![Heating the oil](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/5起锅烧油.jpg)

5. Use chopsticks or a spoon to put the battered eggplant and pork slices in the pan, fry until golden on both sides, then fry for another **3-6 minutes** (**the oil will decrease while frying; add more as needed**)

![Start frying](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/6开始煎.jpg)

6. Sprinkle with salt, toss evenly, and plate

![Salted and ready to serve](../../../dishes/staple/茄子肉煎饼/7撒盐准备起锅.jpg)

## Additional Notes

* Fry over low heat
