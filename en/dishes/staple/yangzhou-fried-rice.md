# How to Make Yangzhou Fried Rice

This is an upgraded fried rice with plenty of ingredients: separate, fluffy grains of rice, tender egg, shrimp, luncheon meat and diced vegetables for distinct layers of texture—a classic staple of Huaiyang cuisine. It's rich in high-quality protein, carbohydrates and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. There are many steps, but they're intuitive and fairly friendly to beginner cooks; you just need patience with the prep. It takes about 1 hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1769 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cold cooked rice (drier is better)
- Eggs
- Frozen peeled greasyback shrimp
- Canned luncheon meat
- Green peas
- Carrot
- Corn kernels (optional)
- Green onion
- Oil
- Salt

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1-2 people.

Per portion:

- Cold cooked rice 500g
- Eggs 2-3
- Frozen headless, peeled greasyback shrimp 10-15
- Canned luncheon meat 150g (Shanghai Maling ham luncheon meat recommended, 340g per can; use half a can at a time)
- Green peas 30g
- Carrot 30g
- Corn kernels 30g
- Green onion 1 stalk
- Oil 30-40ml
- Salt 12-15g

## Steps

1. Dice the carrot 0.2cm*0.2cm*0.2cm and set aside
2. Dice the luncheon meat 0.2cm*0.2cm*0.2cm and set aside
3. Separate the green onion into white and green parts, cut each into 0.25-0.5cm pieces, and keep them separate
4. Crack the eggs into a bowl, beat evenly, and set aside
5. Boil the carrot, peas and corn until cooked, scoop them out and set aside (don't pour out the water)
6. Boil the shrimp until cooked, scoop out and set aside (you can pour out the water now)
7. Heat the wok and add oil; see the hot-wok, double-oil method in [Learn Stir-frying and Pan-frying](../../tips/learn/stir-frying-and-pan-frying.md)

  - For the second addition of oil, use 20-30ml. Once that oil is hot, slowly pour in the eggs (keep the stream from the bowl about 0.5cm wide) and don't stir

8. As soon as the eggs set, scoop them out and set aside
9. Add the luncheon meat, peas, carrot, corn and shrimp to the wok, stir-fry for 1-2 minutes, then plate and set aside
10. Rinse the wok with water to wash away any debris, making sure the inside is clean (oil is fine but no bits)
11. Heat the wok with oil (10ml) and fry the green onion whites until fragrant
12. Turn to low heat (if the oil is too hot, you can turn off the heat for 1-2 minutes), add the rice, and quickly smash and toss it with the spatula, making sure the rice is evenly coated in oil and the grains are separate
13. Add the eggs and keep smashing so the eggs break up and mix fully with the rice
14. Turn to high heat, add all the other prepared ingredients, and stir-fry quickly for 1-2 minutes
15. Sprinkle in the salt and stir-fry until fully mixed
16. Sprinkle in the green onion greens and stir-fry for 1 minute
17. Turn off the heat and plate
