# How to Make Wuhan Hot Dry Noodles

Hot dry noodles (reganmian) are one of Wuhan's most iconic snacks, part of Hubei cuisine. Chewy, smooth alkaline noodles are coated in thick sesame paste and topped with pickled long beans, dried radish and minced meat—savory, slightly spicy and rich. It mainly provides carbohydrates and protein, and sesame paste is also rich in calcium. It's quick and easy; beginners can pick it up easily, and it takes only about a dozen minutes from boiling the noodles to mixing them.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1341 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Alkaline noodles made specially for hot dry noodles
* Green onion
* Pickled long beans
* Minced meat
* Garlic water
* Meat broth
* Dried radish
* Sesame paste
* Chili oil
* Ground pepper
* Soy sauce
* Salt
* Chicken bouillon powder

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Alkaline noodles for hot dry noodles (250g)
* Green onion (10g)
* Pickled long beans (20g)
* Minced meat (30g)
* Garlic water (30ml)
* Meat broth (30ml)
* Dried radish (50g)
* Sesame paste (40ml)
* Chili oil (0-10ml)
* Ground pepper (0-10g)
* Soy sauce (5ml)
* Salt (3g)
* Chicken bouillon powder (0-3g)

## Steps

1. Bring water to a boil, add the alkaline noodles, blanch for 25 seconds, and scoop them out
2. Sprinkle with salt, chicken bouillon and ground pepper
3. Thin the sesame paste with 90ml of water, stir well, then add it
4. Add 5ml of soy sauce, plus 30ml each of meat broth and garlic water
5. Add the dried radish, minced meat, pickled long beans and chopped green onion
6. Mix well and dig in

## Additional Notes

* Add chili oil to taste
