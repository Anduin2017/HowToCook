# How to Make Pizza Dough

![Example: green and red pepper ham pizza](../../../dishes/staple/披萨饼皮/001.jpeg)

This pizza dough uses a cold overnight ferment, giving a crust that's soft with a bit of chew—a home-style Western bake. It mainly provides carbohydrates and a little good fat. The process is simple but requires patience while the dough ferments, so it's fairly beginner-friendly. From prep to baking, including the overnight chill, it takes about 10-14 hours, so prepare it a day ahead.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 537 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

**Ingredients**

- All-purpose flour
- Water (warm)
- Angel dry yeast
- Salt
- Olive oil
- Granulated sugar

**Tools**

- Oven
- Parchment paper
- Pizza stone (better if you have one; an ordinary baking tray also works)
- Rolling pin (not required)

## Calculation

An 8 ~ 9 inch pizza needs about 125g of flour, about one person's worth (just about enough for an adult with a big appetite)

Ingredient ratio

```text
Flour : Water : Olive oil : Yeast : Salt : Sugar
100   : 70    : 7         : 1     : 0.6  : 0.6
```

Example

To make 4 crusts at once, you need:

- Flour 125g x 4 = 500g,
- Water 70 x 5 = 350g,
- Olive oil 7 x 5 = 35g,
- Yeast 1 x 5 = 5g,
- Salt 0.6 x 5 = 3g,
- Sugar 0.6 x 5 = 3g

## Steps

1. Dissolve the yeast in the prepared warm water, stirring briefly. Set aside
2. Take the flour and add the salt, olive oil and sugar in turn
3. Combine the water and flour, stirring as you add the water until it's all in
4. Stir until no dry flour is visible
5. Put it in a container about three times the size of the dough, seal, and refrigerate (4 degrees). **Wait 8~12 hours; generally, make it at night and it's ready the next day**
6. Check that the dough has risen **to about twice its original size, which means it's ready**
7. Take the risen dough, divide it evenly into four, and cover each with plastic wrap. Set aside
8. Dust the board with a bit more flour than usual and get ready to knead
9. Since it's a fairly wet dough, it's less sticky once coated in flour. No need to knead much; once the surface is a bit smooth, it's enough
10. Stretch it by hand or roll it flat with the rolling pin. It doesn't have to be round, as long as it's an even thickness and fits in your oven
11. Lay down parchment paper, put the crust on it, add your chosen toppings to taste, and sprinkle with shredded cheese
12. For fruit pizza, bake with top heat at 180 degrees and bottom heat at 220 degrees for 16 minutes
13. For meat and vegetable pizza, bake with top heat at 200 degrees and bottom heat at 230 degrees for 18 minutes
14. Drizzle with mayonnaise or any other sauce you like, and enjoy~

## Additional Notes

- Pay close attention to the water ratio
- Oven quality varies a lot; the first time, watch how the heat affects the pizza
- Flour not specifically labeled as cake or bread flour is generally all-purpose flour
- Watery fruits such as watermelon or oranges can't be used as toppings
