# How to Make Hot and Sour Fern Root Noodles

Hot and sour fern root noodles are smooth, chewy, tangy and spicy—a home-style cold dish that can also be eaten as a staple. Fern root noodles are rich in starch and dietary fiber, providing energy for the body. This dish is especially simple and very beginner-friendly, taking only 10 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 318 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Fern root noodles (juegen fen)
* Shaanxi chili oil (youpo lazi)
* Soy sauce
* Zhenjiang black vinegar
* Bird's eye chili (optional)
* Garlic (optional)
* Green onion (optional)
* Salt
* Sugar
* A fairly deep pot

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Soy sauce : vinegar : chili oil = 3 : 2 : 2 (the exact amount of sauce depends on how many noodles you have; this ratio just keeps the flavor heading in the right direction)
* If the sauce tastes too mild, add 2 to 5 grams of salt
* For a more savory sauce, add 2 grams of sugar

## Steps

### Cooking the Noodles

1. Fill the pot about 3/5 deep with water and bring to a boil
2. Once boiling, add the fern root noodles and cook over medium-low heat for 8 minutes
3. Take them out

### Mixing the Sauce

1. Add the soy sauce, vinegar and chili oil in the ratio above
2. Dip a chopstick in and taste
3. If the soy sauce flavor is a bit strong, add the prepared salt
4. If it's not savory enough, add the prepared sugar
5. Stir thoroughly until most of the granular seasonings have dissolved

### Plating and Finishing

1. Get a bowl
2. Add the sauce you just mixed
3. Rinse the noodles in cold water and put them in the sauce
4. Mix thoroughly
5. Chop the prepared green onion, garlic and bird's eye chili and sprinkle over the noodles
6. Done (｡･∀･)ﾉﾞ

## Additional Notes

* Green onion, ginger and garlic are extra seasonings; they add flavor, but it's fine without them
* The exact amounts of sugar and salt depend on your taste; add as you see fit
* A bowl is recommended for serving, since a plate makes eating the noodles a bit harder
* The overall flavor is fairly spicy; if you can't handle spice, use less chili oil and skip the bird's eye chili
