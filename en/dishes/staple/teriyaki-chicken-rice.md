# How to Make Teriyaki Chicken Rice

Teriyaki chicken rice is a sweet-and-savory dish that goes great with rice: the chicken thighs are pan-fried until fragrant and then coated in a rich sauce, tender and full of flavor. Chicken thigh is rich in high-quality protein, and broccoli and carrot add dietary fiber and vitamins. It takes a little deboning skill, but overall it's not complicated, and beginners can succeed by following the steps. It's on the table in about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 1670 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Chicken legs
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- Honey (optional)
- Cracked black pepper
- Ground black pepper
- Salt
- Broccoli (if you don't have any, use mild green peppers instead)
- Carrot
- Dark soy sauce
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Cooking oil
- Water
- Cooked rice

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for 1-2 people.

Per portion:

- Chicken legs 500 g (about 3)
- Cooking wine 15 ml
- Light soy sauce 30 ml
- Honey 15 ml (if you don't have any, use 20 g of white sugar + 5 g of corn starch + 0.5 ml of lemon juice or vinegar instead)
- Cracked black pepper 5 g
- Ground black pepper 5 g
- Salt 2.5 g
- Broccoli 50 g
- Carrot 50 g
- Dark soy sauce 12 ml
- Garlic 10 g (smashed, no need to mince)
- Ginger 10 g
- Cooking oil 15-25 ml
- Water 30 ml
- Cooked rice 300-400 g

## Steps

1. Take a chicken leg and place it skin side down on the cutting board
2. Use the tip of the knife to gently cut along the outline of the bone, from one end to the other
3. Slowly push the meat away with your fingers or the back of the knife to expose the bone. If you hit membrane, cut it with the knife tip
4. Once the meat is fully separated, hold one end of the bone, twist gently and pull it out
5. Repeat these steps to debone all the chicken legs
6. Marinate the deboned chicken with ground black pepper, cracked black pepper, salt and ginger slices for 5 minutes
7. In a bowl, mix the cooking wine, light soy sauce, honey, dark soy sauce and water
8. Blanch the vegetables for 1-2 minutes until cooked, then remove
9. Heat the pan with 15 ml of oil and fry the smashed garlic until fragrant
10. Add the chicken and fry over medium-low heat until golden on both sides; if the pan seems too dry, add 5-10 ml more oil
11. Add the prepared sauce, cover, and braise over medium-low heat (low on a gas stove) for 5-10 minutes until cooked through and the sauce is thick and bubbling
12. Cut into pieces, arrange on the rice with the vegetables, drizzle with the sauce from the pan, and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- If using the sugar and starch substitute, make sure the sugar and starch are completely dissolved before adding to the pan, or it will burn easily
- Stir every 3-5 minutes while braising to avoid hot spots
- If the sauce forms big bubbles, turns dark brown, or fine caramel crystals appear at the edges, turn off the heat immediately
