# How to Make Microwave Chinese Sausage Claypot Rice

![Microwave Chinese sausage claypot rice](../../../dishes/staple/微波炉腊肠煲仔饭/微波炉腊肠煲仔饭.png)

This is a simple, quick Cantonese-style staple: the rice soaks up the fat from the Chinese sausage and becomes rich and savory, paired with crisp greens and carrot for layered textures. It's rich in carbohydrates, protein and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. It's made in the microwave, very beginner-friendly, and ready to enjoy in about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 1039 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Tools
  - Microwave
  - 2 large bowls (microwave-safe bowls recommended)
  - 1 small bowl
- Ingredients
  - Rice 200 ml
  - Chinese sausage 1
  - Egg 1
  - Carrot 1
  - Salt
  - Oil 15 ml
  - Light soy sauce 10 ml
  - Green onion 1 stalk

## Calculation

1 serving.

## Steps

1. Rinse the rice and put it in the `rice bowl`, add 400ml of water, and **cover with a lid**
2. Microwave on high for `6` minutes, preparing the ingredients while the rice cooks

  - Slice the sausage
  - Wash the greens
  - Slice the carrot
  - Chop the green onion
  - In the `greens bowl`, put the greens and carrot slices, add 10 ml of oil and 5 g of salt
  - In the `small bowl`, add 10 ml of light soy sauce and 5 ml of oil

3. After 6 minutes, take the bowl out with a towel or oven mitts; the rice should be about 80% cooked
4. Arrange the sliced sausage on top of the rice and microwave on high for another `2` minutes
5. Take out the sausage rice, put in the `greens bowl`, and microwave on high for `4-5` minutes
6. Arrange the greens on top of the sausage rice, crack in an egg, and microwave on high for another `40-60` seconds to taste
7. Take out the sausage rice; it's basically done now.
8. Put in the `small bowl` and microwave on high for `30` seconds
9. Drizzle the heated soy sauce over the sausage rice and sprinkle with chopped green onion
10. Eat any extra greens dipped in soy sauce
