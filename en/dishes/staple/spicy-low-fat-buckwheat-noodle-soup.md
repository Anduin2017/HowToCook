# How to Make Spicy Low-Fat Buckwheat Noodle Soup

The numbing, spicy broth combines hot pot base with peanut butter and milk for a rich, smooth taste with a distinct mala flavor. Paired with high-fiber buckwheat noodles and fresh vegetables, it's low in calories and filling, good for when you're trying to lose fat. There's no difficulty at all and no cooking skills needed; it takes about 20 minutes before you can dig into the pot.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 790 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Seasonings: hot pot soup base, peanut butter, whole milk, light soy sauce, chili oil, vinegar, Sichuan pepper oil
* Ingredients: semi-dried buckwheat noodles, baby napa cabbage, lettuce
* Washing basin, small pot 18cm across

> Suggested purchases:
> Buckwheat noodles: Shishanghui buckwheat noodles on Pinduoduo, 400g per pack for 4.9 yuan, makes 4 portions
> Hot pot base: Xiaolongkan traditional clear-oil hot pot base on Taobao tastes best, 29.8 yuan a pack, makes 20 portions
> Peanut butter: Yingge peanut butter on Pinduoduo, 24.8 yuan for two jars, makes 68 portions
> Sichuan pepper oil: Wufeng Lihong Sichuan pepper oil on Pinduoduo, 400ml, 32 yuan for two bottles, makes 80 portions
> Others: anything; it won't affect the taste

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Semi-dried buckwheat noodles 100g
- Baby napa cabbage 8 leaves (150g in total)
- Lettuce 6 leaves (80g in total)
- Hot pot base 25g
- Peanut butter 15g
- Whole milk 150ml
- Light soy sauce 6ml
- Chili oil 10ml
- Vinegar 20ml
- Sichuan pepper oil 10ml
- Water 500ml

## Steps

1. Wash the baby napa cabbage and lettuce and set aside
2. Pour 500ml of water into the pot, turn on high heat, add the buckwheat noodles and baby napa cabbage, and wait for the water to boil
3. Once boiling, turn to low heat and add the hot pot base, peanut butter, milk, light soy sauce and chili oil; once it boils again, cook for 5 minutes
4. Add the lettuce and cook for another 2 minutes
5. Add the vinegar and Sichuan pepper oil, turn off the heat, and eat straight from the pot.

## Additional Notes

* Be sure to choose semi-dried buckwheat noodles for the best texture
* Feel free to add other things, such as hot pot balls or egg dumplings
* The hot pot base, peanut butter and milk are a must—they're the big three of the flavor
* If you can't handle sour, leave out the vinegar
