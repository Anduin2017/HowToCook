# How to Make Stir-fried Spaghetti with Beef

![Spaghetti](../../../dishes/staple/炒意大利面/a.jpg)

This stir-fried spaghetti is soft yet springy, with fatty beef slices and a rich sauce—a home-style staple with a Western touch. It's rich in carbohydrates and protein for a quick energy boost. It's simple; beginners can master it easily, and it takes about 30 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 406 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Spaghetti
- Thinly sliced fatty beef
- Ketchup / black pepper sauce (choose one)
- Rapeseed oil (other vegetable oils also work)

## Calculation

- Spaghetti 50 grams / person
- Fatty beef 5 slices / person
- Cooking oil 5ml / 50 grams of spaghetti

## Steps

1. Add 250 grams of water / person
2. When the water boils, add the spaghetti and cook over medium heat for 15 - 20 minutes (this pasta is usually fairly firm; taste before taking it out—if the center is still a bit hard, keep cooking)
3. Drain the pasta and set aside on a plate
4. Heat the wok, add cooking oil, and when the oil is medium-hot, add the pasta and stir-fry for one minute (if it's too dry, add a little water)
5. Add 10 grams of ketchup, the beef and 2g of salt, and keep stir-frying for one minute
6. Serve
