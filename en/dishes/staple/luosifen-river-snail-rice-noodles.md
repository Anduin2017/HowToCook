# How to Make Luosifen (River Snail Rice Noodles)

Luosifen is a specialty snack from Liuzhou, Guangxi: a fresh, rich broth that's sour, spicy and refreshing, with smooth, chewy rice noodles. Its flavor combines the savoriness of river snail broth, the distinctive fermented taste of sour bamboo shoots and the kick of chili oil, in distinct layers. As a staple, the rice noodles provide plenty of carbohydrates, the snail meat and toppings add some protein, and the sour bamboo shoots help whet the appetite and aid digestion. It's simple—just cook the noodles and season in order—and very beginner-friendly. From boiling water to serving takes as little as ten minutes, a quick way to sort out a meal.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 1109 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

> For a home-style recipe, making luosifen from scratch starting with river snails and sour bamboo shoots isn't realistic, so this recipe uses a packaged luosifen kit.

### Ingredients

- One packaged luosifen kit, which should contain:
  - Rice noodles
  - Snail meat packet (may be in the toppings packet)
  - Soup base packet
  - Topping packets such as sour bamboo shoots, peanuts, tofu skin and wood ear mushrooms
  - Seasoning packets such as vinegar and chili oil

### Tools

- Pot

- Induction cooktop / stove

- A pair of chopsticks

## Calculation

- In my experience, one packaged luosifen kit is enough for one person per meal (even though the pack looks big)
- Water 1L

## Steps

1. Add water to the pot and bring to a boil
2. Add the rice noodles and cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring with chopsticks so they don't stick together
3. Add the soup base packet, to taste
4. Add some of the topping packets, such as wood ear mushrooms, peanuts and snail meat (these need to cook a while to absorb flavor)
5. Add the seasoning packets, to taste
6. Stir, then scoop into a bowl
7. Add the remaining topping packets, such as sour bamboo shoots and tofu skin (these shouldn't soak in the broth too long)
8. Enjoy

## Additional Notes

- For chewier noodles, shorten the cooking time in step 2
- If you want to add a fried egg to your luosifen, see a fried egg tutorial
- Choose toppings according to your taste
