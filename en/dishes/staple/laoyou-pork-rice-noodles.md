# How to Make Laoyou Pork Rice Noodles

![Laoyou pork rice noodles - preview image 1](../../../dishes/staple/老友猪肉粉/老友猪肉粉.jpg)

Laoyou ("old friend") pork rice noodles are sour, spicy, savory and fragrant, with smooth rice noodles and the distinctive rich flavor of fermented black beans and sour bamboo shoots—a typical Nanning home-style taste. Pork provides high-quality protein, and rice noodles give a quick carbohydrate boost. The overall difficulty is medium; remember to soak the rice noodles in advance. Once practiced, it's on the table in about forty minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 927 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Rice noodles
- Pork
- Sour bamboo shoots
- Chopped chilies (duojiao)
- Fermented black beans (douchi)
- Garlic
- Cooking wine
- Light soy sauce
- White sugar
- Rice vinegar
- Salt
- Oil
- Potato starch
- Ground pepper

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Rice noodles (250g; remember to soak in 50-degree warm water for half an hour)
- Pork (50g)
- Sour bamboo shoots (50g)
- Chopped chilies (15g of chilies after chopping; adjust to taste)
- Fermented black beans (30g)
- Garlic (10g)
- Cooking wine (10-20ml)
- Light soy sauce (15ml)
- White sugar (5g; leave it out if you don't like sugar)
- Rice vinegar (5ml)
- Salt (5ml)
- Oil (15ml)
- Potato starch (15ml)
- Ground pepper (10ml)

## Steps

1. Put all the pork in a bowl and season with cooking wine, salt, light soy sauce, starch and ground pepper. Set aside
2. Heat the wok without oil, add all the sour bamboo shoots, and stir-fry until dry, leaving a space in the middle of the dried shoots
3. Add 10ml - 15ml of cooking oil, all the garlic, chopped chilies and fermented black beans into the space in the middle of the shoots, push everything to the middle and fry until fragrant
4. Add all the seasoned pork, then add 10ml of light soy sauce and stir-fry for one minute
5. Add 5ml of rice vinegar, 10ml of light soy sauce and 450ml of water and bring to a boil
6. Once the water boils, add the rice noodles soaked in warm water, cook for another 3 minutes, and serve

## Additional Notes

- Remember the rice noodles must be soaked to taste good; unsoaked noodles taste terrible!
