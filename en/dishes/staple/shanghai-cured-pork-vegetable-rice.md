# How to Make Shanghai Cured Pork Vegetable Rice

This is a home-style staple full of Shanghai alleyway charm (xianrou caifan): the mellow aroma of cured pork and the sweetness of greens, brought together by lard, soak into every grain of rice. There's crispy rice crust on the bottom while the vegetable stems stay crisp and tender, with distinct layers. Greens are rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, and cured pork provides protein and energy, a balanced mix of meat and vegetables. It's not complicated, but you need to master the trick of adding the leaves separately, which is a little challenging for beginners. It takes about 1 hour from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 847 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Rice
- Greens (Shanghai bok choy recommended)
- Cured pork (lightly salted)
- Winter bamboo shoots (optional)
- Lard
- Cooking wine (optional)
- White sugar (optional)
- Ground white pepper (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is enough for 3 people.

Per portion:

- Rice 300 g
- Water 310 ml (300 ml for firmer rice, 325 ml for softer; add an extra 20 ml if adding bamboo shoots)
- Greens 400 g
- Cured pork 150 g
- Winter bamboo shoots 100 g
- Lard 15 g
- Cooking wine 15 ml
- White sugar 0-3 g
- Ground white pepper 1 g

## Steps

1. If adding bamboo shoots, slice them thinly, put them in cold water and boil for 10 minutes to remove the astringency
2. Cut the cured pork into 1 cm cubes
3. Put the cured pork and bamboo shoots in a cold wok with 10 g of lard, the cooking wine and sugar, and stir-fry over medium-low heat until translucent and sizzling
4. Chop the greens, keeping the stems and leaves separate: cut the stems into 0.5 cm squares and the leaves into rectangles 2-3 cm long and 1-1.5 cm wide
5. Add the stems to the wok and stir-fry until jade green
6. Rinse the rice, put it in the rice cooker, and add the water
7. Spread the fried cured pork and stems over the rice
8. Start the normal rice cooking mode. In the last 10 minutes, open the lid and quickly spread the leaves on top
9. When done, let it rest for 5 minutes, then drizzle with 5 g of lard and the white pepper and mix vigorously

## Additional Notes

- Spread the fried cured pork and stems on top of the rice—never stir them in while the rice cooks
- Try not to buy "jiaxiang xianrou" (hometown cured pork); expect it to be very salty. If it's not lightly salted cured pork, mix in some diced fresh pork belly, use less cured pork, or cut off the lean part and soak it in 5% sugar water for 20 minutes.
