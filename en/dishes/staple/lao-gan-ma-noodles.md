# How to Make Lao Gan Ma Noodles

A spicy, appetizing quick home-style noodle dish: chewy noodles coated in the fermented black bean chili aroma of Lao Gan Ma chili crisp, with soy sauce for savoriness—simple but full of flavor. As a staple it quickly provides carbohydrates and energy, and capsaicin helps stimulate the appetite. There's no barrier at all; beginners can handle it easily, and it takes only about ten minutes from boiling water to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 502 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Noodles
* Lao Gan Ma chili crisp
* Soy sauce

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

* Water 1 liter
* Noodles 120 grams * portions
* Lao Gan Ma 15ml * portions
* Soy sauce 5ml * portions

## Steps

1. Pour the water into the pot and bring to a boil
2. Add the noodles evenly to the pot
3. Stir while cooking so the noodles don't clump together
4. When you can lift a noodle with chopsticks and it slides off naturally, wait another 30 seconds, then turn off the heat
5. Transfer the noodles to a bowl
6. Add the Lao Gan Ma and soy sauce according to the amounts above
7. Mix the noodles, Lao Gan Ma and soy sauce evenly with chopsticks
8. Eat
