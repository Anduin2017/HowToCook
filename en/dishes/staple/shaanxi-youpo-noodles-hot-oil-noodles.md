# How to Make Shaanxi Youpo Noodles (Hot Oil Noodles)

This traditional northwestern noodle dish is known for its chewy pulled noodles and the spicy aroma created by pouring hot oil over them—an everyday staple known to everyone in Shaanxi. The noodles are rich in carbohydrates, and with greens and bean sprouts they provide quick energy in a sensible combination. The whole process is simple and clear, very beginner-friendly, taking only about 30 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 771 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Fresh noodles (pulled noodles, chemian, are best) ![Pulled noodles](../../../dishes/staple/陕西油泼面/chemian.png)
- Salt
- Green onion
- Garlic
- Dried chili flakes (or Qin chili flakes)
- Cooking oil
- Light soy sauce
- Zhenjiang black vinegar
- Leafy greens (bok choy or spinach)
- Pot

## Calculation

Usually 3-4 noodle strips per person (4 for a bigger appetite); add more for a large appetite

- Salt 2g
- Green onion 1 stalk
- Garlic 3 cloves
- Dried chili flakes 15g
- Cooking oil 50ml
- Light soy sauce 10ml
- Black vinegar 5ml
- Leafy greens 50g
- Bean sprouts 50g (optional)

## Steps

### Prep

1. For pulled noodles, press lightly along the top and bottom with a rolling pin (or by hand if you don't have one), hold the left and right ends with both hands, and gently pull them apart
2. Chop the green onion and mince the garlic. Set aside
3. Wash the greens and bean sprouts (if using). Set aside

### Cooking and Seasoning

1. Fill the pot with plenty of water, bring to a boil on high heat, and add 1g of salt
2. Add the noodles, stirring gently with chopsticks to keep them from sticking, and once they float, cook for another 1-2 minutes
3. In the last minute of cooking, add the greens and bean sprouts to blanch
4. Scoop out the noodles, greens and bean sprouts, drain, and put them in a bowl
5. Top the noodles with the light soy sauce, black vinegar, chopped green onion, minced garlic and dried chili flakes in that order; you can also add some sesame seeds

### Pouring the Oil

1. Pour the cooking oil into a wok, adding Sichuan peppercorns, star anise, cassia bark and bay leaves (if using), fry over low heat until fragrant, then remove the spices
2. When the oil reaches 7/10 heat (just starting to smoke), quickly and evenly pour it over the chili flakes in the bowl—you should hear a loud sizzle
3. Mix all the seasonings and noodles thoroughly with chopsticks and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- Technical summary
  - The choice of chili flakes is key; Shaanxi Qin chili flakes are recommended—fragrant but not too hot. One of the "eight wonders of Shaanxi": chili oil counts as a dish 😂
  - The oil temperature is the key to success: too low and the chili isn't fragrant, too high and it burns easily
  - If you like meat, add minced meat to make saozi youpo noodles
  - Traditional Shaanxi youpo noodles emphasize "one green, two white, three red, four green"—the color combination of the noodles, garlic, chili and green onion
