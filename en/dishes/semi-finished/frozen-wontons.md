# How to Make Frozen Wontons

Frozen wontons have thin wrappers, tender fillings and a clear, savory broth—a convenient home-style flour-based dish. They mainly provide carbohydrates and protein for a quick energy boost. It's simple and very beginner-friendly, done entirely in a rice cooker, taking about 55 minutes from boiling the water to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 540 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* One bag of unexpired frozen wontons (even better if it comes with a seasoning packet)
* Rice cooker (Xiaomi smart rice cooker recommended)
* Salt (if the frozen wontons have no seasoning packet)
* Chicken bouillon powder (if the frozen wontons have no seasoning packet)
* Ground pepper (if the frozen wontons have no seasoning packet)
* Sesame oil (if the frozen wontons have no seasoning packet)
* Cilantro, 1 stalk (optional)

> Note: frozen wontons may be made differently depending on the cooking tool. Only the "rice cooker" method is described here.

## Calculation

* One person can usually eat 12～20 wontons per meal
* With all the wontons in the rice cooker, use 2~3 times the amount of water that would just cover them (one person's wontons need about 600ml of water)

## Steps

### Boiling the Water

1. Pour the water into the rice cooker and run it on the stew or cook mode for 35 minutes. When you lift the lid, the water should be boiling.

### Adding the Wontons

1. Carefully put the frozen wontons into the water, taking care not to burn yourself.
2. You can give them a quick rinse in water before putting them in the rice cooker.
3. If the wontons came with a seasoning packet, add it to the water now as well.

### Cooking the Wontons

1. Close the rice cooker and run the same stew or cook mode for 20 minutes.

### Serving

1. Ladle all the wontons into a bowl along with enough water to cover them.
2. If you didn't add a seasoning packet earlier, season now with salt, chicken bouillon, ground pepper and sesame oil to taste.
3. You can also sprinkle 5~8 cilantro leaves on top for flavor (only for people who don't mind cilantro).

## Additional Notes

* After cooking, you can also eat them with a poached egg ([Microwave Sunny-Side Up Egg](../breakfast/microwave-sunny-side-up-egg.md)).
