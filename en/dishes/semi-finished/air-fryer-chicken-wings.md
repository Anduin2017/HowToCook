# How to Make Air Fryer Chicken Wings

![Chicken wings](../../../dishes/semi-finished/空气炸锅鸡翅中/鸡翅中_0.jpg)
![Chicken wings](../../../dishes/semi-finished/空气炸锅鸡翅中/鸡翅中_1.jpg)

This is a convenient, quick home-style dish: the wings are crispy outside and tender inside, with their own rich, fatty aroma, and better suited to your taste than fast-food ones. Chicken wing midjoints are rich in high-quality protein and fat, with plenty of energy. It's very beginner-friendly; hands-on time is just a few minutes, and you can be eating in about forty minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 504 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Required: chicken wing midjoints (Tyson New Orleans-style wing midjoints are recommended; on sale on JD, a bit over 30 yuan for a 454g bag of about 12. I've also tried Shangxian and Sunner brands, which weren't as convenient. Tyson's come pre-marinated; if you have time, buy unmarinated ones and marinate them yourself.)
- Optional: dried basil (sprinkled on purely for looks)
- Optional: Yunnan Danshan dipping powder (instead of barbecue seasoning)

## Calculation

- Chicken wing midjoints 6 (Tyson New Orleans-style; other brands such as Sunner may be larger, so adjust to your appetite)

## Steps

1. Take the wings out of the freezer, place them wing side down in a foil tray, sprinkle with dried basil, cover with plastic wrap, and let them thaw naturally for 20 minutes
2. Sprinkle with dried basil and air fry at 200°C for 10 minutes
3. Flip, sprinkle with dried basil, and air fry at 200°C for 7 minutes

## Additional Notes

- An air fryer with good temperature control is recommended, or they may burn

If you follow this guide and find problems or possible improvements to the process, please open an Issue or Pull request.
