# How to Make Lazy Egg Tarts

![Finished egg tarts](../../../dishes/semi-finished/懒人蛋挞/懒人蛋挞.png)

This dessert uses ready-made tart shells and egg tart filling: a flaky, crisp shell and smooth, tender filling with caramelized spots—the easiest version of a classic Cantonese dim sum. The main ingredients, eggs and milk, provide high-quality protein and calcium, making it a good family snack. You just assemble and bake, so it's very friendly even to complete beginners. It takes about 40 minutes from preheating the oven to out of the oven.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 137 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- 1 oven (one with separate top and bottom heat is best, but not required)
- 1 pair of oven mitts
- 1 box of egg tart filling and 1 box of tart shells, bought online (large supermarkets nearby such as Carrefour or Walmart also have them)

## Calculation

Per portion:

- 1 tart shell
- About 10ml of egg tart filling, ideally filling 4/5 of the shell

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees for 10 minutes
2. Place the tart shells on the baking tray and pour about 10ml of filling into each. The exact amount depends on the size of the shells; usually fill them 4/5 full
3. Put the tray in the oven and bake with top and bottom heat at 190 degrees for 10 - 20 minutes. If you want the caramelized brown spots on top to appear faster, set the top heat higher, usually 200 - 210 degrees
4. They're done when the filling has caramelized brown spots and the shells are fully puffed and glistening with butter

## Additional Notes

- While baking, you can adjust the time according to the size of the brown spots if needed
- Add 10g of grated cheese to each tart for cheese egg tarts~
