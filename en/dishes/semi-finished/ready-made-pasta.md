# How to Make Ready-Made Pasta

This is a quick Western-style meal made with store-bought ready-made pasta: springy, chewy noodles with a rich ready-made sauce. It's mainly carbohydrates for a quick energy boost, with some protein. The difficulty is extremely low, foolproof even for beginners, and it takes only about 2 minutes from turning on the heat to plating.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 578 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* 1 pack of ready-made spaghetti (Pulmuone brand recommended)
* 50 ml of water
* Frying pan or microwave

> Different types of pasta have different cooking times; this is the standard for spaghetti.

## Calculation

- 2 people, 1 meal: 520g (by weight of the ready-made product)

> Using the above, calculate the amount you need according to your needs (including but not limited to your usual appetite, mood and whether you exercised before the meal).

## Steps

### Method 1 - Frying Pan

1. Heat the pan
2. Pour 50 ml of water into the pan
3. Add the noodles and stir-fry for 1 minute
4. Pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute
5. Plate

### Method 2 - Microwave

1. Put the noodles on a "microwave-safe" plate
2. Pour the included sauce over the noodles
3. Add 50 ml of water
4. Microwave at 700W for 2 minutes
5. Take out, toss well, and enjoy

## Additional Notes

- Traditional spaghetti is very long. Shorter spaghetti became popular from the second half of the 20th century, typically 25–30 cm long.
- "Spaghettoni" is thicker spaghetti. "Spaghettini" is thinner. "Capellini" is even thinner.
