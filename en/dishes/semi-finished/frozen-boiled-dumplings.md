# How to Make Frozen Boiled Dumplings

This is a Chinese home-style flour-based dish made quickly with store-bought frozen dumplings: thin wrappers, generous filling, savory and delicious. With dipping sauces of vinegar, ginger, garlic and so on, it gains extra layers of flavor. As a meal, it provides plenty of protein and carbohydrates, nutritious and filling. There's nothing difficult about it; beginners can make it easily, and it takes only about a quarter of an hour.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 502 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* One bag of unexpired frozen dumplings

## Calculation

* One person can usually eat 7～10 dumplings
* Each dumpling needs about twice its own volume of water (when the dumplings go into the pot, the water should be one to two times as deep as the dumplings are tall, i.e. if the dumplings are 1 high, the water is 1~2 high)

Use the above to calculate the proportions of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. Over medium heat, pour water into the pot and wait for it to boil.
2. Put the dumplings into the pot.
3. You can give them a quick rinse in water before putting them in.
4. After adding the dumplings, you can stir the water with a ladle or spatula, but be careful not to hit the dumplings, so they don't stick to the pot and tear, or stick to each other and leave the stuck parts undercooked.
5. You don't need to stir often—on average 3 seconds every `30` seconds. Once the dumplings float, you can stop.
6. When the dumplings float and the water boils again, scoop one out with a ladle and check. If the wrapper is still undercooked, add 80ml of cold water with the ladle to cool the water down, then keep cooking until it boils again. Repeat this checking and stirring; at most two additions of water will cook them through.
7. Once all the dumplings are floating (about 8 minutes after adding them), scoop them into a plate or bowl with a spatula or slotted spoon, and eat.
8. After eating, wait for the water in the pot to cool, pour it out and scrub the pot promptly with dish soap; otherwise the flour cooked in the water will form a sticky residue on the sides of the pot after a while.

## Additional Notes

There are some supplementary variations for this dish, including but not limited to:

Add the following ingredients:

* Black vinegar 10ml
* Ginger, one small piece, 50 grams
* Sesame oil, 2 drops
* Garlic / garlic paste, 3 cloves per person

* Consider eating with black vinegar. Recommended amount: 10-20ml.
* Consider julienning the ginger and mixing it with 20ml of black vinegar in a small bowl as a dipping sauce for richer flavor.
* Consider adding 1~3 drops of sesame oil to the black vinegar and stirring as a dipping sauce.
* Consider adding pounded garlic paste to the black vinegar and stirring as a dipping sauce. (Garlic breath will linger; if you need to talk face to face with others after the meal, skip it or clean your mouth)
