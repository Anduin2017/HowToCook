# How to Make French Fries

![French fries](../../../dishes/semi-finished/炸薯条/炸薯条.jpg)

French fries are crispy outside and soft inside, golden and salty—a widely loved fast-food snack. Potatoes provide carbohydrates and dietary fiber, but fries are fairly high in calories whether deep-fried or air-fried. Using frozen fries and an air fryer, it's simple and beginners can do it easily. It takes about 25 minutes from preheating to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 1132 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- 1 bag of frozen fries (McCain recommended)
- 1 air fryer (if you like them crispy, avoid no-name brands)

> Note: fries may be made differently depending on the cooking tool. Only the "air fryer" method is described here.

## Calculation

- As a main dish: 400g per person per meal (by weight of the frozen product)
- As a snack: 1/4 of the main dish amount ±50g per person per meal

> Using the above, calculate the amount you need according to your needs (including but not limited to your usual appetite, mood and whether you exercised before the meal).

## Steps

### Opening the Fries

1. When opening a large bag of frozen fries, make the opening small; this effectively reduces frost forming on the fries during long storage.

### Preheating the Air Fryer

1. Plug it in and preheat at 200℃ for 5 minutes.
2. Preheating makes sure the air fryer is already at cooking temperature when the food goes in.
3. Note: only take the fries out after preheating; don't let the fries soften before frying.

### Frying

1. Take out the fries, put them in the air fryer, and cook at 200℃ for 20 minutes.
2. When taking out the fries, note that frozen fries already have oil on them, so if you're going to multitask (say, playing a game on your computer) while they cook, use tongs to handle them.
3. At 5~10 minutes, you can pull out the basket and shake it so the fries cook evenly and don't stick together.

### Crisping (Optional)

1. At 10~15 minutes, pull out the basket and spray a layer of oil over the now-dry fries, covering about 2/3 of their surface.

### Taking Out and Plating

1. If you like them crispy, take them out and dance around holding the basket so air passes over the fries, then plate; if you like them soft, plate directly. Even better with a dipping sauce or drizzled with sauce.

## Additional Notes

- Ketchup, mayonnaise, honey mustard, [garlic soy sauce](../condiment/garlic-soy-sauce.md)... the path to leveling up your fries lies in exploring sauces and sauce combinations.
