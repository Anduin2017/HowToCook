# How to Make Air Fryer Lamb Chops

![Air fryer lamb chops - preview image 1](../../../dishes/semi-finished/空气炸锅羊排/羊排.jpg)

This dish is super lazy to make. The air fryer renders out the excess fat, and the result is crispy outside, tender inside, with a strong garlic aroma; the flavor mainly depends on the quality of the lamb itself. Lamb is rich in high-quality protein and trace elements such as iron and zinc, which help warm the body and strengthen the constitution. The difficulty is moderate and beginners can pick it up easily. Hands-on time is only 10 minutes, and including marinating it's on the table in about 40 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 473 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Required: lamb chops (recommended: Grand Farm New Zealand frenched lamb shoulder chops from JD, 500g/bag with three chops, 42 yuan on sale)
- Required: black pepper steak seasoning mix (lazy)
- Required: garlic sauce (Chuanwazi brand recommended, also because it's lazy)
- Required: paper towels
- Optional: butter (buy the small single-serve packs on JD)
- Optional: barbecue seasoning
- Optional: dried basil
- Optional: air fryer rack (with a rack you need less oil and the bottom cooks more easily, but it's a hassle to wash. Without it, the chops absorb more flavor. Your choice)

## Calculation

- Lamb chop: 1 chop, about 160g
- Black pepper steak seasoning 5g
- Garlic sauce 20g
- Butter 1 small pack (10g) or barbecue seasoning 20g

## Steps

1. Put the lamb chops in a bowl and rinse off the blood with water
2. Pat the chops dry with paper towels, rub both sides with the black pepper seasoning and garlic sauce, and marinate for 20 minutes
3. Put the rack in a foil bowl, put the lamb chops on the rack, sprinkle with dried basil, put the butter or barbecue seasoning on top, and air fry at 180° for 10 minutes
4. Flip the chops, sprinkle with dried basil, put butter (scooped from the foil bowl) or barbecue seasoning on top, and air fry at 180° for 5 minutes (add a bit more time if you like; the time here is one that won't burn them)

## Additional Notes

- An air fryer with good temperature control is recommended, or they may burn
