# How to Make Frozen Tangyuan

![Frozen tangyuan](../../../dishes/semi-finished/速冻汤圆/速冻汤圆.jpg)

Frozen tangyuan (glutinous rice balls) make a quick, convenient microwave dessert: soft, chewy skins with sweet fillings, a traditional Chinese snack. They mainly provide carbohydrates for a quick energy boost. It's extremely easy; beginners can do it, and it takes only about 6 minutes from start to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 568 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Frozen tangyuan
- Microwave

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Frozen tangyuan: 11. The number depends on the size of your bowl. Make sure the highest tangyuan is no higher than 5mm below the rim of the bowl.

## Steps

1. Take out the frozen tangyuan and put them in a bowl.
2. Pour in boiling water until the tangyuan are submerged.
3. Microwave on high for 4 minutes.
4. If the tangyuan have all absorbed water and swelled, they're cooked.
5. If not, heat for another minute.

## Additional Notes

- Put the tangyuan in first and then the hot water, not the other way around; otherwise dropping the tangyuan into hot water may splash and burn you, and you can't control the water level, so it may overflow.
- If the bowl is too hot to take out of the microwave, use a towel, or with some microwaves you can take it out together with the turntable.
