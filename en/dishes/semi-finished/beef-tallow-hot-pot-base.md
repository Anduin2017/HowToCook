# How to Make Beef Tallow Hot Pot Base

This Chongqing hot pot base uses a large amount of beef tallow as its foundation, combined with ciba chili paste and a variety of spices. The resulting base is numbing, spicy, rich and intensely aromatic—the soul of cooking tripe, duck intestines and other hot pot ingredients. Beef tallow and lard provide plenty of energy, and chilies and Sichuan peppercorns warm the body and stimulate the appetite, but it's very high in fat, so eat it in moderation. The process requires precise control of oil temperature and stir-frying heat, which is challenging for beginners. It takes about 1 hour from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★★

Estimated calories: 585 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

### Ingredients

- Beef tallow 4500 g
- (Vegetable oil or rapeseed oil) 1000 ml
- Pure lard 500 g
- Doubanjiang (Pixian) 1000 g
- Ciba chili paste 3000 g
- Mature ginger (sliced) 250 g
- Scallion (cut into segments) 100 g
- Onion (shredded) 100 g
- Garlic (sliced) 200 g
- Fermented black beans (chopped) (Yongchuan) 10 g
- Doumuzi (fermented soybean starter) 140 g
- Red Sichuan peppercorns 150 g
- Aged oil ? ground spice mix 100 g : whole spice mix 150 g
- Malt powder (for meaty aroma) 12.5 g
- Baijiu (52% ABV) 150 ml
- Aged oil ?? dried chili powder 15 g

### Spices

- Sand ginger (shannai) 5 g
- Star anise 10 g
- Black cardamom (caoguo) 10 g
- Cinnamon 10 g
- Cloves 5 g
- Fennel seeds 10 g
- Angelica root (baizhi) 10 g
- Long pepper (bibo) 5 g
- Xiangcao herb 10 g
- Xiangguo spice 10 g
- Hawthorn 10 g
- Dried tangerine peel 5 g
- Lemongrass 10 g
- Amomum (sharen) 10 g
- White cardamom 10 g
- Bay leaves 10 g

#### Ground Spice Mix

Grind the spices above in a grinder into granules of about 4 mm and set aside.

#### Whole Spice Mix

Rinse the spices above and set aside.

### Tools

- Grinder

## Calculation

- Each batch of ingredients makes 7.5 kg of hot pot base / aged hot pot oil

## Steps

1. Put the wok on high heat, add the beef tallow and heat to 8/10 heat (240±10°C), then add the `mature ginger, scallion, onion and garlic (100g each)` and fry until dry (to absorb the off-odors, i.e. the gamy smell of the tallow), then scoop them out and discard.
2. Add the `(vegetable oil || rapeseed oil) and pure lard`, and when the oil temperature drops to 5/10 heat (150±10°C), add the `ciba chili paste` and keep stir-frying for 5-8 minutes.
3. Add the `doubanjiang` and break it up, then switch to **medium-low heat** and stir-fry slowly until the solids look slightly whitish and sandy (making a rustling sound).
4. When the oil looks cherry red, add the `ginger slices (150g) and garlic (100g)` and fry until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
5. Add the `fermented black beans and doumuzi` and fry until fragrant, then add the `red Sichuan peppercorns and fennel seeds` and fry until fragrant.
6. (For aged oil) add the ground spice mix at this point
7. Add the `malt powder` and stir to break it up, then add the `baijiu` and stir.

### As a Hot Pot Base

1. Transfer to containers and let rest in a cool environment (10-20°C) for 5 days before use for the best results.

### As Aged Hot Pot Oil

1. Transfer to a container, add the `dried chili powder`, stir well, and let rest for 24 hours before making the **aged oil**.
2. Put the base into a pot and add boiling water equal to 3/5 of the total, then bring to a boil over high heat (base: 2/5, boiling water: 3/5).
3. Once boiling, foam will appear on the surface; skim it all off.
4. Switch to **medium-low heat** and simmer slowly to draw out the flavor (about 25-30 minutes), then strain out the solids.
5. Once the **oil and water have separated** in the container, skim all the **oil** off the surface into another container.
6. Pour the **oil** skimmed in the previous step back into a **clean pot**, and cook it until all the water in the oil has been **boiled off**, then transfer to a container. This is the **aged hot pot oil**.

## Additional Notes

- **Warning: dry your hands before working! Water getting into the hot oil can cause an explosion and severe burns!**
