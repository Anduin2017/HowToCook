# How to Make Wood Ear Mushroom Salad

Wood ear mushroom salad: since the supplies handed out included a lot of dried goods, and wood ear mushrooms are a fairly healthy food, this is a good way to use them. The cold-dressed method is also relatively simple.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 129 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Dried wood ear mushrooms (fresh/rehydrated ones also work, but they must not have been soaked long ago—they must be fresh)
* Garlic cloves
* White sugar
* Bird's eye chili
* Salt
* Sesame oil
* Light soy sauce
* Vinegar
* Wasabi (optional)

## Calculation

Per portion (1 person):

* Dried wood ear: 20g / fresh wood ear: 120g
* Garlic: 2-3 cloves
* Bird's eye chili: 2
* Salt: 2 g
* Sugar: 5-10g (to taste)
* Light soy sauce: 15ml
* Vinegar: 15ml
* Sesame oil: 5ml
* Wasabi: (about 2cm)

## Steps

1. Rehydrate the dried wood ear in about 400ml of water for about 45 minutes. (Skip this step if using fresh wood ear)
2. Remove the tough root ends from the rehydrated wood ear (see images 4, 5, 6) and rinse thoroughly.
3. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add the wood ear, and boil on high heat for 1.5-2 minutes.
4. Mince the garlic and bird's eye chilies and put them in a bowl (medium to large), then add the salt, sugar, light soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil and wasabi in the amounts above.
5. Drain the wood ear and add it to the bowl from the previous step.
6. Mix thoroughly and plate.

## Additional Notes

* Adjust the seasonings to taste; if you don't like wasabi, leave it out.
* It tastes even better after the wood ear cools and absorbs the flavor, about 15 minutes.
