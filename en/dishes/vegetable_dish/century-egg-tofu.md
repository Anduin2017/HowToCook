# How to Make Century Egg Tofu

Century egg tofu (pidan doufu) is a refreshing, appetizing cold home-style dish: the century egg (songhuadan) is bouncy and smooth, the silken tofu melts in your mouth, and the savory, slightly tangy flavor goes great with rice. Tofu is rich in high-quality plant protein and calcium, and century eggs provide iron, vitamin A and other nutrients; together they are easy to digest and absorb. It requires almost no skill, beginners can make it easily, and it takes only about 5 minutes from prep to table.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 566 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Century eggs (pidan)
- Silken tofu (lactone tofu)
- Light soy sauce
- White sugar
- Vinegar (Zhenjiang black vinegar recommended)
- Sesame oil (optional)
- Chili oil (optional)
- Crushed peanuts (optional)
- Chopped green onion (optional)
- Cilantro (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Century eggs 2
- Silken tofu 1 box
- Light soy sauce 15 ml
- White sugar 2.5g
- Vinegar 15 ml
- Sesame oil 15 ml
- Chili oil 10 ml
- Crushed peanuts 10 g
- Chopped green onion 15 g
- Cilantro 1 sprig

## Steps

1. First peel the century eggs and cut each into four wedges.
2. Cut the silken tofu into cubes or slices and place in the middle.
3. Mix the light soy sauce, sugar, vinegar, sesame oil and chili oil into a sauce and drizzle it over.
4. Finally, optionally sprinkle with crushed peanuts, chopped green onion and cilantro.

## Additional Notes

- Blanching the tofu first removes its beany taste
- When cutting the century eggs, rub a little sesame oil on the knife to keep them from sticking
- Reference: [涼拌皮蛋豆腐 by 低配版小當家 - iCook](https://icook.tw/recipes/437531)
