# How to Make Tomato Tofu Soup

A light, savory home-style thick soup (tanggeng): sweet, tangy and appetizing, with a smooth texture. Tomatoes are rich in vitamin C and lycopene, and with eggs and tofu it provides high-quality protein for balanced, easily absorbed nutrition. It's simple—just cutting, stir-frying and boiling—so beginners can master it easily, taking about 15 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 242 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Tomato
* Egg
* Tofu
* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Starch
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Green onion
* Ginger
* Boiling water

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Tomato 1
* Egg 1
* Tofu 100 g
* Cooking oil 5 ml
* Salt 2 g
* Starch 5 g
* Chicken bouillon powder 2 g
* Green onion 0.5 stalk
* Ginger 1 slice
* Boiling water 350 ml

## Steps

1. Dice the tomato small, crack the egg into a bowl and beat, and cut the tofu into cubes. Set aside
2. Heat oil in the wok, add the ginger slice, after 5 S add the tomato and stir-fry for 30 S
3. Add the boiling water to the wok and bring to a boil; after 60 S pour in the beaten egg and the tofu
4. When the soup returns to a boil, add the starch slurry and stir 2 times in one direction
5. Add the chicken bouillon, salt and green onion, and serve after 30 S
