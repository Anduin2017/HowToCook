# How to Make Steamed Pumpkin

A home-style steamed dish that keeps the pumpkin's natural flavor, preserving as much of its natural sweetness and nutrition as possible: soft, smooth and sweet, good as either a dessert or a staple. Pumpkin is rich in β-carotene and dietary fiber, good for eyesight and digestion. It's extremely simple—beginners can do it easily—and takes only 15-20 minutes from cutting to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 80 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Pumpkin
- Steamer

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1-2 people.

Per portion:

- Pumpkin 300 g
- Drinking water 1000 ml (for the steamer)

## Steps

1. Wash the outside of the pumpkin and remove the pulp and seeds.
2. Cut the pumpkin into slices about 2 cm thick and set aside.
3. Add 1000 ml of drinking water to the steamer pot.
4. Arrange the pumpkin slices evenly on a plate.
5. When the water in the steamer boils, put the plate of pumpkin in.
6. Cover and steam over high heat for **15-20 minutes**, until the pumpkin is soft and a chopstick goes through easily.
7. Turn off the heat and carefully take out the plate.

## Additional Notes

- Different varieties of pumpkin vary in sweetness and texture. Older pumpkins are usually sweeter and more floury.
- Pumpkin skin is nutritious; if you like, you can steam it with the skin on, but wash it thoroughly.
- Steaming time depends on the size and thickness of the pieces and the variety of pumpkin. Testing with a chopstick is a good way to check doneness.
