# How to Make Spinach and Egg Stir-fry

Spinach and egg stir-fry is a light, refreshing quick home-style dish: tender spinach and fragrant eggs, a popular flavor in both northern and southern China. Spinach is rich in iron, folate and vitamins, and eggs provide high-quality protein and lecithin; together they help nourish the blood and build strength. The whole dish is simple and perfect for cooking beginners, taking only about 15 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 350 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Spinach
- Eggs
- Cooking oil
- Salt

## Calculation

For 1 person:

- Spinach 350g
- Eggs 2
- Cooking oil 15ml
- Salt 5g

## Steps

1. Trim the roots off the spinach, wash it, put it in a basket, and blanch
2. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat
3. Heat the wok and add 10ml of oil
4. When the oil is hot, pour in the beaten eggs and stir-fry over medium heat for 15 seconds—first fry it into an omelet, then cut it into small pieces with the spatula
5. Turn off the heat, transfer the egg pieces to a plate, and don't wash the wok
6. Turn the heat back on and add 5ml of oil; when the oil is hot, add the spinach, stir-fry over high heat for 15 seconds, then add the egg pieces back and toss evenly
7. Add 5g of salt and 100ml of drinking water and stir-fry over high heat for 10 seconds
8. Turn off the heat and plate
