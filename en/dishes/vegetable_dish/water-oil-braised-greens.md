# How to Make Water-Oil Braised Greens

Water-oil braised greens are a simple, quick home-style side: boiling water with a few drops of oil and seasoning makes leafy greens tender and glossy, fresh with a light oil aroma. This method helps improve absorption of fat-soluble vitamins, making the greens more nutritious than eating them raw. It's easy and very beginner-friendly, usually taking only 10 minutes from washing to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 92 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Oyster sauce (optional)
* Leafy green vegetables

## Calculation

Leafy greens contain a lot of water and may look very bulky while you prep them. They shrink quickly once heated.

* Leafy greens: 300g ~ 500g

## Steps

1. Wash the vegetables
2. Add 150ml of water to the pot and bring to a boil. (The water doesn't need to fully cover the vegetables)
3. Add 3g of salt
4. (Optional) Add 3ml of oyster sauce
5. Add 2ml of cooking oil
6. Add the vegetables, toss once, then cover and braise for 1 minute
7. Plate

## Additional Notes

* If you're not sure about the saltiness, add less salt at first and taste before serving, adjusting as needed
* You can use chicken stock or bone broth instead of water for better flavor
* Don't braise for too long
