# How to Make Edamame Boiled with Dried Plums

Edamame boiled with dried plums (huamei) is a sweet, tangy, appetizing home-style side dish: fresh and tender, with the natural fruity aroma of plums. Edamame is rich in plant protein and dietary fiber, which help replenish energy and aid digestion. It's very simple and beginners can pick it up easily; the whole process takes just 35 to 45 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 454 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Edamame (in pods)
* Huamei (salted dried plums)
* Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Edamame 300 g
* Dried plums 6
* Salt 2 g

## Steps

1. Add the salt to water and soak the edamame for 15 minutes
2. Bring water to a boil, add the edamame and dried plums, and boil for 20-30 minutes
3. Take out and dig in
