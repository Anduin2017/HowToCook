# How to Make Silky Scrambled Eggs

![Finished silky scrambled eggs](../../../dishes/vegetable_dish/炒滑蛋/炒滑蛋.jpg)

Silky scrambled eggs (chao huadan) are a classic home-style dish, tender and smooth with a rich egg flavor and a light milky note. Eggs are rich in high-quality protein and various vitamins, nutritious and easily absorbed. It's very approachable; beginners can master it easily, and it takes only about 5 minutes from prep to plate—an ideal choice for refueling quickly when you're busy.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 391 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs (pasteurized eggs are best)
- Milk

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Eggs 4
- Milk 30ml
- Cooking oil 10ml

## Steps

1. Beat the eggs with the milk and 5ml of cooking oil until evenly mixed. Set aside
2. Heat a frying pan on high for about 30s and add 5ml of cooking oil
3. Heat for 30s, turn to low, and add the beaten eggs
4. Leave them in the pan for 5 seconds, then slowly push the egg from the edges toward the center with a spatula
5. Once the eggs are mostly set, turn off the heat and plate

## Additional Notes

- Finally, sprinkle with some black pepper or salt to taste.
