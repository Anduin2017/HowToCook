# How to Make Corn with Pine Nuts

Corn with pine nuts (songren yumi) is a sweet, crisp, quick home-style dish: crunchy, fragrant pine nuts and sweet corn, a widely popular northern Chinese home-style flavor. Corn and pine nuts together provide plenty of dietary fiber, unsaturated fatty acids and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. It's simple and beginners can handle it easily; it usually takes only about fifteen minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 580 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Corn kernels (sweet corn recommended)
* Toasted pine nuts
* Carrot (optional, for color)
* Cooking oil
* Granulated sugar
* Salt
* Starch
* Water

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Corn kernels 200 g (canned sweet corn, or cooked yourself)
* Toasted pine nuts 30 g
* Carrot 50 g (diced small; optional)
* Cooking oil 15 ml
* Granulated sugar 10 g
* Salt 1 g
* Starch 5 g
* Water 20 ml (for the starch slurry)

## Steps

1. Blanch the corn and diced carrot for 1 minute in advance, drain and set aside
2. Heat the wok, add cold oil, stir-fry the diced carrot briefly, then add the corn and stir-fry
3. Add the sugar and salt and stir-fry evenly
4. Mix the water and starch into a slurry, pour into the wok, and stir-fry quickly so the sauce thickens slightly
5. Add the toasted pine nuts and toss evenly
6. Plate

## Additional Notes

* Dry-toast the pine nuts over low heat in advance until lightly golden and crisp for even better flavor
* If using canned corn, drain it first so it doesn't release too much water while stir-frying
* Don't use too high a heat when stir-frying, so nothing burns and the pine nuts don't scorch
