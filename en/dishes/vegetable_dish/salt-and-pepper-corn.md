# How to Make Salt and Pepper Corn

A crispy, tender, savory snack with the dry-fragrant texture you often find in Sichuan restaurants, and the natural sweetness of corn when you bite in. Corn provides plenty of carbohydrates and dietary fiber, and sesame seeds add good fats and vitamin E. There are a few more steps but they're simple; it takes a little patience to shake off the water and coat the corn, and it's fairly beginner-friendly. It takes about 30 minutes from thawing to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 693 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Corn kernels
- Salt and pepper seasoning (jiaoyan)
- Sesame seeds
- Oil
- Starch
- Two plastic sieve baskets
- Several sheets of paper towel

## Calculation

This dish is the same size as a standard order at a Sichuan restaurant

- Corn kernels (bagged) 350g
- Starch (enough to completely cover the surface of the corn in the pan, 40 - 70g)
- Salt and pepper seasoning 10g
- Sesame seeds 10g

## Steps

1. The corn kernels come pre-shelled; just thaw them by soaking in warm water for 15 minutes or boiling for 5 minutes on the stove.
2. Take one basket—call it BoxA—line it with paper towels, and pour in the thawed corn.
3. Shaking shaking shaking! - until the paper towels are completely wet.
4. Take the second basket, BoxB, line it with paper towels, and pour all the corn from BoxA into BoxB.
5. Shaking shaking shaking! - until the paper towels are completely wet.
6. Repeat this several times, until there are no obvious water droplets on the corn but it's still moist.
7. Pour in plenty of starch, enough to completely cover the corn.
8. Shaking shaking shaking! - until the starch coats the corn.
9. Turn on the stove - put on the pan - add oil, covering the bottom of the pan as much as possible but not too much.
10. When the oil is at 8/10 heat, add the starch-coated corn.
11. Fry over medium heat for 30s first without stirring, or the starch will fall off.
12. Toss gently for 3 minutes, then serve.
13. The most important step: sprinkle with 3g of salt and pepper seasoning and the sesame seeds!
14. Your fragrant "salt and pepper corn" is ready

## Additional Notes

Don't shell the corn yourself—it's hard on your hands. Just order "pre-shelled corn kernels" from a grocery delivery app; it arrives in 30 minutes, then thaw in warm water.
If thawing in boiling water is slow, boil it! (Don't boil the corn itself—boil it in its package with one corner torn open
