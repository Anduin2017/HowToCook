# How to Make Sugared Tomatoes

![Sugared tomatoes - preview image 1](../../../dishes/vegetable_dish/糖拌西红柿/火山飘雪.jpg)

This is a home-style cold dish perfect for summer: sweet, tangy and refreshing, and easy to make. Tomatoes are rich in vitamin C, stimulate the appetite and cut through richness. Beginners can make it easily; the whole process (including chilling) takes about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 104 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Tomatoes
- White sugar
- Refrigerator

## Calculation

One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Tomatoes 2 (about 100g each, 200g in total)
- White sugar 20g

## Steps

1. Score an asterisk (*) shape into the tomato skin with a knife
2. Stick a chopstick into the stem end of the tomato and turn it over a gas flame for 10 seconds (or pour boiling water over it for 30 seconds), until the skin curls at the edges
3. Strip the tomatoes completely of their skins
4. Cut each tomato into eight pieces (cutting along the segments keeps more juice), remove the stem end, and set aside
5. Once everything is cut, arrange an even layer of tomato on a plate
6. Sprinkle with sugar, and repeat the previous step until all the tomatoes are used
7. Chill in the refrigerator for 10 minutes
8. Your plate of sugared tomatoes is ready: nutritious, delicious, sweet, tangy and refreshing—cooling and light on a summer day

## Additional Notes

Please be careful with knives during preparation.

If you follow this guide and find problems or possible improvements to the process, please open an Issue or Pull request.
