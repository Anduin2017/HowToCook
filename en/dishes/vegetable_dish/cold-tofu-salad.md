# How to Make Cold Tofu Salad

This cold dish is refreshing and tasty, rich in plant protein and calcium, low in fat and healthy. It's quick and simple; beginners can usually finish it in just 10 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 343 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Tofu (firm northern-style tofu recommended)
- Green onion
- Garlic
- Light soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Vinegar (optional)
- White sugar (optional)
- Chili oil (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Tofu 250 g (about one standard block)
- Green onion 10 g
- Garlic 2-3 cloves
- Light soy sauce 15 ml
- Sesame oil 5 ml
- Vinegar 5 ml (optional)
- White sugar 2 g (optional)
- Chili oil 5 ml (optional)

## Steps

1. Cut the tofu into 2 cm cubes and set aside.
2. Bring 500 ml of drinking water to a boil in a pot over high heat.
3. Add the tofu cubes and boil for **1-2 minutes** to remove the beany taste and firm up the tofu.
4. Drain the tofu, put it in a bowl, and set aside.
5. Wash and chop the green onion. Set aside.
6. Peel and mince the garlic. Set aside.
7. In a clean small bowl, add 15 ml of light soy sauce, 5 ml of sesame oil, 5 ml of vinegar (optional) and 2 g of sugar (optional).
8. Add the minced garlic.
9. Stir well until the sugar dissolves and the sauce is evenly mixed.
10. Pour the sauce evenly over the tofu.
11. Sprinkle with the chopped green onion.
12. Drizzle with 5 ml of chili oil if you like (optional).
13. Mix gently with chopsticks or a spoon and serve.

## Additional Notes

- For cold tofu salad, use firmer northern-style tofu, which doesn't break easily and has a better texture.
- For a lighter taste, leave out the vinegar and chili oil.
- Adjust the sauce ratio to taste.
