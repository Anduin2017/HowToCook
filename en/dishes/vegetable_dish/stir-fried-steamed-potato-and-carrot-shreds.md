# How to Make Stir-fried Steamed Potato and Carrot Shreds

This stir-fried potato and carrot "zhengcai" uses a "steam first, then stir-fry" method; the finished shreds are separate, soft and savory. Potatoes and carrots are rich in potassium and β-carotene for balanced nutrition. The whole process is quick and simple, very easy for beginners, taking only 20 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 610 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Potatoes
- Carrot
- Green onion
- Garlic cloves
- Salt
- Li Dachu brand stir-fry seasoning
- Li Dachu brand meat-flavor seasoning
- MSG
- Dried chili pepper

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Potatoes 320 grams
- Carrot 200 grams
- Green onion 20g
- Garlic 3 cloves
- Salt 1/2 teaspoon
- Li Dachu brand stir-fry seasoning 1/2 teaspoon
- Li Dachu brand meat-flavor seasoning 1/2 teaspoon
- MSG 1/4 teaspoon
- Dried chili pepper half

## Steps

1. Cut the carrot and potatoes into fine shreds (you can use a grater)
2. Coat the carrot and potato shreds in flour (the carrot shreds should look like little sausages, the potato shreds like noodles—separate strands that fall apart loosely when picked up by a dry hand without sticking)
3. Steam the **loosened and mixed** potato and carrot shreds for **5** minutes in an electric steamer / for **5** minutes on high heat after the steamer starts steaming
4. **Note: don't cover the lid right away; leave it uncovered at first, and once the steam starts rising, cover it (to prevent sticking)**
5. Turn on high heat, add oil, after 15 seconds add the green onion and stir-fry until fragrant / stir-fry for 40 seconds, then add the mixed potato and carrot shreds and stir-fry
6. After 20 seconds, add the Li Dachu stir-fry seasoning, then the Li Dachu meat-flavor seasoning, then the salt, then the MSG; after a moment, serve and turn off the heat
