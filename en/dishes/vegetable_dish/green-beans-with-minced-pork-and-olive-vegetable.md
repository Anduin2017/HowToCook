# How to Make Green Beans with Minced Pork and Olive Vegetable

![Finished green beans with minced pork and olive vegetable](../../../dishes/vegetable_dish/榄菜肉末四季豆/榄菜肉末四季豆.JPG)

Green beans with minced pork and olive vegetable (lancai) is a home-style stir-fry with strong Chaoshan flavor: savory, crisp and tender, and great with rice. Green beans are rich in dietary fiber and vitamins, minced pork provides high-quality protein, and Chinese olive vegetable brings a distinctive savory-salty flavor. The steps are clear and beginners can pick it up fairly quickly; just make sure the green beans are thoroughly cooked. It takes about thirty minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 720 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Green beans
* Pork belly
* Chinese olive vegetable (lancai)
* Garlic
* Bird's eye chili (leave out if you don't eat spicy food)

## Calculation

* Green beans 220g
* Pork belly 100g
* Olive vegetable 20g
* Garlic 10g
* Bird's eye chili 10g

## Steps

1. Wash the green beans, pull off the strings, and cut into evenly sized small pieces. Set aside.
2. Smash and mince the garlic. Set aside.
3. Cut the bird's eye chilies into evenly sized small pieces. Set aside.
4. Remove the skin from the pork belly and mince it. Set aside.
5. Heat the wok, add 20ml of oil to coat it, pour out the hot oil, then add 10ml of cold oil. This is the legendary "hot wok, cold oil", mainly to keep the minced pork from sticking.
6. If you don't have a spare oil container to pour into, you can skip coating the wok: just add the oil and then the minced pork, and stir-fry over low heat for two minutes to render out the lard.
7. Once the pork is fragrant, add the minced garlic, olive vegetable and bird's eye chili and stir-fry until fragrant.
8. Add the green beans and stir-fry over medium heat for at least 5 minutes. Be sure the green beans are **thoroughly cooked**, or they may cause food poisoning.
9. Once the green beans are cooked, drizzle 2ml of soy sauce around the edge of the wok, then add 2g of salt, 1g of chicken bouillon, 1g of ground pepper and 0.5g of sugar.
10. Toss to mix the seasonings evenly.
11. Serve and plate.
