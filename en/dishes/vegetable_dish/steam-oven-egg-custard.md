# How to Make Steam Oven Egg Custard

This is a smooth home-style steamed egg custard made quickly in a steam oven; the texture is adjustable—less water makes it springier, more water makes it more tender. It's rich in high-quality protein and various nutrients, and easy to digest and absorb. The difficulty is moderate; beginners can master it with a little care, and it takes only about 15 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 118 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Salt
- Cooking oil
- Light soy sauce (optional)
- Steam oven

## Calculation

For 1 person:

- Egg 1
- Salt 1g
- Cooking oil 5ml
- Light soy sauce / Weijixian 6ml (optional seasoning)

## Steps

1. Crack an egg into a bowl and beat
2. Add 1.0-1.5 times the egg's volume of 60-degree purified water to the bowl and stir well

  - Within the correct range, less water makes it springier and more water makes it more tender
  - Note: outside the range, too little water makes it too firm and too much makes it too runny

3. Add 1g of salt

  - Salt plays an important role in adding base flavor and helping it set

4. Add 5ml of cooking oil
5. Strain the egg mixture and skim off the foam (optional; without straining, the steamed custard will have bubbles and look less nice)
6. Make sure the steam oven's water supply is filled enough (if unsure, fill the tank)
7. Put the bowl of thoroughly mixed egg into the steam oven
8. Set to **100 degrees Celsius** and steam for **10 minutes**
9. Open the steam oven (note: steam bursts out the moment it opens, so open it slowly)
10. Take it out (you can add light soy sauce for seasoning)
11. Enjoy
