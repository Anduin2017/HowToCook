# How to Make Hot and Sour Shredded Potatoes

A classic hot, sour and appetizing home-style dish: crisp, bright and colorful, a widely loved quick stir-fry. Potatoes provide carbohydrates and potassium, and chili peppers are rich in vitamin C, helping stimulate the appetite. The steps are simple and very beginner-friendly, taking only about 20 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 374 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Potatoes
- Garlic
- Green pepper
- Red pepper
- Dried chili peppers
- Green onion
- Light soy sauce
- Aged vinegar (chencu)
- Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Potatoes 240g (the thinner and longer the shreds, the better)
- Garlic 4 cloves
- Green pepper 0.5
- Red pepper 0.5
- Dried chili peppers 3
- Green onion 1 stalk
- Light soy sauce 5ml
- Aged vinegar 10ml
- Salt 2g
- Cooking oil 10-15ml

## Steps

1. Peel the potatoes and cut into fine shreds (or use a grater).
2. Rinse the potato shreds in water to remove excess starch, then blanch them for 10 seconds. Drain and set aside.
3. Chop the green onion; smash and mince the garlic (divide into two equal parts); cut the dried chilies into small pieces; shred the green and red peppers.
4. Heat the wok, heat the oil over low heat, and add half the green onion (the white part), half the garlic and the dried chilies and stir-fry until fragrant.
5. Add the green and red peppers and stir-fry briefly, then add the potato shreds and stir-fry until they change color.
6. Add 5ml of light soy sauce and 10ml of aged vinegar, add the remaining half of the garlic and 2g of salt, and toss quickly.
7. Sprinkle with the remaining green onion before serving, toss, and plate.

## Additional Notes

- Be sure to rinse all the starch off the potato shreds, or they'll stick together
- Serve as soon as possible after adding the garlic and salt, to keep the garlic aroma and preserve the texture.
