# How to Make Northern Shaanxi Stewed Long Beans

This is a northwestern Chinese farmhouse-style stew: soft long beans, floury potatoes and a rich tomato sauce, tangy and appetizing. It's rich in dietary fiber, vitamins and carbohydrates for balanced nutrition. It's extremely simple, the amounts can be adjusted to taste, and it's almost impossible to fail—especially beginner-friendly. It takes only about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 191 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Long beans
- Potato
- Tomato
- Twisted green chili (luosijiao, optional)
- Salt
- Light soy sauce
- Five-spice powder
- Oyster sauce
- Green onion
- Ginger
- Garlic

## Calculation

Note: because ingredients vary individually, this recipe serves 2

- Long beans 300g * 2 people
- Potato 1 * 2 people
- Tomato 1 * 2 people
- Twisted green chili (optional) 2 * 2 people
- Salt 6g * 2 people
- Light soy sauce 6ml * 2 people
- Five-spice powder 3g * 2 people
- Oyster sauce 6ml * 2 people
- Green onion 3 rings
- Ginger 2g
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Chopped cilantro (optional), to taste

## Steps

1. Chop the green onion, mince the garlic, and shred the ginger. Set aside.
2. Remove the strings from the long beans and cut into *2-10cm* pieces. Set aside.
3. Peel the potato and cut into *1cm³* cubes. Set aside.
4. Peel the tomato and cut into *1cm³* cubes. Set aside.
5. Seed the chilies and cut into *0.15cm-wide* strips. Set aside.
6. Heat oil in the wok (10ml - 15ml); once it smokes, add the green onion, ginger and garlic and stir-fry until fragrant;
7. Add the long beans and stir-fry until they change color (from dull green to bright green);
8. Add the potato cubes and stir-fry for 30s;
9. Add hot water (just covering the vegetables), cover, and simmer until the potatoes are *soft* (check with a chopstick);
10. Add the tomato, salt, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, five-spice powder and chilies, and simmer until the tomato breaks down into a sauce (stir to keep it from sticking);
11. Add the chopped cilantro and serve.

## Additional Notes

- After adding the tomato and continuing to simmer, stir occasionally to keep the bottom from burning
