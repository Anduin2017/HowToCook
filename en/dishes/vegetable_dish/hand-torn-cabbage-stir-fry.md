# How to Make Hand-Torn Cabbage Stir-fry

Hand-torn cabbage (shousi baocai) is a classic tangy, spicy and crisp Hunan dish: the cabbage is crisp, tender and flavorful, and the pork belly dry-fragrant without being greasy—appetizing and great with rice. Cabbage is rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber, and pork belly adds protein and energy. You need to master high-heat quick stir-frying, which is a little challenging for beginners, but following the steps you can succeed. It takes about 20 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1663 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Cabbage
* Pork belly
* Bird's eye chili
* Cooking oil
* Cooking wine
* Light soy sauce
* Zhenjiang black vinegar
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Ginger
* Garlic
* Garlic sprouts
* Salt

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Cabbage 1 head
* Pork belly 200 g
* Bird's eye chili 2
* Cooking oil 60 ml
* Cooking wine 5 ml
* Light soy sauce 5 ml
* Black vinegar 5 ml
* Chicken bouillon powder 2 g
* Ginger 2 slices
* Garlic 2 cloves
* Garlic sprouts 0.5 stalk
* Salt 5 g

## Steps

1. Cut the cabbage in half and remove the white core [see image 1]
2. Tear the cabbage by hand, put 2 g of salt in a bowl, rinse the cabbage and drain [see image 2]
3. Prepare the ginger slices, garlic, bird's eye chilies and garlic sprouts [see image 3]
4. Slice the pork belly, rinse in water and set aside
5. Add 30 ml of cooking oil to the wok, add the cabbage and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute, then add 3 g of salt and keep stir-frying for 2 minutes, then take it out and set aside
6. Add 30 ml of cooking oil to the wok, add the pork belly and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute
7. Add the ginger slices and other aromatics and stir-fry for 1 minute
8. Add the cabbage back and stir-fry, add the black vinegar, cooking wine, chicken bouillon and light soy sauce, keep stir-frying over high heat, and serve after 2 minutes

## Additional Notes

* In step 5, adjust the stir-fry time to the actual situation; generally stir-fry until the cabbage is about 70% cooked, meaning it has released some water and softened
* In step 5, adding salt locks in the cabbage's moisture while giving it a little flavor
* In the last step, adjust the stir-fry time flexibly to the situation and your preferred texture
