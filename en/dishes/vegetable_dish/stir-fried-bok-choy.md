# How to Make Stir-fried Bok Choy

This is a refreshing home-style vegetable dish, crisp and tender, savory with a sweet finish. Bok choy is rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, which help digestion. It's very simple—even complete beginners can handle it—and takes only about 10 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 152 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Bok choy (qingcai)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

- Bok choy 100g * portions
- Garlic 2 cloves * portions
- Cooking oil 10-15ml (just enough to cover the bottom of the wok)
- Salt 2g * portions
- Drinking water 70ml * portions
- White sugar 5g * portions

## Steps

1. Break the bok choy into small pieces, rinse with water, and set aside.
2. Mince the garlic and set aside
3. Heat the wok over medium or high heat, then add 10-15ml of cooking oil. Wait another 30 seconds for the oil to heat up.
4. Add the bok choy and stir-fry until it softens (about 1 minute).
5. Add the minced garlic
6. Pour in the calculated water—it should fully moisten or nearly cover the bok choy—add the salt (2g * portions), and keep stir-frying for about 1 minute.
7. Finally add the sugar, cover, and heat over low heat for 2 minutes.
8. Plate.

## Additional Notes

Adding sugar makes the bok choy greener.
