# How to Make Roasted Eggplant

Roasted eggplant is intensely fragrant, charred outside and tender inside, with the spiciness of cumin and garlic soaking into the soft, silky flesh—an easy way to recreate street barbecue flavor at home. Eggplant is rich in vitamin P and anthocyanins, which help protect the cardiovascular system and act as antioxidants. It requires an oven, but the steps are clear and beginners can finish in about half an hour, making it a great quick dish for family meals.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 145 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggplant
- Cooking oil
- Soy sauce (light soy sauce)
- Minced garlic
- Chili pepper
- Cumin
- Salt

## Calculation

For two people:

- Eggplant 1 (about 200g)
- Cooking oil 20-30 ml
- Soy sauce 4-6 g
- Bird's eye chili 1 (about 20g)
- Minced garlic 3-4 cloves
- Cumin 1-3 g
- Salt 0.5-2 g

## Steps

1. Put the soy sauce, cumin, salt, minced garlic and chopped bird's eye chili in a bowl and mix well. Set aside
2. Wash the eggplant and pat the surface dry with paper towels
3. Prick one side of the eggplant 4-8 times with a fork
4. Coat the entire surface of the eggplant with 15-25ml of cooking oil
5. Set the oven to 200℃ (fan on, high heat) and preheat for 2 minutes
6. Put the eggplant on the middle or upper rack and roast for 12-15 minutes (done when the skin is wrinkled and you can press it in 0.3-0.5cm)
7. Take out the eggplant and cut a slit lengthwise down it with a knife. The slit should be centered, stopping 1-1.5cm from each end
8. Insert a small knife or fork into the slit and cut the inside of the eggplant lengthwise
9. Gently pry the slit open and pour in the sauce prepared in step 1
10. Put the eggplant back in the oven at 200℃ and roast for 4-7 minutes
11. Take it out and turn off the oven

## Additional Notes

The final result is about the same as the roasted eggplant from the barbecue stall downstairs—so good!

**Caution:**
Water on the surface of the eggplant, not coating it fully with oil, or not pricking it with a fork may cause **the eggplant to burst while roasting**

**Possible improvement:**
Instead of cutting a slit the first time, you can simply cut the eggplant in half lengthwise, score the flesh, and spread the sauce on
