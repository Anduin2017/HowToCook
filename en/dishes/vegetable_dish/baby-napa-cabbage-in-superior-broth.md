# How to Make Baby Napa Cabbage in Superior Broth

This baby napa cabbage in superior broth is sweet, fresh and tender, a home-style vegetable dish especially good when you're trying to lose weight. Baby napa cabbage is rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, low in fat and calories, and century egg and luncheon meat add flavor without adding too much. The difficulty is moderate; beginners can try it, and it takes only about 20 minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 158 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Baby napa cabbage
- Century egg (pidan)
- Luncheon meat (or ham sausage)
- Green onion
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Salt
- Sugar
- Starch

## Calculation

Note: this dish only comes in a version for 2-4 people.

- Baby napa cabbage 700g
- Enoki mushrooms 10g (to taste; leave them out if you don't like "see you tomorrow" mushrooms 😂)
- Century egg 1 (optional)
- Luncheon meat (ham sausage can substitute)

## Steps

1. Wash the baby napa cabbage, cut it in half lengthwise, then into segments.
2. Cut 3g of green onion into short pieces. Slice 10g of garlic. Cut 10g of ginger into small slices.
3. Dice the century egg, and dice the ham sausage or luncheon meat (1cm cubes)
4. Wash the enoki and pull them apart
5. Boil water and blanch the cabbage for ten seconds, then scoop it out.
6. Heat the wok, add cold oil: heat the wok, swirl oil through it, pour it out, then add a little fresh oil.
7. Turn to low heat and add the green onion, ginger and garlic, frying until fragrant.
8. Add 300g of water (enough to cover the cabbage), then add the cabbage, enoki and luncheon meat
9. Season with oyster sauce, sugar, salt and MSG and bring to a boil.
10. Cook for 3 minutes. Once boiling, start plating: serve the cabbage, put the century egg on top, and pour the broth over
