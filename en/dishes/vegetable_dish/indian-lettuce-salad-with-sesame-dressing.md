# How to Make Indian Lettuce Salad with Sesame Dressing

A refreshing home-style cold dish that cuts through richness: the mellow sesame paste coats crisp Indian lettuce (youmaicai), with sweet, sour, salty and savory flavors intertwined—very appetizing. Indian lettuce is rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, and sesame paste adds calcium and healthy fats for balanced nutrition. There's no difficulty—just cut and toss—and it's on the table in a dozen or so minutes, perfect for beginners and busy times.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 191 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Indian lettuce (youmaicai)
* Sesame paste
* Soy sauce
* Vinegar
* Oyster sauce
* White sugar
* Sesame oil
* Garlic
* Salt

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1-2 people.

Total:

* 1 head of Indian lettuce (about 200g) * portions
* 15ml vinegar * portions
* 5ml soy sauce * portions
* 10ml sesame paste * portions
* 5ml sesame oil * portions
* 5g sugar * portions
* 10ml oyster sauce * portions
* Two **bulbs** of garlic * portions

## Steps

1. Smash and mince the garlic
2. Put the vinegar, soy sauce, sesame paste, sesame oil, sugar, oyster sauce and minced garlic in a bowl and stir well
3. Cut the Indian lettuce into segments no longer than 4cm
4. Put the lettuce in a larger basin, pour in the sauce from the bowl, and mix thoroughly.

## Additional Notes

* Peanut butter can be used instead of sesame paste
* The sesame paste must be mixed evenly with the lettuce for it to taste good.
