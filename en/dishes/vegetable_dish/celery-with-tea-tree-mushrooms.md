# How to Make Celery with Tea Tree Mushrooms

![Finished celery with tea tree mushrooms](../../../dishes/vegetable_dish/芹菜拌茶树菇/芹菜拌茶树菇.jpg)
![Minxing brand tea tree mushrooms](../../../dishes/vegetable_dish/芹菜拌茶树菇/闽星茶树菇.jpg)

This is a refreshing, crisp cold dish: the crisp celery and the chewy tea tree mushrooms (chashugu) complement each other for a layered texture. It's rich in various vitamins and minerals—nutritious and appetizing. It's very simple, beginners can master it easily, and it takes only about 30 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 127 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Minxing brand tea tree mushrooms (jarred)
- Celery
- Sesame oil
- Oyster sauce
- Weijixian (premium light soy sauce)
- Salt

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion serves 1-2 people.

Per portion:

- Minxing tea tree mushrooms 1 jar
- Celery 250-300g (30cm-tall celery seedlings are recommended; without them you can use wild celery or regular celery, but blanch it briefly to cook it)
- Sesame oil 5ml
- Oyster sauce about 7ml
- Weijixian 3ml
- Salt about 2g

## Steps

1. (Skip this step if using celery seedlings) Boil a kettle of water and set aside
2. Remove any yellow leaves from the fresh celery seedlings or celery and wash. Set aside
3. (Skip this step if using celery seedlings) Pick the leaves off the celery and put them in a separate bowl; cut the celery stalks into 2-3 mm-wide strips with a knife. This makes the celery cook faster and more evenly, so it's crisper and more refreshing
4. Cut the celery seedlings into 4cm pieces. Set aside
5. (Skip this step if using celery seedlings) Turn on the heat, pour the boiled water from the kettle into the pot and wait for it to bubble and boil
6. (Skip this step if using celery seedlings) Blanch the celery strips in the pot; after about 20 seconds add the celery leaves, and 5 seconds later turn off the heat, take everything out and rinse in cold water. Set aside
7. Sprinkle the blanched celery or celery seedlings in the bowl with the prepared salt, sesame oil, oyster sauce and Weijixian, and mix well
8. Add the tea tree mushrooms to the bowl and mix well
9. Plate
10. Dig in

## Additional Notes

- When blanching, the water should cover the celery
- If the celery leaves are too old and tough, you can leave them out
