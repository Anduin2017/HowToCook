# How to Make Zucchini and Egg Stir-fry

![Zucchini and egg stir-fry](../../../dishes/vegetable_dish/西葫芦炒鸡蛋/西葫芦炒鸡蛋.jpeg)

Zucchini and egg stir-fry is a light, tasty quick home-style dish: tender, juicy zucchini and fluffy, fragrant eggs, with tomato adding a sweet-and-sour note—great with rice. Eggs provide high-quality protein and lecithin, and zucchini contains plenty of vitamin C and dietary fiber, for a balanced combination. It's simple and very beginner-friendly; most beginners can finish it in just 15 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 224 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Zucchini
- Eggs
- Tomato (optional)
- Salt
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

For 2 people:

- Zucchini 500g
- Tomato 100g
- Eggs 3
- Cooking oil 10-20ml
- Salt 6g

## Steps

1. Wash the tomato, cut into small pieces, and set aside
2. Wash the zucchini, cut into diamond shapes about 4cm per side, and set aside
3. Crack three eggs into a bowl, beat well, and set aside
4. Heat the wok and add 5ml - 10ml of cooking oil
5. Pour in the eggs and keep stir-frying until set; break them into small pieces with the spatula, transfer to a bowl, and set aside
6. Add 5ml - 10ml of cooking oil to the wok, add the tomato, and stir-fry until soft
7. Add the zucchini and toss evenly, add 6g of salt, turn the heat down and **wait 4 - 5 minutes**
8. Add the eggs back and stir-fry over medium heat for 15 seconds
9. Turn off the heat and plate
