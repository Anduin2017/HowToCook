# How to Make Home-Style Egg Tofu

Home-style egg tofu (Japanese tofu) is a quick home-style dish that's crispy outside and tender inside: a golden crispy shell around silky egg tofu, with green peppers, carrots and other vegetables, in either a savory or sweet-and-sour sauce. Egg tofu is made mainly from eggs and is rich in high-quality protein, and with wood ear mushrooms and vegetables it's nutritionally balanced. It takes a little skill to fry the tofu, but the difficulty is moderate and beginners can try it. It's on the table in about 30 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 946 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Egg tofu (tamago tofu / "Japanese tofu")
- Green pepper
- Carrot
- Ham sausage (optional)
- Wood ear mushrooms (optional)
- Onion (optional)
- Potato starch
- Garlic
- Oil (for frying the tofu; enough to cover a bit more than half of it)
- Light soy sauce 8 ml
- Oyster sauce 15 ml
- Salt (savory version)
- Chicken bouillon powder (savory version, optional)
- Ketchup (sweet-and-sour version)
- Granulated sugar (sweet-and-sour version)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Egg tofu 300 g (about 3 tubes)  
- Green pepper 120 g (1 medium)
- Carrot 50 g
- Ham sausage 50 g (about 1)
- Wood ear mushrooms 30 g (about 10 g dried, rehydrated)
- Potato starch 80 g
- Onion 30 g
- Garlic 10 g (about 2 cloves)
- Oil 160-165 ml
- Light soy sauce 8 ml
- Oyster sauce 15 ml
- Salt 2 g (if adding chicken bouillon, use only 0.5g or none)
- Chicken bouillon powder 3 g
- Granulated sugar 10 g
- Ketchup 15 ml

## Steps

1. Slice the carrot, cut the pepper into thin pieces, and chop the green onion and garlic
2. Open the egg tofu package and cut the tofu into rounds about 1 cm thick
3. Put the starch on a flat plate, ready to coat the tofu
4. Gently place the tofu on the starch and coat the top, bottom and sides, taking care not to coat too thickly
5. Put 150 ml of oil in a frying pan, enough to cover a bit more than half of the tofu. When the edges turn golden, flip and fry the other side
6. When both sides are done, take them out and put them on a clean plate
7. Add 10-15 ml of oil to the wok, add the green onion and garlic, and once fragrant, add the green pepper, carrot, ham sausage and wood ear mushrooms
8. Add the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, salt, chicken bouillon, sugar and ketchup
9. Toss gently until evenly colored

## Additional Notes

- Don't let the fried tofu sit too long; prepare the other ingredients in advance
