# How to Make Stir-fried Long Beans

This is a home-style stir-fry with a rich soy flavor: the long beans (doujiao) are tender and well-seasoned, savory, spicy and great with rice. They're rich in dietary fiber and various vitamins, which help digestion. It's simple and beginners can handle it easily, taking about 20 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 107 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Long beans
- Bird's eye chili
- Green onion
- Garlic
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Salt

## Calculation

Note: because ingredients vary individually, this recipe serves 2

- Long beans 250g
- Bird's eye chili 2
- Green onion 3 rings
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Light soy sauce 6ml
- Dark soy sauce 2ml
- Oyster sauce 6ml
- Salt 6g

## Steps

1. Chop the green onion and mince the garlic. Set aside.
2. Mix the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce and salt into a sauce. Set aside.
3. Cut the bird's eye chilies into rings. Set aside.
4. Remove the strings from the long beans and cut diagonally at 45° into *4-10cm* pieces. Set aside.
5. Heat oil in the wok (10ml - 15ml); once it smokes, add the green onion and chili and stir-fry until fragrant;
6. Add the long beans and stir-fry for *30s*,
7. Add the sauce and stir-fry over high heat for *2 minutes*
8. Pour in 150ml of water
9. Turn to medium-low heat, cover and braise for 8-10 minutes
10. Add the minced garlic and serve.

## Additional Notes

- Cutting the long beans takes some knife skill; if you're not comfortable, use scissors
