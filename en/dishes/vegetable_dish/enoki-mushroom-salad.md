# How to Make Enoki Mushroom Salad

Enoki mushroom salad is a quick, simple home-style appetizer: crisp, tender and silky, savory and slightly tangy. Enoki mushrooms are rich in dietary fiber and various vitamins and are fairly low in calories. It only involves blanching and seasoning, very beginner-friendly, and quick to prepare when you're busy. It takes only about 10 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 163 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Enoki mushrooms
- Green onion
- Garlic
- Light soy sauce
- Vinegar
- White sugar (optional)
- Sesame oil (optional)
- Chili oil (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Enoki mushrooms 150 g (about 1 small pack)
- Green onion 5 g
- Garlic 2 cloves
- Light soy sauce 15 ml
- Vinegar 10 ml
- White sugar 3 g (optional)
- Sesame oil 5 ml (optional)
- Chili oil 5 ml (optional)

## Steps

1. Cut off the root end of the enoki, rinse clean with water, and set aside.
2. Wash and chop the green onion. Set aside.
3. Peel and mince the garlic. Set aside.
4. Bring 1000 ml of drinking water to a boil in a pot over high heat.
5. Add the enoki and boil for **1-2 minutes** until softened.
6. Drain the enoki, put in a larger bowl, and set aside.
7. In another clean small bowl, add 15 ml of light soy sauce, 10 ml of vinegar, 3 g of sugar (optional) and 5 ml of sesame oil (optional).
8. Add the minced garlic.
9. Stir well until the sugar dissolves and the sauce is evenly mixed.
10. Pour the sauce evenly over the enoki.
11. Sprinkle with the chopped green onion.
12. Drizzle with 5 ml of chili oil if you like (optional).
13. Mix gently with chopsticks and serve.

## Additional Notes

- Don't blanch the enoki too long, or it affects the texture.
- Adjust the sauce ratio to your taste.
- If you don't eat spicy food, leave out the chili oil.
