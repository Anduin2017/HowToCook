# How to Make Tomato and Egg Stir-fry

A sweet, tangy and appetizing home-style dish: tender eggs wrapped in soft, juicy tomatoes—a classic taste of Chinese home cooking. Eggs provide high-quality protein, and tomatoes are rich in vitamin C and lycopene, for balanced, easily absorbed nutrition. The steps are simple and the ingredients easy to find, making it ideal for kitchen beginners; it takes only about 15 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 252 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Tomatoes
* Eggs
* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Sugar (optional)
* Chopped green onion (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person

Total:

* Tomatoes = 1 (about 180g) * portions
* Eggs = 1.5 * portions, rounded up
* Cooking oil = 4ml * number of eggs
* Salt = 1.5-2g * portions
* Sugar = 0-2g * portions
* Chopped green onion = 0-10g * portions

## Steps

1. Wash the tomatoes
2. Optional: peel the tomatoes

  - Scald the skin with boiling water, then put the tomatoes into cold water and peel off the skin

3. Remove the tomato stems and cut into pieces no more than 4cm per side; these are the `tomato pieces`
4. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add `1g * portions` of salt, and beat well; this is the `beaten egg`

  - Consider adding 1ml of vinegar to the eggs; this removes any eggy smell and makes them fluffier

5. Heat the wok and add the cooking oil
6. When the oil is hot, pour in the `beaten egg`. Stir-fry until the eggs set and turn slightly golden; this is the `half-cooked egg`
7. Turn off the heat. Transfer the `half-cooked egg` to a plate, then turn the heat back on

  - Note: don't wash the wok

8. Add the `tomato pieces`, pressing and stir-frying with the spatula for 20 seconds, or until the tomatoes are soft
9. Add the `half-cooked egg` back to the wok and toss evenly

  - Consider adding 10ml of ketchup and 50ml of water for more sauce
  - You can also add other cooked meats and ingredients

10. Add the remaining salt, sugar (optional, if you prefer a sweeter version) and chopped green onion (optional), and toss evenly
11. Turn off the heat and plate

## Additional Notes

This dish has many versions depending on taste preferences, including but not limited to:

* Quick method:
  - Add the `tomato pieces` directly to the wok with the `half-cooked egg` and stir-fry them together until the tomatoes are soft
  - Continue with step 10
* Replace "add salt" with "add two drops of light soy sauce"
