# How to Make Pan-fried Tofu with Green Onion

Charred outside and tender inside, with a strong green onion aroma, savory and mildly spicy—a home-style flavor. Tofu is rich in plant protein and calcium, and with green pepper and green onion it's nutritionally balanced. The difficulty is moderate: you need to fry the tofu slowly over low heat, but beginners who follow the steps patiently can do it well. The whole process takes about 30 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 364 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Plain tofu
- Green onion
- Green chili pepper
- Salt
- Chicken bouillon powder
- Frying pan

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

- Number of tofu blocks = portions * 0.8, rounded up.
- Number of chili peppers = 1.5 per three people.
- Number of green onions = 2 stalks per three people.
- Salt = portions * 3g.
- Chicken bouillon = portions * 1.5g.

Using the above, calculate the amounts of ingredients you plan to use, adjusting to taste.

## Steps

1. Rinse the tofu. Cut into slices about 5 mm thick and place on a plate.
2. Wash the green onion, remove the roots, chop finely and set aside.
3. Wash the chili peppers, cut them open, remove the seeds, cut into 1cm * 1cm pieces and set aside.
4. Heat the pan and add portions * 9ml of oil.
5. Spread the oil evenly over the bottom of the pan, arrange the tofu evenly, and fry over low heat until golden, then flip.
6. Once both sides are golden, transfer to a plate and set aside.
7. Add more oil to cover the bottom of the pan, add the chili peppers and stir-fry over high heat, pressing them with the spatula for 3 minutes.
8. Add the tofu, stir-fry, add the salt and chicken bouillon, stir-fry over medium heat for 1 minute, then add 10 ML of water and reduce over high heat.
9. Before serving, sprinkle with the chopped green onion and plate.
