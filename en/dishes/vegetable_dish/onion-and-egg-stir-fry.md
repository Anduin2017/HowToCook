# How to Make Onion and Egg Stir-fry

Onion and egg stir-fry is a savory home-style dish with a strong onion aroma: tender egg and sweet onion, a national favorite that goes great with rice. Eggs are rich in high-quality protein and various vitamins, and onions contain dietary fiber and antioxidants, making this a nutritionally balanced quick dish. It's extremely simple and very beginner-friendly, taking only about 5 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 562 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Eggs
* Onion
* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Green onion
* Cooking wine

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Eggs 2
* Onion 50 g
* Cooking oil 50 ml
* Salt 2 g
* Green onion half a stalk
* Cooking wine 2 ml

## Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a large bowl, add the sliced onion and salt, and beat for 60 s
2. Heat oil in the wok, pour in the eggs, fry one side for 30-45 s, then flip and keep stir-frying for 2-3 minutes, then sprinkle with cooking wine and serve
3. Plate the eggs and sprinkle with chopped green onion
