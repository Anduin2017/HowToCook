# How to Make Microwave Steamed Egg Custard

![Microwave steamed egg custard](../../../dishes/vegetable_dish/鸡蛋羹/微波炉鸡蛋羹.jpg)
Microwave steamed egg custard (jidan geng) is a simple, quick home-style steamed egg dish with a smooth, delicate texture, especially good as a late-night snack or quick bite. Eggs are rich in high-quality protein and various vitamins, providing essential nutrition, and dried tiny shrimp add calcium. This dish is very beginner-friendly—no heat-control skills needed—and takes only about 10 minutes from beating the eggs to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 160 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

You can make at most three portions at a time. For more than 3 people, make it in several batches.

- Eggs
- Water

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person

Total:

- Eggs 2 * portions
- Water 200ml * portions
- Dried tiny shrimp (xiapi) 10 * portions (optional)
- Green onion 5g * portions (optional)
- Salt 3g * portions
- Soy sauce (optional)
- Sesame oil 1ml (optional)

## Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a microwave-safe ceramic bowl and beat them with chopsticks.
2. Add the water and salt and stir well.
3. Add the dried shrimp to the bowl and stir well, making sure they don't clump together.
4. Chop the green onion into pieces 0.6±3mm per side, add to the bowl, and stir well.
5. Put the bowl and its contents in the microwave, cover the container with plastic wrap or a microwave-safe ceramic plate (note: don't seal it; leave room for expansion), and heat for 2 minutes (500W).
6. Carefully remove the plastic wrap or other cover, then heat for another 2 minutes.
7. If your microwave has no turntable, slowly rotate the bowl 180 degrees horizontally to make sure the contents heat evenly.
8. Add the sesame oil.
9. Carefully take the bowl out of the microwave (it's really hot).
10. If you choose to add soy sauce, add just enough that it flows across the surface and coats it in the thinnest layer.
11. Happily enjoy your egg custard

## Additional Notes

- 1 egg is about 50±5g. If using other kinds of eggs, adjust the amount of water proportionally.

- Be sure to beat the eggs thoroughly, or a minor egg explosion may occur.

- The eggs and water must not exceed 2/3 of the bowl's capacity, or it may overflow.
