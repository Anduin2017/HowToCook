# How to Make Simple Stir-fried Cauliflower

A common home-style vegetable dish, crisp and tender. It's rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber. It's quick and simple; beginners can usually finish it in just 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 211 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cauliflower
- Garlic
- Salt

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people (as a side dish).

Per portion:

- Cauliflower about 300 g (about 1/2 of a medium head)
- Garlic 2-3 cloves
- Salt 3 g
- Cooking oil 15 ml
- Drinking water 50 ml (for stir-frying)

## Steps

1. Wash the cauliflower and break or cut it into small florets; slice the thick stems. Set aside.
2. Peel and slice the garlic. Set aside.
3. Bring 1000 ml of drinking water to a boil in a pot over high heat.
4. Add the cauliflower and boil for **2-3 minutes**, until it turns lighter in color and softens slightly.
5. Drain the cauliflower and set aside.
6. Heat the wok, add 15 ml of cooking oil, and heat over high heat.
7. Add the garlic slices and stir-fry quickly until fragrant.
8. Add the blanched cauliflower, turn to medium-high heat, and stir-fry quickly for about **2 minutes** so it heats evenly.
9. Add 3 g of salt and keep stir-frying evenly.
10. Drizzle 50 ml of drinking water around the edge of the wok, cover, and braise for **1 minute** to help the cauliflower cook through and absorb the flavor.
11. Remove the lid, toss quickly, and serve.

## Additional Notes

- Blanching the cauliflower in advance shortens the stir-frying time and helps it cook through.
- Adjust the stir-frying time to how soft you like your cauliflower; for crisper cauliflower, cook it for less time.
