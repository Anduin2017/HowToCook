# How to Make Garlic Chive and Egg Stir-fry

Garlic chive and egg stir-fry is a classic home-style dish: fragrant chives and tender, savory eggs—simple to make and nutritious. Garlic chives (jiucai) are rich in vitamins and fiber, and eggs provide high-quality protein. It's simple and the ingredients are easy to find, making it ideal for kitchen beginners; it takes only about 10 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 180 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Garlic chives
* Eggs
* Cooking oil
* Salt

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person

Total:

* Garlic chives = 100g * portions
* Eggs = 2 * portions
* Cooking oil = 10ml * portions
* Salt = 2g * portions

## Steps

1. Wash the garlic chives and drain
2. Cut the chives into pieces about 3cm long; these are the `chive pieces`
3. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat; this is the `beaten egg`
4. Heat the wok and add the cooking oil
5. When the oil is hot, pour in the `beaten egg` and stir-fry quickly until the eggs set into pieces; take them out and set aside—these are the `scrambled eggs`
6. Add 5ml more cooking oil to the wok, add the `chive pieces`, and stir-fry quickly over high heat for 30 seconds
7. Add the `scrambled eggs` back to the wok and toss evenly
8. Add the salt and toss evenly
9. Turn off the heat and plate

## Additional Notes

* Garlic chive and egg stir-fry calls for quick stir-frying over high heat; cooked too long, the chives lose their aroma and crisp texture
* If you prefer softer chives, stir-fry a little longer
