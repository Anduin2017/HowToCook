# How to Make Stir-fried Diced Lotus Root

Stir-fried diced lotus root is a quick home-style vegetable dish: crisp, tender lotus root cubes, savory with a slight heat, very good with rice. Lotus root is rich in dietary fiber and vitamins and is said to cool and moisten. It's simple; as long as you pay attention to blanching and seasoning, kitchen beginners can make it easily. It's on the table in about 20 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 214 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Scallion (large, Chinese leek-type)
- Bird's eye chili
- Lotus root
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Oil

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Scallion 1 section
- Bird's eye chili 1-2 (depending on how spicy you like it)
- Lotus root 1 section
- Light soy sauce 30 ml
- Dark soy sauce 15 ml
- Oyster sauce 15 ml
- Cooking oil 10-15ml

## Steps

1. Cut the scallion and bird's eye chili into small pieces and set aside
2. Peel the lotus root, cut into pieces no larger than 3cm, and put them in water (to prevent oxidation and darkening)
3. Put 500ml of cold water in the wok and bring to a boil
4. Add the lotus root to the boiling water, blanch for 2 minutes, then take out onto a plate
5. Pour out the water, heat the wok until dry, and add 10-15 ml of cooking oil
6. When the oil is hot, add the scallion and bird's eye chili and fry until fragrant
7. Add the prepared lotus root and stir-fry over high heat
8. Add the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and oyster sauce
9. Stir-fry for 2 minutes and serve

## Additional Notes

- Choose lotus root with yellowish-brown skin and thick, white flesh. If it's dark or has an off smell, don't eat it.
- Try not to use iron cookware, which makes lotus root turn dark
