# How to Make Hunan Vinegar-Blasted Eggs

This is a quick home-style Hunan dish that goes great with rice: tangy, spicy, savory and fragrant, with eggs fried until crispy and puffy and soaked in a rich sauce—very appetizing. Eggs provide high-quality protein and lecithin, and black vinegar helps stimulate the appetite. It's extremely simple and beginner-friendly, taking only about ten minutes from prep to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 471 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Bird's eye chili
- Green onion
- Garlic
- Oil
- Zhenjiang black vinegar
- Light soy sauce
- Oyster sauce (optional but recommended)
- White sugar (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Eggs 4
- Bird's eye chili 10 g (about 3)
- Cooking oil 35-50 mL
- Green onion 10 g
- Garlic 5 g
- Black vinegar 15 mL
- Light soy sauce 10-15 mL
- Oyster sauce 10 g
- White sugar 0-5 g

## Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl without beating them
2. Cut the green onion into 3cm segments
3. Put the garlic and bird's eye chilies in a garlic chopper and chop finely
4. In a small bowl, mix the black vinegar, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and water into a sweet-and-sour sauce
5. When the oil is hot, pour in the eggs. Once set, break them into large pieces with the spatula, add the minced garlic and chili, and pour in the sauce
6. Reduce over high heat, adding the green onion just before serving

## Additional Notes

- In my own testing, Laohenghe naturally brewed black vinegar with no additives tastes best; the vinegar aroma is the soul of this dish.
