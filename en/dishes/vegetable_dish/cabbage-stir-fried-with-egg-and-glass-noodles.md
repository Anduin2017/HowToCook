# How to Make Cabbage Stir-fried with Egg and Glass Noodles

Cabbage stir-fried with egg and glass noodles is a savory, quick home-style dish: crisp, sweet shredded cabbage blends with chewy glass noodles and tender egg for layered textures. Cabbage is rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, egg provides high-quality protein, and carrot adds nutrition for a balanced dish. You need to soak the glass noodles in advance, so there are a few more steps, but they're not complicated, though somewhat challenging for beginners. It takes about 1 hour 20 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 570 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Cabbage
* Eggs
* Glass noodles
* Carrot
* Rapeseed oil
* Salt, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce
* Green onion, garlic, dried chili peppers

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Cabbage half a head
* Eggs 2
* Glass noodles 1 bundle
* Carrot half
* Rapeseed oil 20 ml
* Salt 2 g, light soy sauce 15 ml, dark soy sauce 10 ml, oyster sauce 10 ml
* Green onion half a stalk, garlic 2 cloves, dried chili peppers 5

## Steps

1. Shred the carrot and cabbage and set aside
2. Soak the glass noodles in cold water for 1 hour, then put them in a pot with boiling water and cook until soft, then drain and set aside
3. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add salt, and beat for 15 seconds
4. Chop the green onion, garlic and chilies into small pieces and set aside
5. Heat oil in the wok, add the eggs, scramble until cooked, and take them out
6. Add more oil, then the green onion, garlic and dried chilies and stir-fry for 8 seconds
7. Add the carrot and shredded cabbage and stir-fry for 30 seconds
8. Add the glass noodles
9. Add the seasonings: 15 ml of light soy sauce, 10 ml of dark soy sauce, 10 ml of oyster sauce and 2 grams of salt
10. Add the scrambled eggs and stir-fry for about 15 seconds
11. Plate
