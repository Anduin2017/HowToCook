# How to Make Smashed Cucumber Salad

This is a refreshing, appetizing home-style cold dish, crisp and tender with a pleasant tangy aroma. Cucumber is rich in water and vitamins, which help cool you down in summer and are good for the skin. It's very simple and beginner-friendly: just smash, season and marinate for 15 minutes, about 20 minutes in total.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 107 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Cucumber
* Vinegar
* Soy sauce
* Garlic

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Total:

* Cucumber 200 grams * portions
* Vinegar 7.5 ml + 4 ml * portions
* Soy sauce 5 ml + 2.5 ml * portions
* Garlic 3 cloves * portions
* Salt 0.4 grams + 0.2 grams * portions
* Sesame oil 5 ml + 2 ml * portions
* Oyster sauce 5 ml

## Steps

1. Smash the cucumber flat with a kitchen knife, then chop into pieces 3 cm long
2. Put the cucumber pieces in a bowl
3. Smash and mince the garlic
4. Add 5 grams of white sugar to the bowl, mix well and marinate for 15 minutes (marinating with sugar tastes better and keeps the cucumber looking good rather than limp)
5. Add the vinegar, soy sauce, salt, oyster sauce and garlic to the bowl in order and mix well
6. Add the sesame oil and mix well

## Additional Notes

* Sometimes the ends of a cucumber are bitter; wash, cut them off and check
* Eat right away, or prepare in advance and chill in the refrigerator (not the freezer) before eating
* If refrigerating, wrap tightly in plastic wrap and eat within 8 hours, or the cucumber will over-marinate and lose its crispness
* Peeling the cucumber is recommended for a better texture
