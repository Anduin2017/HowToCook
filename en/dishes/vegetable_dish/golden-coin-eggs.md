# How to Make Golden Coin Eggs

Golden coin eggs (jinqian dan) are a Hunan-style home dish, crisp outside and tender inside, savory and mildly spicy; the egg slices fried golden look like copper coins, a symbol of good fortune. Eggs paired with green and red chilies are rich in high-quality protein and various vitamins for balanced nutrition. The difficulty is moderate—beginners can master it with a little practice—and it takes about 30 minutes in total to make this great rice companion.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 427 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- Long green chili (xianjiao)
- Bird's eye chili
- Doubanjiang (fermented broad bean paste)
- Garlic
- Green onion
- Cornstarch (optional)
- Cooking oil
- Light soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Sugar

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Eggs 5
- Cornstarch 40 g
- Cooking oil 20-35 ml
- Long green chili 15 g
- Bird's eye chili 6-15 g (about 2-5)
- Doubanjiang 15 ml
- Garlic 10 g (about 2-3 cloves)
- Green onion 3 g (about 1-2 stalks)
- Light soy sauce 15 ml
- Oyster sauce 15 ml
- Sugar 5 g

## Steps

1. Put the eggs in cold water, bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer over low heat for 8 minutes
2. Rinse under cold tap water to make peeling easier
3. Mince the garlic (pieces no larger than 1 mm), and chop the long green chili and bird's eye chili into small bits (about 2-3 mm)
4. Cut each boiled egg along its short axis into 4 pieces of similar size
5. If you plan to use starch, coat each egg slice in starch and shake off the excess
6. Add 25 ml of oil to the wok (20 ml if not using starch) and add the egg slices
7. Fry until lightly golden, add 10 ml more oil (10 ml if not using starch), and flip
8. When the second side is lightly golden, add the long green chili, bird's eye chili and garlic and fry for about a minute, then flip
9. Then add the doubanjiang and fry for a few dozen seconds, then flip
10. Mix the light soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar into a sauce, add it, toss the wok gently, and serve

## Additional Notes

- For the authentic version, use 15 g of bird's eye chili; if you can't handle spice, reduce it to 6 g, and you can remove the seeds
- A nonstick pan is recommended; with a nonstick pan, you can use 5 ml less oil the first time
- Make sure the eggs are fully set before peeling
- Don't stir-fry too much, or they'll fall apart
