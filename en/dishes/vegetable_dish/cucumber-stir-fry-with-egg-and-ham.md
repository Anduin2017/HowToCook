# How to Make Cucumber Stir-fry with Egg and Ham

This is a refreshing, quick home-style stir-fry, pleasantly savory: crisp cucumber, soft eggs, and ham for extra flavor—an everyday Chinese home-style dish. Eggs provide high-quality protein, cucumber is rich in vitamins and water, and ham adds flavor, for overall balanced nutrition. The steps are simple and the difficulty very low, making it very friendly for kitchen beginners. It takes only about 10 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 337 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cucumber
- Eggs
- Ham sausage
- Salt
- Light soy sauce
- Red chili pepper (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Cucumber 1 (about 200g)
- Eggs 2
- Ham sausage 1 (about 40g)
- Red chili pepper 1 (optional)
- Cooking oil 10ml
- Light soy sauce 3ml
- Salt 2g

## Steps

1. Wash the cucumber, cut into half-moon slices, and set aside
2. Cut the ham into half-moon slices and set aside
3. Chop the red chili pepper (optional) and set aside
4. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat; this is the `beaten egg`
5. Pour 5ml of cooking oil into the hot wok
6. When the oil is hot, turn to low heat, pour in the `beaten egg`, break it up with chopsticks, and stir-fry until the eggs set and turn slightly golden; this is the `half-cooked egg`. Take it out and set aside
7. **Don't wash the wok**: add 5ml of cooking oil, add the cucumber slices, and **stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute**
8. Add the `half-cooked egg` back to the wok, season with 2g of salt and 3ml of light soy sauce, then immediately add the ham slices and chopped chili (optional) and toss evenly
9. Turn off the heat and plate

## Additional Notes

- Ham is salty, so add it after seasoning so the ham doesn't pick up extra salt
