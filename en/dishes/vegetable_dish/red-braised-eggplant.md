# How to Make Red-Braised Eggplant

Red-braised eggplant is a rich, savory home-style dish that goes great with rice: soft chunks of eggplant soak up the sauce, and bell pepper and tomato make it pleasantly tangy and spicy. Eggplant is rich in dietary fiber and vitamin P, and the fried chunks are fragrant without being greasy. It requires battering, deep-frying and slow braising; there are a few more steps, but beginners can still try it—pay attention to the details and you'll succeed. It takes about 45 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★★

Estimated calories: 547 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Garlic
- Scallion (large Chinese green onion)
- Green chili pepper
- Onion
- Tomato
- Green eggplant
- Salt
- Soy sauce
- Egg
- Flour
- Starch

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people

Total:

- Number of green eggplants = portions * 0.7
- Green chili pepper = portions * 0.5
- Onion = portions * 0.3
- Tomato = 1
- Scallion = half a stalk
- Garlic = 3 cloves
- Egg = 1
- Flour = number of green eggplants * 150 g
- Starch = flour / 4 g
- Soy sauce = number of eggplants * 7 g (rounded up)

## Steps

1. Wash the green eggplants, green chilies, tomato, onion and scallion.
1. Cut the scallion into 5 mm pieces, peel and smash the garlic, cut the tomato into 6 cubic centimeter chunks, and cut the green chili and onion into 5g pieces.
1. Cut the eggplant into diamond-shaped chunks (first cut 2cm-thick slices, then cut the slices into 2cm strips, and finally cut the strips diagonally into chunks...).
1. Put the flour into a bowl and add small amounts of water a little at a time, stirring evenly into a thick batter.
1. Add the starch and 30 g of water and stir evenly.
1. Crack the egg into the bowl, add (flour / 20) g of salt, and stir evenly.
1. Add the eggplant chunks to the batter and stir so every side is coated.
1. Turn on high heat, heat the wok and add 500 ml of oil. When you see a wisp of smoke rising from the oil, turn to low heat and add the eggplant chunks one by one with chopsticks. Once all the eggplant is in, turn to medium heat until the chunks turn golden, then take them out and pour off the oil.
1. Add (portions * 5) g of oil, add the garlic and scallion and stir-fry for 15 seconds, add the green chili and stir-fry for 30 seconds, add the tomato and stir-fry for 30 seconds.
1. Add the fried eggplant chunks and add water to 0.8 times the height of the ingredients in the wok.
1. Add the soy sauce and (portions * 3) g of salt.
1. Wait until the sauce is thick (the liquid level is about 0.2-0.3 times the height of the remaining ingredients), then remove the lid, plate, and turn off the heat.
