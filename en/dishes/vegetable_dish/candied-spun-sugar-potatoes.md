# How to Make Candied Spun-Sugar Potatoes

Candied spun-sugar potatoes (basi tudou) are a classic Shandong dish: golden, with long fine threads of sugar, crispy outside and soft inside, sweet and delicious. Potatoes are rich in carbohydrates and vitamin C for a quick energy boost. This dish is somewhat difficult; beginners need to watch the heat when cooking the sugar so it doesn't crystallize or burn bitter. It takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 1158 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Potatoes
* Cooking oil
* Starch
* Granulated sugar
* Water
* Sesame seeds

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Potatoes 2
* Cooking oil 300 ml (enough for the potatoes to float)
* Starch 30 g
* Granulated sugar 120 g (use plenty of sugar, so the potatoes get a thick coat of syrup for the spun-sugar effect)
* Water 100 ml
* Sesame seeds 5 g

## Steps

1. Peel the potatoes and cut into evenly sized small chunks. Add the starch (no water) and toss so the starch coats the surface of the potatoes
2. Heat oil in the wok, add the potato chunks, and fry, turning slowly, for 5-7 minutes, until a chopstick pierces them
3. Take out the potatoes and set aside in a large bowl
4. Add the water and sugar to the wok and slowly stir the sugar in one direction until it turns brown
5. Add the potatoes back, toss for 30 s, and serve
6. Plate the potatoes and sprinkle with sesame seeds

## Additional Notes

* Don't fry the potatoes too thoroughly, or they won't hold their shape when you pull the sugar threads and plate them
* Consider collecting the leftover cooking oil back into an oil pot.
