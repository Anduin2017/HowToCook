# How to Make Red-Braised Winter Melon

Red-braised winter melon is a glossy, reddish, savory home-style vegetarian dish: soft, tender and full of flavor, a popular Chinese braise. Winter melon is rich in water, dietary fiber and vitamin C; it's considered cooling and good for relieving heat and reducing swelling, and with simple seasonings it's nutritious and easy to digest. This dish is fairly beginner-friendly with clear steps—you just need to get the heat and seasoning right—and takes about 25 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 519 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Winter melon
* Cooking oil
* Cooking wine
* Starch
* Light soy sauce
* Dark soy sauce
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Green onion
* Minced ginger
* Oyster sauce

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Winter melon 300 g
* Cooking oil 50 ml
* Cooking wine 2 ml
* Starch 10 g
* Light soy sauce 10 ml
* Dark soy sauce 15 ml
* Chicken bouillon powder 3 g
* Green onion 0.5 stalk
* Minced ginger 1 piece
* Oyster sauce 15 ml

## Steps

1. Peel the winter melon and cut into small cubes no more than 2cm per side
2. Heat oil in the wok, add the winter melon, and fry, turning slowly, for 2 minutes until the surface turns golden
3. Take out the winter melon and set aside in a large bowl
4. Using the oil left in the wok, add the minced ginger, light soy sauce and oyster sauce in turn and stir-fry for 15 s
5. Add the winter melon back, stir-fry for 30 s, then add boiling water to cover the melon and boil over high heat for 10 minutes
6. Add the dark soy sauce for color and keep cooking until the winter melon is soft (a chopstick goes in easily)
7. Add the chicken bouillon, cooking wine and green onion and stir-fry for 30 s, then transfer the winter melon to a large bowl
8. Keep the remaining liquid in the wok, add the starch slurry, bring to a boil, and pour the sauce over the winter melon
