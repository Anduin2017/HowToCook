# How to Make Dry Pot Cauliflower

Dry pot cauliflower (ganguo huacai) is a Hunan-style home dish: crisp, tender and dry-fragrant cauliflower with richly browned pork belly and a slight heat. Cauliflower is rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber, and pork belly adds protein and energy. The difficulty is moderate—just be sure to drain thoroughly after blanching and render the fat from the pork belly—suitable for beginners with some basics. It takes about 40 minutes from prep to plate.

## Ingredients and Tools

- Cauliflower
- Pork belly
- Chili peppers
- Light soy sauce
- White sugar
- Garlic
- Salt
- Oil

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Cauliflower 400 g
- Pork belly 100 g
- Chili peppers 1-2
- Light soy sauce 10 ml
- White sugar 5g
- Garlic 3-4 cloves
- Salt 2 g
- Oil 10 ml

## Steps

1. Soak the cauliflower, florets down, in lightly salted water for 20 minutes. Then rinse and break into small florets with a small knife
2. Blanch in boiling water for 1 minute, then immediately rinse under cold water until completely cool, and drain
3. Slice the pork belly thinly, cut off the white part of the garlic sprouts and smash with the back of a knife, and cut the small red chilies into pieces
4. Heat the wok with oil, and when hot, add the scallion whites and fry until fragrant
5. Add the pork belly slices and stir-fry over medium heat until they've completely changed color, then keep frying a bit longer to render out some of the fat
6. Add the red chili pieces and the cauliflower and toss a few times
7. Add 10 ml of light soy sauce
8. Then add 5 g of sugar, turn to high heat, and keep stir-frying for 1 minute
9. Cut the green leafy part of the garlic sprouts into segments, add to the wok, toss a few times, turn off the heat, cover, and let it sit for 1 minute
