# How to Make Three Mushrooms in Oyster Sauce

Three mushrooms in oyster sauce is a savory, silky quick home-style dish: the mushrooms soak up the oyster sauce and are smooth and chewy, with a slight heat—fresh and not greasy. It's rich in plant protein, dietary fiber and various vitamins, and broccoli rounds out the nutrition. It's simple, beginners can master it easily, and it takes only about 15 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 222 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Shiitake mushrooms
* Brown beech mushrooms (shimeji)
* White beech mushrooms
* Bird's eye chili
* Bell pepper
* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Cooking wine
* Starch
* Light soy sauce
* Chicken bouillon powder
* Green onion
* Minced ginger
* Oyster sauce
* Broccoli

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Fresh shiitake mushrooms 2
* Brown beech mushrooms 30 g
* White beech mushrooms 30 g
* Bird's eye chili 1
* Bell pepper 0.5
* Cooking oil 10 ml
* Salt 5 g
* Cooking wine 2 ml
* Starch 10 g
* Light soy sauce 10 ml
* Chicken bouillon powder 3 g
* Green onion 0.5 stalk
* Minced ginger 1 piece
* Oyster sauce 5 ml
* Boiling water 350 ml
* Broccoli 100 g

## Steps

1. Remove the dirt from the base of the brown and white beech mushrooms and break them apart
2. Slice the shiitake mushrooms (0.5-1 cm thick; thicker slices are chewier than thin ones)
3. Pour the starch into a small bowl, add 50ml of water, and stir until dissolved with no lumps (a starch slurry). Set aside
4. Bring water to a boil, add the broccoli, boil for 3 minutes, and put it in a bowl. Set aside
5. Wash the pot and boil water again, add 5 g of salt, add the brown beech, white beech and shiitake mushrooms, and boil for 1 minute
6. After 1 minute, take them out and drain
7. Heat oil in the wok; when the oil starts forming small bubbles, add the minced ginger, bird's eye chili and bell pepper and stir-fry for 30 S
8. Add the mushrooms, then the light soy sauce, oyster sauce and chicken bouillon in turn; toss evenly, then pour in the starch slurry
9. Cook over medium heat until the sauce reduces, add the cooking wine and chopped green onion, and serve
10. Arrange the broccoli around it
