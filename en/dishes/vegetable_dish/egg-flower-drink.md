# How to Make Egg Flower Drink

A simple home-style remedy drink (jidanhua) that comes in sweet and savory versions, with a silky, tender texture—a folk remedy for soothing the throat. It's rich in high-quality protein and lecithin, easy to digest and absorb, and can relieve a dry, itchy throat. It's extremely simple—beginners can do it after reading once—and takes only about 5 minutes from boiling the water to finishing.

Estimated difficulty: ★

Estimated calories: 107 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggs
- White sugar (optional, for the sweet version)
- Salt (optional, for the savory version)
- Whisk (optional)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 1 person.

Per portion:

- Egg 1 (about 50 g without the shell)
- Boiling water 100 - 150 ml
- White sugar 5 - 10 g (for the sweet version)
- Salt 1 - 2 g (for the savory version)

## Steps

1. Crack the egg into a bowl.
2. Using chopsticks or a whisk, beat the egg in one direction until the white and yolk are completely mixed and uniform in color. (This takes about 1 - 2 minutes)
3. Add the sugar or salt to the egg and stir briefly.
4. Prepare freshly boiled water at 100 ℃.
5. **While stirring the egg quickly with chopsticks, pour the boiling water into the egg in a thin stream**. Make sure the boiling water and egg mix thoroughly.
6. Keep stirring for a moment until the egg is completely cooked into even, pale yellow egg flowers.

## Additional Notes

- To guard against salmonella that may be present in raw eggs, use freshly boiled water at 100 ℃ to cook the egg completely in an instant.
