# How to Make Garlic Broccoli

This home-style vegetable dish stands out for its strong garlic aroma and simple seasoning: blanched broccoli is tender or slightly crisp, light and delicious. It's rich in vitamin C, dietary fiber and various antioxidants—a nutritionally balanced quick vegetable dish. It's not difficult, beginners can master it easily, and it usually takes about 15 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 187 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Broccoli 1 head
- Garlic 3-4 cloves
- Light soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- White sugar

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2 people.

Per portion:

- Broccoli about 200 g (about 1/2 of a medium head)
- Garlic 3-4 cloves
- Light soy sauce 10 ml
- Oyster sauce 5 ml
- White sugar 2 g

## Steps

1. Cut the broccoli into small florets and wash.
2. Peel and mince the garlic. Set aside.
3. Add 1000 ml of drinking water to a pot and bring to a boil over high heat.
4. Add the broccoli and **boil for 2-3 minutes** over high heat, until it turns bright green and softens.
5. Take out the cooked broccoli, drain, arrange on a plate, and set aside.
6. Heat the wok and add 10 ml of cooking oil. When the oil is hot, add the minced garlic and stir-fry over low heat until fragrant.
7. Add 10 ml of light soy sauce, 5 ml of oyster sauce, 2 g of sugar and 30 ml of drinking water.
8. Bring the sauce in the wok to a boil.
9. Pour the garlic sauce evenly over the broccoli on the plate.

## Additional Notes

- Don't blanch longer than recommended, or it will affect the broccoli's texture and nutrition.
- For crisper broccoli, shorten the blanching time.
