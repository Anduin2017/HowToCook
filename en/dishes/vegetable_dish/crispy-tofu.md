# How to Make Crispy Tofu

Crispy tofu is crunchy outside and tender inside, with a savory-sweet sauce—a quick dish that goes great with rice. Tofu is rich in plant protein and calcium, and eggs provide high-quality protein for a balanced combination. You need patience to pan-fry a golden crispy crust, but the steps are clear and beginners can master it; the whole process takes about 30 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 840 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Firm tofu
- Eggs
- Light soy sauce
- Dark soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- White sugar
- Cornstarch
- Frying pan

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Firm tofu 1 block (buy 1.25 blocks per person at the market)
- Eggs 2 * number of tofu blocks
- Cornstarch 50 g * number of tofu blocks
- Light soy sauce 20 g
- Oyster sauce 10 g
- Dark soy sauce 5 g
- White sugar 10 g

## Steps

1. Beat the eggs and set aside
2. Mix the sauce (20 g light soy sauce + 10 g oyster sauce + 5 g dark soy sauce + 10 g sugar + 10 g cornstarch + 200 ml water)
3. Slice the firm tofu (personal suggestion, for reference only: 5 slices per person, 1.2 cm thick)
4. Pour the cornstarch onto a plate, coat the tofu slices in starch, then dip in the egg, and set aside
5. Heat the pan and add 18ml of cooking oil. Wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up
6. Arrange the egg-coated tofu slices evenly in the pan to cover the bottom, and fry over low heat until golden, then flip
7. Once both sides are golden, pour in the sauce so every piece of tofu is bathed in it, and cook over high heat for 3 minutes until the sauce thickens
8. Turn off the heat
