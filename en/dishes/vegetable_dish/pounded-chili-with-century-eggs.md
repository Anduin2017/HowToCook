# How to Make Pounded Chili with Century Eggs

Pounded chili with century eggs (leijiao pidan) is a humble-looking but very flavorful home-style cold dish: the charred aroma of green peppers blends with the rich smoothness of century eggs, tangy, spicy and appetizing, especially good with summer meals. Century eggs are rich in protein and minerals, green peppers provide plenty of vitamin C, and garlic helps stimulate the appetite. The whole dish is simple, beginners can handle it easily, and it takes only about 15 minutes from prep to finish.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 448 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Century eggs (pidan)
- Long green peppers (sometimes called xianjiao; referred to below as "green peppers")
- Green onion
- Garlic
- Bird's eye chili (optional)
- Cooking oil
- Light soy sauce
- Aged vinegar (chencu)
- White sugar
- Sesame oil
- A fairly deep small metal bowl

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Century eggs 2
- Green peppers 4 (10-15cm long, 2-4cm wide)
- Green onion (about 10cm is enough; the green part is best)
- Garlic 3-4 cloves
- Cooking oil 10-20ml
- Light soy sauce 15-20ml
- Aged vinegar 15-20ml
- White sugar 6-10g
- Sesame oil 5-7ml
- Bird's eye chili 3-4

## Steps

1. Wash the green peppers, remove the stems, slit them along the side, remove the seeds, and press them flat on the cutting board. Set aside (be sure to remove the seeds, or they'll pop in the wok)
2. Cut the green onion into half-centimeter pieces. Set aside
3. Peel and mince the garlic. Set aside
4. Peel the century eggs. Set aside
5. Cut the bird's eye chilies into 5-10mm pieces. Set aside
6. Heat the wok and add 10ml - 20ml of cooking oil
7. Add all the green peppers, turn to low heat to keep the wok hot, and fry until the peppers are soft (test with a chopstick—it should go straight through)
8. Turn off the heat and put the century eggs and green peppers into the small metal bowl
9. Option 1: if you have a rolling pin and pounding won't bother the neighbors, pound the century eggs and peppers in the bowl with one end of the rolling pin until they're mixed
10. Option 2: tear the green peppers by hand into strips about half a centimeter wide, and crush the century eggs with a fork
11. Add the bird's eye chili
12. Pour in the light soy sauce, aged vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, and the other prepared ingredients not yet used
13. Stir evenly
