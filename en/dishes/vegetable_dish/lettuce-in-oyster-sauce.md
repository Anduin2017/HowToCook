# How to Make Lettuce in Oyster Sauce

Lettuce in oyster sauce is a refreshing, savory home-style dish: crisp lettuce drizzled with a garlicky oyster sauce, savory with a touch of sweetness—a quick vegetable dish common in both Cantonese and Sichuan restaurants. It's rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, which help digestion. It's very beginner-friendly, taking only about 15 minutes from washing to serving.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 125 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Lettuce
- Oyster sauce
- Garlic
- Light soy sauce
- Salt
- White sugar
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

This dish is the same size as a standard order at a Sichuan restaurant

- Lettuce 1 head (200 g ± 50)
- Oyster sauce 6-8 ml
- Garlic 4-5 cloves (crushed or minced)
- Light soy sauce 6 ml
- Salt 0.5 g
- White sugar 1 g
- Cooking oil 5-8 ml

## Steps

1. Wash the lettuce and remove any rotten leaves.
2. Heat the wok, add 1 L of (cold) water, then add 2 ml - 3 ml of cooking oil and 0.5 g of salt, and wait for the water to boil.
3. When the water boils, add the lettuce, blanching **every leaf** for 10 s.
4. Take out the lettuce, drain, and plate.
5. Mix the sauce: put 10 ml of light soy sauce, 6-8 ml of oyster sauce, 0.5 g of salt and 1 g of sugar in a bowl, mix well, and stir in 10-15 ml of (cold) water.
6. Turn the heat back on, heat the wok, add 5-8 ml of cooking oil, and when the oil is hot add the garlic.
7. When the garlic becomes fragrant, pour in the sauce, bring it to a boil, and immediately turn off the heat.
8. **Pour** the sauce evenly over the lettuce.

## Additional Notes

This dish is rich in vitamins, simple to make, and refreshing without being "heaty".
