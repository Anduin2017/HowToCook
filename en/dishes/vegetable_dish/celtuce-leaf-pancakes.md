# How to Make Celtuce Leaf Pancakes

Celtuce leaf pancakes are a simple, quick home-style snack: crispy outside and tender inside, with the fresh aroma of celtuce (wosun) leaves. Celtuce leaves are rich in vitamins and dietary fiber, and the eggs add high-quality protein for balanced nutrition. No complicated technique is needed—just mix and fry both sides—so beginners can pick it up easily. It takes only about 10 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 477 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Celtuce leaves
* Eggs
* Cooking oil
* Light soy sauce
* Starch
* Chicken bouillon powder

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Celtuce leaves 50 g
* Eggs 2
* Cooking oil 30 ml
* Light soy sauce 5 ml
* Starch 15 g
* Chicken bouillon powder 2 g

## Steps

1. Finely chop the celtuce leaves, add the eggs, starch, light soy sauce and chicken bouillon, mix well and set aside
2. Heat oil in the wok, pour in the celtuce leaf batter and spread it evenly over the pan
3. Fry the first side for 120 S, flip and fry for another 60 S, then serve

## Additional Notes

* Celtuce leaves were chosen because the vegetable boxes sold in a certain city almost always include celtuce; you can swap in garlic chives or similar
* Blanched celtuce leaves dressed cold taste bitter, so they're used here as a pancake ingredient instead
