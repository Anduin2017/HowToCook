# How to Make Braised Eggplant and Potatoes

Braised eggplant and potatoes is a northeastern Chinese home-style stew: soft, savory, with mild heat from chili peppers and a garlic aroma—great with rice. Eggplant is rich in anthocyanins and dietary fiber, potatoes provide carbohydrates and vitamin C, and pork adds protein, so it's balanced and filling. The process is simple, but you need to manage the heat when stir-frying and reducing the sauce, which is a bit of a challenge for beginners; estimated difficulty is medium. It takes about 45 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 623 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Eggplant
- Potatoes
- Pork
- Chili peppers (green chili peppers, **not chili powder or chili oil**)
- Soy sauce
- Salt
- Garlic

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2-3 people

Total:

- Number of eggplants = portions * 1 (about 150g each)
- Number of potatoes = portions * 1 (about 150g each)
- Pork = portions * 180 g
- Soy sauce = portions * 15 ml
- Salt = portions * 5 g
- Chili peppers = 50 g (for seasoning, so use this amount regardless of how many people)
- Garlic = 3 cloves (for seasoning, so use this amount regardless of how many people. Note: 3 small cloves, **not 3 whole heads**)

Using the above, calculate the amounts of ingredients you plan to use.

## Steps

1. Wash the eggplant, potatoes and chili peppers; peel and smash the garlic.
2. Cut the eggplant and potatoes into chunks of about 6 cubic centimeters, the chili peppers into 5 g pieces, and the pork into 3 cm strips.
3. Turn on the heat, heat the wok, and add portions * 13 ml of oil.
4. When you see a wisp of smoke rising from the oil, add the chili peppers.
5. Once the chili is fragrant, immediately add the pork and stir-fry with a spatula for 30 seconds.
6. Add the potatoes and stir-fry for 30 seconds.
7. Add the eggplant and stir-fry for 30 seconds.
8. Add the soy sauce and salt and keep stir-frying for 5 minutes.
9. Add water to 0.9 times the height of the ingredients in the wok, and cover.
10. Wait until the water level is 0.1 times the height of the remaining ingredients, then remove the lid, add the garlic, stir well, and turn off the heat.

## Additional Notes

In steps 5-7, note: if there's no liquid (oil or water) at the bottom of the wok, add 5 ml of water before continuing to stir-fry.
