# How to Make Enoki and Egg Tofu Pot

Enoki and egg tofu pot is a richly sauced home-style dish that goes great with rice: the tofu is crisp outside and tender inside, and the enoki mushrooms are silky and flavorful. Enoki mushrooms are rich in amino acids and dietary fiber, and egg tofu provides high-quality protein—nutritious and appetizing. The steps are simple and beginners can master it easily; it takes about 30 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 606 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Enoki mushrooms
- Egg tofu (Japanese tofu / tamagodofu)
- Bird's eye chili
- Light soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Salt
- Sugar
- Cooking oil

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Enoki mushrooms 1-2 bunches
- Egg tofu 2 tubes
- Bird's eye chili 3-5, chopped
- Garlic 2-3 cloves
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Oyster sauce 5ml
- Dark soy sauce 3ml
- Sugar 3g
- Cooking oil 10-15ml

## Steps

1. Slice the tofu, pan-fry over low heat until golden on both sides, and set aside.
2. Mince the garlic; mix the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar and 100ml of water into a sauce and set aside.
3. Heat the wok and add oil. When the oil is hot, add the bird's eye chili and garlic and stir-fry until fragrant. Add the enoki first and stir-fry until soft, lay the fried tofu flat on top of the enoki, pour in the sauce from step 2, braise for 5 minutes, then reduce over high heat.

## Additional Notes

- Be sure to stir-fry the enoki until soft first
- Try not to stir the tofu, as it breaks easily
