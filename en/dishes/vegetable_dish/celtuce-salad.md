# How to Make Celtuce Salad

A crisp, refreshing home-style cold side dish: the celtuce (stem lettuce) becomes especially crisp and tender after salting, with a slight heat that whets the appetite. It's rich in vitamins and dietary fiber and not high in calories. It's simple and beginner-friendly, taking about twenty minutes from prep to table, and tastes even better chilled.

Estimated difficulty: ★★

Estimated calories: 295 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

* Celtuce (stem lettuce)
* Carrot
* Bird's eye chili
* Ginger
* Garlic
* Salt
* Cooking oil

## Calculation

Per portion:

* Celtuce 1
* Carrot 0.25
* Bird's eye chili 2
* Ginger 1 slice
* Garlic 2 cloves
* Salt 5 g
* Cooking oil 25 ml

## Steps

1. Peel the celtuce and cut into small strips. Cut the carrot into strips, put both in a large bowl, add the salt, mix, and let sit for 10 minutes
2. Rinse the salted celtuce with water 1-2 times
3. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add the celtuce, boil for 1 minute, then drain and put it in a large bowl
4. Heat oil in the wok, add the ginger slice, garlic cloves and bird's eye chilies and fry for 30-45 s, then pour over the celtuce
5. Mix thoroughly and plate

## Additional Notes

* The carrot isn't required; it's just for color
* After plating, add extra light soy sauce, sugar, black vinegar, sesame seeds and so on to taste
* Cut the celtuce strips to whatever size you like, but peel off plenty of the skin, or it really affects the texture
* Even better chilled
