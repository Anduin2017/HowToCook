# How to Make Aloo Gobi

This Indian home-style vegetable dish is known for being dry, fragrant, golden and richly spiced: soft potatoes and cauliflower with slightly charred edges, in North Indian style. Potatoes and cauliflower are rich in dietary fiber and vitamin C for balanced nutrition. It's simple and beginner-friendly, taking about 25 minutes from prep to plate.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 668 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Potatoes
- Cauliflower
- Onion
- Tomato
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Green chili
- Cilantro
- Cooking oil
- Cumin seeds
- Turmeric powder
- Red chili powder
- Ground coriander
- Garam masala
- Salt
- Wok (with lid)

## Calculation

Before cooking, decide how many portions to make. One portion is just enough for 2-3 people.

Per portion:

- Potatoes 2 (about 250g), peeled and cut into chunks (about 2cm)
- Cauliflower 1 (about 300g), broken into small florets
- Onion 1 (about 100g), chopped
- Tomato 1 (about 100g), chopped
- Ginger 8g, grated
- Garlic 3 cloves (about 9g), minced
- Green chili 1, chopped
- Cooking oil 30ml
- Cumin seeds 3g
- Turmeric powder 3g
- Red chili powder 3g
- Ground coriander 5g
- Garam masala 3g
- Salt 5g
- Cilantro 15g (for garnish)

## Steps

1. Peel the potatoes and cut into chunks about 2cm
2. Break the cauliflower into evenly sized small florets; cut large ones smaller with a knife
3. Pour 30ml of cooking oil into the wok and heat over medium heat
4. When the oil is hot, add 3g of cumin seeds and fry until *they darken and smell fragrant* (about 10 seconds; be careful not to burn them)
5. Add the chopped onion and stir-fry until *the onion is translucent and lightly golden* (about 3-4 minutes)
6. Add the ginger, garlic and green chili and stir-fry for 1 minute
7. Add the chopped tomato and stir-fry until *the tomato breaks down* (about 2-3 minutes)
8. Add 3g of turmeric, 3g of red chili powder, 5g of ground coriander and 5g of salt
9. Stir-fry for 1 minute so the spices blend with the vegetables
10. Add the potatoes and stir-fry for 2 minutes to coat them in spices
11. Add the cauliflower and toss gently
12. Cover, turn to medium-low heat, and **cook covered for 12-15 minutes**, opening the lid every 3-4 minutes to stir and prevent sticking
13. When *the potatoes are easily pierced with a chopstick and the cauliflower has slightly charred edges*, sprinkle in 3g of garam masala
14. Stir-fry for 1 minute
15. Turn off the heat, sprinkle with cilantro, and plate

## Additional Notes

- This is a dry-style dish, so no water is needed. If it seems too dry and is sticking, you can add a very small amount of water (about 15-30ml), but not too much.
- Don't cut the potatoes too small, or they'll fall apart.
- Slightly browned cauliflower edges are normal and actually add flavor.
- Aloo gobi became widely known in the West thanks to the 2001 British comedy film "Bend It Like Beckham".
- This dish goes well with naan, roti or rice.
