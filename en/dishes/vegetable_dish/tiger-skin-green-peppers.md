# How to Make Tiger-Skin Green Peppers

Tiger-skin green peppers (hupi qingjiao) are a home-style dish: the skin is slightly charred and wrinkled, the texture soft with a little crunch, savory with a sweet-and-sour note—very appetizing and great with rice. Green peppers are rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber, which help strengthen immunity and aid digestion. The process is simple—you just need to master pressing the peppers and controlling the heat—so it's fairly beginner-friendly, usually taking about 15 minutes.

Estimated difficulty: ★★★

Estimated calories: 341 kcal

## Ingredients and Tools

- Green peppers
- Garlic
- White sugar (the soul of the dish)
- Vinegar
- Light soy sauce
- Salt
- A mortar or fairly deep bowl

## Calculation

Per portion:

- Green peppers 5, ideally 10-15cm long
- Garlic 2-3 cloves
- Oil 20ml
- White sugar 15g
- Light soy sauce 15ml
- Black vinegar 15ml
- Salt 4g

## Steps

1. Remove the stems from the green peppers and rinse under the tap.
2. Cut the peppers into long strips, about 3-4 strips per pepper lengthwise.
3. Peel and mince the garlic to about 2mm x 2mm x 2mm.
4. `Seasoning 1`: pour 20ml of oil into a small bowl and add the minced garlic.
5. `Seasoning 2`: put the sugar, light soy sauce, vinegar and salt into the mortar (bowl) or similar container and stir.
6. Pour `Seasoning 1` into the wok, turn on the heat, and when it's 50% hot add the peppers. Don't stack the pepper strips; lay them in the wok individually.
7. Keep pressing the peppers with the spatula, flipping them at the right time.
8. Stir-fry for about 2 minutes; when the pepper skins wrinkle, pour in `Seasoning 2`.
9. Turn up the heat, stir-fry for another 30s, and plate.
